Sidebar toggle

A prototype for review. Nothing here is built yet. Try each version, then choose one.

Version
Screen

Press the sidebar button next to the bell, or press [ on your keyboard.

A: Panel button

A button beside the bell with the sidebar icon most apps use for this: a window with its left pane filled and an arrow showing which way it will move. On the rail it sits directly under the mark, so it stays where you last saw it, and the arrow turns to point outward.

Why it works

  • Always visible, never hidden behind a hover.
  • The icon is one people already know from Gmail, Notion and VS Code.
  • It stays at the top in both states and doesn't take a menu row.

Trade-offs

  • It is an icon, not a word, so a first-time user may need the tooltip to be sure.
  • The brand row gets one more control, which fits at 240 px but leaves no room for another.

B: Edge handle

A small round handle on the sidebar's right edge, level with the mark, with an arrow that points the way the edge will move. The edge itself darkens while you point at the handle, so it reads as "drag this edge in".

Why it works

  • The arrow says which way it goes, and it sits on the edge that moves.
  • The brand row stays as it is today.

Trade-offs

  • It hangs 12 px over the page. DESIGN.md says controls don't float over content, so this would need a recorded exception.
  • The handle is drawn at 24 px. Its hit area is padded to 44 px, but it still looks small.
  • A handle on the edge is less familiar than a button in the header.

C: Labelled row

A "Collapse sidebar" row at the bottom of the menu, written out in words with its shortcut key. On the rail it becomes the expand icon in the same spot.

Why it works

  • The clearest of the three: it says exactly what it does.
  • The key next to it teaches the shortcut.

Trade-offs

  • It sits at the bottom, away from the top of the screen where people look for it.
  • It takes a row, and roles with long menus (RDCO, Administration) push it further down.
  • It looks like a page link but does something different.

What's wrong today

The IRIS mark is the only thing that collapses the sidebar. It looks like a logo, so nothing tells you it can be pressed. The only hint is a "Collapse sidebar" tooltip, which you only see if you happen to point at the mark first.

The same in every version

  • The mark is just the logo again. It takes you to your home page and no longer toggles anything.
  • The [ key collapses and expands the sidebar, except while you are typing in a field.
  • The button's tooltip names the action and its key on hover and on keyboard focus, and screen readers hear "Collapse sidebar" or "Expand sidebar", plus whether it is expanded.
  • Your choice is saved in this browser, as it is now.
  • On a tablet the same button opens the full sidebar over the page. Escape, the button again, or a tap on the page closes it.
  • On a phone nothing changes: the drawer keeps its Close button.
  • Only existing IRIS tokens are used. No new colour, type size or font.
