# IRIS

IRIS distinguishes interim registration information from approved institutional policies.

## Language

**Interim data notice**:
The temporary explanation of IRIS registration data use presented before the university's approved Terms of Service and Privacy Policy replace it.
_Avoid_: Approved Privacy Policy, Terms of Service

**Signup acceptance**:
A registrant's recorded agreement at account creation, distinct from consent for an individual research disclosure. Historical signup acceptance does not establish agreement to replacement documents.
_Avoid_: Disclosure consent, acceptance of all future policies

**Disclosure consent**:
A submitter's recorded consent for an individual research disclosure, distinct from signup acceptance and ownership of the research.
_Avoid_: Signup acceptance, transfer of research ownership
