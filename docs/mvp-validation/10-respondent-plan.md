# 10 — Respondent Plan

Who participates, how many, by what method, and who must be retained between phases.

---

## The population is bounded

**This is the fact that governs the whole plan.** IRIS's clearance workflow involves four CIT-U offices. If each has 1–3 staff performing clearance reviews, the eligible SME population is roughly **4–12 people**. You cannot recruit a thirteenth ITSO reviewer if ITSO has two.

The methodological consequence: Phase 3's SME strand is a **census of the accessible population**, not a sample. No power calculation applies, no sampling frame is needed, and no inferential generalisation is claimed ([11](11-analysis-plan.md)).

**Exact figures: NEEDS RDCO DATA** — [09](09-baseline-data.md), items 22–23.

## Phase 1 — Weeks 1–2

Depth over count.

| Type | Target | Minimum | Method | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SMEs | **1–2 per office (4–8)** | **4 — one per office** | Demonstration + interview | 45–60 min |
| Students | 3–5 | 3 | Demonstration + hands-on | 30–45 min |
| Advisers | 1–2 | 1 | Demonstration + interview | 30 min |
| Decision-makers | 1–2 | 1 | Demonstration + interview | 30 min |
| **Total** | **9–17** | **9** | | |

**Four SMEs is the hard floor** — one per office. With fewer, an office's perspective is simply absent from the design refinement, and that office is also missing from Phase 3.

**On the 30-respondent target.** This is a programme-level figure across both phases, not a Phase 1 quota. Thirteen people interviewed properly produce more actionable refinement than thirty questionnaires from people who were never walked through the system. **NEEDS ADVISER CONFIRMATION** that a phased distribution toward 30 total is acceptable, and whether the count is per-phase or cumulative.

## Phase 3 — Weeks 11–12

| Type | Target | Minimum | Method | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SMEs — controlled comparison** | **Census: all available reviewing staff** | **4** | Scenario session, both conditions | 90 min |
| SMEs — organic pilot | Same people, real submissions | 4 | Passive instrumentation + exit interview | 20 min |
| Students — SUS | 8–12 | 6 | Pilot use + SUS | 10 min |
| Students — NFR-U2 timed task | **Exactly 5** *(SRS specifies five)* | 5 | Timed submission after onboarding | 15 min |
| Advisers | 2–3 | 1 | Pilot use + SUS + exit interview | 30 min |
| Decision-makers | 1–2 | 1 | Exit interview | 30 min |
| **Total** | **20–27** | **17** | | |

**SUS requires ≥ 3 role groups** (`NFR-U1`) — satisfied by students, advisers and office staff.

**The NFR-U2 count is fixed at five** by the SRS validation method, not chosen.

**If fewer than 4 SMEs are available:** report the achieved N explicitly and reframe the comparison as a **multiple case study** rather than presenting weak aggregates ([11](11-analysis-plan.md)). Do not pad the N with people who do not perform clearance reviews.

## Retention

**Participants recruited in Phase 1 should carry into Phase 3.** This is not administrative convenience — it is methodologically material.

| Reason | Effect |
|---|---|
| The SME population is bounded | There is no second pool. Losing one office in Phase 1 likely loses it in Phase 3 |
| Familiarity reduces Phase 3 orientation time | A 90-minute session is already long |
| Longitudinal insight | *"You saw this in Week 2 — what changed?"* is available only with retention |
| Trust | Institutional staff give franker feedback the second time |

**Ask for the Phase 3 commitment at first contact**, not in Week 10. It is question 15 of the SME guide and question 8 of the end-user guide ([06](06-validation-instruments.md)).

**Track it:**

| Participant | Type | Office | Phase 1 | Phase 3 committed | Phase 3 attended |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |

**Retention target: ≥ 75% of Phase 1 SMEs.** Below that, escalate — it likely signals a scheduling or relationship problem rather than individual availability.

---

## Recruitment

**Route.** Through RDCO as the institutional contact — approaching office staff directly, unannounced, is likely to fail and may damage the pilot commitment.

**Sequence.**

1. **Week 1, day 1** — written request to RDCO covering both the baseline data ([09](09-baseline-data.md)) and participation
2. **Week 1** — RDCO nominates contacts per office
3. **Week 1** — individual invitations with purpose, duration and consent information
4. **Week 2** — sessions
5. **At each session** — request the Phase 3 commitment
6. **Week 10** — confirm Phase 3 scheduling

**Students and advisers** may be recruited directly through the department, subject to whatever the ethics process requires (**NEEDS ADVISER CONFIRMATION**).

**Incentives.** None assumed. If the programme permits any, apply them consistently and disclose them in the thesis.

---

## Selection criteria

**SMEs — include:** currently performs clearance or approval reviews · from one of the four offices · has handled at least one resubmission.
**SMEs — exclude:** anyone who contributed to IRIS's design or requirements — they cannot assess the model impartially, and their inclusion would be a defensibility problem.

**Students — include:** has submitted or will submit research or an IP disclosure.
**Students — exclude for the NFR-U2 timed task:** any prior IRIS exposure. The requirement specifies *first-time* users, so Phase 1 student participants are ineligible for that specific task in Phase 3 — recruit fresh participants for it.

**Advisers — include:** currently supervises students who submit Proposals.

**Decision-makers — include:** authority over process adoption or budget.

---

## Ethics and consent

**NEEDS ADVISER CONFIRMATION** on the required process. Planned regardless:

- Written or recorded informed consent before every session
- Explicit permission for audio recording, separately from participation consent
- Right to withdraw at any point, with data destroyed on request
- Anonymisation in all outputs — role and office, never names
- RA 10173 compliance for participant data and timing records
- Session recordings stored encrypted, deleted after transcription and analysis ([13](13-data-collection-plan.md))
- **No real unpublished IP** in evaluation scenarios

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Fewer than 4 SMEs available** | Contribution cannot be evaluated as designed | Escalate in Week 1. Reframe as multiple case study. **This is a project-level risk, not a scheduling inconvenience** |
| **Pilot commitment declined** | No organic strand | Strand B is self-contained and unaffected ([03](03-final-evaluation-plan.md)) |
| Phase 1 SMEs unavailable in Phase 3 | Lost continuity | Commitment secured at first contact; over-recruit in Phase 1 where an office has capacity |
| Office availability clusters badly | Sessions cannot be scheduled | Book Week 10; offer multiple slots; be willing to travel to their office |
| Participants have IRIS design involvement | Bias | Excluded by the criteria above |
| Ethics approval delays | Sessions blocked | Raise in Week 1, not Week 3 |
