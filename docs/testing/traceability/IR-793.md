# IR-793: Settings shows a person's AI answer and lets them change it

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** ADR-054 decisions 11 to 13, under IR-779. Builds on IR-791, which added `POST /users/me/ai-answer/` and made a paper keep its Contact person's answer. No FR- id.

**What changed:**

- `features/settings/AiAnswerPanel.tsx`, the "May AI use your papers?" panel on Settings → Data privacy & consent. It reads `GET /auth/notice/` and shows the current answer and the notice version it was given under, with its date. **Change to No** or **Change to Yes** opens a confirmation:
  - **Yes to No** says it only affects papers from now on, and that earlier papers keep their Yes and stay in use by AI (decision 12). It sends `{ai_consent: false}`.
  - **No to Yes** asks "Which papers does your Yes cover?" with All my papers and Only papers from now on, neither preselected (decision 13). "All" says the text will be sent to AI companies and can't be recalled. Saving without a choice says to choose and sends nothing. It sends `{ai_consent: true, papers}` and says how many papers now hold the Yes.
  - A refusal shows the server's reason. Keep my answer backs out.
- **No per-paper control:** the panel says each paper keeps the answer it started with and there is no setting per paper. No paper screen has a per-paper AI control: IR-791 removed the publish step's consent.
- `features/auth/notice/NoticeAnswerFields.tsx`: the papers question moved into an exported `WhichPapers` component, which the notice screen and Settings both use, so the wording lives in one place. The notice screen behaves as before.
- `settingsParts.tsx` gains `Message`, shared with IR-787's panels. `api/accounts.ts` gains `changeAiAnswer`.

**Evidence (2026-11-08, Windows):**

- Vitest, `features/settings/AiAnswerPanel.test.tsx` (8 tests, through the Settings page):

  - No to Yes shows both choices unchecked, with "All" describing that the text can't be recalled, and axe passes; saving without a choice says to choose and sends nothing;
  - each choice is sent, and the count of papers updated is said;
  - Keep my answer sends nothing;
  - a refusal shows the server's reason;
  - at 360 px every button row wraps and no text is clamped.
