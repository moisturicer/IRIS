## ADR-034 §2 (retrieval outcome classification)

**Requirement:** **Retrieval reports why it returned nothing** — no FR- id; IR-459 (retrieval reports why it returned nothing), a prefactor under [IR-456](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-456) — the evidence decision, Phase 0 and a reader-invisible shadow pilot. "Nothing was found", "everything was withheld by the disclosure gate" and "retrieval raised" collapsed into one empty list and one `no_sources` answer, so a refusal could not be diagnosed and ADR-034 §2 needed a distinction the code could not make

**Design:** Supports [ADR-034](../../adr/034-ungrounded-answers-as-a-distinct-state.md) §2; contradicts no ADR, which is why it merges ahead of ADR-035. The counts carry **no reader-visible change and no API change**, keeping the indistinguishability [ADR-014](../../adr/014-ai-gateway-as-a-service.md) and IR-153 chose at the response boundary (IR-460, empty and withheld results indistinguishable at the response boundary, owns that boundary)

**Implementation:** `apps/ai/retrieval/diagnostics.py` — six outcomes (`failed` → `empty` → `withheld_all` → `none_relevant` → `found_relevant` → `found_unassessed`), `ChunkBuckets` (four terminal buckets as **sets of chunk ids**, plus `gate_evaluated`), `RetrievalConfiguration`, `StageDiagnostics` and a total `classify`. **Classification is over the selection stage only** — the full-recall counts are diagnostics and take no part in it, because tying them together left a reachable state with no applicable outcome (no-op reranker: 100 recalled, 8 reach the gate, all 8 withheld, nothing kept, no floor, every outcome false). `apps/ai/retrieval/ports.py` — `RetrievalResult.diagnostics` carries the recall stage, and `with_passages(..., diagnostics=...)` is the one field a decorator may deliberately replace; everything else still carries forward (the IR-334 lesson). `apps/ai/retrieval/reranking.py` — `_gate_runs` and `_observed` record what was recalled, gated and trimmed, on both the early-return and the reranked path. `apps/ai/answers/selection.py` — `SourceSelection.select()` returns a `Selection` (passages + `RetrievalDiagnostics`); `apply()` is unchanged, so the eval harness still measures the final set with no model. `apps/ai/answers/service.py` — `_retrieve_and_gate` logs the outcome and classifies a raise as `failed` **and re-raises**, so a bug in our own query still reaches the caller. **Absent, not zero:** `below_floor` is absent everywhere (no floor until IR-396) and the degraded path reports no recall stage at all; a gate that ran on nothing reports 0, which is a different fact. `none_relevant` is unreachable until IR-396 and `classify` is already total over it

**Test:** `apps/ai/tests/test_retrieval_diagnostics.py` (33) — `ClassificationTests` is **table-driven** over every reachable combination of (S, withheld, below-floor, kept, assessed), including the no-op-reranker case, and fails if a seventh outcome is added without a row; `PartitionTests` asserts disjointness and exhaustiveness **on the sets**, not on four numbers that happen to add up; `AbsentNotZeroTests` pins the absent/zero distinction on the degraded path; `CountsSurviveTests` drives reranking, fusion and degradation and asserts the report survives each; `NothingReachesTheWireTests` asserts over real `/ai/ask/`, `/ai/search/` and conversation-replay **responses** that no count, bucket name or outcome reaches the wire, and that a withheld-everything ask still reads `no_results`

**Evidence:** `pytest apps/ai` — **1540 passed** 2026-10-06 against the compose Postgres/pgvector (33 of them new); run three times, no failures. `flake8 apps/ai` clean, `manage.py check` clean. The classifier and partition tests need no database. No frontend change and no serializer change, which the HTTP assertions above are the evidence for

**Status:** **BUILT and executed.** Nothing branches on the outcome yet — it is recorded and logged, which is what IR-460 (the response boundary) and ADR-035's shadow pilot consume. `relevance_assessed` is `False` everywhere, so surviving evidence is `found_unassessed` — **unassessed, never irrelevant** — until IR-396 builds ADR-033 §3's floor and flips it. A derivable `relevance_assessed` field and a `completion` field are deliberately **omitted**; `degraded` is kept, being underivable
