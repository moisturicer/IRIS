## IR-947

**Requirement:** **Discover filters by a year range (from–to)** — no FR- id; subtask of IR-552

**Design:** decided by Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-16, answering the card's three open questions: (1) the Filters panel shows two controls, **Year from** and **Year to**, each defaulting to "Any year", and one year is the same From and To; (2) **the single `year` URL key is retired, not kept for old links** — everything uses `year_from` and `year_to`; (3) saved feeds store `year_from`/`year_to` only, and a one-time data migration turns each stored `year` into both. No ADR describes the year filter, so none changes.

**Implementation:** Frontend: `DiscoverFilters.year` becomes `yearFrom` and `yearTo`; `discoverQuery.ts` reads and writes `year_from`/`year_to`, drops a malformed end, swaps a range written backwards, and never reads `year`: it removes it from the address, so an old link is corrected rather than carried along; `listParams` sends only the ends that are set (the list endpoint already took both); `feedCriteria` says "Years: 2018 to 2021", "2018 onward", "up to 2021" or "Year: 2019". `DiscoverFilterBar` shows the two controls, and `withYear()` keeps a range from ending before it starts (a From after the To moves the To up, and the reverse); the range counts as one active filter. `DiscoverPage` offers the catalogue's years to both ends, plus any year the URL holds that the catalogue does not list. `FeedQueryKey` and `scripts/query-keys.json` replace `year` with `year_from` and `year_to`, each a four-digit year, as safe as `year` was (IR-918 registry: adding a key is a review decision named here). Backend: `apps/library/feeds.py` accepts `year_from`/`year_to` (four digits each), refuses a From after the To, and refuses `year`; migration `library/0008_savedfeed_year_range` (data only) turns `{"year": Y}` into `{"year_from": Y, "year_to": Y}`, and backwards turns a range into `year` from its start (narrower, never wider).

**Test:** `discoverQuery.test.ts`: every field including the range, either end open, a backwards range put right, a malformed end or the retired `year` falls back to the default, round-trip, a feed stored in the new keys, the four ways a range is described, and list params for each end set. `DiscoverPage.test.tsx`: both controls are labelled; a range with either end open reaches the list endpoint and the URL and counts as one filter; a From after the To moves the To; `?year=` is not read; For you, URL restore, safe defaults, the catalogue's years and saving filters into a feed use the range. `urlPrivacy.test.ts` writes a range under allowed keys. Backend `apps/library/tests.py`: a feed saved with a range, open ends and one year accepted, and a malformed end, a backwards range and `year` refused.

**Review:** a standards and a spec review found one defect, fixed: an old `?year=` stayed in the address (unapplied) because `writeDiscoverQuery` removes only keys Discover owns; it now also removes retired keys, with a unit and a page test. The from-after-to check is now one function, `endsBeforeItStarts`, shared by the filter bar and the URL parser. `docs/testing/CORE_JOURNEYS.md` row 6a names the range. Left as is: the stray-year ordering in the options list, and a manual (not automated) migration check, recorded below.

**Deliberate test changes:** every test that used the single `year` (Discover page, `discoverQuery`, `urlPrivacy`, saved feeds) now uses `year_from`/`year_to` (in "keeps filters across feeds", choosing From 2009 under a To of 2026 now sends `2009`–`2026`, not `2009`–`2009`), and the Filters label test names "Year from" and "Year to" instead of "Year (completed)", because the card's decisions replace the single year. `e2e/specs/07-discover.spec.ts` sets Year from and Year to to the same year instead of choosing one Year.
