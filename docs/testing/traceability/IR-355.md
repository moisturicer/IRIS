## IR-355

**Requirement:** **Following a cross-paper citation keeps the originating Paper Chat conversation** — the panel stays on the conversation the reader followed a citation from

**Design:** [ADR-026 §9, §Amendment — 2026-10-06](../../adr/026-conversational-retrieval-and-memory.md) (the conversation owns its scope; a citation it produced may pin it); [ADR-019](../../adr/019-persisted-unified-conversation-history.md) one Conversation per Record, unchanged; Jira IR-355

**Implementation:** `lib/citedPage.ts` gains `CitationOriginContext` and `followedFromPaperChat`, and `citationNavigationState` takes an optional origin, so a citation followed from Paper Chat carries `origin: "paper-chat"` beside the citation it already carried for the highlight; `CitationText` and `OpenPassageLink` read the context, and `PaperChatPanel` provides it around its transcript. `PaperChatPanel` holds its own subject record: it is the page's record when the panel opens, stays put on an arrival marked from Paper Chat, and moves to the page's record on any other arrival or on "Chat about this paper instead". The conversation is found by the subject, and its widen resets only when the subject changes. While pinned the header reads "Chatting about <X>", and the scope control and the composer's accessible name name X rather than "this paper". A history entry restores the chat it was left with, so Back and Forward follow the mark the entry carries. `PaperViewPage` no longer replaces the whole page with `LoadingSkeleton` when moving between records: only the main column becomes `MainColumnSkeleton` (and the rail is withheld), so the panel stays mounted, and it passes `arrival` only once the record the URL names has loaded. The conversation id is cleared while a new one loads, so a question typed mid-switch cannot land in the old conversation. No backend change

**Test:** `citedPage.test.ts` (the origin mark, and its absence for every other navigation), `PaperViewPage.test.tsx` (on X with All papers on, following a citation to Y keeps X's conversation and header and widen with no second fetch; the panel stays mounted while Y loads; a follow-up goes to X's `conversationId` with `widen: true`; "Chat about this paper instead" switches to Y's conversation, named Y, scope reset; closing and reopening, reaching Y by a plain link, and a page that only remembers a marked citation all show Y's own conversation; a same-paper citation pins nothing; changing the panel position keeps the pin; while pinned the scope control and the composer name X, never "this paper"; Back to X re-aligns, while Forward to Y and Back while hopping between the conversation's citations keep the pin; axe clean while pinned)

**Evidence:** CI on the PR (not run locally, per the team's CI-first rule)
