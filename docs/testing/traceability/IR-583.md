## IR-583

**Requirement:** **Highlights stay tied to the Manuscript version they came from** — no FR- id; subtask 6 of IR-553 (User Stories 12–13)

**Design:** [ADR-065](../../adr/065-highlights-are-bound-to-the-manuscript-bytes.md) (**Proposed**, 2026-11-12, awaiting acceptance by Lee Jasmin Adolfo) · [ADR-064](../../adr/064-private-reading-notes.md) §6 · [ADR-031](../../adr/031-pdf-citation-overlay.md) · [16-paper-view-and-review.md](../../ui-ux/16-paper-view-and-review.md) amendment 2026-11-12

**Implementation:** `apps/library`: `ReadingHighlight` (owner, Record, `manuscript_revision`, `manuscript_sha256`, page, quote, prefix, suffix, regions), migration `0004_readinghighlight`, `highlights.py` (validation, `current`/`stale`, the unavailable shape) and `GET /library/highlights/` (the account list), `GET`/`POST /library/highlights/<record_id>/`, `DELETE /library/highlights/<record_id>/<id>/`. The server stamps the identity (`records.versions.served_manuscript_identity`); the client sends only the revision it was shown, from the new `X-Manuscript-Revision` header on `GET /records/<id>/manuscript/`, and a mismatch is `409 manuscript_changed` with nothing stored. A stale highlight keeps its original identity and is never moved; `open_version` names a Version that still holds its bytes. An unreadable Record is a `404` on the per-Record routes and an `{id, record_id, available: false}` entry in the list. Not in Django admin, no `AuditEvent`; declared in `accounts/deletion/registry.py` (DELETE) and `accounts/personal_data/registry.py` and `export.py` (`reading_highlights`: the quote only while readable). Frontend: `SelectionMenu` (one menu of `SelectionAction`s, so IR-581 adds its own entry), `ReaderHighlights` (percentage-positioned marks, `aria-hidden`), `PaperPdfReader` (selection or find match opens the menu on the current paper only; passes the revision it was opened at), `usePdfDocument` (keeps that revision), `useReadingHighlights` (list, save, remove; a save with no revision sends nothing), the Highlights list in `NotesPanel` (page, quote, "From an earlier version of this paper", Open, Remove), and `PaperViewPage` (draws only highlights made on the bytes shown; Open lands like a citation, in the stale highlight's own Version where the reader may open it).

**Test:** backend `apps/library/test_highlights.py` (identity, page, quote and regions stored; kept apart from the note; the same passage twice is one; newest first; malformed bodies stored nothing; a replaced Manuscript refuses the write with `manuscript_changed` and stores nothing; no identity is `manuscript_unavailable`; a replaced Manuscript makes the old highlight stale without moving it; same revision with different bytes is stale; a new highlight is current beside a stale one; `open_version`; another reader, KTTO, RDCO, ITSO, IERC, Adviser, System Admin and the Record's owner never read or remove one; an owner in the body is ignored; the same 404 for an unreadable Record on read and write; revoked access withholds quote, context, regions and state everywhere and returns with access; the reader can still remove one they cannot open; the account list mixes readable and unavailable; cascade on Record delete; a guard that no other app names the model; not in admin), `apps/records/test_manuscript.py` (the header is the served revision, absent with none), `apps/tests/access/table.py` (`library-highlights`, `library-record-highlights`, `library-highlight`), `accounts/test_account_deletion.py` and `accounts/test_personal_data_export.py`. Frontend `PaperPdfReader.test.tsx` (no menu without actions; the menu for a passage; the action receives the passage and the revision from the header, or null; nothing on an earlier version; marks on their own page only; axe), `NotesPanel.test.tsx` (empty prompt; list apart from the editor; Open on a current highlight; stale labelled in words and not openable on the paper shown; opens in its version only where openable; remove and a failed remove keeps it; load failure and retry; axe) and `PaperViewPage.test.tsx` (save against the revision opened and draw it; a replaced paper says so and draws and saves nothing; a stale highlight is not handed to the reader; Open from Notes lands on page and region; a stale one opens in its Version and is drawn only there; nothing read on Overview). Deliberate test changes: the page test's `@/api/library` mock gains `highlights`, `saveHighlight` and `deleteHighlight`, and its mocked reader renders the new props; `NotesPanel.test.tsx`'s harness passes the new `highlights`, `onOpenHighlight` and `canOpenVersion` props.
