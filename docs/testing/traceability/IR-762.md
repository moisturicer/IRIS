# IR-762

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**An Adviser's Call waits for RDCO to confirm it, and only RDCO features a Call.** Built to [ADR-050](../../adr/050-calls-for-papers-and-rdco-confirmation.md) decisions 8-12, and its amendment of 2026-11-15 (accepted when #808 merged), which the owner decided while this was built. Written 2026-11-15.

## What changed

- **Status** (`Opportunity.status`, `core.enums.CallStatus`): `pending` ("Waiting for RDCO"), `confirmed` or `rejected`. There is no expired status (amendment point 2).
  - RDCO's Calls are confirmed at once; an Adviser's start pending.
  - The model's default is pending, so a Call saved by a path that forgets to decide stays off the board.
  - Migration `opportunities/0003` marks every existing Call confirmed. It also adds `review_reason` and `reviewed_at`, and sorts by deadline only.
- **Who sees what** (`OpportunityQuerySet.visible_to`):
  - The board and the calendar serve confirmed Calls only.
  - A pending or rejected Call is seen by its poster and RDCO. Anyone else gets the same 404 as a missing Call, from the same lookup.
- **New routes:**
  - `GET /opportunities/requests/`: RDCO's queue, pending Calls whose deadline hasn't passed, oldest first.
  - `GET /opportunities/mine/`: a poster's own Calls in every status.
  - `POST <id>/confirm/`, `POST <id>/reject/` (reason required) and `POST <id>/remove/` (reason required): RDCO only.
  - Confirm and reject send back the Call's `updated_at` as RDCO read it (amendment point 4). Each decision locks the Call's row and answers 409 if the Call:
    - isn't waiting any more, including one whose deadline has passed: "This Call isn't waiting for RDCO any more."
    - was edited since: "This Call changed since you opened it. Open it again to see what changed."
  - A Call deleted while a decision or removal was being made answers 404.
- **Editing:**
  - An Adviser's edit sends a confirmed or rejected Call back to pending and off the board, clearing the reason.
  - Editing a pending Call keeps it pending. A request that changes nothing isn't an edit.
  - RDCO's edits never change a status.
  - Each edit runs in one transaction holding the Call's row. A concurrent decision can't be overwritten by a save that read the row before it, and the job can't delete a Call mid-edit. RDCO's notice goes out after the commit, naming the Call as edited.
- **Past deadline** (amendment point 1): an Adviser can't save a Call, new or edited, whose paper deadline is before Manila's today. RDCO stays unchecked.
  - The check is on the deadline the save would leave. An Adviser past their deadline can still edit by moving it forward, so `can_edit` stays true.
- **Removal** (amendment point 3):
  - A poster deletes their own Call in any status. RDCO deletes its own, a colleague's, or one whose poster's account is gone.
  - RDCO deletes an Adviser's Call only through `remove/`, with a reason the Adviser receives. A plain DELETE of one is refused with 400.
  - `remove/` serves only that case. Used on a Call that needs no reason, it answers 400 "This Call needs no reason. Delete it instead."
- **Lapsed requests** (amendment point 2): `apps.opportunities.tasks.remove_lapsed_requests`, scheduled daily at 00:15 Manila (`lapsed-call-requests`).
  - It deletes pending and rejected Calls past their deadline, each under its own row lock, with one notice queued on that commit.
  - A second run finds nothing to delete, so it sends nothing.
- **Featured:**
  - Ignored from anyone but RDCO, on create and edit.
  - RDCO setting it on a rejected Call is refused with 400.
  - It no longer moves a Call up the board.
- **Notices, in-app only** (`apps/notifications`, migration `0012` seeds five types):
  - RDCO gets a broadcast on a new or returned request. It is bound to the Call (task kind `call_request`), so it settles once RDCO decides, the deadline passes or the Call is gone (ADR-069). A resubmission first settles the earlier notice, then sends a new one.
  - The Adviser is told on confirm, reject, removal and lapse. The lapse notice reads "…passed before it was confirmed. You may post a new one.", which fits a pending and a rejected Call alike.
  - A notice that can't be written never fails the act that sent it.
- **Capabilities** on every Call: `can_edit`, `can_delete`, `can_remove`, `can_confirm`, `can_reject`, `can_feature` and `edit_returns_to_pending` (`apps/opportunities/capabilities.py`). The list adds `can_review_requests`.
- **Personal-data download:** `opportunities_posted` adds `status` and `review_reason`.
- **Also:**
  - ADR-050's amendment and its row in the ADR index.
  - `scripts/seed_demo_opportunities.py` seeds RDCO's Calls as confirmed.
  - The frontend types (`types/opportunities.ts`, `api/opportunities.ts`) and the fixtures that build a Call.
- **Not here:** audit events are IR-763. It should cover the lapse job's deletions too (amendment point 2). The screens are IR-765.

## Tests

- **`backend/apps/opportunities/test_call_review.py`**, through the HTTP API:
  - Starting status by role, and a status sent by the client is ignored.
  - Who sees a pending Call: its Adviser and RDCO 200; a Student and another Adviser 404, identical to a missing Call. The calendar 404s for everyone.
  - The queue: pending only, a lapsed request left out, RDCO only. `mine`: own Calls in every status with the reason; posters only.
  - Confirm and reject:
    - Reject needs a reason, which the Adviser receives.
    - A rejected Call stays visible to its Adviser with the reason.
    - Stale decisions answer 409, as does a decision on a Call edited since RDCO opened it. A decision without `updated_at` is a 400. Only RDCO decides.
    - Each notice's exact wording is checked.
  - RDCO's request notice settles "Confirmed on …" after a confirm, and "No longer waiting" after the Adviser deletes the Call or its deadline passes.
  - Edits:
    - A confirmed Call goes back to pending: the earlier notice is settled and a new one sent.
    - A rejected Call resubmits; a pending one stays, with no notice.
    - RDCO's edits keep their status; a request that changes nothing changes nothing.
    - RDCO's new notice names the edited title.
  - Past deadline: refused for an Adviser's new and edited Call, today accepted, RDCO unchecked. An Adviser may move a passed deadline forward.
  - Featured: ignored from an Adviser, set by RDCO on pending and confirmed, refused on rejected, and not reordering the board.
  - Removal:
    - An Adviser deletes their own in any status. RDCO deletes its own, a colleague's and an orphan's with no notice.
    - RDCO's plain DELETE of an Adviser's Call is refused; `remove/` needs a reason, which the Adviser receives, and refuses a Call that needs none. Only RDCO removes.
  - Capabilities for RDCO, the Adviser and a reader on each status, including an Adviser past the deadline and a lapsed request.
  - The personal-data download's new fields.
- **`backend/apps/opportunities/test_lapsed_requests.py`**, the job called directly with the clock frozen at 00:30 Manila on 2026-11-16:
  - Pending and rejected Calls due the 15th are deleted. Those due the 16th, and confirmed ones, are kept.
  - One notice each; a second run deletes and sends nothing; an orphan is deleted with no notice.
  - The beat entry runs it daily at 00:15 Manila and names a registered task.
- **`backend/apps/opportunities/test_call_status_migration.py`:** rewinds to `0002`, migrates, and checks an existing Call comes out confirmed and a new one starts pending; then reverses.
  - Written after the migration, so to check it bites, the migration was changed to leave existing Calls pending: the test failed, and passed again once it was restored.
- **`backend/apps/tests/access/`:** rows for the five new routes, and detail and calendar on a pending Call.
  - The matrix caught a leak while this was built: the calendar answered a pending Call with a different 404 body from a missing one's. It now filters in the lookup.
- **`backend/apps/opportunities/tests.py`, changed deliberately:** `test_featured_sorts_before_a_sooner_deadline` is removed, because Featured no longer moves a Call (decision 12). `FeaturedTests` pins the new order. `make_opportunity` now stores `confirmed`, since the model defaults to pending.
- **Frontend:** `tsc` passes for `frontend/` and `e2e/`.
- **Not done:**
  - Nobody has used these routes by hand, and no screen exists yet (IR-765).
  - Celery beat hasn't been seen firing the job in a running stack.
  - The row locks are exercised in one process only; no test races two requests.
