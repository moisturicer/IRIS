## IR-1278 — Ask IRIS reads a paper's recorded details with `paper_details` under `research-13`

**Basis and scope.** IR-1278; authority ADR-049 and its 2026-11-23 amendment, which is **Proposed** (drafted by an AI agent; it needs the owner's decision before the pin ships). Before this, the runtime could name, count, list and read papers, but not say what was recorded about them: authors, the Adviser, `publication_year`, `year_accomplished`, keywords and the abstract were unreachable, and every year meant `year_completed`.

**What exists now.**
- **`ResearchTools.details(handles)`** (`ai/research/tools.py`, `DETAILS_SQL`, `PaperDetails`). Up to five paper handles this run issued (`DETAILS_PAPERS`); per readable paper, what the Publish form (`RecordWriteSerializer`) records plus `ip_type`: title, authors in entry order, Adviser's name, `year_completed`, `year_accomplished`, `publication_year`, record type, Area, PSCED area, keywords, abstract, origin, `is_ip`, `ip_type`, `for_commercialization`, `community_extension`, `requires_ethics_review`, requested offices. Read under the grant and the row policy: an unreadable, revoked, out-of-scope or never-issued handle is left out, and a call that reads none is `unavailable`/`unknown_handle`.
- **`iris_authz.research_advisers(bigint[])`** (`backend/apps/ai/migrations/0051_research_adviser_names.py`). SECURITY DEFINER. The migration grants the runtime EXECUTE and the verifier its columns when those roles exist, and hands the function to the verifier when run as a superuser; `provision_research_roles` does all of it too, and gives the verifier `first_name`, `middle_initial`, `last_name` and `adviser_name`, and grants EXECUTE to `iris_ai_runtime` alone. Returns the catalogue's name (`User.get_full_name()`) or a deleted Adviser's kept name, only for Records in `research_record_ids()`; never an email.
- **`paper_details` tool** (`ai/agent/tools.py::PAPER_DETAILS`, `_detailed`, `detail`). Offered only under `research-13` (`ai/agent/prompts.py::DETAILS_*`; instructions are research-12's plus one paragraph). The result is `metadata_only`, lists unread handles as `not_found`, and says `recorded_by: submitter`. Its years join the run's `figures`, so they may be stated without a passage handle (a number in the abstract still needs a cited passage); type, Area and PSCED names join `terms`. Title, abstract, Adviser, authors and keywords are quoted as paper text. Prefetchable in a tool group.
- **New AGENT runs pin `agent-research-13`/`research-13`** (`ai/runs/agent_profile.py`). Earlier pins keep their tools and bytes.
- Reader activity shows "Read paper details" (`ai/runs/public.py`); an outage names "reading paper details" (`ai/agent/assurance.py`).
