## IR-659

**Requirement:** every sign-in decision and every registration is audited (ASVS 4.0.3 7.2.1, Level 2), and no audit row holds a password, token or link (7.1.1). The five outcomes: refusal of an unverified account, refusal of a locked account, an axes lockout, registration, and email activation. Sub-task of IR-652; no FR- id.

**Design:** Jira IR-659. Decided 2026-11-15 (Hermoine Zozobrado), asked before building:

1. **A lockout writes one row per attempt axes refuses**, the one that trips the lock and each one during it, as `FAILED_LOGIN` with `reason: too_many_attempts`. No new type. Those requests used to write `FAILED_LOGIN` with no reason, read as a wrong password; now the lockout callable writes the row and the view does not.
2. **Registering writes a new type, `ACCOUNT_REGISTERED`**, against the new account, with the role it asked for. The existing `RECOVERY_REQUESTED` names no account and records an email being queued, not an account being made.
3. ~~Every refusal carries the client address (`ip`).~~ **Reversed 2026-11-16 (Hermoine Zozobrado): no refusal stores the client address**, over the ticket's "records the username tried and the client IP". Audit rows are insert-only (IR-166) and the retention cleanup may not register them (IR-794), so an address in one could never expire. Shipped in PR #818, removed in its follow-up.
4. **A refusal names the account only when the password was right** (unverified, locked by RDCO). A wrong password stays unattached.

Activation needed nothing: it already writes `EMAIL_VERIFICATION_COMPLETED` against the account (IR-643), driven through the real confirm endpoint by `test_email_verification.AuditTests`, which also checks no audit row holds an address or a token.

**Implementation:**

- `backend/apps/accounts/sign_in_audit.py`: `refused(email, reason, user=None)` writes the one `FAILED_LOGIN` shape: `email` and `reason`.
- `backend/apps/accounts/views.py`: `LoginView` writes `invalid_credentials` (also when a password change commits between the check and the session), `unverified` and `locked_by_admin`, and leaves a request axes refused to the lockout callable. `RegisterView` writes `ACCOUNT_REGISTERED` inside the account's transaction.
- `backend/apps/accounts/lockout.py`: `lockout_response`, axes's `AXES_LOCKOUT_CALLABLE`, writes `too_many_attempts` with the username axes was given, on both the WSGI and the ASGI call.
- `backend/apps/audit/models.py` and migration `audit/0031`: `ACCOUNT_REGISTERED`. `frontend/src/types/audit.ts` files it under Account.
- `docs/evaluation/AUDIT_EXPORT.md`: a "Signing in and registering" section with each type's metadata.

**Tests:** `backend/apps/accounts/test_sign_in_audit.py`, through `POST /auth/login/` and `POST /auth/register/`, each event read back from `AuditEvent`:

- a wrong password and an unknown email: `invalid_credentials`, no account, the address; the password typed appears in no row;
- unverified and locked by RDCO: the reason, against that account;
- a successful sign-in writes no refusal, and neither the password nor either token appears in any row;
- no refusal stores the client address, neither the peer nor the forwarded client;
- with axes on: the attempts before the limit are `invalid_credentials` and the one that trips it `too_many_attempts`; an attempt during the lock with the right password is `too_many_attempts` and signs no one in; under ASGI too;
- registering writes one `ACCOUNT_REGISTERED` against the new account with `requested_role`; a refused registration writes none.
