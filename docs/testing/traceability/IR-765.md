# IR-765

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**RDCO's Call Requests page, and an Adviser sees where their Call stands.** Built to [ADR-050](../../adr/050-calls-for-papers-and-rdco-confirmation.md) decisions 8, 10-12 and its amendments of 2026-11-12 (the page sits at `/rdco/call-requests` in the RDCO section) and 2026-11-15 (a lapsed request is deleted, not expired; a decision names the version RDCO read; which removals need a reason), and [ADR-051](../../adr/051-system-admin-account-deletion-and-consent.md). The server side is IR-762's. Written 2026-11-16, on top of IR-764 (PR #846), which it needs.

## What changed

- **Call Requests** (`features/opportunities/CallRequestsPage.tsx`, `/rdco/call-requests`, in the RDCO section after Download Requests): every Adviser's Call waiting for RDCO, oldest first, with every field (About in full, the link written out, Posted by and when).
  - **Confirm** asks first, naming the Call, then puts it on the board.
  - **Reject** needs a reason the Adviser receives (up to 2,000 characters, as the server allows).
  - **Featured** sets or clears the star, and the page keeps the server's new `updated_at`, so a later decision names the version RDCO now sees.
  - A **stale decision** (the server's 409, or a Call gone) shows the server's words in a toast and reloads the list; it never pretends it worked.
- **On the board:** RDCO removes an Adviser's Call with a required reason (`can_remove`, `POST …/remove/`), and sets or clears Featured on a confirmed Call (`can_feature`). A poster gets a **My calls** link.
- **My Calls** (`features/opportunities/MyCallsPage.tsx`, `/opportunities/mine`, RDCO and Advisers): every Call the viewer posted, with its status in words ("Waiting for RDCO", "Confirmed", "Rejected") and RDCO's reason on a rejected one. **Edit and send again** opens the form, which says first that saving sends the Call back to RDCO (IR-764); **Delete** asks first.
  - **Expired:** the card's "expired Calls" no longer exist. Since the 2026-11-15 amendment, a request whose paper deadline passes is deleted by the daily job, and its Adviser gets a notice that opens the board, where a new one can be posted.
- **Notifications open the right place.** A Call's notices carry no Record, so they had nowhere to go. The server now sends a `target` with each notification (`NotificationSerializer.get_target`):
  - a request waiting for RDCO opens Call Requests, and once settled (decided or lapsed) opens nothing, since it has left the queue;
  - "Call Confirmed" opens the board;
  - "Call Rejected" opens My Calls, where the Call waits with RDCO's reason;
  - "Call Removed" and "Call Request Lapsed" open the board: the Call is deleted, so My Calls wouldn't hold it.
  - The type names are constants in `notifications/services.py`, shared by the sender and the serializer.
  - The bell and the Notifications page both read it through `notificationHref`, so they agree. Other notices open their Record as before.
- **Controls come from the API:** every button reads the Call's own `can_confirm`, `can_reject`, `can_feature`, `can_remove`, `can_edit` and `can_delete`. No screen compares role names; only the route table says who may open My Calls, as for every page.
- **Shared pieces:**
  - `CallDetails` draws a Call the same way on all three pages.
  - `callActions.tsx` holds what the three pages do alike: loading a list (`useCallList`), reporting a failed act (`useReportCallFailure`, worded by `callFailure`), the Featured toggle and its button, and the delete flow.
  - `RdcoNoteDialog` takes a `kind`, `"note"` or `"reason"` (2,000 characters, filed under `reason`), and an optional `onFailure`. The Call screens report a refusal as a toast, while a refusal of the text itself stays under the field, linked to it. Download and Delete Requests are unchanged.
- **Docs:** `docs/ui-ux/02-information-architecture.md` §5 gains both screens.
