## IR-272

**Requirement:** **A reviewer requests a revision against the version they reviewed, and the party that asked may withdraw it** — the precondition of ADR-003's clearance-aware resubmission on the new model; touches FR-M5-03 (stable label only)

**Design:** [ADR-032 §5, §11](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md), amended 2026-10-08 (IR-272) · design settled with the project lead 2026-10-08 (grilling; 9 decisions in Jira) · `CONTEXT.md` *Revision request* · Jira IR-272

**Implementation:** `apps/reviews/revisions.py`: `request_revision` lets the holder of an *opened* seat (Adviser, ITSO/IERC/KTTO or RDCO), on a new-model record, write their own `declined` Review — carrying the reason and `Review.version` — and an open `ResubmissionRequest`; one open request per party; the seat stays in review and no assignment, seat or clearance changes. Refuses 404 / 403 (no open seat) / 400 (legacy pipeline, blank reason, unopened seat, the party already asked). `withdraw_revision_request` lets any seat holder of the asking party withdraw (no reason). `decision_blocked_reason` refuses accept & route while any request is open (IR-270/271 reuse it); `office_blocked_reason` refuses Clear and Record finding to the asking office only. Owners are notified of both acts. Endpoints `POST /records/<id>/request-revision/` and `POST /records/<id>/revision-requests/<request_id>/withdraw/`; record detail's `revision` flag (with every open request, reason gated by `may_read_review`); tracker rows gain a derived `changes_requested`, resubmissions gain `version`. Frontend: *Request Revision…* (`request_revision`, EvaluationPage copy verbatim) and *Withdraw revision request* (`withdraw_revision`) in F6's action bar, *Accept & route* disabled with the server's reason, the owner's *Revision requested* banner on Overview, and *Changes requested* on the strip. The legacy Resubmit is no longer offered on a new-model record (IR-273 builds *New version*)

**Test:** `apps/reviews/test_revisions.py` (REST seam, one class per criterion plus the grilled decisions) · `paper-view/RequestRevisionDialog.test.tsx` · `paper-view/ReviewActionBar.test.tsx` · `paper-view/ReviewRoutingTracker.test.tsx` · `features/records/capabilities.test.ts`
