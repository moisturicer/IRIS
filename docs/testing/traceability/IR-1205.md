## IR-1205

**Requirement:** Every letter of the "PROPERTY OF / CIT-U / DO NOT DISTRIBUTE" mark is inked from the same bottom edge to the same top edge of its line, on a downloaded copy and over the reader's pages. Wording, line widths and the rest of the look stay as IR-1198 left them

**Design:** Jira IR-1205; ADR-048, Amendment 2026-11-17 (the IR-1205 correction)

**Implementation:** Strokes have flat ends, so an upright stopped where its centre line did, half a stroke short of the outer edge of a crossbar or a curve, and I, N, T, Y, U, P, R and F came out about a quarter shorter than O, C, S, D, E and B. In `backend/apps/records/watermark.py`, strokes that end open at the top or bottom of a letter now run past it (`MARK_OVERSHOOT`), and `_mark_lines` clips to `_line_bands`, one band per line from the outer edge of its bottom strokes to that of its top ones, which cuts them flat. Y's arms and R's leg were re-angled so the cut ends stay inside the letter's width. `frontend/src/features/records/paper-view/ReaderWatermark.tsx` mirrors the glyphs and clips its path to the same bands (`markPath().bands`, an SVG `clipPath`)

**Test:**

- `backend/apps/records/test_download_watermark.py`, `MarkWordingTests`: `test_every_letter_fills_its_line_from_bottom_to_top` (each letter's ink reaches both edges of its band) and `test_every_line_is_cut_to_one_height` (the three bands). The line widths test is unchanged and still passes.
- `frontend/src/features/records/paper-view/ReaderWatermark.test.ts`: the same three bands, and the rendered path is clipped to them.

**Evidence:**

- 2026-11-17, locally: `MarkWordingTests` pass (4 tests). Run against the IR-1198 glyphs, `test_every_letter_fills_its_line_from_bottom_to_top` fails for F, I, N, P, R, T, U and Y. The database-backed tests in the file run in CI, since local Postgres has no pgvector. `flake8` clean on both files.
- 2026-11-17, locally: `vitest run src/features/records/paper-view/` passes (55 files, 965 tests); `tsc --noEmit` and `eslint` clean.
- 2026-11-17, local renders (not the live site): the download overlay on a Letter page, through pypdfium2, and the reader's SVG, through headless Edge, show the same shapes, with every letter level at the top and the bottom of its line.
