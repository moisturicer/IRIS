# IR-869: under restart-all, every reopened office gets a fresh due date

**Requirements:** FR-M5-01 (a stable label only). The rule is [ADR-043](../../adr/043-review-turnaround-reminders-and-escalation.md) §5, as corrected on 2026-11-04.

ADR-043 §5 says every assignment that reopens on a new Version gets a fresh due date. Under `RESTART_ALL`, ADR-032 §5's IR-632 amendment reopens every completed office whose clearance resets, alongside the parties that asked. Before this fix, `submit_new_version` started a clock only for the parties that asked. A reopened office kept the due date from its previous round, which had already passed, so the reminder job sent its coordinator **"Review Overdue" at once**, and its reviewer got no "due soon" notice. (IR-869's card and the first version of this note said the office had *no* due date. The reminder test below showed what actually happened.) So the two arms of ADR-004's comparison differed in reminders and escalation as well as in who reviews again, which breaks IR-137's criterion "the two arms differ in nothing but the policy".

The clock loop now runs over every party that reviews the new Version (`rereview`) instead of only those that asked (`apps/reviews/new_version.py`). `CLEARANCE_AWARE` is unchanged, because there the parties that review again are the ones that asked. An office still reviewing when the Version arrives reopened nothing, so it keeps its date under both policies.

**Evidence:** `apps/reviews/test_deadlines.py::VersionDeadlineTests`

- `test_restart_all_gives_every_reopened_office_a_fresh_date`: ITSO reopens with `due_at`, `original_due_at` and `clock_started_at` taken from the Version. IERC, which asked, gets the same. RDCO, still reviewing, keeps its date.
- `test_restart_all_reopened_office_is_reminded_on_its_fresh_date`: runs the real reminder job (`run_deadline_notifications`) the day the Version arrives. The reopened ITSO reviewer and the requesting IERC reviewer each get "Review Due Soon", and nobody gets "Review Overdue". Before the fix, ITSO's coordinator got "Review Overdue" and ITSO's reviewer got nothing.
- `test_restart_all_reopened_office_with_no_turnaround_gets_no_date`: with no Turnaround row, the reopened office still gets no date (ADR-043 §2).
- The existing `test_new_version_refreshes_only_requesting_party_and_suspends_revision_clock` still passes, which covers the `CLEARANCE_AWARE` side.

All three new tests failed before the fix and pass after it. A second local run on 2026-11-04, `pytest apps/reviews/test_deadlines.py apps/reviews/test_reminders.py`, gave **49 passed, 0 failed**. CI passed all nine checks on the first two tests. Run on 2026-11-04 on Windows against a throwaway pgvector container: `pytest apps/reviews/test_deadlines.py apps/reviews/test_new_version.py apps/reviews/test_revisions.py apps/reviews/test_decisions.py` gave **123 passed, 8 failed**. The 8 failures are the PDF-upload tests in `test_new_version.py` (`UploadVersionTests`, `ExtractionFollowsSubmissionTests`). They fail the same way on the unchanged code, with "PDF checking is temporarily unavailable", because `apps/documents/pdf_inspection.py` imports the Unix-only `resource` module. CI runs them on Linux.

**Not changed:** the FR-M5-01 row's status.
