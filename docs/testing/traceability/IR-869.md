# IR-869: under restart-all, every reopened office gets a fresh due date

**Requirements:** FR-M5-01 (a stable label only). The rule is [ADR-043](../../adr/043-review-turnaround-reminders-and-escalation.md) §5, as corrected on 2026-11-04.

ADR-043 §5 says every assignment that reopens on a new Version gets a fresh due date. Under `RESTART_ALL`, ADR-032 §5's IR-632 amendment reopens every completed office whose clearance resets, alongside the parties that asked. Before this fix, `submit_new_version` started a clock only for the parties that asked. A reopened office reviewed again with no new due date, and no reminder or escalation was sent. So the two arms of ADR-004's comparison differed in reminders as well as in who reviews again, which breaks IR-137's criterion "the two arms differ in nothing but the policy".

The clock loop now runs over every party that reviews the new Version (`rereview`) instead of only those that asked (`apps/reviews/new_version.py`). `CLEARANCE_AWARE` is unchanged, because there the parties that review again are the ones that asked. An office still reviewing when the Version arrives reopened nothing, so it keeps its date under both policies.

**Evidence:** `apps/reviews/test_deadlines.py::VersionDeadlineTests`

- `test_restart_all_gives_every_reopened_office_a_fresh_date`: ITSO reopens with `due_at`, `original_due_at` and `clock_started_at` taken from the Version. IERC, which asked, gets the same. RDCO, still reviewing, keeps its date.
- `test_restart_all_reopened_office_with_no_turnaround_gets_no_date`: with no Turnaround row, the reopened office still gets no date (ADR-043 §2).
- The existing `test_new_version_refreshes_only_requesting_party_and_suspends_revision_clock` still passes, which covers the `CLEARANCE_AWARE` side.

Both new tests failed before the fix and pass after it. Run on 2026-11-04 on Windows against a throwaway pgvector container: `pytest apps/reviews/test_deadlines.py apps/reviews/test_new_version.py apps/reviews/test_revisions.py apps/reviews/test_decisions.py` gave **123 passed, 8 failed**. The 8 failures are the PDF-upload tests in `test_new_version.py` (`UploadVersionTests`, `ExtractionFollowsSubmissionTests`). They fail the same way on the unchanged code, with "PDF checking is temporarily unavailable", because `apps/documents/pdf_inspection.py` imports the Unix-only `resource` module. CI runs them on Linux.

**Not changed:** the FR-M5-01 row's status.
