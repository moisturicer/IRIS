# IR-812: owners and the Adviser set or extend an embargo, and only the Adviser or RDCO release it

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** ADR-052 decisions 5 to 8 and 10, and its IR-812 Amendment. No FR- id.

**The acts:**

- `POST /records/<id>/embargo-set/` with `{"embargoed_until": date}`: owners or the Adviser, on a submitted paper. Sets an embargo, moves an active one's date, or holds a released paper again. The date is required and can't be in the past, so no act blanks one.
- `POST /records/<id>/embargo-release/`: the Adviser or RDCO, on an active embargo. It stamps who and when, keeps the date, and sends the paper on through `send_to_ai_on_commit`, which queues its indexing and asks again for its AI Overview. The check still decides, so an IP mark or missing consent holds it back.
- Both refuse a draft, which keeps `embargo-answer/`. Who may act is checked before where the embargo stands, so a refusal says nothing about it.
- Setting an embargo where the check could already have let text out (answered "no", or released) adds a `notice` saying text already sent can't be recalled.

**Earlier chat answers:** a Turn citing a paper the check now refuses stays out of `_recent_turns`, older-Turn context, recall and `plan_turns`, through `apps/ai/history.py::sendable_record_ids`. `embed_turn_answer` checks again right before sending, and an answer finished as an embargo lands isn't embedded. The reader keeps the Turn in their transcript, and a release lets it back in.

**Audit:** `EMBARGO_ANSWERED`, `EMBARGO_SET`, `EMBARGO_EXTENDED` and `EMBARGO_RELEASED`, written by `apps/records/embargo.py` inside each act. Keys are in `docs/evaluation/AUDIT_EXPORT.md`. There is no backfill.

**`capabilities`** carries `set_embargo` and `release_embargo`, and `apps/tests/access/table.py` has both routes.

**`apps/tests/test_enum_vocabulary.py` needed no change, though the ticket names it.** These are `AuditEvent` constants, which it doesn't govern, and no new enum was added.

**Tests changed deliberately:**

- `test_capabilities.py`: every submitted row gains `set_embargo` for owners and the Adviser, and there's a new `under_embargo` row.
- `test_conversation_state.py`: `_record` now answers for its paper, because a Turn citing an unanswered paper is correctly withheld. Those tests revoke by visibility, which is unchanged.
- `test_memory_http.py`: the window is read inside the test's own root, since it now asks the root what may be sent.
- `test_controller_history_versions.py`: its paper is answered, so those tests are about stale versions alone.
- `readable_turns` is renamed `sendable_turns`, because that's what it returns now. No other assertion changed.

**Tests:** `apps/records/test_embargo_acts.py` (each role and state over HTTP, the date rules, the notice, audit rows, capabilities) and `apps/ai/tests/test_embargo_every_way_out.py` (`TestTheActsThroughTheRealGate`, `TestEarlierAnswersThatQuoteThePaper`: real gate, fake vendors, and each history test first shows the Turn does go out while the paper is clear).

**Evidence, 2026-11-02:**

- **Failing first:** before the history change, four tests reproduced the leak. After an embargo was set, an old chat answer quoting the paper still reached the next prompt, the window, recall and the backfill plan. Each test first shows the Turn going out while the paper is clear, so a pass can't come from an empty window.
- **Full backend suite on this code, before merging `main`:** 5,576 passed, 1 failed, 1 xfailed. The failure is local-only and untouched here: `test_the_proxy_audit_pins_the_proxy_set_it_audited` (Windows line endings).
- **Frontend:** 130 files and 1,704 tests passed, `tsc` clean.
- **A real server, on a copy of one developer's dev database migrated forward through `audit/0014`:** the seeded dated paper, over real HTTP as `student@`, `adviser@`, `rdco@` and `itso@cit.edu`. Each role was offered the right acts. The student's and ITSO's releases and RDCO's set got 403, and a null date got 400. The Adviser extended it, RDCO released it, a second release got 400, and the student held it again and was told text can't be recalled. It left one audit row per act, and the release put one job on the `embedding` queue. **This ran before the review's fixes**, which reshaped the draft refusal and the history check, and both are covered by the suites above.
- **Not run:** no worker consumed the job and no real vendor was called. There's no screen for these acts until IR-817.

**Known limits:**

- **It goes by what a Turn cites,** the only sources a Turn stores. An answer repeating a paper without citing it still goes back to the model. See ADR-052's IR-812 Amendment.
- **A broker outage at the moment of release** leaves the release standing and the paper unqueued, with only a log line, the same as `submit`. `backfill_embeddings` catches it up, and so does setting and releasing it again.
- **A route override reuses its Turn's stored rewrite** (`override_turn.resolved_question`). That's the reader's question reworded rather than paper text, so it isn't filtered.
- **`ConversationState`'s topic and filters** still go to the planner. They describe the reader's request. Its paper handles do check disclosure.

**Migration:** `audit/0014_embargo_events` changes the `event_type` choices only, with no schema or data change.

**Status:** Implemented; awaiting review.
