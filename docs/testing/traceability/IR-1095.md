# IR-1095: Discover: the whole card opens the paper, and a question is offered Ask IRIS

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Epic IR-920 (S2 Epic: Frontend UI/UX Redesign). [`frontend_ui_audit_spec.md`](../../frontend_ui_audit_spec.md) §4.6.

## What changed

- **The whole card opens the paper.** `ResearchCard`'s title link was already stretched over the card (IR-405); this adds the missing half: keyboard focus on the title now draws its ring on the stretched `::after`, so the ring outlines the whole card instead of the title text. Save, Cite and Not interested stay separate controls above the link (`z-10`). Discover's cards have no author links (the byline is text), so nothing else needed lifting. The change is in the shared card, so My Library and My Reviews rows get the same ring.
- **A question gets one line, not a permanent one.** Once the results are in (not while loading or after a failed load), when the search ends in "?", or opens with a question word and runs to at least three words, one line above the results offers "Ask IRIS this question", a link to `/ai` carrying the question as the existing editable draft (`askHandoffState`, IR-559). A catalogue search ("dengue forecasting", "can opener") shows nothing. The empty state of a search with text offers "Ask IRIS instead" beside Clear filters; a filter-only empty state does not.
- **Search is by word.** `searchTerms` splits what was typed into words, drops punctuation and stopwords, and `listParams` asks the catalogue for those words (DRF `SearchFilter` already requires every word, in title, abstract or authors, but not "can", "?" or "the"). The URL keeps the reader's own sentence. If every word is a stopword the typed words are kept. Cards highlight each matched word (`highlightTerms`) in the byline, abstract and reading-card hook.
