## IR-1168 — A follow-up sees which Turn it is answering and what that Turn cited

**Basis and scope.** IR-1168 under IR-1152; authority ADR-049 (2026-11-16 amendment, IR-1168). Built on IR-1166's `research-9`. Plan: "Ask IRIS answer quality plan (main)", Q3.3.

**What changed (`agent-research-10` / `research-10`, the new default pin).**
- `ai/runs/store.py`, `ai/runs/executor.py`: a claim loads each earlier Turn's citations as `PriorTurn.cited`, (Record id, page) in citation order.
- `ai/agent/executor.py`: under research-10, each earlier Turn's question is labelled `[IRIS earlier Turn N]`, N being the number `recover_turn` takes. Under its answer go the papers it cited that the reader can still read, with fresh paper handles and pages. Readability is the run's one `recall` read under the reader's grant, so an unreadable paper is left out, and a Turn with nothing readable left gets no list. If the history would fill the window or reach the compaction threshold with the lists, at opening or later as research grows it, every list is dropped before any Turn is compacted.
- `ai/agent/prompts.py`: research-10's text is research-9's plus one passage on using the Turn numbers and the cited pages.
- `ai/runs/agent_profile.py`: new runs pin `agent-research-10`. Earlier pins get earlier Turns exactly as before, and their text is unchanged.

**Tests.**
- `backend/apps/ai/tests/test_ai_runtime_ir1168_contract.py` (recording provider): numbered Turns with their cited titles and pages; recovery by the number shown; an unreadable paper not listed; a Turn with no citations shown without a list; a cited paper's source carried; research-9's messages unchanged by citations; a history that would reach the compaction threshold loses the lists and keeps every Turn, at opening and after a search grows it, and with room keeps them; research-10's label and list texts are pinned byte for byte.
- `backend/apps/ai/tests/test_ai_runtime_turn_sources_db.py`: a claim reloads a Turn's cited papers and pages in citation order, and none for an uncited Turn.
- Updated deliberately: `test_ai_runtime_prompts_contract.py` records research-10's prefix digest (every pin must be recorded); `ai/tests/test_agent_profile.py` and `test_ai_runtime_executor_selection_db.py` expected the default pin `agent-research-9`; it is now `agent-research-10`.

**Runs.** Locally: `test_ai_runtime_ir1168_contract.py` and `test_ai_runtime_prompts_contract.py` (25 passed), `test_ai_runtime_turn_sources_db.py` (4 passed, real PostgreSQL). The full suite runs on CI.

**Not measured.** No paid evaluation run has been made on research-10. The lists add tokens to every follow-up.
