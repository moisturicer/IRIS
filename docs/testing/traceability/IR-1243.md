## IR-1243

**Requirement:** On My Reviews, "Overdue only" reads as a filter, set apart from the two sort chips, so picking a sort is not expected to switch it off. What the controls do is unchanged: the sort and the filter combine

**Design:** Jira IR-1243 (an office staff tester, 2026-11-19, who expected "Longest waiting" to clear "Overdue only"). Option 1 of three: separate the controls visually. Making them exclusive was rejected because it would remove overdue reviews in a chosen order

**Implementation:** `frontend/src/features/review/MyReviewsPage.tsx`: the sort chips sit in a group labelled by a visible "Sort", and "Overdue only" in its own group labelled by a visible "Filter", behind a divider, drawn square-cornered with a check box (`toggleClass`) instead of as a pill. Both chip kinds are 44 px tall on phones and on touch screens (`min-h-11 md:min-h-9 touch:min-h-11`); this also applies to Done's outcome chips, which share `chipClass`. No backend change

**Test:** `frontend/src/features/review/MyReviewsPage.test.tsx`, "labels the sort and sets Overdue only apart as a filter, not a third sort (IR-1243)": both labels are visible, "Overdue only" is in the Filter group and not in the Sort group, and choosing a sort after it leaves it pressed and asks the server with `sort: "waiting", overdue: true`. The IR-622, IR-1181 and IR-1210 tests pass unchanged

**Evidence:**

- 2026-11-19, locally: the new test failed first (no "Filter" group), then `vitest run src/features/review` passed (101 tests); `tsc --noEmit` and `eslint` clean. `npm run test:changed`: 158 tests passed.
