# IR-1141: Discover list rows show the paper's real first page

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Follows IR-1082 (epic IR-920), which replaced the first-page render with a drawn outline.

## What changed

- `PaperThumbnail` takes `recordId` and `available` and draws the manuscript's first page (pdf.js, page 1) over the 96 px outline. The outline is the placeholder until the page is drawn, and stays when the record has no manuscript or the read fails. Size, `aria-hidden` and the `sm` breakpoint are unchanged.
- It fetches only once the row is within 300 px of the viewport (`IntersectionObserver`).
- `DiscoverResultCard` passes `record.id` and `has_manuscript ?? file_count > 0`.

## Tests

- `PaperThumbnail.test.tsx`: page drawn and the PDF handle destroyed; no fetch without a manuscript; a failed read keeps the outline.

## Evidence and limits

- Ran locally 2026-11-15: the new file and `DiscoverPage.test.tsx` (107 tests) pass; `tsc --noEmit` clean. Full suite left to CI.
- **Not checked in a browser or against a real catalogue.**
- **Known cost:** each visible row downloads its whole manuscript to draw page 1, as before IR-1082. IR-1130 (a small first-page image endpoint, closed) is the fix; this does not do it.
