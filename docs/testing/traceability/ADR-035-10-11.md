## ADR-035 §10 · §11

**Requirement:** **The pilot reports what it found** — no FR- id; [ADR-035](../../adr/035-ask-iris-decides-whether-it-needs-evidence.md) (**Accepted** 2026-10-06). IR-467 under [IR-456](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-456)

**Design:** ADR-035 §10 (two instruments, never conflated; no accuracy from shadow) · §11 (what is out of scope; the pilot is evidence for `on`, not authorization)

**Implementation:** `apps/ai/evaluation/pilot_report.py` (pure) and `manage.py report_evidence_pilot`: curated and shadow sections with separate denominators, coverage broken out (eligible/completed/missing → skipped/failed/unfinished/no-row; reclaimed, fenced, mismatched as overlays), inconclusive categories with the figure withheld, `not recorded` and `unavailable` instead of inference, a sample-plan check that refuses a confirmatory report without a complete predeclared plan, the three unmeasurable properties and the `on` gates stated, deterministic JSON and Markdown. `eval_evidence` provenance gains `prompt_digest` and `generation` on a `--model-decision` run

**Test:** `apps/ai/tests/test_evidence_pilot_report.py` (23 pure + 2 `db_required`)

**Evidence:** All 25 pass 2026-10-08 against the compose Postgres, with `test_evidence_shadow.py` and `test_shadow_off_snapshot.py` (58 together). Report run on available artifacts: curated `proxy_starter` detector-only (113 of 113 annotated after the question set was extended; every category at 10 or more except `mechanism`, which is all positives), no model run, no shadow traffic, no sample plan. The detector alone catches 1 of 33 `mechanism` questions; that is the case for the model half, not a defect

**Status:** **BUILT; the pilot itself has not produced a trustworthy measurement.** No model run, no shadow traffic (ADR-035 §11 retention check unrecorded), no declared exploratory or confirmatory plan. Not Done
