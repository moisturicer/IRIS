## IR-411

**Requirement:** **Paper View sections and the capabilities adapter** — F5 of the IR-374 frontend redesign; no FR- id (FR-M5-03's document vocabulary and FR-M2-01's edit path are touched, stable labels only)

**Design:** [ADR-032 §10](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) (one Paper View driven by capabilities) · [frontend_redesign_spec](../../frontend_redesign_spec.md) §4.6, §4.8 phase 1, §4.10, Appendix F · F5, Appendix K · Jira IR-411

**Implementation:** `features/records/capabilities.ts` is the one place that decides what a viewer may do with a record: phase 1 derives ADR-032 §10's keys from `can_act`, `can_request_document`, ownership and `workflow_state` (`tag_ip` and `attach_file` stay role-derived, inside the adapter; `continue_draft` and `attach_file` join ADR-032 §10 by its 2026-10-06 amendment); IR-418 makes it a pass-through. `PaperViewPage` renders four sections in `?section=` (Overview, Paper, Review, Files), each only under its capability, and falls back to Overview; a section change replaces the history entry and is not an arrival, so Paper Chat's IR-355 pin survives it. *Mark as completed* is removed (ADR-032 retires the act). `FilesSection` replaces `DocumentsPage`: every upload through `UploadDropzone`, a requested slot answered by the slot upload (IR-346), an Other item by its own dropzone, and an office taking part attaches or removes its own supplementary file. `EditDetailsDialog` (on `MetadataForm`) replaces `EditRecordPage` and its wizard steps, offered only under `edit_details`. `document-requests/itemStatus.tsx` is §4.10's one vocabulary (Requested · Uploaded · awaiting review · Accepted · Replacement needed) in the banner, Files and the reviewer list. `/records/:id/documents` and `/records/:id/edit` redirect (`router/redirects.tsx`). `ReviewerDocumentRequests` takes `reviewing` from the caller instead of a role hook. No backend change. **Banner upload (IR-471):** the Action-required banner keeps its compact *Upload* per item (§4.10 as amended 2026-10-06), and the button gains what `UploadDropzone` gives Files: an inline progress bar (`aria-valuenow`, fed by `uploadForRequestItem`'s `onProgress`) in place of the button, so a row cannot start a second upload; a failure alert carrying the server's reason with *Retry* that re-sends the same file; a live region per row (as `UploadDropzone` has one per zone) announcing each start and finish, a failure only through its alert; and keyboard focus carried to the bar, to *Retry*, and back to the row. Added to IR-471's scope on 2026-11-01: a refresh that fails after an upload keeps the list and any upload still running on screen, with an alert inside the panel (only a failed first load replaces it); the Upload label takes `FOCUS_RING`'s keyboard-only ring through `:has(:focus-visible)`; and `components/shared/ProgressBar.tsx` is the one determinate bar, drawn by both `UploadDropzone` and the banner. **Byline complete (IR-472):** the Record detail now carries a read-only `adviser_name` (full name, else email; null with no adviser) under the record's own visibility, and the byline reads authors · adviser · college · year (spec §4.6) — `apps/records/test_adviser_name.py` (API seam, including the IR-153 404) and `PaperViewPage.test.tsx` ("the byline credits the adviser")

**Test:** `features/records/capabilities.test.ts` (the adapter as a table) · `test/roleGuard.test.ts` (the source-scan guard, with fixtures it must catch) · `PaperViewPage.test.tsx` (sections by capability, URL, arrow keys, header actions, Edit details, invariant 5, not-found; the IR-352/354/355/372 tests kept) · `FilesSection.test.tsx` (IR-346's tests carried over from `DocumentsPage.test.tsx`, the vocabulary, retry) · `EditDetailsDialog.test.tsx` · `router/redirects.test.tsx` · `ActionRequiredPanel.test.tsx`
