# IR-1015: Cost and uptime guards on the IRIS demo server

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Epic IR-805 (S2 Epic: Deployment). It covers what the first deployment, 2026-11-10 (IR-806), never set up.

## Decisions (Hermoine Zozobrado, 2026-11-11)

1. **A template of their own,** `deploy/aws/iris-guards.yaml`, given an instance ID, instead of parameters on `iris-demo.yaml` (the ticket's "part of the CloudFormation stack"). That way the guards work today on the hand-built `iris-demo`, and later on a server made by IR-1013's template.
2. **Verified now on `iris-demo`,** not at the next migration. The guards cost nothing, and the site is down only for the test stop and start.

## What changed

- `deploy/aws/iris-guards.yaml` has three parts, each optional:
  - **Budget:** an `AWS::Budgets::Budget` for the whole account (`MonthlyBudgetUsd`, default 100). It emails `BudgetEmail` at 80% of the forecast and 100% of actual spend. There is no budget when the email is empty.
  - **Auto-recover:** a CloudWatch alarm on `StatusCheckFailed_System` (maximum, two 60-second periods) with the `ec2:recover` action. On by default.
  - **Daily stop and start:** two EventBridge Scheduler schedules at `StopTime` and `StartTime` in `ScheduleTimeZone` (default `Asia/Manila`). They run under a role that may only `ec2:StopInstances` and `ec2:StartInstances` this one instance. Off by default.
- A template rule refuses one time without the other.
- `deploy/tests/check_restart_policies.py` fails when a service in `docker-compose.prod.yml` other than the one-shot `migrate` lacks `restart: unless-stopped` or `always`. That policy is what brings IRIS back after a scheduled start or a recovery.
- The `server-bootstrap` CI job lints both templates and runs that check.
- `deploy/README.md` covers what each part does, how to create and change the stack, and what happens after a start.
