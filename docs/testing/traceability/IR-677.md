## IR-677

**Requirement:** The paper page shows where the reader's own download stands, and saves a copy only through the download route

**Design:** Jira IR-677 (spec IR-672); ADR-048

**Implementation:** Record detail carries a server-authored `download` block (`download_state` in `apps/records/download_service.py`: `exempt`, `granted` with its expiry, `none`, `pending`, `declined`, `revoked`, `expired`, plus the server's message), null when signed out. `PaperDownloadControl` picks Download, Request download, Request pending or Request again from it, and Download calls `/records/<id>/download/`. The reader no longer saves its own bytes, and an earlier version offers no download. Notes, Cancel, RDCO's note and My Library's view are IR-743

**Test:** `apps/records/test_download_state.py`; `PaperDownloadControl.test.tsx`; `PaperPdfReader.test.tsx` (saving a copy)

**Evidence:** Passed locally (backend in Docker; Vitest); see PR

**Status:** **PARTIAL** until IR-743
