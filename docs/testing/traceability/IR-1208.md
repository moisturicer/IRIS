## IR-1208

**Requirement:** An Adviser's My Reviews link shows how many reviews wait on them, in the same badge style as RDCO's queues ("9+" above nine, with "N pending" for screen readers). The count is the server's, never includes what they can't open, and disappears at zero

**Design:** Jira IR-1208 (team testing, 2026-11-17)

**Implementation:**

- **Backend:**
  - `apps/reviews/my_reviews.py`: `waiting_count(user)` is My Reviews' To review count, through the same `_to_review_count` the tab uses, so the badge and the tab are one number.
  - `apps/records/views_dashboard.py`: `GET /dashboard/stats/` adds `reviews_waiting_mine`. It is 0 for a role that reviews nothing.
- **Frontend:**
  - `components/layout/Sidebar.tsx` reads `reviews_waiting_mine` from the stats call it already makes for My Library's dot, re-read on every address change. It shows the count as My Reviews' badge.
  - A failed read shows no count rather than a stale one.

**Scope note:** the badge shows for every reviewer whose My Reviews is listed (Adviser, ITSO, IERC, KTTO, RDCO), not only the Adviser. It is the same To review number for each, and RDCO's own queue badges are unchanged.

**Test:**

- `backend/apps/records/test_dashboard_stats.py`, `ReviewsWaitingCountTests`:
  - a record sent to the Adviser counts 1;
  - the count equals the To review tab (2);
  - an opened seat is no longer counted;
  - zero when nothing waits;
  - the owner counts 0.
- `frontend/src/components/layout/Sidebar.test.tsx`, "the My Reviews count (IR-1208)": "My Reviews, 1 pending" links to `/review`; 12 shows "9+" and is read as "12 pending"; zero shows no count.
