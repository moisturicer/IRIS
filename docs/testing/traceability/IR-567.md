## IR-567 For you from the Areas I choose, with a reason on every item

**Design:** [ADR-062](../../adr/062-personal-discovery-recipe-signals-and-snapshots.md) (Proposed, IR-946) records the decisions. The behaviour follows the IR-557 spec text (user stories 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 9 and 12, metadata recipe v1). IR-567 shipped in PR #289 without a traceability file; this one was written afterwards, on 2026-11-07, from the code and tests on `main`, so it states only what they show.

- **Choosing Areas is optional and private.** `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE /records/interests/` read, replace and reset the reader's chosen classification and PSCED Areas, stored in `DiscoveryInterests` (migration `records/0018`). An unknown Area is a 400 and changes nothing. A `PUT` that changes nothing does not bump the revision. The owner always comes from authentication.
- **The feed.** `GET /records/for-you/` (`apps/records/discovery.py`, `get_feed`) lists the published catalogue the reader may read, through `Record.objects.visible_to(user)` and the published statuses. It narrows by Discover's catalogue filters before ranking. Chosen Areas are round-robined in stable Area-ID order, each in added-date order, each Record once, then the rest of the catalogue in added-date order. Older research stays eligible, and a Record in two chosen taxonomies is not repeated.
- **Latest, said honestly.** With no usable signal the response `mode` is `latest` and every reason is `{"kind": "latest"}`; Discover then says to choose an Area rather than calling a recency list personalised.
- **A reason on every item.** An item from a chosen Area carries `{"kind": "selected_area", "area": ...}`, shown as "Because you chose …"; otherwise "Latest research".
- **Stable pagination.** The first page stores its order in a `DiscoveryFeedSnapshot` owned by the reader (migration `records/0018`). The cursor is signed, owner-bound, expires after 24 hours and carries the original catalogue filters. A cursor presented by another account is a 400. An interest change makes an old cursor stale (409, `stale_cursor`), and Discover then asks the reader to refresh. A Record withdrawn or deleted after the snapshot is gone from later pages, with no duplicates.
- **Interface.** `DiscoverPage` offers For you, Latest and Trending (`?feed=` in the URL), an Areas dialog the reader opens themselves ("Choose Areas" or "Edit Areas"), and loads further pages with the cursor. Cards render through `DiscoverResultCard`, which also shows the first-page cover (`ManuscriptCover`, with a "First page not available" placeholder) and a one-sentence hook; this file does not record separate test evidence for those two card parts.
- **Later additions on the same feed:** IR-576 (Not interested, Undo, reset) and IR-575 (saved Records' Areas), each with its own file.

**Tests:** `apps/records/test_discovery_feed.py` covers a no-signal feed labelled Latest with older work eligible, round-robin by stable Area ID with real reasons, PSCED selection and unknown Areas, catalogue filters narrowing before ranking, ties and two-taxonomy Records not repeating, a snapshot owned by its reader and stale after an interest change while revalidating visibility, a filtered cursor keeping its original criteria, and interests private to each account. `DiscoverPage.test.tsx` covers the three feeds, personalised results with the API's explanation, a direct For you link, the honest Latest notice, saving Areas, Load more with the cursor, and a stale cursor refreshing the list.
