## IR-1203: A Documents tab for authors and reviewers, and documents open in the reader

**Basis and scope.** IR-1203 is subtask 5 of IR-1199, the review-page cleanup. It has no FR- id. It takes over the Paper View half of IR-973 and does not wait on IR-971, as decided on 2026-11-17. Decisions made with Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-17:
- **Two PRs.**
  - (a) is this one: the Documents tab, which replaces the "All documents" drawer.
  - (b) will open documents in the reader, including PNG and JPG supplementary files served inline with their own type. SVG is never rendered, and Word files are download-only.
- **Moved to IR-1204:** "Threads list every document's threads together, labelled by document; opening one on another document switches the reader to it". No thread is tied to a document until IR-1204's whole-document comments exist.
- **Upload types** are IR-1220: PDF, Word, PNG and JPG only.

**Status: PARTIAL.** Part (a) merged as PR #872. Part (b) is split in two, also decided with Hermoine on 2026-11-17:
- **PDF half (this change):** a PDF document opens in the reader. Ask IRIS and private highlights are offered on the manuscript only, and Changes compares manuscripts only.
- **Image half, after IR-1220:** PNG and JPG in the reader, images served with their own content type, and SVG never rendered.

**What changed (part a):**
- **Tabs** (`ReviewWorkspaceLayout.tsx`):
  - Reviewers get Documents · Conversation · Threads · Changes · Details.
  - Authors get Readiness · Documents · Conversation · Threads · Details.
  - Each opens on their first tab: a reviewer on Documents, and an author on Readiness. A reviewer opened on Conversation until the review of this change, when Hermoine decided (2026-11-17) that they should land on Documents.
  - A reviewer looking again at a new Version still opens on Threads, with its response to reviewers (IR-876).
  - The pane falls back to its opening tab when the shown tab moves to the right column.
  - The Documents tab mounts only once it is first shown, as the drawer did, and then stays mounted, so an upload under way survives a tab switch (`useShownOnce`, shared with Changes). An author who stays on Readiness fetches no slots, files or requests.
- **The drawer is gone.**
  - `DocumentsDrawer` is removed, along with the `documentsOpen` and `onDocuments` props.
  - `ReviewWorkspace.tsx` is deleted. Its only job had been to draw the drawer over `ReviewWorkspaceLayout`; without it the file only passed its props on. The props (`ReviewWorkspaceProps`) moved into `ReviewWorkspaceLayout.tsx`, which the page and the tests now import. Part (b)'s new state ("which document the reader shows") belongs to `PaperViewPage`, which builds both the reader and the Documents tab, so it would not have used the wrapper either.
  - The author's Documents button on Readiness is removed.
  - Readiness's `open_documents` link, whether in the reviewer's Details or the author's Readiness, opens the Documents tab, and opens the pane when the window is narrow.
  - The page turns `?documents=open`, or an old `?section=files` link, into `showDocuments`. The workspace opens the tab, then the page drops the parameter so the tab is the viewer's own again. The Paper tab's rail link (`PaperDocuments`) keeps `?documents=open`, so it now lands on the tab.
- **`DocumentRow.tsx`** is the one row component that IR-971 (labels, Templates) and IR-973 (the Publish list) will reuse.
  - Each row shows the document's name, its latest version ("v2 · Rhea Owner · Nov 3, 2026", or "Not uploaded yet"), the request status chip, a note (who asked, and why the last upload was refused), and the controls the caller passes.
  - A versions toggle ("2 versions") keeps every version one click away. Its `versions` prop carries the count and the list together, so they cannot disagree.
  - A row is a `group` named by its document. The Files section's cards were `region` landmarks, one per document.
- **`FilesSection.tsx` → `DocumentsTab.tsx`.**
  - It draws as rows: the Manuscript, each slot, each requested "Other" item, then Supplementary attachments.
  - It uses the same endpoints and checks, with the same upload, request and removal behaviour, and reviewers' Request documents still at the top.
  - The manuscript row now shows its latest Version.
  - A slot's version downloads under its stored file's own extension (`Ethics Clearance v2.png`). It used to always save as `.pdf`, which would misname the images IR-1220 allows. A stored name with no extension is still saved as `.pdf`.
- **`UploadDropzone`** takes `compact`: one small Upload button that is still a drop target. Validation, progress, failure and retry are unchanged. Its accessible name is its label, which starts with the one word drawn on it (WCAG 2.5.3).
- **Tour** (`tour/tourSteps.ts`): Documents is described among the pane's sections.
- **Smoke:**
  - New viewport-matrix row `paper-view-review-documents`: the owner's Review opened by `?documents=open`, with the Documents tab as its primary action.
  - The "remove an attached file" dialog case now names `DocumentsTab.tsx` as its source.
  - The review-action dialog cases no longer name `ReviewWorkspace.tsx` as a dialog source. It no longer draws a dialog, and `dialog-coverage.spec.ts` refused the entry.
  - The shared `paper-view` screen answers the Documents tab's three reads (slots, files, document requests) with empty lists, because a participant's Review now reads them as it opens.
  - Screens and states take an optional `open` step that runs after the page loads (`open-screen.ts`). The review-timeline states, the timeline's empty state, its forced-cell test and the Saved replies dialog use it, or an explicit click, to open Conversation first.

### Part (b), PDF half: documents open in the reader

**What changed:**
- **Open** (`DocumentsTab.tsx`):
  - A slot's latest version, each of its older versions, and each PDF supplementary attachment get an Open button ("Open Consent Paper version 2"). The latest is opened from the row, whichever manuscript Version the reader shows.
  - Anything that is not a PDF, such as a Word file, is download-only, with no Open.
  - `readerDocument.ts` holds what a shown document is (`ReaderDocument`) and the extension check, which moved there from `DocumentsTab.tsx`. A slot version with no extension counts as a PDF, since slots took only PDFs; an attachment with none does not open.
- **The reader shows it** (`DocumentInReader.tsx`, `PaperPdfReader.tsx`, `usePdfDocument.ts`):
  - A strip above the reader, the group "Shown in the reader", names it ("Consent Paper · version 2 · a supporting document, not the manuscript") with **Back to the manuscript**.
  - The reader reads the bytes through the document's existing view route, `?inline=true` on `/documents/uploads/<id>/download/` or `/documents/files/<id>/download/`. No backend change was needed. `documentsApi.viewUpload` and `viewFile` now take the reader's abort signal.
  - Find, zoom and page navigation work as on the manuscript. Download saves the document under its own name.
  - The manuscript's extras are left off: no selection actions (Save highlight, and so Ask IRIS), no saved highlights drawn, no place kept on the account, and no thread pins or Comment. The reader ignores Review's annotations while a document is shown.
- **Back to the manuscript** (`PaperViewPage.tsx`, `ReviewWorkspaceLayout.tsx`):
  - The shown document is page state, kept only for the address it was opened at. Any change of view (a Version, a section, a citation) shows the manuscript again. Reloading the page shows the manuscript.
  - The layout's new `onShowManuscript` is called whenever a thread or Changes shows a place in the paper, so Changes and threads always land on the manuscript. Changes compares manuscript Versions only; documents are never in it.
  - On a narrow window the pane closes when a document opens (the layout's `readerShows`), as it does for *Show in paper*.
- **Audit:** opening a document writes the same `DOWNLOAD` audit event its view route already wrote for in-browser viewing. Nothing new is recorded.
- **Smoke:**
  - New viewport-matrix row `paper-view-review-document-open`: the owner's Review with the slot's version opened in the reader, with Back to the manuscript as its primary action.
  - The Documents row's mocks became `reviewDocumentsMocks`, shared by both rows.
  - The matrix now runs a screen's optional `open` step after the page loads, as `openScreen` already did.
