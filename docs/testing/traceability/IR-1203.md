## IR-1203: A Documents tab for authors and reviewers, and documents open in the reader

**Basis and scope.** IR-1203 is subtask 5 of IR-1199, the review-page cleanup. It has no FR- id. It takes over the Paper View half of IR-973 and does not wait on IR-971, as decided on 2026-11-17. Decisions made with Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-17:
- **Two PRs.**
  - (a) is this one: the Documents tab, which replaces the "All documents" drawer.
  - (b) will open documents in the reader, including PNG and JPG supplementary files served inline with their own type. SVG is never rendered, and Word files are download-only.
- **Moved to IR-1204:** "Threads list every document's threads together, labelled by document; opening one on another document switches the reader to it". No thread is tied to a document until IR-1204's whole-document comments exist.
- **Upload types** are IR-1220: PDF, Word, PNG and JPG only.

**Status: PARTIAL.** Part (a) is done. Clicking a row to open the document in the reader, images in the reader, "Ask IRIS and private highlights on the manuscript only", and "Changes compares manuscripts only" are part (b).

**What changed (part a):**
- **Tabs** (`ReviewWorkspaceLayout.tsx`):
  - Reviewers get Documents · Conversation · Threads · Changes · Details.
  - Authors get Readiness · Documents · Conversation · Threads · Details.
  - A reviewer still opens on Conversation and an author on Readiness. Documents comes first in the reviewer's row but is not where the pane opens.
  - The pane falls back to its opening tab when the shown tab moves to the right column, as it did before Documents was added.
  - The Documents tab mounts only once it is first shown, as the drawer did, and then stays mounted, so an upload under way survives a tab switch. A viewer who never opens it fetches no slots, files or requests. Mounting it eagerly broke every strict-mock smoke screen that opens Review.
- **The drawer is gone.**
  - `ReviewWorkspace.tsx` drops `DocumentsDrawer`, along with the `documentsOpen` and `onDocuments` props.
  - The author's Documents button on Readiness is removed.
  - Readiness's `open_documents` link, whether in the reviewer's Details or the author's Readiness, opens the Documents tab, and opens the pane when the window is narrow.
  - The page turns `?documents=open`, or an old `?section=files` link, into `showDocuments`. The workspace opens the tab, then the page drops the parameter so the tab is the viewer's own again. The Paper tab's rail link (`PaperDocuments`) keeps `?documents=open`, so it now lands on the tab.
- **`DocumentRow.tsx`** is the one row component that IR-971 (labels, Templates) and IR-973 (the Publish list) will reuse.
  - Each row shows the document's name, its latest version ("v2 · Rhea Owner · Nov 3, 2026", or "Not uploaded yet"), the request status chip, a note (who asked, and why the last upload was refused), and the controls the caller passes.
  - A versions toggle ("2 versions") keeps every version one click away.
  - A row is a `group` named by its document. The Files section's cards were `region` landmarks, one per document.
- **`FilesSection.tsx` → `DocumentsTab.tsx`.**
  - It draws as rows: the Manuscript, each slot, each requested "Other" item, then Supplementary attachments.
  - It uses the same endpoints and checks, with the same upload, request and removal behaviour, and reviewers' Request documents still at the top.
  - The manuscript row now shows its latest Version.
- **`UploadDropzone`** takes `compact`: one small Upload button that is still a drop target. Validation, progress, failure and retry are unchanged. Its accessible name is its label, which starts with the one word drawn on it (WCAG 2.5.3).
- **Tour** (`tour/tourSteps.ts`): Documents is described among the pane's sections.
- **Smoke:**
  - New viewport-matrix row `paper-view-review-documents`: the owner's Review opened by `?documents=open`, with the Documents tab as its primary action.
  - The "remove an attached file" dialog case now names `DocumentsTab.tsx` as its source.
