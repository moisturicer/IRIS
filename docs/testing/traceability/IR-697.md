## Discover's search finds people as well as papers (NFR-S4)

**Basis and scope.** IR-697, subtask 3 of IR-694 (public profiles and people search), builds on IR-696's public-profile eligibility and payload rules. The Discover frontend's People row and Papers / People switch are IR-701.

**What exists.**

| Piece | Where |
|---|---|
| `GET /users/search/` with `q`, `college`, `department`, `role`, `page` and `page_size` | `PeopleSearchView`, `apps/accounts/people_search.py` |
| Name and research-interest matching; college/department IDs; Student, Faculty and office-role filters | `people_search.search()` |
| Safe person rows with photo, name, affiliation, role and published-paper owner count | `people_search._person_payload()` |
| Signed-in-only access | `PeopleSearchView.permission_classes`, `user-search` authorization-matrix row |
| Eligibility shared with public profiles: active, verified, unlocked, undeleted and an approved profile role | `public_profile.open_profiles()` |
| HTTP-level coverage | `apps/accounts/test_public_profile_search.py` |

**Decisions.**

- The people endpoint accepts `q`, matching Discover's URL and the IR-701 frontend contract, so the same search text and selected catalogue filters can request a people page.
- College and department filters take numeric IDs, matching Discover's existing catalogue filters. `role=Faculty` maps to the stored Adviser role; `role=office` selects the four office roles, and a named office role can be requested directly.
- Results use `{count, next, results}` for the filtered people total and the requested page. `page_size` defaults to 20 and is capped at 50. Published-paper count includes only Discover-visible published papers the person owns; it excludes proposals, drafts and advised-only papers.
- The response is allow-listed and never serializes `User`, so email, lock/approval flags and other account-security details are absent.

**Tests.** `apps/accounts/test_public_profile_search.py` exercises name and interest matching, college, department, Faculty and office-role filters, hidden accounts, signed-out refusal, paging, safe fields and paper counts at the HTTP boundary. `apps/tests/access/table.py` declares the endpoint for signed-in roles.
