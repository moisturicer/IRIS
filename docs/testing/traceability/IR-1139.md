# IR-1139: Review actions follow the prototype for every role

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Epic IR-920 (S2 Epic: Frontend UI/UX Redesign). Built on IR-1131, which made the Review workspace look like `iris-uiux-audit/prototype`. This ticket is how each role acts there. Thesis-critical: the footer is where a reviewer clears, asks for a revision or decides, and RDCO publishes.

## What changed

- **Footer actions** (`ReviewFooterActions.tsx`, `YourPartFooter.tsx`). One row: "Your part" on the left; on the right, a *More review actions* ⋯ menu, at most one secondary pill and one filled primary.
  - RDCO on a cleared paper: *Accept & publish…*, with *Keep unlisted…* beside it. The menu holds *Request revision…*, *Reject…* and *Request documents*.
  - An office reviewer or the Adviser: one *Finish review…*. The menu holds *Request documents* and *Add a colleague…*.
  - Before the review is opened or claimed: *Open review* or *Claim* is the primary, and every other granted act is in the menu.
  - Each button is a capability the server granted. A held one is disabled, with the server's reason as its description.
- **A verdict button opens *Finish your review* on that verdict** (`useThreadsWorkspace.tsx`, `FinishReviewDialog.tsx` `initialVerdict`). So *Accept & publish…* and *Reject…* still send the reviewer's drafts with the verdict (ADR-032 §7, IR-875). A verdict that is held opens on *Comment only* instead.
- ***Finish your review***: *Your decision* comes first, each option written "**Label** · what it does". Then *Summary (what the authors and other reviewers read first)*, with the draft count under it. *Keep reviewing* is a quiet button.
- **RDCO's publish under embargo** (`apps/reviews/decisions.py`). Record detail's `decision.outcomes` now lists `publish` for RDCO while the paper is embargoed, and `blocked_outcomes.publish` carries the words `decide/` refuses it with ("Under embargo[ until …]. Release the embargo, then publish."). So `accept_publish` is offered, held. The footer shows *Accept & publish…* disabled with that line, plus *Release embargo…* where `release_embargo` is granted. It uses the Overview card's own dialog, now exported from `EmbargoCard.tsx`. **`decide/` is unchanged**: it still checks `outcomes_for`, which leaves out `publish` while embargoed (ADR-052 decision 14).
- **Threads** (`threads/ReviewThreads.tsx`):
  - The filter is one segmented control, *Open N · Yours N · Resolved N · All N*.
  - *New comment* sits on the right, with "Select a passage in the paper and press C, or use New comment." under it.
  - A card's "p. N · vN" is a link to the passage (named "Go to page N of vN").
  - The quote sits in a grey box.
- **Changes is always a tab** (`ReviewWorkspaceLayout.tsx`). On the first version it says "Nothing to compare yet" and asks the server for nothing. From the second version on, it is the comparison, as before.

## Not changed

- **The Adviser's wording follows ADR-047, not the prototype.** The prototype's "Clear and route · you choose the offices that review it next" is the alternative ADR-047 rejected. The option stays *Accept & send to RDCO*.
- **The offices are still chosen in the second dialog.** After *Finish your review* (offices to send it to, and suggested specialist offices) they are not checkboxes inside the first dialog. Inlining them is follow-up work.
- *Comment only* stays an option in *Finish your review* (no functionality removed). RDCO reaches it from *Keep unlisted…* or any verdict button.
- *Add a colleague…* is also still in Details › Reviewers (IR-685); the footer menu is a second way in.
- The Changes tab has no count yet.
