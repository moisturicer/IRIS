# IR-690: Comments on published papers: write, reply, edit and delete

**Requirements:** no FR- id. [ADR-032](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) §7, amendment 2026-10-31 (IR-689, spec IR-420), and its IR-690 build notes (2026-11-10). Reports and RDCO removal are IR-691.

## Decisions (ADR-032 §7, IR-690 build notes)

1. A deleted comment that is part of a conversation keeps a "[deleted]" marker: one that has a reply, deleted or not, or that is itself a reply. Only a standalone comment with no replies disappears.
2. The paper's owners hear of each new top-level comment, not of replies. A reply tells the writer of the comment it answers.
3. `can_report` is false until IR-691 adds reporting.
4. Posting has its own budget: 10 a minute and 200 a day per person.

## What changed

- `PublicComment` (`apps/records/models.py`, migration `records/0038`): a paper, its writer, the text, `reply_to`, `created_at`, `edited_at`, `deletion_kind` (`CommentDeletion`: writer or moderator), `deleted_at`, `deleted_by` and `removal_reason`. It is separate from `reviews.ReviewComment`.
- `apps/records/public_comments.py`, routes on `RecordViewSet`:
  - `GET` and `POST /records/<id>/comments/`, answering `{comments, count}` in the shape Paper View's Comments section (IR-692) reads;
  - `PATCH` and `DELETE /records/<id>/comments/<comment_id>/`.
- Only a published paper takes or lists comments. A paper the caller may not read, or one that is not published, answers the same 404 as a missing paper. Nothing is deleted when a paper stops being public.
- Any signed-in account writes and replies. `reply_to` must be a comment on the same paper that is not deleted. The text is plain, up to 2,000 characters, and keeps its line breaks.
- Only the writer edits (`edited_at`, no earlier wording kept) or deletes; the paper's owners get a 403.
- A marker carries no name and no text, and the stored text never leaves the server. `count` counts the comments that are not deleted.
- Each comment carries the writer's name, its date, `edited_at`, `is_author` (the writer owns the paper), `can_edit`, `can_report`, `reply_to` and `deletion_kind`.
- Posting spends `public_comment_burst` and `public_comment` (`API_THROTTLE_COMMENT_BURST`, `API_THROTTLE_COMMENT`). Reading, editing and deleting spend nothing.
- Two in-app notification types:
  - *Paper Comment*: the owners hear of a new top-level comment by someone else.
  - *Comment Reply*: a writer hears when someone else replies.
- The access matrix declares the four routes. The personal-data export carries `comments` (a deleted one without its text). On account deletion a comment stays, shown as "Former user".

## Evidence

- `apps/records/test_public_comments.py`: 28 tests at the REST seam:
  - only published papers; a paper in review, a draft and an Unlisted paper are a 404 identical to a missing one, even to their owner; a paper that stops being public hides its comments and deletes nothing; signing in is required;
  - anyone writes; the shape, the Author badge, the flat oldest-first thread, a reply to a reply; a reply to another paper's comment, a deleted one or a missing one is refused; plain text, its bounds and its line breaks; the count; no Report offered;
  - posting past the budget is a 429 while reading still works, and another person has their own budget;
  - the writer edits, shown edited, with no earlier wording kept; neither another reader nor the paper's owner edits or deletes; a deleted comment cannot be changed; a comment on another paper is not found;
  - a standalone deleted comment disappears; a deleted comment with a reply, and a deleted reply, leave markers with no name and no text; the text stays stored and no response carries it;
  - notices: owners for top-level comments by others, a writer for a reply, nobody for their own;
  - no module under `apps/ai/` reads the comments table.

  The tests were written before the code but first run after it, so to prove they can fail, two rules were broken on purpose:
  - Showing every deleted comment as gone made the two marker tests fail.
  - Letting anyone edit made the writer-only test fail.
- Review fixes, 2026-11-10:
  - `reply_to` is `RESTRICT`, not `PROTECT`, so removing a paper removes its whole conversation. `PROTECT` blocked a paper with replies from being removed; the new test fails under it with `ProtectedError`.
  - Posting a reply locks the comment it answers.
  - An account with no name on file shows as "IRIS user", never its email.
- `test_personal_data_export.py`: their comments are theirs, and a deleted one has no text.
- `apps/tests/test_throttle_configuration.py` now lists the two new budgets in its keys, defaults and override cases. This is a deliberate change: it pins every budget.
- Run 2026-11-10 on Windows:
  - against a throwaway pgvector container, 492 passed: `test_public_comments`, `apps/tests` (access matrix, enum vocabulary, throttle configuration, traceability shape), `test_personal_data_export` and `test_account_deletion` all pass;
  - flake8 is clean on the changed files.
- Migration on a fresh copy of the dev database (18 Records): `records` up to 0038 applied cleanly. The copy was dropped afterwards.
