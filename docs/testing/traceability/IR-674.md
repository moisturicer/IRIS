## IR-674

**Requirement:** RDCO alone decides a download request, cannot decide their own, and only RDCO is notified of a new one

**Design:** ADR-048 §3; Jira IR-674

**Implementation:** `decide_download_request` (`apps/records/download_service.py`) is the one decision behind `approve`, `decline` and `PATCH`, which also makes `PATCH` notify the requester. The dead `IsStaff` on the two actions is removed: `get_permissions` already overrode it, so other offices were never admitted. `notify_download_request` broadcasts to RDCO only

**Test:** `apps/records/test_download_request_decisions.py` through the API, plus the authorization matrix's `download-request-*` entries. Before the fix 7 tests failed, on self-decision, `PATCH` notification and KTTO notification; the other-office refusal already passed

**Evidence:** 2026-10-31, local Docker (rebuilt backend image): `apps/records apps/notifications apps/tests apps/accounts` 548 passed, 11 skipped

**Status:** **IMPLEMENTED**
