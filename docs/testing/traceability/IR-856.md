# IR-856: connected entry labels and visitor navigation

**Requirements:** NFR-U1, NFR-A1

Sign in and registration share the left-edge-connected folder shape, with a concave return into the sheet's top border. The Sign in and current Identity labels are noninteractive; the current registration list item carries `aria-current="step"`, and completed steps remain links. Both screens retain IR-769's single Help dialog action in the masthead and account-switch link below the form. The footer places the existing privacy/terms links at left and copyright at the far right on wide screens, stacking on phones.

The initial page tests failed on the duplicated account link and current-step link semantics before implementation. The page tests now query the current step through its semantic list item. Targeted test results after synchronizing with main, CI, and review evidence are recorded in the PR. Browser captures at 360 px, desktop, and a 200% zoom equivalent, plus focus and axe results, are in [the evidence index](../evidence/IR-856/README.md).

IR-858 owns approved legal destinations. Existing interim policy dialogs are preserved; no policy approval is implied.
