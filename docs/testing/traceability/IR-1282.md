# IR-1282: Withdraw and delete a paper in review from My Library

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** an owner can withdraw and delete a paper that is still in review from its My Library row, with the same red trash can a draft has, and one confirmation. Requested by Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-23. No FR- id.

**Design:** no ADR change. The act already existed: `DELETE /records/<id>/` on a paper in review removes it at once and ends its review, and its Adviser and offices are told (IR-508, ADR-032 §10 Amendment of 2026-10-11; IR-274's withdrawal; IR-517's notices). Record detail offered it as `withdraw_submission`, but no screen rendered it.

## What changed

- **Backend:** `/records/mine/` rows carry `delete_act`, from the same `DELETE_CAPABILITY` Record detail uses: `delete_record`, `withdraw_submission`, `request_deletion`, or null while RDCO decides a delete request. Every listed record is the viewer's own (`owned_by`), so the status alone decides it.
- **Frontend:** `DeleteDraft.tsx` becomes `DeleteRecord.tsx`, with a `kind` of `draft` (unchanged words) or `withdraw`:
  - trash can named "Withdraw and delete “<title>”", tooltip *Withdraw and delete*;
  - dialog *Withdraw and delete this paper?*: "“<title>” leaves review and is removed with its files. Your Adviser and any office reviewing it stop, and are told. It can't be restored."
- `SubmissionRow.tsx` gives a row the draft trash can for a draft and the withdraw one when `delete_act` is `withdraw_submission`, in the same place (rightmost under *Updated*, IR-1279).
- **Not in scope:** accepted work (`request_deletion`, a request RDCO decides) and rejected records (`delete_record`). They get no trash can here; each needs its own wording.
