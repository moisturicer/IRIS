# IR-573

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**A paper reopens where the reader left off, in the right Manuscript version.** Built to [ADR-044](../../adr/044-account-backed-research-library.md)'s amendment of 2026-11-16 (IR-573), Proposed and accepted when this PR merges. It records IR-553's reading-resume rules and the owner's decisions of 2026-11-16: 200 papers kept, only the current paper, put back silently. Written 2026-11-16.

## What changed

- **One table** (`library/0007`), not in Django admin, writing no audit event. `ReadingPosition` holds one row per reader and Record:
  - the page, and how far down it the top of the view was (0 to 1);
  - the Manuscript revision and SHA-256 the reader was served.
  
  Database checks keep the page at 1 or more and the fraction between 0 and 1. Both foreign keys cascade, so deleting the account or the Record deletes the row.
- **Route** `GET`/`PUT /library/positions/<record_id>/` (`apps/library/views.py`, rules in `positions.py`):
  - **Owner and visibility:** the owner is always the signed-in reader, and a Record they can't read is the same 404 as a missing one on both methods.
  - **GET** answers `{"position": …}` or `{"position": null}`. It gives a place back only while its revision and SHA-256 are still what the reader is served. It deletes one that isn't, and reading creates nothing.
  - **PUT** takes `{manuscript_revision, page, fraction}` and answers 204. A page past 100,000 is a 400. A revision that isn't served now is `409 manuscript_changed`, and a Manuscript with no identity is `409 manuscript_unavailable`. Either way nothing is stored. The check and the write hold the Record's row, so an upload landing at the same moment waits and then deletes the place. Saving trims the reader's positions to the 200 most recently read.
- **Replaced Manuscripts** (`apps/library/signals.py`, connected in `apps.py`). Saving a Record with a new or removed identity, or saving a Version, deletes every position on bytes that neither the Record's own file nor its latest Version still has. `apps/records` doesn't import the library.
- **Account data:**
  - Deleting an account deletes its positions.
  - The personal-data download gains `reading_positions`: page, fraction and when, with the title only while readable.
- **Frontend:**
  - **`useReadingResume.ts` (new)** asks where the reader left off when a paper opens, and puts the place back only if all of these hold:
    - it was read on the bytes shown (`X-Manuscript-Revision`);
    - no citation, highlight or `?page=` is landing, or still being re-read;
    - the answer is a well-formed place;
    - the reader hasn't moved first;
    - the page exists.
  - **Saving:**
    - Where the reader is gets saved 1.5 s after they stop, at most 10 s apart while they keep going, and at once when the tab is hidden or they leave the paper.
    - Nothing is saved until they move or a citation lands them somewhere; following a citation counts as reading there.
    - A save from one session is never sent in the next (`sessionEpoch`).
    - A 409 (replaced) or 404 (no longer readable) stops saving.
    - Any failure leaves the reader as it was.
  - **`PaperPdfReader`** gets a `keepPlace` prop. It is ignored for an earlier Version and for a visitor who isn't signed in. The place is put back exactly, with no citation context above it, so reopening never creeps up the paper. `useReadingPosition` now reports each anchor it measures.
  - **`PaperViewPage`** passes `keepPlace`, except while a citation from the address is being re-read. Publish's preview doesn't.
- **Docs:**
  - ADR-044's new amendment and its README row.
  - `docs/ui-ux/17-ui-states-matrix.md` says why resuming has no state of its own.
  - The smoke mocks answer the new calls.

## Tests

- **`backend/apps/library/test_positions.py`** (25), through the HTTP API:
  - **Resume:**
    - No position at first, and reading creates none.
    - A saved place comes back with its revision, bound to the served revision and SHA-256.
    - Saving again moves the one row.
    - A second device of the same account resumes there.
    - Malformed pages, fractions and revisions (including booleans, strings and a page of 10^12) are 400s that store nothing, while 0 and 1 are accepted.
  - **Identity:**
    - A revision no longer served is a 409 that stores nothing, and a Manuscript with no identity is refused.
    - A Manuscript replaced by `.update()` gives nothing back, and the row goes.
    - An upload through `save()` deletes every reader's place on the old bytes.
    - Removing the Manuscript deletes them all.
    - A save without new bytes keeps them.
    - The owner's unsubmitted upload and the latest Version keep their places while an older file's goes.
    - A new Version deletes the places on the one it supersedes.
    - Deleting a Record deletes the places on it and no other.
  - **Ownership and visibility:**
    - Another reader and the paper's author never see it, and another reader's save never moves it.
    - An unreadable Record is the same 404 as a missing one, and a paper that becomes unreadable withholds its place.
    - A signed-out caller gets 401.
  - **Retention:**
    - The limit is 200.
    - A new paper past the limit drops the least recently read, counting a re-read as recent.
    - The limit is per reader.
  - **Guard:**
    - No file in `backend/apps`, `backend/core` or the `ai/` runtime names the model or its table. The allowed files are the library's models, views, routes, `positions.py` and `signals.py`, and the two account registries.
    - The model isn't in Django admin.
    - IR-756's guard now uses the same scan (`offending_lines`) and checks the same thing.
- **`apps/accounts/test_personal_data_export.py`:** the download lists the person's own positions, with the title only while readable.
- **`apps/accounts/test_account_deletion.py`:** confirming a deletion removes the person's positions.
- **`apps/tests/access/table.py`** declares the route, which passes the access and authorization matrices.
- **Frontend:**
  - **`PaperPdfReader.test.tsx`** (18 new), the reader with the library API mocked:
    - **Restoring:**
      - Put back exactly, with no context gap.
      - A citation wins, and the old place isn't asked for.
      - A place on other bytes, past the last page, or not a place at all is ignored, and saving still works.
      - A reader who moved first keeps their place, and it is saved.
    - **When nothing is kept:**
      - An earlier Version, a visitor, and a caller without `keepPlace` keep nothing.
    - **Saving:**
      - Saved after a still moment, with the revision shown, and at least every 10 s while moving.
      - Saved at once when the page is hidden, or when the reader leaves the paper.
      - Nothing saved before a move, even when the old place couldn't be read.
      - Never sent in the next session.
      - A 409 stops saving and leaves the reader usable, a 404 stops saving, and another failure tries again.
  - **`PaperViewPage.test.tsx`** (2): the paper view asks the reader to keep the place, and doesn't while a citation from the address is being re-read.
- **Review.** A standards and a spec review of the change led to the page cap, the row lock on PUT, ignoring a malformed answer, stopping on 404, keeping no place while a citation is re-read, the Record-deletion test, and ADR point 6 saying a citation's landing counts as reading there.
- **The tests were broken on purpose to check they catch it.** With an earlier Version allowed to keep a place, the session check removed, or the citation context gap used to restore, each matching test failed.
- **Test change:** the reader tests fake `requestAnimationFrame` along with the clock, and let the opening frame pass on the real clock first. Without that, whether a scroll was measured depended on how fast the run was, and failed 3 to 5 of the new tests at random.
- **Migration, on a copy of one developer's dev database** (18 Records, 19 accounts):
  - It migrated to `0007` on top of IR-572's and IR-756's tables.
  - None of the copy's Records has a Manuscript identity (they predate fingerprints), so two readable ones were given one on the copy.
  - As a student, two places were saved (204) and read back. A revision the student wasn't served answered 409.
  - An upload through `save()` deleted the replaced paper's place.
  - `migrate library 0006` reversed it with a row present, and `0007` reapplied. `makemigrations --check` reports no changes.
- **Run 2026-11-16** on Windows against a throwaway pgvector container, one process. Before the change, `test_positions.py` failed on the missing model.
- **Not done:**
  - The full backend suite didn't run locally (low memory). CI runs it.
  - No real-browser test opens a paper on one device and reopens it on another. The two-device case is shown on the backend, and the reader tests feed it the server's answer.
  - Zoom and breakpoint changes after a restore rely on the existing geometry tests (IR-352).
