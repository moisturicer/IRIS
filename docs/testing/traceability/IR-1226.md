## IR-1226

**Requirement:** **Acting on a paper's review takes a seat on it that was not withdrawn, and a removed Adviser stops reading the review.** [ADR-032](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) §10, *Amendment, 2026-11-18 (IR-1226)*, and [ADR-022](../../adr/022-explicit-document-requests.md) Amendment 7. Both were accepted with PR #895's merge on 2026-11-18. Ashlee Sean approved the security review verbally, relayed by Hermoine Zozobrado. Part of IR-1199; no FR- id.

Decided with Hermoine on 2026-11-18 and 2026-11-19, while planning the code:
- **Reading (point 2):** an Adviser seat counts only while its holder is the record's current Adviser. Office seats count in any state. This is written into ADR-032 as a dated correction.
- **Acting (point 1):** the person's latest seat on each party must not be withdrawn, so an earlier `done` seat grants nothing after a later withdrawal.
- **The coordinator's console gets a Withdraw action.** Before anyone commits, it says when withdrawing would finish the office's review.

**Implementation, by point of the amendment:**
- **1, acting takes a seat** (`core/permissions.py`): `acting_parties(user, record)` and `may_act_on_review(user, record)` hold the rule. It is used by:
  - Conversation and threads: post, reply, edit, delete a draft, Post a draft, resolve and reopen (`comments._writer`, `_Viewer.acts`, `threads.resolve_refusal`, `threads.reopen`);
  - the `comment_review` and `open_general_thread` capabilities;
  - document requests: create (`tracker.requestable_parties` now needs a seat that was not withdrawn on the active assignment), and decide or withdraw (`requests.is_requester`, the serializer's `can_manage`);
  - office files: attach (`requestable_parties`) and remove (`attachments.removable_parties`).
  - Someone who reads but may not act gets a 403 that says why: *"You were taken off this paper's review, so you can read it but no longer act on it."* (`acting_refusal`).
- **2, reading:** `is_record_participant` leaves out Adviser seats unless their holder is `record.adviser`.
- **3, Conversation:** writing it goes through point 1's rule.
- **4, the warning:** `apps/reviews/removal.py` `unresolved(record, user)` counts the drafts, open threads, open document requests and open revision requests. The server states them:
  - beside *Replace Adviser*, as the record's `adviser_leaving`;
  - on each console seat row, as `unresolved` and `withdraw_completes_office`, along with the `withdraw` action;
  - in a Decision's `closes.assignments[].leaving`, for everyone but the decider.
  - The frontend's `RemovalWarning` words them in `ReplaceAdviserDialog`, `ReassignDialog` and the new `WithdrawSeatDialog`. `closingLines` adds one line per reviewer, used by the Decision and Finish review dialogs. It warns and never blocks.
- **5, drafts are discarded:** `removal.discard_drafts` runs inside each removal's transaction: `replace_adviser`, `seats.reassign`, `seats.withdraw` and `decisions.decide`. It runs for everyone except the decider, and skips anyone who still acts on the review through another seat.
- **6, requests stay open:** nothing withdraws them. Whoever now holds the party's seat decides or withdraws them.
- **7, the frontend has no rule of its own:** every offer is the server's.

**Tests:** `apps/reviews/test_review_seats.py`, at the HTTP routes:
- Who reads after a removal (4):
  - a replaced Adviser gets 404 on the published paper, including one who finished an earlier Adviser seat;
  - an Adviser put back reads again;
  - a removed office reviewer still reads.
- A removed reviewer acts on nothing:
  - post, reply and Conversation are 403;
  - edit and resolve are 403;
  - reopen is 403;
  - the listing offers no `can_*` and no `comment_review`.
- Who still acts: the owner, a seated reviewer, and a reviewer whose seat is done.
- Document requests take a seat:
  - an unseated member or coordinator is refused and offered nothing;
  - after a reassign, the request stays open and only the new seat holder withdraws it.
- Office files take a seat: an unseated member or coordinator is refused, and so is a removed reviewer.
- Drafts are discarded at reassign, withdraw, *Replace Adviser* and a Decision, where the decider's own draft is kept. Sent threads and requests stay.
- The warning's counts appear on the record, on the console row (including `withdraw_completes_office`) and in a Decision's `closes`.
