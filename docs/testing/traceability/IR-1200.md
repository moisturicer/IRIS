## IR-1200: the Adviser reads and downloads the documents of the papers they advise

**Basis and scope.** IR-1200, subtask 1 of IR-1199 (the review-page cleanup). Authority: [ADR-022 §Amendment 6](../../adr/022-explicit-document-requests.md), 2026-11-17, security review approved verbally by Ashlee and relayed by Hermoine Zozobrado. It touches NFR-S4 (server-side authorization) and has no FR- id.

**Why.** An Adviser opening a paper's documents saw "Could not load this record's files". The four listing routes and the two download routes asked `owns_or_staffs_record`, which admits owners and office staff only, so each answered the Adviser with 403.

**What changed.**
- `core.permissions.may_read_record_documents`: `owns_or_staffs_record`, plus whoever `record.adviser` names now.
- `authorize_record_documents(..., reading=True)` uses it on `records/<id>/slots/`, `uploads/?record=`, `files/?record=` and `files/download-all/`. `uploads/<id>/download/` and `files/<id>/download/` call it directly.
- Upload (`submit/`, `uploads/create/`) and delete (`uploads/<id>/`, `files/<id>/`) are unchanged and still refuse the Adviser.
- The access table gains `READS_DOCUMENTS`, where the `adviser` column reads OK, on those six routes.

**Tests.** `apps/documents/tests/test_record_document_authorization.py::AdviserReadsDocumentsTests`:
- the Adviser gets 200 from all six read routes;
- the Adviser is refused all four writes, and nothing is stored or removed;
- a replaced Adviser and an Adviser of other papers are refused;
- access still holds once the paper is published.

The class inherits IR-153's owner, staff, stranger and anonymous tests and reruns them on a record that has an Adviser. `apps/tests/test_access_matrix.py` declares the new outcomes.

**Test changed on purpose.** `test_document_access_control.py::ObjectIdRouteExistenceOracleTests::test_visible_record_without_document_access_is_still_403` used the Adviser as its example of a caller who sees the Record but has no document access. Amendment 6 makes that untrue. It now uses a signed-in student reading the published paper. A new `test_the_adviser_downloads_but_does_not_delete` in the same class covers the Adviser.

**Runs.** 2026-11-17, Windows, against a throwaway pgvector container:
- `test_record_document_authorization.py -k Adviser`: 12 passed and 6 failed. The 6 are the inherited upload cases, which fail the same way on the unchanged `RecordDocumentAuthorizationTests`, because `apps/documents/pdf_inspection.py` imports the Unix-only `resource` module. CI on Linux runs them.
- `apps/tests/test_access_matrix.py`: 25 passed.
- `test_document_access_control.py`, `test_document_requests.py` and `reviews/test_version_documents.py`: the failing IDs on this branch match those on unchanged `main` (25, all PDF uploads, the same Windows cause), once the one test above was updated.

**Not done.** No browser check as an Adviser yet. The frontend already gates Upload and Remove on `owner`, so it needed no change.
