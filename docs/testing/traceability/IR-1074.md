# IR-1074: Form fields, placeholders and stone-surface metadata meet contrast

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Epic IR-920 (S2 Epic: Frontend UI/UX Redesign). Audit findings A11Y-01, A11Y-02 and A11Y-10 ([`frontend_ui_audit_spec.md`](../../frontend_ui_audit_spec.md) §4.10). Decision D1 was taken on 2026-11-14 in IR-1072 and is recorded in `DESIGN.md` "Decided changes". Relates to IR-896 (WCAG acceptance).

## What changed

- **Tokens** (`frontend/tailwind.config.js`): colour `field` (#8f8884, 3.49:1 on white) and type role `control` (14/20).
- **Primitives** (`Input`, `Select`, `fieldClasses`, `CHECKBOX`): outline `border-field`, placeholder `placeholder:text-stone-600` (Quiet Ink #57534e), helper text `text-stone-600`, type step `text-control`. `Button` `md`, `lg` and `page` take `text-control`; the reader toolbar's 13 px controls are untouched.
- **Hand-built fields** that outlined themselves in `stone-200` or `stone-300`, or set a `stone-400`/`stone-500` placeholder, now use the same classes: Notices, Discover people filters, rename-in-place inputs, Library and Saved searches, Find in the PDF, Notes, comment composer and editor, document request notes, Settings' fields, the Ask IRIS and Discover composers' outlines.
- **Placeholders with no class of their own** (`frontend/src/index.css`): Tailwind's Preflight paints them gray-400 (2.5:1); a base rule now makes them Quiet Ink.
- **Metadata on stone** (A11Y-10): the icon tiles on `bg-stone-100` (empty and status screens, Ask IRIS avatar, Library tiles) move from `stone-500` to `stone-600`. The record bar, reader toolbar and Ask IRIS header already used `stone-600`.
- `DESIGN.md` and `docs/ui-ux/01-design-system.md` §0 mark D1 built.

## Tests

- `frontend/src/components/ui/fieldContrast.test.tsx` (new): Field Stone is at least 3:1 on white and Quiet Ink at least 4.5:1 on white, `stone-50`, `stone-100` and `stone-200` (computed from the token values); `control` is 14/20; `Input`, `Select`, `fieldClasses` and `CHECKBOX` carry the classes; and a source scan fails any `<input>`, `<textarea>` or `<select>` tag outlined in `stone-50` to `stone-300` or with a `stone-300` to `stone-500` placeholder class.
- **Changed deliberately:** `typeScale.test.ts` lists the type roles; `control` is added to `ROLES`, because D1 adds it as a role, not a ladder step.

## Evidence

- Contrast is computed from the token values; jsdom cannot measure it.
- **Checked in a browser (the hand check the ticket asks for), partly:** the sign-in page on the dev server at the 375 px phone preset (the nearest the pane has to 390 px): both fields compute to a `rgb(143, 136, 132)` outline (#8f8884) and the email placeholder to `rgb(87, 83, 78)` (#57534e); a screenshot shows both clearly outlined. **Not checked:** 1440 px, and any screen other than sign-in (Settings, Library, Find in the PDF, the composers, the comment fields). Whoever reviews should look at those at both widths before this is called done.
- **Known limits:**
  - **Native checkboxes** (nine use `accent-brand` with no outline class, and the five that use `CHECKBOX` get no visible `border-field` either, because the app has no forms plugin and a native box ignores `border-color`) keep the browser's own border, about #767676, which is 4.5:1 on white. D1's "checkbox outlined in Field Stone" is therefore **not built** for checkboxes; it needs a custom-drawn box, which is a separate change.
  - **A11Y-10 is partly done.** The record bar, reader toolbar and Ask IRIS header already use Quiet Ink, and the five icon tiles on `bg-stone-100` moved to `stone-600`. About 160 other `text-stone-500` uses remain and were not each checked against the surface they sit on; the disabled Button (`stone-500` on `stone-200`, about 3.8:1) belongs to IR-1088.
  - A field whose classes are assembled in a constant (not an inline `className`) is outside the scan; `SettingsPage` was found by hand. The scan forbids weak outlines; it does not require one.
  - Hand-built fields keep their own text size; only the primitives, `Settings`' two constants and `Button` `md`, `lg` and `page` took `text-control`. `Input` `lg` and `Select` `sm` and `lg` keep their own sizes.
  - The tailwind colour is `field`, DESIGN.md's front matter calls the same value `field-stone`.
