## IR-1311

No FR- id. Supersedes IR-1279's trash can on a draft's row (noted in [`IR-1279`](IR-1279.md)) and the Overview tab of IR-410.

**Requirement:** My Library is one list chosen from one rail, with nothing shown twice, and a row's acts beyond opening it in one ⋯ menu. Asked for by the project owner, who chose direction B (library rail) from three prototypes (`iris-uiux-audit/prototype/my-library.html`), and said that no existing function may break.

**Design:** `docs/ui-ux/01-design-system.md`, IR-1311 amendment; `DESIGN.md`, My Library. The rail names Your research (authors only), Reading, Folders and Requests. Below 52rem of the page's own width it is one native picker. The list is in the address (`?section=`, `&folder=`), and every value the tabs used still opens the same list.

**Implementation:**

- `frontend/src/features/library/libraryViews.ts`: the lists, and reading and writing them in the address.
- `LibraryRail.tsx`, `ViewHeader.tsx`, `LibrarySearch.tsx`, `SavedPaperList.tsx` (new); `MyLibraryPage.tsx`, `MyResearchList.tsx`, `SubmissionRow.tsx`, `SavedView.tsx`, `DeleteDraft.tsx` (rewritten).
- Removed: `LibraryOverview.tsx`, `FilterChips.tsx`, `ReadingPanel.tsx` (the Folder picker), `LibraryRecordTable.tsx` (table and card grid) and its test, whose Cite case moves to `MyLibraryPage.test.tsx`.
- `frontend/src/features/accounts/RowMenu.tsx`: optional `checked` (a `menuitemradio`) and `separatorBefore` items. Existing menus are unchanged.
- `frontend/tailwind.config.js`: the `library-wide` container variant.

**Removed on purpose:** the grid/list layout toggle (one ruled list), and the Overview tab, whose three panels repeated Needs your action, My submissions and Saved papers.

**Test:**

New cases: the phone picker, old and new addresses, Reading status from a row's menu (and clearing it), Cite only on published papers, Select papers, Resume history, a folder's address.
- `libraryViews.test.ts`: old tab values, readers never on a research list, round trips that keep `publish`.
