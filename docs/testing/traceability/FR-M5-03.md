Reading a Record, published or visible to an office through `visible_to()`, does not grant its requests, messages, comments or request history. Only owners and authorised submitters, and the workflow parties relevant to the request, may read them. IR-262 as shipped did not meet this: the list endpoint and the tracker's `document_requests[]` both used `visible_to()`. **[IR-349](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-349), 2026-09-24, implements the fix:** one predicate, `apps/documents/requests.py` `may_read_requests(record, user)`. It admits the owner (`RecordOwner`) or the request's `requested_by`, and otherwise intersects `tracker.staffable_parties` with `tracker.participating_parties`: every party that held an assignment, active or closed, recorded a `Review` or signed a `RecordClearance`. It also counts every requesting `party`. The list `GET` answers 403 to a viewer who can see the Record but fails the predicate, and 404 when the viewer cannot see it, unchanged. For the same viewer the tracker serves `document_requests: null` and every party row's `awaiting_document: null`, while `workflow_state` and its label are unchanged. Record detail is untouched. The frontend types are nullable (`DocumentRequest[] | null`, `boolean | null`), and for `null` `ReviewRoutingTracker` renders no document-request section and no ◐. There is no separate "finding" model, so "acted on" means a `Review` or a signed clearance. **An RDCO user is a participant on every Thesis and Project**, because RDCO staffs Intake and Intake always holds the entry assignment. RDCO's "no access" case therefore arises only on a Proposal before it is routed to RDCO. A non-participant can still infer the requester when `awaiting_document` is shown and only one party holds the Record; the generic state is visible by design (§Amendment 5). Its two open points were settled on 2026-09-24 (ADR-022 §Amendment 5). **Access is by participation, not role:** owners and submitters, plus anyone who can staff a party that holds, held or acted on an assignment on the Record, or that created or took part in a request. An unassigned named Adviser, and RDCO before it has held or acted on the Record, have no access. **The generic `workflow_state = awaiting_document` stays visible** to anyone who may view the Record. The requesting party, messages, history and fulfilment, accept, reject and withdraw details do not, and that includes the tracker's per-party `awaiting_document` flag, which is `null` for non-participants.
