# IR-1093: Sign-in fields: no misleading placeholders, a Show toggle, the headline as the h1

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Epic IR-920 (S2 Epic: Frontend UI/UX Redesign). Audit findings US-07 and A11Y-08 ([`frontend_ui_audit_spec.md`](../../frontend_ui_audit_spec.md)).

## What changed

- **No placeholder in the password field** (`components/auth/LoginForm.tsx`): the `••••••••` that read as a saved password is gone. The email field's `name@university.edu` names no institution (IR-768) and stays.
- **The Show toggle was already there** (IR-204): its visible word is Show/Hide and its accessible name swaps between "Show password" and "Hide password", which announces the state. No `aria-pressed` was added on top, because a swapped name plus a pressed state announces the state twice. This ticket only pins it with a test.
- **The visible headline is the page's `<h1>`** (`components/auth/EntryFrame.tsx`): on a screen with a lede, "Your research has a place." is the `<h1>` and the logo masthead is a plain container. A screen without a lede (email verification, pending, declined) keeps the masthead as its `<h1>`, so every entry screen still has exactly one. The shared frame means **Create an account** gets the same change: its headline "Begin with your research identity." is its `<h1>`.

## Tests

- `features/auth/LoginPage.test.tsx`: the password field has no placeholder and the email's names no institution; Show switches the field to text and the button's name to "Hide password"; the headline is the only level-1 heading and "Welcome back" sits at level 2; the masthead still shows IRIS and the institution's logo and holds no heading.
- `features/auth/entryHeadings.test.tsx`: on login and signup the single `<h1>` is the headline; the existing checks (one h1, title at level 2, no skipped level, nothing a breakpoint can hide) still hold for all five screens.
- **Changed, deliberately:** the login test "names IRIS and the pilot institution in the masthead, the page's only top-level heading" asserted the old structure (masthead as `<h1>`). It is replaced by the two tests above; the masthead's name is still asserted, by the text and the logo's alt text.
