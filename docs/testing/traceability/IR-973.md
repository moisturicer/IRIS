# IR-973: Compact requirement rows in Publish and the Documents tab, with their Templates

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Story IR-969. [ADR-063](../../adr/063-rdco-sets-requirements-and-templates.md) decision 6 (compact rows in Publish and in Paper View; a document request shows its Template; Publish links to Guidelines and points out the chosen Adviser's Templates), with decisions 3 (a label never blocks submitting), 4 (the Manuscript is the fixed first requirement), 5 (a hidden requirement leaves Publish) and 8 (Templates download; Advisers share them for Proposals).

## What changed

- **One row, everywhere.** `DocumentRow` (IR-1203) gains a Required/Optional label (`RequirementLabel`) and a Template download (`TemplateDownload`, moved out of the Guidelines page). The Documents tab, Publish's new Documents step and Guidelines all draw a requirement through them.
- **Where labels and Templates come from:** Guidelines' own list, `GET /documents/guidelines/` (IR-974), read by `features/guidelines/requirementGuide.tsx`. A paper's slots (`records/<id>/slots/`) say what it holds; the guide says what RDCO asks for. A slot the guide doesn't list (one RDCO removed, or one a reviewer made for one paper) gets no label and no Template. If the guide doesn't load, the rows still work and a line says the labels and Templates didn't load, with Try again (CODING_STANDARDS F1). No backend change was needed for this.
- **Publish has four steps:** Your manuscript → Details → **Documents** → Review & submit.
  - **Documents** lists the Manuscript (with the type's Manuscript Template), then each of the type's requirements, labelled, with its Template and a small PDF Upload into the draft's slot.
  - It says only the manuscript is needed to submit, and that uploads are PDF only, so a Word or Excel file is saved as a PDF first (the owner's decision, 2026-11-20).
  - It links to the chosen type's Guidelines in a new tab.
  - **For a Proposal**, it points out the chosen Adviser's own Templates (from `adviser-templates/`, narrowed to that Adviser in the browser). Nothing shows when they share none, and a Thesis or Project shows none.
  - **Continue and Submit are never blocked** by an empty Required slot (decision 3). If the documents don't load, the step says so and still continues.
  - Review lists the documents added, with Edit going back to them. A complete draft still resumes at Review (now step 4): documents never hold one back.
  - This replaces IR-408's rule that Publish asks for no supporting documents.
- **A document request** (the owner's Action required list) offers the requested slot's Template. An "Other" item has no slot, so it has none. The guide is read only while a request is shown.
- **The upload control is shared:** `paper-view/rowUploads.tsx` holds the compact PDF upload with its per-row progress and Retry, used by the Documents tab and the Documents step. The byline helper moved to `DocumentRow`.
- **Backend clean-up (from the card's comment):** `RecordUploadSerializer.get_uploaded_by_name` fell back to `uploaded_by.username`, a field `User` doesn't have, and `RecordFileSerializer`'s fell back to the uploader's email. Both now fall back to no name (`None`). No account can be nameless (sign-up and `createsuperuser` require a name), so this removes dead code rather than fixing a live bug, and no test was added (the owner's decision, 2026-11-20).
