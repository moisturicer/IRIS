## IR-419

**Requirement:** **Review comments: a private, versioned discussion in the review timeline** — ADR-032 §14's "review timeline and `ReviewComment`", renamed T5 "review comments" by the frontend spec's Appendix L; security-relevant; no FR- id

**Design:** [ADR-032 §7, §10 (IR-479 Amendment), §12](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) · [frontend_redesign_spec](../../frontend_redesign_spec.md) §4.7, §4.11 · Jira IR-419 (and its 2026-10-08 hand-off from IR-416) · ADR-032 §10 Amendment 2026-10-30 (IR-419)

**Implementation:** `ReviewComment` (`reviews/0020`): record, author (`SET_NULL`), body, version (the latest `RecordVersion` when written; null on a record with none, never an invented v1), `reply_to`, `created_at`, `edited_at`. `apps/reviews/comments.py`. **Reads** follow `may_read_review`, per §10's IR-479 Amendment ("reviewer names and review comments are read by participants only"), which admits a member of an office holding the record now; anyone else gets 404, word for word the 404 of a missing record, so an office that sees the record learns nothing of its discussion. Settled by the project lead on 2026-10-30 in [ADR-032 §10's IR-419 Amendment](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md), which also amends §7's table, §12 and Security Impact to match. **Writes** are `is_record_participant`'s (§7): a pool member who reads is refused 403. A draft is refused (400). `GET`/`POST /records/<id>/review-comments/`, `PATCH /records/<id>/review-comments/<cid>/` (author only). The tracker carries `review_comments`, `null` to a non-reader as `reviews` is; record detail carries none. Capability `comment_review`: a participant, on a submitted record. **Implementation choices for review:** threads are one level deep (a reply to a reply joins its thread); an edit that changes nothing does not stamp `edited_at`; each comment carries a server-computed `can_edit`; a body is at most 10,000 characters; there is no delete and no notification. Frontend: comment entries in the Review timeline with the version tag beside them as on a review, replies listed under the comment they answer, *Edited*, a composer at the bottom, Reply and Edit, all shown only under `comment_review` / `can_edit`. **Public discussion** (`PublicComment`, IR-420) does not exist yet, so "never mixed" is proven from this side only: review comments reach no reader but `may_read_review`'s

**Test:** `apps/reviews/test_review_comments.py` (REST seam, one class per criterion: who reads, version and order, replies and edits, never public, the tracker) · `apps/records/test_capabilities.py` (`comment_review` added to the role × state table, probed against its endpoint) · `apps/tests/test_access_matrix.py` (`record-review-comments` GET/POST and `record-review-comment` PATCH declared per role; the world gains one owner's comment) · `paper-view/timelineEntries.test.ts` · `paper-view/ReviewTimeline.test.tsx` (accessible tree, axe) · `paper-view/PaperViewPage.test.tsx` (the composer follows `comment_review`)

**Evidence:** Run locally 2026-10-29 in a one-off backend container against Postgres (`test_review_comments.py`, `test_capabilities.py`, the reviews, records and core suites) and the frontend's `tsc`, lint and Vitest. Re-run 2026-10-30 after rebasing onto main (migration renumbered `0020`, after main's `0019` merge node): `makemigrations --check` clean; `test_review_comments.py`, `test_access_matrix.py` and `test_capabilities.py` **47 passed**; frontend `tsc --noEmit` clean, eslint 0 errors, Vitest `paper-view/` **412 passed** (27 files). The access matrix caught the refusal's distinguishable 404 body, fixed here with a regression test
