## IR-569

**Requirement:** **Paper view: one record bar, a full-height reading workspace that follows the content width, and one Ask IRIS everywhere** — no FR- id; subtask 2 of IR-552 (User Stories 10–11, 22); supersedes IR-527

**Design:** Jira IR-569 · IR-552 spec amendments R2–R4 and settled decisions (14 October) · `rea` investigation iris-20261012 (alphaXiv PDF proposal and layout preview) · [16-paper-view-and-review.md](../../ui-ux/16-paper-view-and-review.md) amendment 2026-10-14

**Implementation:** `paper-view/ReadingHeader.tsx` (new): the record bar (Back, Paper's one-line `h1` and version, sections as a recessed track with a sliding thumb), replacing the floating switch and `PaperChatLauncher` (deleted). `AskIrisFab.tsx` (new): Ask IRIS as a round icon at the bottom right, shown while its panel is closed. `PaperChatDock`: the dock modes and the position menu are gone; the panel is one component in three places (Paper column or drawer, Overview/Files column, phone view), its width is `usePaperChat().width` (450px default, 320px to half the workspace, dragged or arrowed by `ResizeEdge`, kept in `localStorage`), and the scope chip is in the composer. The Overview is wide: outlined chips, author chips, View as the filled action (Save where there is no paper), the AI Overview as plain page text with no card or provenance label, a Copy action, and an article rhythm (`OVERVIEW_MARKDOWN`: lead paragraph, near-black Garamond section headings without a rule, maroon list markers) with citations as "p8" (`CitationText pageMarkers`), Record details that fold away (rows or column, 300ms) while Ask IRIS is open, a hairline between the reading sections, a disabled Comments section (`CommentsSection`, function is IR-420), a "busy" message when the overview is rate-limited, and the overview's own `ai_overview` throttle (600/hour) so a page view and its polling no longer spend Ask IRIS's `ai_query` 60/hour, and Record details beside the page (sticky, 22rem) while the `overview` container is 64rem or wider; the Related works cards lose their accent border. `paper-view/controls.ts` states the one control scale (44px touch, 36px pointer, `rounded-lg`, 13px semibold) that the record bar, panel, find row, version picker and Download use, and `Button size=icon` is now 44/36px. The IRIS mark on the collapsed rail opens it, and Back keeps its label on phones. `PaperViewPage` lays Paper out as a full-height workspace; `AppShell` treats `/records/:id` as full-bleed and the route has no breadcrumb. `tailwind.config.js` adds the `reading-split` (640px) and `reading-wide` (1000px) container variants on the page's `reading` container; `paneLayout.readingTier` holds the same numbers for React, and an `overview-wide` variant on the Overview's own container. `PaperPdfReader` takes `contained`/`fill` from the page instead of `(min-width: 1024px)`, which is gone, and opens at 140% (fitted under 640px); `ReviewSection` follows the same container. On Overview and Files the page is its own `overview` container beside a sticky panel column, and opening the sidebar closes Ask IRIS where the page would give way (always over the paper). Entering Paper opens Ask IRIS and calls `ui.store.enterReadingRail` (never saved; `leaveReadingRail` restores). Drawer and single tiers make the bar and paper inert, give the panel `role=dialog aria-modal`, hold focus in it (except for a dialog opened on top), close on Escape and return focus to Ask IRIS. `aiApi.conversations.findForRecord` replaces `findOrCreateForRecord`: opening creates no Conversation, the first question does; the draft lives in `usePaperChat`. The panel is renamed Ask IRIS and sits on the canvas behind hairlines
