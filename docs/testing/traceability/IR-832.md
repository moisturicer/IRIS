# IR-832 — Readable Manuscript duplicate checks

The accepted original-byte SHA-256 from IR-831 is checked at Manuscript attachment and draft submission. PostgreSQL transaction advisory locks serialize the same digest; `Record.objects.visible_to(user)` supplies the current readable population. Hidden and deleted records never produce a conflict or candidate. Same-record retries retain the source revision. Supplementary uploads do not use this check.

The version 1 likely-match policy normalizes Unicode, case and punctuation while retaining words and digits. It offers a same-title match, or title-token Jaccard at least 0.8 with a normalized author in common. Five distinct readable records are returned in deterministic order. The acknowledgement stored on the draft binds policy version, source revision, title, authors and candidate IDs; submission recomputes it.

Historical records without a fingerprint remain uncovered until the bounded `backfill_manuscript_fingerprints` management command has completed. It defaults to dry run, reports failures and remaining rows, and supports `--after-id` for bounded resumption. A source replaced during hashing is not updated. A run with failures must retry those records from an earlier cursor. Its invocation and migration rehearsal still need real PostgreSQL verification.

Behavioral evidence: `backend/apps/records/tests/test_manuscript_duplicates.py` exercises renamed bytes, same-record retry, readable/hidden scope, likely-match reasons and acknowledgement reset at the HTTP boundary. Parallel PostgreSQL and Publish keyboard checks remain required before claiming verified status.
