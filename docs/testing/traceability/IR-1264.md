# IR-1264: a lighter record Overview

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** on a paper you own, the Overview said the same thing three times (the "Your submission" card, the full routing tracker in Record details, and the Review tab), and a long title filled the first screen. Requested by Lee Jasmin Adolfo on 2026-11-21, who chose from a clickable prototype ([`record-overview-light.html`](../../ui-ux/prototypes/record-overview-light.html)): people in their own pills, the status in Record details, and the title sized by its length. No FR- id.

**Design:** recorded in [`01-design-system.md`](../../ui-ux/01-design-system.md) §0 (*Record Overview header and Record details*) and `DESIGN.md` (Display, People pills, Record details). The *Preserved* mark, the one place on the Overview where a clearance kept across a resubmission shows, stays in the rail.

## What changed

- **Title** (`titleSize.ts`, `PaperViewPage.tsx`): sized by length from the `overview` container's new `overview-roomy` variant (40 rem), never the window: up to 80 characters 48 px (36 px narrower), up to 160 36 px (28 px), longer 30 px (24 px). Never cut.
- **Byline** (`PersonLink.tsx`): one pill per author and one for the Adviser, an initials disc then the name; the Adviser's disc is white with a ring and the name reads "· Adviser". Links and the "No public profile available" hint are unchanged.
- **Header:** no status chip, and no "Institution · Completed YYYY" line; the year is Record details' *Year Accomplished* row.
- **Review & routing** (`ReviewRoutingTracker.tsx`): the record's status first, then one line per party with its outcome and *Preserved*, then *See review history*, which opens Review. Removed from the rail: *Currently with*, the clearance strip, reviewers by name, routing, document-request and resubmission history (Review's Conversation, Details and Documents hold them).
- **About this record** (`PaperGovernance.tsx`, was *Institutional Governance*): anyone outside the review sees only rows that say something (no em dash, *Not declared*, *Not flagged*); its owners and reviewers see every row. This is decided by participation, never by role (IR-411's guard), so office staff outside the review read as a reader does, which narrows the ticket's criterion 6 ("owners and staff"). Anyone outside the review gets a *Status* row while the record is not published; a reader of a published paper gets none.
- **Removed:** `SubmissionStatus.tsx` ("Your submission"). The revision banner and the document requests at the top of the Overview are what waits on the owner; My Library's links still land on them.
