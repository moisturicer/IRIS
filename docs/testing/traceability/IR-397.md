# IR-397 — Passages are chosen, not truncated

**Affected requirement labels:** none change status. Part of IR-390; ADR-033 §4, amended 2026-11-15.

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Implementation:**

- **Where.** In the AI runtime, which answers readers since IR-957: `ai/research/selection.py` (pure), applied by `ResearchTools.search` and `ResearchTools.hybrid_search` (`ai/research/tools.py`). Django's `SourceSelection` is unchanged.
- **Paper share** (replaced the count cap, ADR-033 amendment 2026-11-15). Outside Paper Chat, one paper fills at most `RUNS_PAPER_SHARE` percent of the budget, its section-mates included (default 50, 0 off). The limit is soft: budget left over after every paper's share goes back in rank order. Keyword search now reads up to 20 candidates so a capped paper's slots can be refilled.
- **Joining.** Adjacent hits of one Manuscript arrive together in reading order; each later part names the passage it `continues` and its repeated text is shown once (`RUNS_JOIN_PASSAGES`). Each part keeps its own handle, page, regions and Figure.
- **Budget.** Requested passages × 700 stored chunker tokens (`RUNS_PASSAGE_TOKENS`, 0 unbudgeted). The top hit is always returned.
- **Migration.** `iris_ai` `0019_research_chunk_sections.sql` adds `token_count` and `section_key` to `research_chunks()`; same rows, same policy.
- **Bibliography headings.** Already shipped before this ticket: "Bibliography", "Works Cited" and "Literature Cited" are in `AI_CHUNK_EXCLUDE_SECTIONS`'s default, tested in `apps/ai/chunking/tests/test_normalizer.py`.

**Tests:** `ai/tests/test_passage_selection.py` (rules and settings, no database), `backend/apps/ai/tests/test_ir397_passage_selection_db.py` (through the run routes as the runtime login under a reader grant).

**Evidence:** 2026-11-15, Windows 11 host: both files pass (13 and 4 tests). The full suite is left to CI on the PR.

**Not done:** no harness measures the runtime's search, so the on-by-default choice is a person's decision, not a measurement. The ticket's "harness run with and without selection" is not recorded.

**Status:** Partial; CI and human review pending.
