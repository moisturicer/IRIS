## IR-269

**Requirement:** **An office reviewer clears or records a finding; an office completes when all its seats are done; RDCO opens only on the specialist path** — touches FR-M5-01 (stable label only)

**Design:** [ADR-032 §3, §4, §10](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md), with its 2026-10-08 Amendments · design settled with the project lead 2026-10-08 (grilling; 16 decisions in Jira) · Jira IR-269

**Implementation:** `apps/reviews/office_review.py`: `record_office_review` writes the seat holder's `approved` or `negative_finding` Review and completes their seat through `seats.complete_seat`. It refuses: 403 when the caller is seated for no specialist office; 400 for the legacy pipeline, any outcome but cleared/finding (offices never reject or publish), a finding with no comment, an unopened seat, or the caller's own office's open document request. `office_completed`, run from `seats._close_if_complete` (so a coordinator's withdrawal completes an office the same way): the clearance takes the review round's outcome (`not_cleared` on any finding; the latest completed round wins), then the hand-back opens RDCO's pool once no specialist office is active (a `RoutingEvent` with no actor; nothing opens if RDCO already holds it), then `notify_office_completed`. Endpoints `POST /records/<id>/office-review/` and `GET /assignments/<id>/add-reviewer/`; record detail's `office_review` flag. Tracker: `TrackerPartyState.NOT_REQUIRED` for RDCO on a new-model record no office has held, the earlier-review outcome, `in_pool`, and per-seat `seats` for participants only. Frontend: *Clear…*, *Record finding…* (`office_review`) and *Add reviewer…* (`add_reviewer`) in F6's action bar; `OfficeReviewDialog` keeps the typed comment through a refusal and through closing the dialog (IR-143's carried criteria); the strip shows ◌ Unassigned, Not required and who reviews per seat

**Test:** `apps/reviews/test_office_review.py` (REST seam, one class per criterion plus the grilled decisions, including the HTTP version of IR-415's two-seat completion) · `paper-view/OfficeReviewDialog.test.tsx` · `paper-view/AddReviewerDialog.test.tsx` · `paper-view/ReviewRoutingTracker.test.tsx` · `paper-view/ReviewActionBar.test.tsx` (a shared blocked reason) · `features/records/capabilities.test.ts`

**Evidence:** CI on the IR-269 PR (#200: flake8, pytest, lint, Vitest, build); per the team rule these were not run locally
