## IR-786 — A person downloads a copy of their own data

**Basis.** [ADR-051](../../adr/051-system-admin-account-deletion-and-consent.md) decision 13 (spec IR-779, subtask 7). Affects FR-M6-06 (a stable label only).

**Code.**

- `GET /api/v1/users/me/data/` (`PersonalDataExportView`, `apps/accounts/views.py`) returns one JSON file of the signed-in person's data. There is no id in the address, so no role can reach another person's copy. The response carries `Content-Disposition: attachment` and `Cache-Control: no-store, private`.
- `apps/accounts/personal_data/export.py` builds it, section by section: account, sessions, papers owned, saved papers and reading status, Discovery interests and dismissals, Ask IRIS conversations, download requests, delete requests, notifications, opportunities posted, what the person wrote as an Adviser or office reviewer, and consent.
- Other people appear as a count or a yes/no, never by name, email or id: other owners (`other_owner_count`), the Adviser (`has_adviser`), a staff member who ruled on a request. Reviews and review comments written by anyone else are left out.
- A Conversation's Turn that cites, lists or compares a paper the person cannot read now stays out whole, through `apps.ai.history.readable_record_ids`, the predicate a reopened Conversation uses. The Conversation records `turns_left_out`.
- No credential is read: the account section is an allowlist, and the recovery, token and download-grant models are declared excluded.
- The download is audited (`AuditEvent.PERSONAL_DATA_EXPORTED`, migration `audit/0016`, metadata is the format and section names only) and rate-limited per person (`API_THROTTLE_DATA_EXPORT`, default `5/hour`, scope `personal_data_export`).
- `apps/accounts/personal_data/registry.py` declares each model that holds a person's data as included (with its section) or excluded (with a reason). `personal_data_models()` finds them from the model graph: every model with a relation to the user model, then every model that cascades from one.

**Tests.** `apps/accounts/test_personal_data_export.py`:

- `DeclarationTests` fails when a model is in neither dict, when a declaration is stale or doubled, or when an included model names a section the export does not build.
- `ExportContentTests` pins the contents, and that the other owner, the Adviser, another person's review comments and notes, another person's conversation and library, and every credential are absent.
- `ConversationRuleTests` pins the reopening rule (a Turn resting on a paper now a draft or deleted stays out; a Turn with no citation stays).
- `ExportEndpointTests` covers sign-in, headers, the audit row, the rate limit and a copy for every role.
- The authorization matrix row `user-me-data` (`apps/tests/access/table.py`): every signed-in role is allowed, anonymous is refused.

**Tests changed deliberately.** `apps/tests/test_throttle_configuration.py` pins the whole default rate table, so it now lists `personal_data_export: 5/hour` and its override key. Nothing it checks was loosened.

**Not done, and why.**

- **Consent history.** IR-789 builds consent versions and withdrawal at the same time. The export holds what is on `main` today: the signup yes/no and each paper's consent stamp, and says `history: not_recorded`. IR-789 adds the real history to `export.consent()`.
- **Comments and notes** are not in IRIS yet; the file says so.
- **The Audit Log** is declared excluded: its rows can name other people, and it belongs to the System Admin. A person who wants their own audit rows would need a decision from the data protection officer (IR-123).
- **The Settings screen** that offers the download is IR-787.
- A reviewer's own notes on a paper are in the file (they wrote them); the owner's copy never carries them.

**Status:** **PARTIAL** — implemented and tested; review pending.
