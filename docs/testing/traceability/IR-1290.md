## IR-1290

**Requirement:** A status says what it means by colour as well as icon and word: green for cleared and kept, amber for in review and reset, a maroon outline (never a fill) for what needs someone. Solid maroon is left to the one primary action, so an overdue chip and "Claim review" no longer share a look. A resubmission that kept two offices and reset one reads green, amber, green on the clearance strip.

**Design:** `DESIGN.md` §Decided changes D7, §Colors, The Binding Rule and The Four Inks Rule; `docs/ui-ux/01-design-system.md` §0. This reverses part of IR-1097's "only Attention is filled". Approved by the project lead in the prototype (sections "One look per meaning", "The review card", "Clearance-aware resubmission, at a glance"). Gold is not part of this change.

**Implementation (frontend only):**

- `tailwind.config.js`: `cleared` (`#1E6B3F`, `line` `#3F8A5A`, `wash` `#EAF3EC`) and `review` (`#8A5300`, `line` `#B07A1E`, `wash` `#FBF0DA`).
- `components/ui/statusTones.ts`: a `cleared` tone; `active` is amber text and outline; `attention` is a maroon outline on white, never filled.
- `components/shared/ClearanceStrip.tsx`: `kept` and `cleared` green, `reviewing` amber, `unclaimed` stone, `revision` and `not_cleared` maroon. Words still come from the server (IR-1083).
- `features/review/MyReviewsPage.tsx`: the due date and hand-back note are stone, and the overdue chip is the attention outline with a warning icon.
- `ReviewRoutingTracker.tsx`, `OfficeConsolePage.tsx` and `Toast.tsx` follow the same tones (cleared green, in-progress amber, error a maroon outline).

**Test:**

- `components/ui/surfaceRoles.test.ts`: five distinct tones, the `cleared` and `review` tokens' values, and no tone filled.
- `test/palette.test.ts`: the new tokens pass while raw `green` and `amber` classes, gold, cream, `brand-50/100/200` and the loose hexes still fail.
