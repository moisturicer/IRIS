## IR-721 — Remove revoked evidence from reopened Conversations and review exports

**Basis and scope.** IR-721 under IR-712; authority ADR-049 §15 and the IR-712 stories 3, 13, 14, 15, 16, 19 and 22. Blocked by IR-728 (Done). Saved-surface and review-artifact revocation integration only: the prerequisite tickets' own tests (IR-717–IR-720) are not moved here.

**What already held, now shown end to end.** `RunStore.reopen`, `status` and the reader journal already withhold a Turn whose recorded sources are no longer current or readable under the reader's grant (`CURRENT_SOURCE_TURN`, `COUNTED_UNREADABLE`, `ai/runs/store.py`), and `ResearchTools.recall` re-reads shown papers under the current grant. IR-721 adds the integrated demonstration of that, and closes one gap.

**Code.**
- **Reviewer packets re-judged at opening** (`apps/ai/evaluation/review_packets.py`, `eval_review_packets --reopen`). A packet written by IR-607 was a frozen copy: its text outlived a later revocation and could be handed to an account that never had access. `reauthorize()` judges every shown turn again by the **opening account's** current access (`research_records`), withholds a turn resting on anything unreadable, and never restores a withheld turn. Reviews, questions and run identity are untouched, and the file that was written is not edited (output goes to `--out`). Shown turns now carry `record_ids` so a packet can be judged without its run file; a packet written before that field existed is judged by its citations. `--run` and `--reopen` are exclusive.

**Tests.**
- `apps/ai/tests/test_ai_runtime_revocation_db.py` (7, real PostgreSQL, restricted `iris_ai_runtime` login, Django-issued grants, recording provider, through the gateway routes):
  - a delivered cited answer, then its source **unpublished**, **soft-deleted** or **replaced**: reopen, status and the reader journal replay (with and without a cursor) show no text, title, citation or fact, the Turn says it is unavailable, and `sources_available` is false; another reader gets a 404 identical to a missing id on all three; the Django record page a citation opens becomes a 404 identical to a missing Record (the replaced source stays readable but not its old revision);
  - an unrelated finding survives in a Conversation that never touched the revoked paper, while a follow-up in the revoked paper's own Conversation is withheld (see below);
  - a follow-up sent after revocation puts none of the revoked answer, quote, title or earlier question into the provider's payload;
  - direct reads under the restricted role of the Record, its chunk set and its chunks, as owner, adviser, office and a stranger: published, then unpublished, then deleted, one connection serving every reader in both orders, and equal to `visible_to`;
  - access ended between the provider's send and the Turn's commit: no Turn carries the text and no citation is stored.
- `apps/ai/evaluation/tests/test_review_packets.py` (+4): a packet reopened after revocation withholds the turn and leaves the review template and question; a copied packet is judged by its new holder's access (another account loses a draft's turn, the owner keeps it); a withheld turn is never restored; an unknown reviewer and a missing source are refused.

**Evidence (2026-11-05).** From `backend/`, in a throwaway container with its own `pgvector/pgvector:pg16` Postgres: `test_ai_runtime_revocation_db.py` (7), `test_review_packets.py` (10), `test_runtime_replay.py`, `test_runtime_smoke.py`, `test_review_campaign.py` and `test_runtime_reader_progress.py` together, 46 passed, 0 failed, 0 skipped. An earlier run of the revocation file had 6 passing and 1 failing (the case described next); the test was corrected and the file re-run whole. No live provider, vendor account or paid call was involved. CI is the quality gate for the rest of the suite; the full backend suite was not run locally.

**A finding that shaped a test.** A follow-up Turn in the same Conversation as a revoked paper is withheld too, even when it answered a different paper: it started by recalling the revoked paper's title as context, and a recall is a recorded dependency. The first draft of the partial-preservation test expected the follow-up to survive and failed. This is the "safe unavailable outcome" the ticket allows and is kept: the test now pins it and shows the unrelated finding surviving in its own Conversation. Finer preservation inside one Conversation would need Turn-level dependency tracking that separates a recalled title from evidence actually used.

**Evidence classes.**
- **Executed:** the tests above (deterministic provider; real PostgreSQL; restricted role; Django-issued grants).
- **Unavailable / not claimed:**
  - no human-reviewed quality, no remote-provider capacity and no live paid call;
  - no independent-process race: the read/send/commit case revokes from a second thread and connection at the send, which is one interleaving, not the IR-608 independent-process proof, so the suite is not registered in `apps/ai/evaluation/scenarios.py`;
  - no browser run: IR-608's `reopened`, `copied` and `exported` browser checks stay `not_measured`;
  - no Ask IRIS Conversation export exists in the repository (the Audit Log CSV is unrelated and System Admin only), so there is no reader export surface to test; "review export" here is the IR-605 packet.
- The pooled-reader case is shown on one connection serving different readers in turn, not on a pooler.

**Not done here.**
- The packet check at opening is a command run by whoever issues or receives the file; it is not a service, and a packet file already copied elsewhere cannot be recalled. A person who read it earlier saw what it held at that moment.
- `reauthorize` judges by the Records a turn cited, as the packet records them; it does not re-derive them from the run file.
- IR-720's untested wiring (nothing rechecked at Turn commit beyond the composer's live-source validation) is its own ticket's gap and is not claimed fixed here, except for the one commit-time case the race test shows on the runtime path.
