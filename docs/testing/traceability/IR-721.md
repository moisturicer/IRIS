## IR-721 — Remove revoked evidence from reopened Conversations and review exports

**Basis and scope.** IR-721 under IR-712; authority ADR-049 §15 and the IR-712 stories 3, 13, 14, 15, 16, 19 and 22. Blocked by IR-728 (Done). Saved-surface and review-artifact revocation integration only: the prerequisite tickets' own tests (IR-717–IR-720) are not moved here.

**What already held, now shown end to end.** `RunStore.reopen`, `status` and the reader journal already withhold a Turn whose recorded sources are no longer current or readable under the reader's grant (`CURRENT_SOURCE_TURN`, `COUNTED_UNREADABLE`, `ai/runs/store.py`), and `ResearchTools.recall` re-reads shown papers under the current grant. IR-721 adds the integrated demonstration of that, and closes two gaps.

**Code.**
- **A Turn stands only while the Turns it carried do** (`ai/runs/store.py`). Each claim journals a `carried` event naming the earlier Turns it was given as history or through a compaction handoff, and `CURRENT_SOURCE_TURN` now follows those records recursively: a Turn whose own research never touched a revoked paper is still withheld if it carried a Turn that did, or carried one that carried one. Before this, Turn 3 of "flood, pond, pond" stayed visible after the flood paper was revoked, although Turn 1's answer was in its request. The event is not reader-visible (`ai/runs/public.py` allowlists by name). A run claimed before this change has no `carried` event and is judged by its own sources alone.
- **Reviewer packets re-judged at opening** (`apps/ai/evaluation/review_packets.py`, `eval_review_packets --reopen`). A packet written by IR-607 was a frozen copy: its text outlived a later revocation. `reauthorize()` judges every shown turn again by the **opening account's** current access (`research_records`) and, where the packet recorded it, each source's active `ChunkSet` at writing (`revisions`), so a replaced manuscript withholds the turn even while the Record stays readable. A turn's dependencies are its cited Records **and** the sources it read without citing (`source_ids`). A withheld turn keeps no answer, citation **or question**, and is never restored. On reopening, `--reviewer-name` is required: another reviewer's review is replaced by a blank template for the new holder, and the same reviewer keeps only claims and evidence on Records still shown. `--lock` with `--reopen` is refused. The written file is not edited. A packet written before `revisions` existed is judged by access alone.
- **A packet is not access control.** It is plaintext: whoever holds the file can read it, `--reviewer` is an argument and not a login, and anyone can edit the file (blanking `record_ids` and `citations` makes a turn look source-free). `--reopen` is the re-check an honest holder runs before reading, and the docs now say so. A real guarantee needs the text not to be stored in the file, or a served surface; **that design choice is the team's** and is not made here.

**Tests.**
- `apps/ai/tests/test_ai_runtime_revocation_db.py` (7, real PostgreSQL, restricted `iris_ai_runtime` login, Django-issued grants, recording provider, through the gateway routes):
  - a delivered cited answer, then its source **unpublished**, **soft-deleted** or **replaced**: reopen, status and the reader journal replay (with and without a cursor) show no text, title, citation or fact, the Turn says it is unavailable, and `sources_available` is false; another reader gets a 404 identical to a missing id on all three; the Django record page a citation opens becomes a 404 identical to a missing Record (a replaced source's Record stays readable; that its old revision is unreadable is shown by the runtime surfaces above, not by this Django check);
  - an unrelated finding survives in a Conversation that never touched the revoked paper, while a follow-up in the revoked paper's own Conversation is withheld (see below);
  - **three Turns** (flood, pond, pond): after the flood paper is unpublished all three are withheld, including Turn 3, whose own research read only the pond paper; a positive control shows Turn 1's answer was in Turn 3's request, and Turn 3's `carried` event names Turns 1 and 2;
  - replay is checked at cursor `after=0` and `after=1`;
  - a follow-up sent after revocation puts none of the revoked answer, quote, title or earlier question into the provider's payload;
  - direct reads under the restricted role of the Record, its chunk set and its chunks, as owner, adviser, office and a stranger: published, then unpublished, then deleted, one connection serving every reader in both orders, and equal to `visible_to`;
  - access ended between the provider's send and the Turn's commit: no Turn carries the text and no citation is stored. The test now also asserts that the revocation fired, that the Record is a draft and that the run reached a final state with a model attempt, so an early failure cannot pass on zero counts.
- `apps/ai/evaluation/tests/test_review_packets.py` (+8): a packet reopened after revocation withholds the turn and its question and keeps the same reviewer's blank review; a copied packet is judged by its new holder's access; a withheld turn is never restored; a replaced source withholds the turn; an uncited source is a dependency; another reviewer's review never passes to a new holder; `--reopen` needs a reviewer name and takes no lock; an unknown reviewer and a missing source are refused.
- **Tests changed deliberately:** the first reopen test now expects the withheld turn's `question` to be `None` (it asserted the question survived, which the ticket's "a reader must not recover this" forbids), and every `--reopen` call passes `--reviewer-name`. `test_ai_runtime_runs_db.py::test_turn_citations_revision_and_journal_commit_together` now expects a `carried` event in its second run's journal, because that run was given Turn 1 as history; the test's point, that a failed commit journals no Turn, is unchanged.

**The follow-up rule, now deliberate.** A follow-up Turn in the same Conversation as a revoked paper is withheld when it depended on that paper, and dependency now has two recorded forms: what its research read (including a recalled title) and the earlier Turns it carried (`carried`). Before the second review only the first existed, so a third Turn that carried the first Turn's answer but recalled only another paper slipped through. The safe outcome the ticket allows is kept: an unrelated finding survives in its own Conversation, while inside one Conversation everything downstream of a revoked Turn is withheld. Gain: nothing reaches a reader through carried context. Cost: an unrelated later finding in the same Conversation is lost too.

**Evidence classes.**
- **Executed:** the tests above (deterministic provider; real PostgreSQL; restricted role; Django-issued grants).
- **Unavailable / not claimed:**
  - no human-reviewed quality, no remote-provider capacity and no live paid call;
  - no independent-process race: the read/send/commit case revokes from a second thread and connection at the send, which is one interleaving, not the IR-608 independent-process proof, so the suite is not registered in `apps/ai/evaluation/scenarios.py`;
  - no browser run: IR-608's `reopened`, `copied` and `exported` browser checks stay `not_measured`;
  - no **live** surface: nothing reads a run's status while it is running and then revokes; only delivered runs are tested;
  - the raw status route without the reader protocol header (`ai/api/runs.py`) returns unfiltered `events` and needs the service secret; it is an operator surface and out of scope here, untested;
  - no Ask IRIS Conversation export exists in the repository (the Audit Log CSV is unrelated and System Admin only), so there is no reader export surface to test; "review export" here is the IR-607 packet.
- The pooled-reader case is shown on one connection serving different readers in turn, not on a pooler.

**Not done here.**
- The packet check at opening is a command run by whoever issues or receives the file; it is not a service, and a packet file already copied elsewhere cannot be recalled. A person who read it earlier saw what it held at that moment.
- `reauthorize` judges by the Records and revisions the packet recorded; it does not re-derive them from the run file. The revision is the active `ChunkSet` when the packet was written, so a manuscript replaced between collection and packet writing is not caught.
- `review.md` documents `--reopen` as a command line; it is exercised through `call_command` in tests and has not been run against a database by hand.
- IR-720's untested wiring (nothing rechecked at Turn commit beyond the composer's live-source validation) is its own ticket's gap and is not claimed fixed here, except for the one commit-time case the race test shows on the runtime path.
