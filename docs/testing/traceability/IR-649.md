# IR-649

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**A reader's saved Calls are stored in their account.** Built to [ADR-050](../../adr/050-calls-for-papers-and-rdco-confirmation.md) decision 13, point 4 of its 2026-11-12 (IR-758) amendment, and its 2026-11-16 (IR-649) amendment (accepted when #823 merged), which records the owner's decisions of 2026-11-16 on the card. Written 2026-11-16.

## What changed

- **A table of its own** (`opportunities/0004`): `SavedCall`, one row per reader and Call, beside the board's code so the board stays removable as a unit (IR-121). Both foreign keys cascade, so a deleted Call leaves every reader's list. Not in Django admin; saving writes no audit event (ADR-044 §3).
- **Routes** (`apps/opportunities/views.py`):
  - `GET /opportunities/saved/`: the reader's saves, newest first.
    - A confirmed Call is listed in full, with its tags, Over ones included.
    - One sent back to RDCO or rejected is `{"call_id", "available": false}` and nothing more, for its poster and RDCO too (amendment point 1).
  - `PUT /opportunities/<id>/save/` saves a confirmed Call: 201 the first time, 200 after. A waiting, rejected or missing Call is the same 404, even for its Adviser and RDCO.
  - `DELETE /opportunities/<id>/save/` unsaves: always 204, with no visibility check, so an unavailable save can still be dropped.
  - The owner is always the signed-in reader. Every route starts from `Opportunity.objects.visible_to()`, then keeps confirmed Calls.
- **`is_saved`** on every Call the API returns: the board, a Call's page, My Calls, RDCO's requests and the saved list. Lists work it out in one `EXISTS` per query, not one per Call.
- **Account data** (amendment point 2):
  - Deleting an account deletes its saved Calls (`deletion/registry.py`).
  - The personal-data download has a `saved_calls` section: each Call's id and saved time, and its title only while it is confirmed (`personal_data/registry.py`, `export.py`).
- **Not here:** IR-766 shows saved Calls in My Library and under the board's Saved filter. It also drops the old browser saves (`lib/opportunityLibrary.ts`) rather than importing them, as the card asks. Until then the board still saves in the browser.

## Tests

- **`backend/apps/opportunities/test_saved_calls.py`**, through the HTTP API:
  - **Saving:** a saved Call is listed in full; saving twice is one save; unsaving twice is 204 both times; newest first.
  - **The owner:** another reader's saves are never listed, and unsaving touches only one's own. A signed-out visitor gets 401.
  - **Only confirmed:**
    - A missing Call, and an address that isn't an id, are 404 for PUT and DELETE alike.
    - A waiting or rejected Call can't be saved by a reader, its Adviser or RDCO.
    - A saved Call sent back to RDCO or rejected reads `{"call_id", "available": false}` for each of them, and in full again once confirmed.
    - An unavailable save can still be removed.
  - **After the board:** a Call deleted by its poster, removed by RDCO, or deleted by the lapse job leaves every list. An Over Call stays listed with its tags (clock frozen at 09:00 Manila on 2026-11-20).
  - **`is_saved`:**
    - The board marks only this reader's saves, and a Call's page clears the mark after unsaving.
    - PUT answers with the Call marked saved, and the saved list marks its Calls.
    - The board takes the same number of queries with 3 saves as with 8.
  - **Not audited:** save and unsave write no `AuditEvent`, and `SavedCall` isn't in Django admin.
- **`apps/accounts/test_personal_data_export.py`:** the download lists the person's own saved Calls with titles, and one no longer confirmed without its title.
- **`apps/accounts/test_account_deletion.py`:** confirming a deletion removes the person's saved Calls with their other private data.
- **Guards:** `apps/tests/access/table.py` declares both routes, with a waiting Call refused to every role on PUT. The deletion and download registry guards declare the table.
- **Mutation check:** with the "confirmed only" filter removed from the views, 11 tests failed.
- **Run 2026-11-16**, on Windows against a throwaway pgvector container, one process:
  - `apps/opportunities`, `test_account_deletion.py`, `test_personal_data_export.py`, the access and authorization matrices, enum vocabulary, source files, traceability shape and `audit/tests/test_event_types.py`: 302 passed.
  - `makemigrations --check` finds nothing, and flake8 is clean.
  - Before the change, the first saved-call test failed on the missing route; the registry guard failed on the undeclared `opportunities.SavedCall.owner`; and DELETE of a non-numeric id raised a `ValueError` (500).
- **Not done:**
  - The full backend suite did not complete locally: it was stopped for low memory. CI ran it on #823, and every job passed.
  - The migration creates an empty table and changes no existing row. It has not been run against a copy of a realistic database.
  - Nobody has used these routes from a screen; that is IR-766.
