## IR-1216

**Requirement:** Workflow analytics is RDCO's alone. It is listed in the sidebar's RDCO section, not under My Reviews. ITSO, IERC and KTTO don't see the page or a link to it, and the API refuses them

**Design:** Jira IR-1216 (Ashlee Sean's decision, 2026-11-17: "just move it under RDCO so that it is not under my reviews"); ADR-043, amendment 2026-11-18, awaiting the project lead's acceptance

**Implementation:**

- **Frontend:**
  - `lib/access.ts`: `reviewAnalytics` is now `/rdco/analytics`, roles RDCO, in the RDCO section, with no parent.
  - `router/redirects.tsx`: `/review/analytics` redirects to `/rdco/analytics`.
  - `MyReviewsPage.tsx`: the header's "Workflow analytics" button is removed.
  - `helpSections.tsx`: Help tells RDCO where the page is now.
  - Comments in `Sidebar.tsx` and `ReviewAnalyticsPage.tsx` are updated.
- **Backend:**
  - The `reviews-analytics` action and route use `IsRDCO` instead of `IsStaff`.
  - `analytics.workflow_analytics` still keeps any other caller to its own office, as a second layer.
- **Docs:** `docs/evaluation/WORKFLOW_ANALYTICS.md` (path and scope) and ADR-043.
- **Test catalogues:** `router/matrixRoutes.json`, `e2e/smoke/support/screens.ts` and `e2e/specs/08-csp-walkthrough.spec.ts` follow the new path, and the ITSO coordinator's CSP walk no longer opens analytics.

**Test:** these tests were changed on purpose, because each pinned the old placement or scope:

- `frontend/src/lib/access.test.ts`: the main list per role, the RDCO group, the nested pages and `only("reviewAnalytics", [RDCO])`, plus its section and path.
- `frontend/src/components/layout/Sidebar.test.tsx`: offices see no analytics link; RDCO sees it in its group, at `/rdco/analytics`, marked current there and not under My Reviews.
- `frontend/src/features/help/HelpPage.test.tsx`: an office gets no analytics section; RDCO's points to the RDCO section.
- `backend/apps/reviews/test_analytics_access.py`: only RDCO gets 200.
- `backend/apps/reviews/test_analytics.py`: one office's figures are read as RDCO narrowed to it; offices are refused the endpoint; the scoping rule is tested on the function.
- `backend/apps/tests/access/table.py`: `reviews-analytics` is now `RDCO_ONLY`.
