# IR-1344: the guided tours' highlight, outline and corner

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** Ashlee Sean asked for this on 2026-11-28 after looking at IR-1342's student tours locally. It covers both the Review workspace tour and the student tours, which share `GuidedTour`. There is no ADR and no FR- id. The corner token is recorded in `docs/ui-ux/01-design-system.md`.

- The highlight around a step's target shows its 2 px maroon (`brand`) outline.
- Its corner is smaller than the 12 px `rounded-control`.

## Design

- **The outline.** The highlight carried `ring-2 ring-brand`, but Tailwind draws a ring as a `box-shadow`. The overlay that darkens the rest of the page is the highlight's own inline `box-shadow`, so it replaced the ring and the outline never showed. This was so since the Review tour was built. The highlight now uses a CSS `outline` (`outline outline-2 outline-brand`). An outline is drawn apart from box-shadows and follows the corner.
- **The corner.** There is a new `borderRadius` key, `spotlight` (6 px, `0.375rem`), used only on the highlight.
  - **Why it gets its own token.** A white cut-out on a dark overlay reads much rounder at 12 px than a row does, though both are 12 px.
  - **Cost.** IR-1043's scale grows from three radii to three plus one single-purpose token. `radius.test.ts` still refuses Tailwind's sizes and any arbitrary `rounded-[..]`.
