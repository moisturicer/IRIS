## IR-598 — conversation set, scorer and controlled-pilot documents

Authority: ADR-042 and general-tools specification §9–§11 (scenarios A01–A15).

**Built.** `apps/ai/evaluation/conversations.py` validates a conversation set (schema, per-turn labels, 100-conversation minimum, per-family coverage, development/held-out leakage by paper title and topic, a named reason for any shared item) and computes per-split lock digests. `conversation_scoring.py` scores human reviews per arm and family with Wilson intervals and separate denominators, refuses arm comparisons whose corpus, models, data policy or limits differ, and sets `enablement_blocked` on any access breach. `manage.py eval_conversations` exposes `--validate`, `--lock`, `--write-lock` and `--score`; it collects nothing and calls no model. `docs/evaluation/general-tools/conversation_set_v1.json` holds 105 draft conversations (62 development, 43 held out, seven per family A01–A15) over the 40-paper arXiv proxy corpus, with `conversation_set_v1.lock.json`. The [pilot report](../../evaluation/general-tools/pilot-report.md) and [rollout runbook](../../evaluation/general-tools/rollout-runbook.md) record what was and was not measured and the open approvals.

**Not built, not measured.** `label_status` is `draft_unreviewed` and `labelled_by` is empty: an AI agent drafted every label, so no score on this set counts until a named person reviews it and the lock is rewritten. No arm was run, no human review, judge calibration, blind preference, latency, cost or capacity measurement exists (capacity unverified). `supported_unambiguous` (the 90% completion denominator, 36 of 105) is an agent drafting choice flagged for review. `--score` needs `--lock`, refuses draft labels without `--allow-draft` and needs run manifests; capacity counts as verified only with real providers, capacity fit, at least 100 distinct cold conversations and a campaign approval id. The proposed targets (90% completion, 95% claim, citation and page support, zero breaches, blind preference) are owner-review proposals, not approved gates. The runbook records one conflict between the ticket's "cohort" wording and spec §10's no-switch rule for Jive Tyler Revalde to settle. No deployment, vendor, data-retention, institutional-transmission or paid-campaign approval is granted.

**Evidence (2026-10-16).** `eval_conversations --validate` on the set: 105 conversations, 36 supported and unambiguous, no coverage gap, no leakage or topic-overlap warning (exact-string checks, evidence titles included; near-synonyms not caught), `valid`; `--write-lock` then `--validate --lock` both `valid`. The three new test files pass (24 tests). `python -m pytest apps/ai/evaluation`: 299 passed, 1 failed; the failure is the known Windows-only `test_the_proxy_audit_pins_the_proxy_set_it_audited` (CRLF checkout changes the proxy file digest) and predates this ticket. The scorer has only seen invented reviews.
