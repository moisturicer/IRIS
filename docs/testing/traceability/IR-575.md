## IR-575 My saved research shapes For you

**Design:** no ADR covers personal discovery. The behaviour follows the IR-557 spec text (user stories 3, 7 and 11, metadata recipe v1 steps 2 and 3), building on IR-567's For you feed and IR-566's account saves. Jira is not a requirements authority, so this gap is recorded here, as IR-576 recorded it: an ADR for personal discovery is still owed.

- **Saved Areas join the chosen ones.** `_saved_areas()` in `apps/records/discovery.py` reads the classification and PSCED of the reader's saved Records, filtered through `Record.objects.visible_to(user)` and the published-catalogue statuses. The union with the chosen Areas goes into the same stable Area-ID round-robin as before. A saved Record with no Area adds nothing.
- **Reasons name what the reader did.** An item in a chosen Area keeps `{"kind": "selected_area"}`; an item whose Area comes only from a save gets `{"kind": "saved_area", "area": ...}`, shown as "Same Area as a paper you saved". The reason never names the saved paper.
- **Saved Records sink.** Inside each Area bucket, Records the reader has not saved come before saved ones, each group in added-date order, so older research stays eligible. A completed Record is necessarily a saved one (`reading_status` lives on the save), so one rule covers "saved or completed".
- **Revocation.** Candidates and signals both go through `visible_to`, so a save the reader can no longer read stops contributing on the next snapshot. A snapshot already frozen keeps its order (it fixes order, not access), but its reasons are rechecked on every page: a saved-sourced Area whose saved Records are no longer readable falls back to `{"kind": "latest"}`, so no explanation names a signal the reader lost.
- **Saves invalidate the snapshot.** `apps/library/views.py` calls `invalidate_feed()` in the same transaction for a new save, a changed `reading_status` and a real unsave. A repeated identical PUT, or a DELETE of nothing, changes no order and does not. `RECIPE_VERSION` is now 2, because entries gain a `source`, so cursors from the old recipe go stale (409).
- **Independence from Not interested.** A dismissed Record still contributes the Area it was saved in: unsaving and "Not interested" are separate acts (user story 7).
- **Never signals:** notes, chats, review activity and private reading. Nothing in `discovery.py` reads them: the only account data it consults is `DiscoveryInterests`, `DiscoveryDismissal` and `SavedRecord`.
- **Payload.** For you rows come from `RecordListSerializer`, so they carry `has_manuscript` and `abstract` for the Discover card (the card requirement recorded on the Jira ticket); a test pins it.
- **Interface:** `DiscoverResultCard` renders the new reason; `ForYouItem["reason"]["kind"]` gains `saved_area`.

**Tests:** `apps/records/test_discovery_feed.py` (IR-575 section) covers the saved signal's effect and reason text, PSCED and area-less saves, chosen and saved Areas sharing the rotation with a chosen Area keeping its reason, saved and completed Records sinking, a dismissed save still counting, a save made unreadable ending the signal, a frozen snapshot no longer naming a withdrawn signal, invalidation on save, status change and unsave but not on repeats, another account's saves never reaching the reader, and the card payload. `DiscoverPage.test.tsx` covers the new reason chip.
