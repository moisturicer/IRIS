# IR-1341: the reader's Comment is greyed out until the review is started

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** Hermoine Zozobrado asked for this on 2026-11-27. There is no FR- id. [ADR-032](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) §10's IR-1341 amendment of 2026-11-27 holds the decision. It was accepted by Lee Jasmin Adolfo with PR #1028's merge on 2026-11-27, as relayed by Hermoine Zozobrado.

- Before the viewer's own review is started, the paper reader's **Comment** is shown but greyed out, with *"Start your review to comment"*.
- The C shortcut and *Comment on this document* do nothing until then.
- An office review nobody has claimed is held the same way, until it is claimed and started.

## Design

- `ReaderAnnotations.commentHeld` (optional): why Comment is held, or null.
- `useThreadsWorkspace` takes `notStarted` and sets `commentHeld` to `COMMENT_HELD` while it is true.
- `ReviewWorkspaceLayout` passes `notStarted`: not the author's view, and either a seat not yet opened or a `review_claim` pending.
- `PaperPdfReader`: Comment is disabled with the reason as its title, drops its C shortcut, and `comment()` does nothing while held.
- `DocumentInReader`: *Comment on this document* is disabled with the same reason.
