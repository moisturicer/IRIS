# IR-814: IRIS reminds owners and the Adviser when an embargo's date arrives, and the date changes nothing

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** ADR-052 decision 9. No FR- id.

**The job:** `CELERY_BEAT_SCHEDULE["embargo-reminders"]` runs `apps.records.tasks.send_embargo_reminders` daily at 00:00 UTC. That is 08:00 in Manila, because Celery runs on UTC here and Manila has no daylight saving. It lands on `default`, which `celery-default` consumes. The task calls `apps/records/embargo_reminders.py::run_embargo_reminders`, which asks the database what is owed.

**Which papers:** every submitted paper that isn't deleted, under an active embargo whose review date has arrived, whatever its status: in review, published, kept unlisted or rejected. Drafts aren't reminded, and neither are released papers or papers that answered "no embargo".

**When:**

- **On the date itself,** even a weekend or holiday. The reminder is in-app, so it waits for the next login.
- **Then every 5 working days,** counted by `apps/reviews/deadlines.py::working_days_between` against the `Holiday` table.
- **At most one reminder is owed at a time,** the latest. After downtime, a superseded one is skipped rather than sent late, as ADR-043's are.

**Who:** the paper's active owners and its Adviser, each checked against `Record.objects.visible_to(user)` before a notice names the paper. RDCO isn't added when a paper has no Adviser. That was decided while building this, to keep to decision 9's list.

**Sent once:** the `EmbargoReminder` ledger (migration `records/0029`) has a unique key on (paper, `embargo_set_at`, step). `embargo_set_at` is the paper's `embargo_answered_at`, which every set and extend restamps. So moving the date starts again from the new date, and so does holding a released paper again, even with the same date. Releasing stops the reminders, because a released paper isn't a candidate. The row is written before the notices and under the paper's row lock. A re-run, a second beat or a missed tick therefore can't send twice. If every notice for a step fails, that step is lost rather than sent twice.

**Changes nothing:** the runner writes notifications and the ledger and nothing else. A test runs four reminders and checks after each one that the paper is still Active with the same date, and still refused by `decision_for_record` for `EMBARGOED`.

**Failures:** a paper whose send raises is logged with its id, and the batch carries on. A single notice that fails is logged by `apps/notifications/services.py`, and the other recipients are still told.

**Notifications:** a new type, "Embargo Review", is seeded by `notifications/0009`. Deadline and embargo notices now share one helper, `_notify_in_app`, and `notify_deadline` behaves as before.

**Docs:** the comment above `CELERY_BEAT_SCHEDULE` and `docs/engineering/DEVELOPMENT.md` §6 now list both reminder jobs, with a command to run this one by hand.

**Tests:** `apps/records/test_embargo_reminders.py` covers:

- the date, weekends, the 5-working-day rhythm, and holidays
- re-runs, downtime, extend, re-hold and release
- which statuses are reminded
- inactive owners, no Adviser, and visibility
- that a reminder changes nothing
- a failed paper and a failed notice
- the beat entry and the task

**Evidence, 2026-11-03:**

- **Failing first:** the test module failed to import before `embargo_reminders` existed. Once it existed, every send failed with "FOR UPDATE cannot be applied to the nullable side of an outer join". The fix locks only the record row (`of=("self",)`), as `reviews/reminders.py` does.
- **After:** `test_embargo_reminders.py`, `apps/reviews/test_reminders.py`, `test_deadlines.py`, `test_deadline_calendar.py`, `test_deadline_migration.py` and `apps/notifications` passed 92. `flake8` and `makemigrations --check` are clean.
- **A copy of one developer's dev database,** migrated forward and given `seed_demo`'s answers:
  - `notifications/0009` and `records/0029` applied, and the type was seeded.
  - Run on the dated demo paper's review date, the job reminded its owner and its Adviser, `student@` and `adviser@cit.edu`, and a second run sent nothing.
  - The paper stayed Active.
  - The copy was then dropped.
- **Full suites:** left to CI.
- **Review:** a standards review and a spec review ran on the diff. They led to the test for one failed notice, the shared notify helper, the at-most-once note, and correcting the two docs that said beat runs one task.
- **Not run:** no real worker or beat process ran the task on a schedule.

**Status:** Implemented; awaiting review.
