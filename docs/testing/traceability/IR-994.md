## IR-994 — Let the runtime agent answer follow-ups, exact counts and listings

**Basis and scope.** IR-994 under IR-955; authority ADR-049 and its 2026-11-08 amendment, which records the owner's four decisions. IR-956's paid run (2026-11-08) found 42 of the agent's 49 failed turns refused at the send guard as `source_unavailable`: every follow-up turn (F08, F09), every exact count (F04) and most listings (F05).

**What exists now.**
- **Counts and listings as metadata.** `ai/admission/guard.py::_metadata_only` admits a tool result marked `metadata_only` when its JSON is exactly an `exact_count` or a `record_list` (fixed keys, typed values, names and titles at most 1000 characters). Any other field or shape is refused as before. `ToolResult.metadata_only` (`ai/agent/protocol.py`) is set only by `count_records` and `list_records`, and survives checkpoints and compaction.
- **`list_records`** (`ai/research/tools.py::ResearchTools.list_records`, `ai/agent/tools.py::LIST_RECORDS`). Every Record the reader may read that matches the metadata filters, by title, with year and a paper handle, at most 50 (`metadata.MAX_LISTED`), with the exact total and `truncated`. Its total is recorded as an exact count, so the answer checks accept a complete list. Offered under the new `research-6` tool version (`agent-research-6`), to which `ai/runs/agent_profile.py` now pins new AGENT runs; runs pinned to `research-1` through `research-5` keep their tools.
- **Follow-ups.** `ai/runs/migrations/0018_turn_sources.sql` adds `iris_ai.turn.sources`. The agent stores every source its history carried at delivery (`Result.sources`), including earlier answers' sources. A claim reloads them into `PriorTurn.sources`; a Turn with none stored (written before this change, or by the deterministic executor) is labelled by its citations and the sources its run's journal recorded (`store.LEGACY_SOURCES`). `_Run._open` sends each earlier answer with those sources, so the guard rechecks them. An answer whose sources the send can no longer carry is replaced by `WITHHELD_ANSWER`, and its question is kept.

**Tests.**
- `ai/tests/test_send_guard.py`: a count and a listing reach the model with no approval; an extra field, an over-long title or a mistyped total is refused; an unmarked count is refused.
- `backend/apps/ai/tests/test_ai_runtime_followups_contract.py` (executor, recording model, the guard's own provenance check on every request): a count is answered; only `research-6` offers `list_records`; a listing reaches the model and a complete list of its titles is delivered; a follow-up carries the earlier answer; an earlier answer whose source is withdrawn is replaced by the note and the run completes; a Turn records sources it read but did not cite and those its history carried.
- `backend/apps/ai/tests/test_ai_runtime_metadata_db.py::test_a_listing_names_every_readable_match_with_an_exact_total` (real PostgreSQL, restricted runtime login): ordering by title, the cap and `truncated`, an unknown Area refused, and an owner's draft listed only for its owner.
- `backend/apps/ai/tests/test_ai_runtime_turn_sources_db.py`: sources stored and reloaded; an older Turn labelled by its citations and journal.
- `backend/apps/ai/tests/test_ai_runtime_checkpoint_contract.py`: `metadata_only` survives a checkpoint.
- Updated deliberately: `ai/tests/test_agent_profile.py` and `test_ai_runtime_executor_selection_db.py` expected the default pin `agent-research-5`; it is now `agent-research-6`.
