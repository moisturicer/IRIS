## IR-797

**Requirement:** **RDCO can clear what blocks an account deletion: replace a paper's Adviser, add an owner, withdraw a paper in review** — ADR-051 decision 10, subtask 17 of IR-779. Unblocks IR-785 (the deletion request) and IR-798 (RDCO's controls); touches FR-M5-01 (stable label only)

**Design:** [ADR-051](../../adr/051-system-admin-account-deletion-and-consent.md) decisions 9 and 10 · [ADR-032](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) §4 (seats) and §10 (the owner's delete, which RDCO's withdrawal mirrors) · IR-260 (an Adviser is never an owner) · Jira IR-797

**Implementation:** `apps/records/rdco_acts.py` holds the three acts, each called under the per-record lock `RecordViewSet._paper_under_lock` takes. `POST /records/<id>/replace-adviser/ {adviser, reason?}` withdraws the old Adviser's open seat and seats the new one on the same active Adviser assignment (`source` assigned, `reassigned_from` and `reason` as a coordinator's reassignment, IR-623), then sets `Record.adviser`; the new Adviser must be an active account holding the Adviser role, not the current Adviser, not an owner. `POST /records/<id>/add-owner/ {email}` adds an existing, active, verified account as a non-primary owner, so the Contact person is unchanged; every account that does not qualify gets one refusal, so the address is not confirmed or denied. `POST /records/<id>/withdraw/ {reason}` requires a reason and calls `soft_delete_record` → `lifecycle.apply` → `reviews.withdrawal.withdraw_review`, the owner's deletion path, with the reason threaded to the audit event. All three are `IsRDCO`, a role gate before the lookup, and refuse a paper not in review. Audit: new `ADVISER_REPLACED` and `OWNER_ADDED` types (migration `audit/0016`), and `RECORD_WITHDRAWN` gains `reason` only for RDCO's withdrawal (documented in `AUDIT_EXPORT.md`). Notices, after commit: the old and the new Adviser in-app; the new owner and the existing owners in-app; on a withdrawal every owner (in-app and email, with RDCO's reason) and each reviewer whose open seat closed (in-app). `record_capabilities` offers `replace_adviser` (only while an Adviser review is open), `add_owner` and `withdraw_paper` to RDCO on a paper in review, from the predicates the acts use

**Test:** `apps/records/test_rdco_acts.py` (HTTP level: each act, its audit event, its notices, every refusal, the 403 for each other role including System Admin, the per-record lock, and a 404 for an unknown paper) · `apps/records/test_capabilities.py` (the three keys against their endpoints, per role and state) · `apps/tests/access/table.py` rows `record-replace-adviser`, `record-add-owner`, `record-withdraw` (RDCO only, run by `apps/tests/test_access_matrix.py`) · the existing `apps/audit/tests/test_workflow_events.py` still pins an owner's withdrawal's metadata, which carries no `reason`

**Evidence:** Written without a runnable Django environment (no Python dependencies installed in the author's checkout), so **none of these tests has been run by the author**; they are left to CI on the PR. The migration `audit/0016` was written by hand from `0015`, and its `AlterField` choices should be confirmed by `makemigrations --check` in CI

**Status:** **PARTIAL** — backend implemented and tested in CI only once the PR's run is green (not run by the author); RDCO's controls are IR-798, and the deletion request that relies on these acts is IR-785
