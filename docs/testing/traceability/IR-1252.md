# IR-1252: Only a published paper reaches the AI

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** every AI path, Ask IRIS and Paper Chat included, reads or sends a paper only while it is published, released from embargo, consented, its IP mark released by KTTO, and not deleted. Publishing, not releasing, queues the paper's reading and indexing. [ADR-052](../../adr/052-each-paper-states-its-embargo-and-a-person-ends-it.md), *Amendment — 2026-11-21 (IR-1252)*, decided by Hermoine Zozobrado, accepted on the merge of #933 (approved by Ashlee Sean, and by Jive Tyler I. Revalde verbally). No FR- id.

## What changed

- **Django's gate** (`apps/ai/policy/disclosure.py`, `records.py`): `DisclosureInputs` gains a fourth fact, `published`, and `may_disclose` refuses with `Reason.UNPUBLISHED`. `inputs_for_record` reads it as `pipeline_status in PUBLICLY_VISIBLE_STATUSES and not is_deleted`. It defaults to unpublished, so a caller that forgets it is refused. Every outbound path already asks this gate, so this one change covers Unstructured's read, Voyage's vectors and reranking, the AI Overview, paper briefs, figure descriptions and formula transcription.
- **Ask IRIS's reads** (`apps/ai/migrations/0050_runtime_reads_only_published.py`): `iris_authz.research_record_ids()` requires the same conditions for the AI runtime's purpose, `reader_session`. Every runtime read goes through the row policies that call it, so search, passages, whole-paper reading, lists, counts, title lookups and a selected passage are all covered, for every reader, the paper's own owners, Adviser and offices included. A paper that stops qualifying is refused at the run's next read. Django's own purposes (`record_read`, `reader_run`) still read by visibility. The migration grants the verifier the columns it reads, and `provision_research_roles`' verifier column list already holds every one of them, so this change depends on no extra deploy step. The card's standing reminder still applies: `deploy.sh` runs neither `provision_research_roles` nor `python -m ai.runs.migrate`.
- **Indexing authority** (`apps/publication/migrations/0011_indexing_needs_publication.py`): `indexing_source()` and `indexed_source_current()` require publication too. The access fence already watched `pipeline_status`, so a paper leaving publication fences outstanding work.
- **Publishing queues the AI work** (`send_to_ai_on_commit`): RDCO's publish Decision (`reviews/decisions.py`), publishing a paper kept unlisted (`records/unlisted.py`), and a declined delete request restoring a published paper (`records/lifecycle.py`). A release still asks the gate, which refuses until the paper is published.
- **Paper Chat** (`PaperViewPage.tsx`): on a paper that isn't published, Ask IRIS isn't offered on the page at all: it doesn't open on entering Paper, there is no Ask IRIS icon, Notes has no Ask IRIS button, and *Ask about selection* isn't shown. Decided by Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-21, instead of a chat beside a paper it can't read: the Ask IRIS page in the sidebar, and Paper Chat on every published paper, still search the published papers. This replaces the card's "answers that the paper isn't available to Ask IRIS yet" for unpublished papers.
- **A published paper embargoed again** keeps its Paper Chat, which simply finds nothing in it, with no reason given. A reader is told why a paper is held back only for a stored No (ADR-054 decision 8); its embargo is shown only to its owners, Adviser and offices (`may_read_embargo`, IR-811). Embargoing a published paper again is rare: RDCO can't publish one that is embargoed.

## Consequence worth knowing

Every paper the AI runtime may read is now published, and every signed-in reader may read a published paper, so **every reader gets the same papers in Ask IRIS**. Answers no longer differ by reader. The answer-quality set's F12 family (two readers with different access) no longer has a difference to measure.

**Migrations on a copy of a realistic database.** The dev database (`iris-db-1`: 18 Records, 8 published) was copied into a scratch database and migrated forward, 138 migrations including `ai.0050` and `publication.0011`. The gate on its real rows allowed 0 papers: the published ones are all still embargoed or unconsented, and 5 also hold an unreleased IP mark. Both migrations were reversed and applied again cleanly. The copy was then dropped.

**Tests changed deliberately**, each marked with an IR-1252 comment: fixtures that stood for "a paper the AI may read" are now published (`corpus.released_for_ai`, `test_chunk_embedding`, `test_backfill_and_promotion`, `test_reextraction`, `test_reader_session_grant_http`, `test_offcampus_extraction`, `test_ai_answer`, `test_embargo`); tests whose old expectation was the gap itself now expect the refusal (a released draft is held at upload whatever its answer; seed_demo's submitted Proposal is refused as unpublished; the owner no longer finds their draft in search, counts, lists or title lookups; `test_embargo_every_way_out` uses a published catalogue paper); and `test_accepted_source`'s replacement-during-embedding test publishes its paper before embedding, steps back to a draft only for the replacement upload, and now asserts the refusal is for the replaced source, so a refusal for the paper's state can't pass it.

**Local-only failures, not from this change.** About 120 backend tests fail on this laptop and on `main` alike: PDF uploads ("PDF checking is temporarily unavailable", the Unix `resource` module) and subprocess tests killed under low memory. CI is the gate for those.

**Not tested:** no end-to-end run through a live model; nothing measures Ask IRIS answers before and after.
