# IR-1339: a published paper reads like anyone's, unless its review is asked for

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** Hermoine Zozobrado asked for this on 2026-11-27 and chose option B in chat. [ADR-032](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) §10 is amended 2026-11-27 (Proposed until its pull request merges). There is no FR- id.

**Why.** On a published paper, every participant got **Overview · Review** wherever they opened it, Discover included. There was no Paper tab, so an author couldn't read their own published paper as its readers do. Because Review keeps text selection for review comments (IR-1326), they also couldn't save highlights or notes on it, or ask IRIS about a passage.

## Design

Frontend only. No endpoint, payload or capability changes.

- `capabilities.ts`:
  - `asksForReview(search)`: true for `?review=open`, `?section=review`, `?section=files` or `?documents=open`.
  - `showsReview(record, viewer, asked)`: a participant, and either the paper is unpublished or the review was asked for.
  - `sectionsFor(record, viewer, asked)`: a participant's published paper gets **Overview · Paper** by default and **Overview · Paper · Review** when asked. Unpublished papers are unchanged.
  - `reviewHref(id, section)`: the link My Library and My Reviews use.
- `useViewLocation`: with `keepReview`, every view change writes `?review=open`, so leaving Review for another tab keeps the Review tab.
- `PaperViewPage`:
  - Reads the address and passes `keepReview` only on a published paper.
  - Record details' review rows follow `showsReview`.
  - A participant who opened their published paper as a reader sees no Versions picker.
- Links:
  - `SubmissionRow` (My Research and the Overview's lists) opens a published paper with `reviewHref`.
  - My Reviews uses `reviewHref(record, opens)`, so IR-1135's Overview landing stays.
  - The office console's `?section=review` links needed no change.

## Tests

**IMPLEMENTED.** Run locally on 2026-11-27.

- `capabilities.test.ts`:
  - "a published paper's review opens only where it is asked for (IR-1339)": sections for an owner and a reviewer, from Discover and from My Library or My Reviews; a reader whatever the address asks; an unpublished paper either way.
  - Each address that asks for the review, and each that doesn't.
  - `reviewHref`.
- `PaperViewPage.test.tsx`, same describe name:
  - The owner from Discover gets Overview · Paper, no Versions picker and no tracker read.
  - From My Library they get three sections on Review.
  - An old `?section=review` link left for Overview keeps `review=open` and the Review tab.
  - A reviewer from My Reviews lands on Overview with Review there.
  - A reader gets no Review even when asked.
- `MyLibraryPage.test.tsx`: a published row links to `?section=review&review=open`, and a row in review keeps its plain link.

**Changed on purpose:**
- `capabilities.test.ts` row "a published record's details are not editable by its owner" now expects Overview · Paper. It stated the IR-879 rule this amendment changes.
- `MyReviewsPage.test.tsx`'s four href assertions now end `&review=open`.
- The smoke tier's review-tracker states (`smoke/support/states.ts`) and `forced-cells.spec.ts`'s empty tracker open their published paper at `?review=open`. On a plain link a participant no longer gets the tracker, which is what they test.

**Also:** `review` is registered in `frontend/scripts/query-keys.json`. Its value is only ever `open`.

**Not verified:** no run against a live stack. The whole smoke tier was run locally against the built app: 563 passed and 1 failed. The failure was a keyboard journey that timed out under parallel load; all 12 keyboard tests pass when run one at a time.
