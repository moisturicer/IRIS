# IR-818: RDCO is warned about an embargo when deciding a paper, and can still publish

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** ADR-052 decision 14. No FR- id.

**The flags:** `apps/reviews/decisions.py::decision_flags` gains `embargo`, the `{state, until}` payload from `apps.records.embargo.state_payload`, for the party RDCO and null for anyone else. RDCO's party needs its open seat, so an RDCO member without one is told nothing.

**Stale on change:** `decision_token` folds in that same payload, so setting, moving, releasing or re-setting an embargo while a Decision dialog is open makes it stale. `decide/` then answers 409 with the fresh flags, and the dialog redraws with the warning. This applies to every decider, the Adviser included: their dialog goes stale too, although it shows no embargo. That was chosen to keep the token one function.

**The warning:** `DecisionDialog.tsx` shows an "Under embargo" region, for an active embargo only, on Publish and Keep unlisted and not on Reject.

- **On Publish,** it gives the review date and says the paper still goes into Discover, but Ask IRIS won't read or cite it until the Adviser or RDCO releases the embargo. The Publish body drops "Ask IRIS can cite it" while the paper is under embargo.
- **On Keep unlisted,** it says the text stays away from outside AI companies until a release, and promises nothing about Ask IRIS.
- **The date is called a review date,** as on Paper View's card, because a date never ends an embargo (decision 9). With no date, the sentence leaves it out.
- **It blocks nothing.** The confirm button stays enabled, and RDCO publishes, keeps unlisted or rejects as before.

**Tests:**

- `apps/reviews/test_decisions.py::EmbargoAtDecisionTests`:
  - RDCO's flags carry the embargo, and the Adviser's and a non-decider's don't.
  - An embargo set while the dialog is open gives a 409 with the fresh embargo, and nothing is decided.
  - All three outcomes still work during an embargo.
  - A paper published during an embargo is published, and `decision_for_record` still refuses it, with `EMBARGOED` as the only reason.
- `DecisionDialog.test.tsx` ("an embargo at RDCO's Decision"):
  - The warning on Publish, with the corrected body, an enabled Publish and axe.
  - The warning on Keep unlisted.
  - No warning on Reject, or without an active embargo.
  - No dash for a missing date.
  - The warning appearing after a stale 409.

**Evidence, 2026-11-03:**

- **Failing first:** four of the six backend tests failed with `KeyError: 'embargo'` before the flags carried it. The two that passed, "RDCO can still decide" and "the gate still refuses", already held, and they pin decision 14. Three dialog tests failed before the warning existed.
- **After:** `test_decisions.py`, `test_proposal_decision.py` and `test_rdco_executive.py` passed 78. `DecisionDialog.test.tsx`, `readingStandards.test.ts` and `ReviewActionBar.test.tsx` passed. `tsc`, lint and `flake8` are clean.
- **Full suites:** left to CI, as decided for these tickets.
- **Review:** a standards review and a spec review ran on the diff. They led to:
  - The review-date wording ("until {date}" read as if the date ended it).
  - Keep unlisted's wording (it implied a release would put an unlisted paper into Ask IRIS).
  - One shared callout style.
  - The token reusing the flags' payload.
- **Not run:** no real server or browser look. The warning sits in an existing dialog, and its 360 px behaviour is the dialog's own.

**Status:** Implemented; awaiting review.
