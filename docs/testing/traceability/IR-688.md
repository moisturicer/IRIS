## IR-688

**Requirement:** A download request carries the reader's note, a decline or revocation carries RDCO's, the reader may cancel a pending request, and RDCO may revoke a live grant

**Design:** Jira IR-688 (spec IR-672); ADR-048 decisions 3, 4, 6, 7 and 9

**Implementation:** `DownloadRequest` gains `note`, `rdco_note`, `revoked_by`/`revoked_at` and `cancelled_at`, and `RequestStatus` gains `cancelled` (migrations records 0023, accounts 0017, audit 0013, notifications 0008). Create needs a note of 1-500 characters. `decide_download_request` needs a note to decline; `revoke_download_grant` and `cancel_download_request` (`apps/records/download_service.py`) back `POST .../revoke/` (RDCO only, live grants only) and `POST .../cancel/` (the requester's own pending request; anyone else's is a 404). Decline and revocation notify the reader in-app and by email with the note, linking to the record. Cancel and revoke are audited. A cancelled request counts as never made. The paper page's `download` block carries `rdco_note`, and the queue rows carry `grant_live`. RDCO's queue shows the reader's note, expiry and RDCO's note ("No note recorded" for older requests), and asks for a note on Decline and Revoke. `DownloadRequestModal` asks why. `AccessRequestsPage` was already deleted by IR-675. The reader's Cancel button, RDCO's note on the paper page and My Library's view are IR-743

**Test:** `apps/records/test_download_request_notes.py` (per state and per role, through the API); `download-request-revoke` and `download-request-cancel` in `apps/tests/access/table.py`; `DownloadRequestsPage.test.tsx`; `PaperDownloadControl.test.tsx` (the note). Existing tests that created or declined a request without a note now send one, because the note is required
