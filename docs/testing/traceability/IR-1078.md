# IR-1078: Errors say who failed and offer Try again; slow loads say they are still working

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Epic IR-920 (S2 Epic: Frontend UI/UX Redesign). Audit findings US-05 and FB-02 ([`frontend_ui_audit_spec.md`](../../frontend_ui_audit_spec.md) §4.3). Relates to IR-1048 (upload refusal message), which this does not change.

## What changed

- **One helper picks the sentence** (`lib/readFailure.ts`, `failureCause` and `failureMessage`). Offline (the browser says so and nothing answered) says "Check your connection and try again." A timeout or a 5xx says "IRIS took too long to answer. Try again." A 4xx says what the server said, else a neutral line, and keeps a 429's wait. A 5xx never carries the server's text. **Beyond the ticket:** a failure with no answer, no timeout and an online browser (a refused connection) says "We couldn't reach IRIS. Try again.", because "took too long" would be untrue there. A caller may put a lead in front ("The notice didn't load.", "Nothing was changed.") or give a write its own unreached wording. `readFailureKind` is unchanged.
- **The ~22 hard-coded strings are gone** from list pages, settings panels, the notice screens, Discover, My Reviews, the console, the paper page, the profile hook, the publish draft, auth recovery and the opportunities dialogs. A test fails if any non-test source outside the helper contains "check your connection".
- **Loaders talk.** `Skeleton` and `Spinner` (md and lg; sm sits beside a verb and stays silent) show "Still working…" after 2 s and "This is taking too long." plus **Try again** after 10 s when given `onRetry`; clicking it reads again and starts the count over. `LoadingRegion` gives the library's own pulsing cards the same. `DataTable` passes `onRetry` through. Wired on Users, Role requests, Notifications, Audit log, My Reviews, the console, Analytics, the admin lists, Sessions and the four library views.
- **Every list failure has Try again.** New `LoadFailedAlert` for pages that keep their filters on screen (Sessions, Audit log, Delete requests, Download requests, Account deletion requests, Retention); an earlier conversation that failed to open now has one instead of "Reload the page". `InterruptedState`'s button is now "Try again", not "Retry".
- **Loaders on dialogs and sections can read again.** The reviewer, route and edit dialogs, Comments, Files and the PDF reader pass `onRetry` to their `Skeleton`; the three reviewer dialogs and the route dialog also replace their plain load-error line with `LoadFailedAlert` (Try again), their text now saying who failed. Comments' own "Retry" is "Try again". The session check (`AuthBootstrap`), `ProtectedRoute` and a lazy route (`DeferredPage`) have no read of their own, so their Try again is `reloadPage()`, after a 10 s stall.
- **Ask IRIS's conversation opener** (`RuntimeReader`, Jive's area) no longer says "Check that the AI service is available": it asks the helper, and the earlier-conversation page does the same. Both are small edits in `features/ai/`, flagged for Jive's review in the PR.

## Tests

- `lib/readFailure.test.ts`: offline, timeout, 5xx, no-answer, 4xx (server reason, caller's words, neutral line, 429), the lead, and the no-hard-coded-string scan.
- `components/ui/SlowLoad.test.tsx` (fake timers): nothing at 1.999 s, text at 2 s, Try again at 10 s that reads again and restarts the wait, no button without `onRetry`, and `sm` adds no words.
- Try again re-reads, tested on `RouteDialog`, `AddReviewerDialog`, `AssignReviewerDialog` and `ReassignDialog` (a 500 then success; `features/review/ReviewerDialogs.test.tsx` is new), and `DeferredPage` (2 s text, 10 s Try again, `reloadPage` called). Comments' existing test now looks for "Try again". `Skeleton`'s `onRetry` wiring in Files, EditDetails, the PDF reader, `AuthBootstrap` and `ProtectedRoute` is covered by `SlowLoad.test.tsx` and the `DeferredPage` case, not by a test of its own.
- `components/shared/FailureStates.test.tsx`, `features/admin/SessionsPage.test.tsx` (new); the Retention and Audit log tests now check the banner's Try again.
- **Changed deliberately:** the button name `"Retry"` became `"Try again"` in seven interrupted-state tests (DeleteRequests, Discover, MyLibrary, PersonProfile, PaperView, MyReviews, OfficeConsole), and in Comments' one test,, because the ticket asks every failure for Try again. The Retention test's "Failed to load retention periods." and the Audit log's "Could not load" became the new lead wording.

## Evidence

- Run locally 2026-11-14: `tsc --noEmit` clean; eslint no errors (two warnings, neither in lines this change added); the full frontend suite passed once (205 files, 2,772 tests) before the dialog work; after it, `npm run test:changed`: 92 files, 1,499 tests passed. CI runs the whole suite.
- **Not checked:** a browser or a real server failure, a screen reader on the new live text, and the Spinner's new wrapper inside `ProtectedRoute` and the table cell.
- **Left as they are, on purpose:** Login's and Signup's "IRIS can't be reached right now… a connection or server problem" stays: those screens cannot tell whose fault it was, and the wording was set under IR-200 and IR-647. `draft.ts`'s `SERVER_MESSAGE` ("IRIS isn't responding right now…") already names IRIS and says to try again; "took too long" would be untrue for a 500. MyReviews' load-more banner appears only after Show more fails, and Show more beside it is the retry. Small inline loaders (a `sm` spinner beside "Loading the notice…") say nothing extra; the ticket names `Spinner.tsx` and `Skeleton.tsx`. Pages whose failure state keeps the screen's own reassurance (Saved papers, the calls board) do not gain the cause sentence.
