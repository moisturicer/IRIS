## IR-902

**Requirement:** **Playwright journeys for research discovery and document viewing: search, apply filters, open a published paper, open its manuscript at `?page=N` and land on that page** — no FR- id; subtask of the browser-test work around IR-631 (IR-894), covering the part of its AC6 that needs no Ask IRIS. The Ask IRIS journey is IR-903

**Design:** Jira IR-902 · [`../CORE_JOURNEYS.md`](../CORE_JOURNEYS.md) row 6a · [`../../../e2e/README.md`](../../../e2e/README.md)

**Implementation:** `e2e/specs/07-discover.spec.ts` (serial, no retries, ends with the accessibility test like every journey spec); `multiPagePdf` in `e2e/support/pdf.ts` (a valid PDF whose every page says which page it is); `publishViaRdco` and the `manuscript` / `yearAccomplished` draft options in `e2e/support/workflow.ts`. The spec creates and publishes its own papers (a six-page Thesis/Research from last year, a Project from this year) through the API, leaves a third as a draft, and reads as a student with no tie to any of them. Nothing calls Ask IRIS, an embedding model or any paid provider: search is the catalogue's text search, filters are list parameters, the reader draws the PDF in the browser

**Test:** Five journeys, each against the criterion it proves:

| Journey | Proves |
|---|---|
| Search finds published papers by what they say, and never an unpublished one | Permitted discovery: a published paper is found by its words; a draft with the same words is not listed; the card shows the declared origin and record type; a search with no match says so and offers *Clear filters* |
| Filters narrow the results, the address holds them, and Clear all undoes them | Type and Year (completed) each narrow the same two papers; the Filters button states the count; the query is in the address and survives a reload |
| A card opens its paper, and Back returns to the same search | Opening a published paper from Discover; Back restores the search text and results |
| The manuscript opens at `?page=N` and lands on that page | `/records/<id>?page=4` of a six-page paper draws page 4 with its own text, the counter reads *Page 4 / 6*, page 4 is inside the reader's view and page 1 is scrolled past |
| A paper that is not published is the same as a missing one to a reader | The draft's page, opened by id, shows no reader and no trace of its title |

axe (serious/critical, WCAG 2.1 A/AA, contrast included) runs on the Discover results, the open Filters sheet and the paper at a page; the 360px reflow check runs on the results, the filtered results and the paper at a page. Findings are collected and asserted in the closing test, so an accessibility defect fails its own check and does not hide the workflow steps

**Evidence:** 2026-11-06, the pinned build on GitHub Actions (`core-journeys.yml`, started by hand with `--grep` for this spec's tests, so the other journeys did not run),: run [42152702320](https://github.com/moisturicer/IRIS/actions/runs/42152702320). **The five workflow journeys passed** (search 4.0 s, filters 4.7 s, open and Back 2.4 s, open at page 4.0 s, unpublished draft 0.9 s). **The closing accessibility check failed with three real findings**, which are application defects and are left red on purpose:

- `[discover-results]` and `[discover-filtered]`: **59px of horizontal overflow at 360px** on Discover. The widest elements are the *For you / Latest / Trending* group (`div.inline-flex.h-11.shrink-0`, right edge 419px) and links in the result cards (byline, right edge 419px / 406px)
- `[discover-filters-open]`: **serious `label-content-name-mismatch`** on the Filters button (`aria-label="Filters, 1 active"` while its visible text reads *Filters* then *1 active*; WCAG 2.5.3 Label in Name, the same pattern as IR-777 and IR-778)

The paper at a page passed axe and the 360px check. Two earlier runs (42151732899, 42152273782) failed on the spec's own mistakes (a `reload()` that replayed a used refresh token; a click before the search had applied) and were fixed in the spec. Locally: `tsc --noEmit` in `e2e/` clean; the Docker engine on the author's machine failed three times, so nothing was run there.

**Status:** **PARTIAL.** The journeys pass; the accessibility check is red until the two defects above are fixed (IR-910 is the open viewport-matrix ticket that may own the overflow; no ticket was found for the Filters label). IR-902 is Done only when that check passes in a pinned run
