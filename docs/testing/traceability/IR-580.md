## IR-580

**Requirement:** **Private notes in the reading workspace, with autosave I can trust** — no FR- id; subtask 5 of IR-553 (User Stories 10–11 and 20)

**Design:** [ADR-064](../../adr/064-private-reading-notes.md) (**Proposed**, 2026-11-12, awaiting acceptance by Lee Jasmin Adolfo; the note retention rule is its §6 and was the ticket's "decision before ready-for-agent") · [ADR-044](../../adr/044-account-backed-research-library.md) · [16-paper-view-and-review.md](../../ui-ux/16-paper-view-and-review.md) amendment 2026-11-12

**Implementation:** `apps/library`: `ReadingNote` (one per owner and Record, `body` ≤ 20,000 characters, `revision`), migration `0003_readingnote`, and `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE /api/v1/library/notes/<record_id>/` (`ReadingNoteView`). Every call filters by `owner=request.user` and rechecks `Record.objects.visible_to()`, `DELETE` included, so an unreadable Record is the same 404 as a missing one and the note's text is kept until access returns. `PUT` carries `{body, base_revision}`; a stale one is a `409` carrying the server's `body` and `revision` and writes nothing. Not in Django admin, no `AuditEvent`. `ReadingNote` is declared in `accounts/deletion/registry.py` (DELETE) and `accounts/personal_data/registry.py` and `export.py` (`reading_notes`: text and revision, the title only while readable). Frontend: `useReadingNote` (autosave 1 s after typing stops; Saving, Saved or Could not save; a failure keeps the draft and never shows Saved; a 409 pauses autosave and shows both texts; a note that could not be loaded is not editable; a tab-close/reload warning while unsaved (in-app navigation is not guarded: ADR-064 §Consequences); no draft in `localStorage`), `NotesPanel` (a focused editor, a private notice and the three conflict choices Keep both, Keep this device’s text and Use the saved note), and `PaperViewPage` giving the one panel slot to Ask IRIS or Notes (`usePaperChat().tool`, `openNotes`, `NotesFab`; one open at a time, focus follows the swap and returns to the icon). `libraryApi.note`/`saveNote`/`deleteNote`.

**Test:** backend `apps/library/test_notes.py` (empty read creates nothing; revision chain; empty body; the cap; payload validation; delete idempotent; one note per owner and Record; anonymous refused; stale and ahead revisions are 409 and write nothing; reconcile against the returned revision; another reader, KTTO, RDCO, ITSO, IERC, Adviser, System Admin and the Record's own owner never read, change or delete it; an owner in the body is ignored; the same 404 on every call for an unreadable Record; text kept through revocation and back with access; cascade on Record delete; a guard that no app outside `apps/library` and the deletion/export registries mentions the model; not in admin), `apps/tests/access/table.py` (`library-note`, all three methods, `READS_RECORD`), `accounts/test_account_deletion.py` and `accounts/test_personal_data_export.py` (notes deleted with the account; exported as the person's own, title withheld once unreadable). Frontend `NotesPanel.test.tsx` (empty state and privacy notice; existing text; no editor when the load failed; autosave against the right revision; Saving before Saved; failure keeps the draft, Try again; leave warning; the conflict keeps both texts, pauses, and each of the three choices; Ask IRIS switch; axe) and `PaperViewPage.test.tsx` (Notes beside Ask IRIS, no read until opened, one occupant at a time with the question draft kept, Paper entry opens Ask IRIS, dialog focus and Escape over the paper, axe). Deliberate test change: the page test's `@/api/library` mock gains `note` and `saveNote`.

**Evidence:** 2026-11-12, worktree: frontend `npm run test:changed` 13 files, 286 tests pass; `tsc --noEmit` clean; `eslint src` 0 errors (2 warnings, neither introduced here: the record-load effect's unused directive in `PaperViewPage.tsx`, and a missing dependency in another file). **Backend tests were NOT run locally**: the machine has no PostgreSQL, Docker or Python dependencies, so `test_notes.py`, the access matrix, the deletion and export tests and the migration were checked only by `py_compile` and by reading; CI is where they first run, and the migration has not been run against a copy of a realistic database. Not checked: the Notes panel in a browser, a real second device, reduced motion, colour contrast (jsdom cannot).
