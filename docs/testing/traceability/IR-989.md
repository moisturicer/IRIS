**Implementation:**

- `apps/ai/evaluation/extractor_gate.py`: the rule (`verdict`, with `arm_from_files` reading `measure` output and an `eval_retrieval` run file) and the structure measures (`measure_document`, ported from IR-851's `headings.py`; `measure_paper` adds Figures, formula crops, pages, credits and Unstructured's line-box outcomes).

**Tests:** `apps/ai/evaluation/tests/test_extractor_gate.py` (new): the rule's three checks, heading nesting (decimal and roman/lettered), captions as headings and deciding signals, LaTeX against OCR formulas, box share and printed pages, a LlamaParse read swapped in and the Unstructured read swapped back to the same measures, and the verdict read from files.
