## IR-566 Saved Records and Reading status follow the reader's account

**Design:** [ADR-044](../../adr/044-account-backed-research-library.md), accepted 2026-10-30, with its §Amendment of 2026-11-01 (reading history moves to the account in IR-756, not here).

- **Backend:** a new `apps/library` app with `SavedRecord` (unique per owner and Record; one nullable `reading_status` from `core.enums.ReadingStatus`, governed by the vocabulary guard). Migration `library/0001_initial` adds one table. Routes: `GET /api/v1/library/saves/`, and `PUT`/`DELETE /api/v1/library/saves/<record_id>/`. The owner always comes from authentication. `PUT` rechecks `Record.objects.visible_to(user)` and refuses with the same 404 as a missing Record. A save that becomes unreadable is listed as `{record_id, available: false}` only. `PUT` and `DELETE` are idempotent. The routes are declared in the IR-615 access table.
- **Frontend:** a single store, `store/library.store.ts`, read by the Save menu on Discover, by the Record page and by My Library. Updates are optimistic, and a failure rolls back with a `role="alert"` message. The store is cleared on every `sessionEpoch` change, and responses for an older session are dropped. Saves no longer write to `localStorage`; the legacy status folders and `iris_starred_records` are never read and are preserved byte for byte for IR-579. Reading history is cleared on sign-in, sign-out and session end. The Star is gone (ADR-044 Q1). My Library gains *All saved* and lists folders and history under *In this browser only*.

**Tests:** `apps/library/tests.py` covers idempotence, one status at a time, ownership in every direction, the 404 for unreadable Records, the unavailable listing, cascade on delete, two devices, and two accounts on one client. `apps/tests/test_enum_vocabulary.py` adds `ReadingStatus`. `store/library.store.test.ts` covers optimistic success, rollback, no storage writes, dropped answers after an account switch or sign-out, and only the newest write applying. `MyLibraryPage.test.tsx` covers account saves by status, unavailable saves and removing them, a visible rollback, two accounts on one browser (saves and history), and legacy keys left untouched. `DiscoverPage.test.tsx` deliberately rewrites three IR-576 tests against the account library and asserts there is no Star. `PaperViewPage.test.tsx` follows the renamed Save control. These ran locally: the four frontend files, 235 passed. The backend suite, `tsc`, lint, build and `makemigrations --check` are left to CI (no local Python environment).

**Status:** Implemented; awaiting review.
