**Reading history follows the reader's account, privately.** Built to [ADR-044](../../adr/044-account-backed-research-library.md)'s amendment of 2026-11-01 (IR-756) and its amendment of 2026-11-16 (Proposed, accepted when this PR merges), which records the owner's decisions of 2026-11-16 on the card. Written 2026-11-16.

## What changed

- **Two tables** (`library/0006`, renumbered after IR-572's folder migration took `0005`), not in Django admin, writing no audit event:
  - `ReadingVisit`: one row per reader and Record, with `viewed_at`. A revisit moves `viewed_at` forward, and only the 50 most recent are kept. Both foreign keys cascade.
  - `LibraryPreferences`: one row per reader, `keep_history` (default on), created the first time the reader changes it, never by reading it.
- **Routes** (`apps/library/views.py`). The owner is always the signed-in reader, and no route reads anyone else's history.
  - `PUT /library/history/<record_id>/`: the paper view says the reader opened a paper, answered 204. A Record the reader can't read is the same 404 as a missing one. While history is paused it is still 204 and nothing is written, not even `viewed_at`.
  - `GET /library/history/`: most recent first. A paper the reader can't read any more is `{"record_id", "available": false}` and nothing else.
  - `DELETE /library/history/`: Clear history deletes every row.
  - `GET` and `PATCH /library/preferences/`: `{"keep_history": true|false}`; anything else is a 400 and changes nothing.
  - Opening a Record (`GET /records/<id>/`) records nothing.
- **Account data** (ADR-044 amendment 2026-11-16, point 2):
  - Deleting an account deletes both tables (`deletion/registry.py`).
  - The personal-data download gains `reading_history` (id, last opened, and the title only while readable) and `library_preferences` (`personal_data/registry.py`, `export.py`).
- **Frontend:**
  - `store/history.store.ts` holds one session's history, cleared on every sign-in, sign-out and session end, with late answers for an older session dropped. It never touches `localStorage`.
  - The paper view sends the visit once a paper has loaded. It sends nothing while paused, or for a visitor who isn't signed in. A pause is asked again on each paper, so resuming on another device is seen; "kept" is asked once a session, since the server refuses to record while paused anyway.
  - My Library's History view reads the account. It's back in the Saved papers Folder picker, beside a "Keep reading history" switch and a one-line notice that history is stored on the account and only the reader can see it. Clear history is the server's DELETE. The Overview's "Recently opened" reads the account too.
- **The old browser copy:** the paper view no longer writes `iris_reading_history`, and nothing reads it. The auth store still wipes it on every sign-in, sign-out and session end, so it is dropped, not imported (amendment 2026-11-16, point 1).
- **Docs:**
  - ADR-044's new amendment and its README row.
  - `PRODUCT.md` no longer calls history browser-only.
  - `docs/ui-ux/17-ui-states-matrix.md` covers History's unavailable and paused states.
  - `docs/ui-ux/01-design-system.md` had no history wording to remove.

## Tests

- **`backend/apps/library/test_history.py`** (23), through the HTTP API:
  - **Recording:**
    - A visit is listed with its Record. A revisit moves it to the top and keeps one row.
    - Only the 50 most recent are kept.
    - `GET /records/<id>/` records nothing.
    - A visit and a clear write no `AuditEvent`.
    - A signed-out caller gets 401 on all three history methods.
  - **Visibility:**
    - Visiting an unreadable Record is the same 404 as a missing one.
    - A visit whose Record becomes unreadable is listed as unavailable, with no title.
    - Deleting a Record removes it from every history.
  - **Ownership:**
    - Two devices of one account share one history.
    - Two accounts on one client never see each other's.
    - A body naming another owner is ignored.
    - Clearing deletes only the caller's rows.
  - **Pause:**
    - On by default, and reading the setting creates nothing.
    - A paused visit is a 204 that records nothing and doesn't move an existing entry, while what is listed stays.
    - Resuming records again.
    - A pause on one device holds on the other, and never reaches another reader.
    - A value that isn't true or false is a 400.
