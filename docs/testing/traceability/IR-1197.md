# IR-1197

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Why a person was signed out is a dialog in front of the sign-in page, not a banner inside the form.** No FR- id; extends IR-645 and IR-1081 (why a session ended) and IR-642 (a password change ends every session). Asked for by Ashlee Sean, 2026-11-17. Written 2026-11-17. PR: [#861](https://github.com/moisturicer/IRIS/pull/861).

## What changed

- **`SessionNoticeDialog`** (`frontend/src/components/auth/SessionNoticeDialog.tsx`) is a modal dialog: dimmed page behind, focus moved in and kept there, named by its title and described by its text. It shows:
  - each reason the server gives for ending a session, with the same title and text as before (`SESSION_END_MESSAGES`), and the session lost on load (`SESSION_LOST_CODE`);
  - the notice after a password change in Settings, which was a banner and could not be closed.
- **One button, "Sign in again", closes it**, as do Escape and a click outside. Closing it:
  - clears the reason from the address and router state, so a reload shows nothing;
  - puts focus in the email field;
  - keeps the page they were on as the destination after signing in.
- **Form errors stay inline** (`AuthAlert`): wrong password, unverified email, IRIS unreachable, rate limit. They answer what the person just did. `AuthAlert` loses its `session` variant and dismiss button, which only the old banner used.
- The smoke suite gains a dialog case that opens the sign-in page with an expired session and runs axe on the dialog (`e2e/smoke/support/dialogs.ts`).
