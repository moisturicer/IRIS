# IR-1326: notes and highlights on published papers only, and none in Review

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** Hermoine Zozobrado asked for this on 2026-11-24, after IR-1325. The design was agreed in chat. There is no FR- id. [ADR-064](../../adr/064-private-reading-notes.md) and [ADR-065](../../adr/065-highlights-are-bound-to-the-manuscript-bytes.md) are each amended 2026-11-24 (Proposed).

**Why.** In Review, selecting text offered *Comment* (shared, part of the review) and *Highlight* (private, going nowhere). The two looked alike on the PDF. Highlight's note also went to Notes, which Review doesn't open.

- Private notes and highlights are kept only on a **published** paper.
- In Review, selecting text is for a review comment alone: no *Save highlight*, and no private highlight drawn.
- Highlights already stored on unpublished papers are **deleted** (migration `library/0009`, once, irreversible). Notes are kept and withheld. A note or highlight on a paper sent back to review later is withheld and returns when it is published again. A highlight can still be removed.

## Design

**Server** (`apps/library/views.py`):
- `annotatable(user)` is `Record.objects.visible_to(user)` narrowed to `pipeline_status = published`.
- `_annotatable_or_404` gates the note's GET, PUT and DELETE, and a Record's highlights GET and POST. An unpublished Record answers the same 404 as a missing one, to its owner too.
- The account's highlight list marks one on an unpublished Record `available: false`, as it does one whose access was revoked.
- Removing a highlight still answers 204 to everyone (ADR-065 §5).
- Reading history and reading position keep `visible_to()` alone, through `_readable_or_404`.

**Screens:**
- `PaperViewPage` sets `notesOffered` (published, the rule Ask IRIS uses) and offers *Save highlight* only when it is true and the tab isn't Review.
- Highlights are read only for the Paper tab or Notes, and none are drawn in Review.
- `SidePanel` shows the Notes button and panel only when `notesOffered`.
- `useSidePanel` reads the note only then.
