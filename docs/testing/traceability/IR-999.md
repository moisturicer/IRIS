## IR-999 · Soft rate limits: a per-minute burst and a daily backstop (NFR-S6)

Each request budget used to count over one long window, so a person who spent it
waited until the oldest request was a day old ("Too many requests, try again in
about 20 hours"). Each budget now counts over two windows. A per-minute **burst**
is what a person can reach, and its refusal clears in under a minute. A daily
**backstop**, far above heavy real use, is what a script reaches.

| Scope | Burst (setting) | Backstop (setting) | Before |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signed out, per address | 180/minute (`API_THROTTLE_ANON_BURST`) | 30000/day (`API_THROTTLE_ANON`) | 100/day |
| Signed in, per person | 180/minute (`API_THROTTLE_USER_BURST`) | 75000/day (`API_THROTTLE_USER`) | 1000/day |
| Ask IRIS, per person | 15/minute (`API_THROTTLE_AI_QUERY_BURST`) | 750/day (`API_THROTTLE_AI_QUERY`) | 60/hour |
| Sign-in, per address | 30/minute (`API_THROTTLE_LOGIN_BURST`) | 3000/day (`API_THROTTLE_LOGIN`) | 300/hour |
| Registration, per address | 15/minute (`API_THROTTLE_REGISTER_BURST`) | 750/day (`API_THROTTLE_REGISTER`) | 60/hour |

The data download (5/hour) and the AI Overview (600/hour) are unchanged. The
numbers were chosen by Ashlee Sean on 2026-11-09, after Jive Tyler I. Revalde
asked for soft limiting. They are a judgement, not a measurement.

**Implementation:** `core/throttling.py` (`AnonBurstThrottle`,
`UserBurstThrottle`) joins DRF's defaults in `REST_FRAMEWORK`.
`LoginBurstThrottle` and `RegisterBurstThrottle` (`apps/accounts/throttles.py`)
count per client address like their backstops. `AIQueryBurstThrottle`
(`apps/ai/views/chatbot.py`) is on every Ask IRIS view and the paper brief, and the
data download also carries `UserBurstThrottle`.

**Security note:** sign-in now allows bursts of 30 a minute per address, with at
most 3000 a day, against 300 an hour before. The lockout is still off (IR-865).

**Evidence:** `backend/apps/tests/test_soft_rate_limits.py` drives the real
endpoints with DRF's clock moved by hand. Signed out and signed in, the burst
refuses with a `Retry-After` of 60 seconds or less and lets the caller back in a
minute later, and the backstop still refuses past its count. Sign-in and
registration bursts refuse alone, and sign-in spends none of the general burst.
Every view whose throttles name a backstop also names its burst.
`backend/apps/tests/test_throttle_configuration.py` covers the defaults,
overrides and malformed values of all eleven keys.
2026-11-09: five of the eight new tests failed on `main` and all eight pass on the
branch. These two files, plus `test_auth_throttles.py`,
`test_personal_data_export.py` and `test_overview_http.py`, passed in a throwaway
PostgreSQL container. Full-suite and CI evidence is on the PR.
