# IR-859 — LlamaParse heading depth from section numbering

**Affected requirement labels:** FR-M3-01 (PDF text extraction). Its matrix status does not change: Docling stays the configured Extractor until IR-842.

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Change:** `_repair_headings` in `apps/ai/extraction/shaping.py`, inside the one shaping function (ADR-056 decision 4), so stored raw results re-shape on read with no paid re-read. Four rules, each pinned below:
- A numbered heading takes its numbering's depth, using `apps/ai/chunking/numbering.outline_depths` (decimal, roman, lettered), below the level LlamaParse gives most top-level sections (ties go to the shallower level).
- An unnumbered heading after the first numbered one becomes a top-level peer when LlamaParse ranks it that high, and otherwise sits one below the open section.
- A table or figure caption returned as a heading becomes text, keeping its source label.
- An unnumbered first heading on page 1, ahead of every numbered section and not front matter, is the title at level 1.

ADR-056 decision 8 is amended.

**Test:** `apps/ai/extraction/tests/test_shaping.py`:
- `test_numbered_headings_take_the_depth_their_numbering_claims`
- `test_roman_sections_and_lettered_subsections_are_numbering_too`
- `test_unnumbered_headings_are_placed_against_the_numbered_sections`
- `test_a_paper_numbering_nothing_keeps_the_vendor_levels`
- `test_a_caption_returned_as_a_heading_becomes_text`
- `test_a_heading_that_only_mentions_tables_or_figures_stays_a_heading`
- `test_a_title_at_section_level_is_raised_above_the_sections`
- `test_front_matter_first_is_not_taken_for_the_title`

The recorded `numbered_headings` test is unchanged and still passes.

**Evidence:** [IR-851's report, "Re-shaped with IR-859"](../../evaluation/runs/20261103-ir851-llamaparse-six/report.md). On the six proxy papers, numbered headings off-level went from 27 of 113 to 0, and captions returned as headings from 11 to 1. recall@10 with reranking went from 0.929 to 0.857, a single question (q76) at 14 questions. Proxy tier only (ADR-023).
