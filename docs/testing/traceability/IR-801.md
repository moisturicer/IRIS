## IR-801

**Requirement:** **Any owner names another owner of the same paper its Contact person, on every paper, at any time until it is deleted** — ADR-051 decision 27, under IR-779. Both people are told in-app, the change is audited, RDCO has no say, and review emails go to the new Contact person from then on. No FR- id. The Paper View dropdown is IR-803.

**Design:** [ADR-051](../../adr/051-system-admin-account-deletion-and-consent.md) decision 27 (decision 28, the hand-over when the Contact person's account is deleted, is IR-785's) · [ADR-054](../../adr/054-each-person-answers-whether-ai-may-use-their-papers.md) decision 10 (IR-791: the paper keeps the answer its Contact person had when the draft was created, so this act leaves `Record.ai_consent` alone) · Jira IR-801

**Implementation:**
- `records.services.set_primary_owner` no longer uses `update_or_create`. It locks the paper's ownership rows, raises `NotAnOwner` before writing anything when the user has no row, then clears every other primary row and sets the target's, in one transaction. It can no longer add an owner.
- `POST /records/<id>/contact-person/ {"owner": <user id>}` (`RecordViewSet.contact_person`, URL name `record-contact-person`) is gated by `IsRecordOwner` and runs under `_paper_under_lock`. The logic is in `apps/records/contact_person.py`.
  - A caller who can't see the paper, and a deleted paper, get a 404.
  - A visible non-owner gets a 403: RDCO, the paper's Adviser, the offices and a reader of a published paper.
  - A 400, with nothing written, for a missing or malformed id, someone who doesn't own the paper, the current Contact person, and an owner whose account is closed. A closed account keeps its ownership row (ADR-051 decision 28's registry rule), and its emails would reach nobody.
  - On success it answers `{"owners": [...]}`, the Contact person first, the same shape as RDCO's `add-owner/`.
- Audit: a new `CONTACT_PERSON_CHANGED` event (migration `audit/0022`, choices only) with `previous_contact` and `contact`; the event's user is the owner who acted. It is documented in `docs/evaluation/AUDIT_EXPORT.md`.
- Notices, after commit: `notify_contact_person_changed` tells the new Contact person and the old one in-app. It reuses the existing "Contact Person Changed" type (`notifications/0010`). No email: the emails themselves are what moves.
- Review emails needed no change: every review notice picks the `is_primary` owner at send time.

**Not here:** no `record_capabilities` key; IR-803's dropdown can offer the choice to any owner. The frontend audit log has no label for the new type, as it has none for `OWNER_ADDED`.

**Test:** `apps/records/test_contact_person.py`, HTTP level, one class per acceptance criterion:
- every record type × draft, in review and published; a non-Contact owner changing it; an owner naming themselves; a deleted paper (404); `ai_consent` unchanged
- refusals: unseen paper (404), visible non-owner (403), malformed bodies, a stranger (refused and not added), the current Contact person, a closed account; `set_primary_owner` called directly with a stranger raises and adds no row
- exactly one: two stale primary rows are left as one; a failure after clearing rolls both back; the ownership rows are taken `FOR UPDATE`
- both told in-app with the right type; one audit event with the paper, both people and the actor; a refusal writes no event and tells no one
- RDCO, ITSO, IERC, KTTO, the System Admin and the paper's Adviser are refused
- the next revision request emails the new Contact person, and every owner still gets the in-app notice
