## IR-1237

**Requirement:** **Clicking a citation opens a Sources panel at the cited passage** — subtask 5 of the Ask IRIS redesign (IR-1229); no FR- id. No API change: everything comes from the Turn's citations (record, page, quote, revision).

**Implementation:** `frontend/src/features/ai/`.
- `components/SourcesPanel.tsx`: lists a Turn's sources with number, paper title (serif), authors and year with the page, the quote, and "Open at page N" (the same paper-reader address citations use, via `lib/citedPage.ts`). The chosen source is highlighted and scrolled into view; opened from the count, nothing is highlighted. Escape or X closes it. Beside the chat on wide pages, over it as a dialog on tablets, a bottom sheet on phones.
- `CitationText.tsx`: with a panel handler a chip is a numbered button named "Source N: <paper title>, page P" with `aria-pressed`; without one (Paper Chat, the AI Overview) it stays a link.
- `ChatMessageBubble.tsx`: "N passages from M papers" (distinct `record_id`s), Copy and Ask again under an answer; Edit and Copy under a question.
- `ChatToolbar.tsx`: right-panel toggle, "Show sources" / "Hide sources", with `aria-expanded`. `RAGChatPage.tsx` and `RuntimeReader.tsx` own the open/selected state.

**Tests:** `RAGChatPage.sources.test.tsx` (page level, recorded conversation fixture), `SourcesPanel.test.tsx` (includes axe), `CitationText.panel.test.tsx`, `ChatMessageBubble.panel.test.tsx`.
