## IR-1244

**Requirement:** Paper View's Overview no longer shows a Documents card or a "Manage documents" link in Record details, for any viewer. A record's attachments are sent only to those who may download them

**Design:** Jira IR-1244 (Ashlee Sean, 2026-11-19). Attachments are review documents. Only owners, staff and the Adviser may download them (IR-1200). The card showed every other reader their filenames and a link that answered 403. Review's Documents tab already lists them for those who may

**Implementation:** `frontend/src/features/records/paper-view/PaperViewPage.tsx` no longer mounts the card; `PaperDocuments.tsx` and its test are deleted. `backend/apps/records/serializers.py`, `get_files`: returns `[]` unless `may_read_record_documents(user, record)`. `?documents=open` still opens Review's Documents tab. `file_count` on listings is unchanged

**Test:**

- `backend/apps/documents/tests/test_record_document_authorization.py`, `test_a_reader_who_may_not_download_is_sent_no_file_list`: on a published record, a signed-in stranger gets `files == []`, and the owner still gets the attachment.
- `frontend/src/features/records/paper-view/PaperViewPage.test.tsx`, "lists no documents in Record details: they are Review's to show (IR-1244)": with an attachment on the record, Record details shows no "Documents", no filename and no "Manage documents" link.

**Evidence:**

- 2026-11-19, locally, in the backend image against Compose Postgres: the new backend test failed first (the stranger was sent `itso-memo.txt` and its link), then `test_record_document_authorization.py` passed (44 passed, 1 skipped, 98 subtests).
- The broader local run of `apps/records apps/documents` was stopped before finishing because the machine was low on memory. It did not fail; CI's full backend suite is the evidence for it.
- The new frontend test failed first, then `vitest run src/features/records/` passed (60 files, 1,076 tests). `tsc --noEmit` and `eslint` clean.
