# IR-1323: The Overview's side cards are fixed and scroll

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** My Library's Overview keeps the author's reading beside what waits on them, not under their records. The right column stacks three cards: *Waiting on you*, *Continue reading* and *Recently opened*. Each is a fixed rectangle whose list scrolls inside it when it's long. The left column is Recent submissions alone. Requested by Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-24, after [IR-1321](IR-1321.md). No FR- id.

**Design:** frontend only. What each card lists comes from the same sources as in IR-1321.

## What changed

- `LibraryOverview.tsx`: a `SideCard` shell, which holds a fixed height, a pinned heading and footer, and a scrolling body.
  - *Waiting on you* is 24 rem tall; the other two cards are 16 rem each.
  - The lists are no longer cut: every waiting paper and every paper marked Reading is listed, plus up to 20 recently opened. *Show all* on *Waiting on you* is gone.
  - *Recently opened* shows an empty card with a hint instead of disappearing.
- `docs/ui-ux/01-design-system.md`: a *My Library Overview side cards* row.

## Tests

**IMPLEMENTED.** Run locally on 2026-11-24: `npm run test:changed` 363 passed and 3 skipped (with `--maxWorkers=2`; at default workers `documentTitle.test.tsx` timed out twice, and it passes alone and on `main`) and `npm run lint` 0 errors. I also checked screenshots from the smoke harness's mock API on a production build:
- at 1280 px with long lists: each card clips its list and scrolls;
- at 360 px: the cards come first, and there's no horizontal scroll;
- with empty lists: the cards keep their size.
