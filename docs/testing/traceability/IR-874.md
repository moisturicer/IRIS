# IR-874: review threads, backend

**Requirements:** no FR- id. The rule is [ADR-032](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) §7's 2026-11-04 amendment, as corrected 2026-11-06 for this ticket. Parent IR-870; the frontend is IR-875, and outdated threads, suggested wording and the response letter are IR-876.

A review comment used to be a flat message. A top-level comment is now a **thread**: it can be anchored to a passage, a reviewer's thread is *Must fix* or *Suggestion*, a seated reviewer's threads are drafts until **Finish review** sends them, and an open *Must fix* holds the approvals ADR-032 §7 names.

What changed:

- **Model** (`apps/reviews/models.py`, migrations `reviews/0024`, `0025`, `audit/0016`):
  - `ReviewComment` gains its party, severity, draft flag, anchor (Version, page, quote, the text either side of it, ADR-031 regions), state, resolution, closing Decision and the owner's answer.
  - `ReviewRound` records each finished review, *Comment only* included. *Comment only* writes no `Review`, so nothing that reads `Review.status` can mistake it for an outcome.
  - `ReviewThreadEvent` holds each thread's history for the timeline.
- **Backfill** (`0025`): every existing top-level comment becomes a general *Suggestion* thread of its writer's party. Nothing is rewritten.
- **Acts** (`apps/reviews/threads.py`, `comments.py`, `finish_review.py`; endpoints in `apps/records/views.py`):
  - `POST review-comments/` takes `severity` and `anchor`.
  - `DELETE review-comments/<id>/` removes a draft.
  - `POST review-comments/<id>/severity|resolve|reopen|answer/` acts on a thread.
  - `POST finish-review/` sends the drafts with a summary and a verdict.
- **Blocks:**
  - The owner's `new-version/` waits for an answer to each open *Must fix* of a requesting party.
  - A party's own open *Must fix* holds the Adviser's *Accept & send to RDCO* and *Accept* (Proposal), and an office's *Clear*.
  - Every Decision closes open threads, with its comment required as the reason while a *Must fix* is open.
  - Each block is called from the service that owns the act.
- **Projections:**
  - `routing.send_to_rdco_blocked`, `office_review.clear_blocked`, `decision.blocked_outcomes`, `decision.reason_required` and `decision.closes.threads`.
  - Readiness's `open_thread` item.
  - The `finish_review` capability.
  - The tracker's `review_rounds`.
  - The thread acts the viewer may take, as `can_*` fields on every comment.
  - The decision token covers the open threads.
- **Audit:** six event types, documented in `docs/evaluation/AUDIT_EXPORT.md` §Review threads.

**Evidence**

- `apps/reviews/test_review_threads.py`, at the REST seam:
  - a thread on the wire, its anchor and its validation
  - drafts seen only by their writer, against the owner, the Adviser, RDCO and a pool member, in the list, the tracker and record detail
  - each verdict through its own service, including a refused verdict that sends nothing
  - every block and every unblocked act, among them RDCO deciding with open *Must fix* threads and a 409 when a thread appears after the dialog was read
  - an owner's 403 on resolving a reviewer's thread
  - severity owned by the party, not the person
  - Readiness parity with each refusal
  - the audit rows
- `apps/reviews/test_review_threads_migration.py`: the backfill, with bodies, times, versions and replies unchanged.
- `apps/records/test_capabilities.py`: ADR-032 §12's table gains `finish_review` for every open-seat holder, probed with a real *Comment only* finish.
- **Changed deliberately:**
  - `test_review_comments.py`: four tests where a seated reviewer's comment was expected to be visible at once. Their reviewer now finishes the review first.
  - `test_decisions.py`: one test, whose exact `closes` dict gains `threads`.

**Known, accepted:**
- The backfill gives *Suggestion* to every existing top-level comment, owners' included, as the acceptance criterion says. A new owner's thread carries none, and either way it never blocks.
- A comment whose writer's account was removed gets no party, which reads as the authors', since its party can't be known.
- An owner may **reopen** a reviewer's thread. ADR-032 §7 excepts owners only from resolving one.

**Not done here:** the frontend (IR-875); outdated threads, suggested wording and the response to reviewers (IR-876); Viewed marks, saved replies and @mentions (IR-877). Until IR-875 ships, a seated reviewer's comment from today's Conversation box is a draft with no button to send it. That was accepted for this ticket, since IR-875 is next.
