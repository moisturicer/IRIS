# IR-791: A paper keeps its Contact person's AI answer, and the disclosure check reads it

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** ADR-054 decisions 5, 6, 9 to 13, 16 and 17 (decision 18 for test data). `FR-M6-02` (stable label). Builds on IR-789 (the notice and a person's answer). Started by Lee Jasmin Adolfo (backend core, `74879e3f`), finished by Ashlee Sean.

**What is built:**

- **The paper keeps the answer (decisions 9, 10).** `Record.ai_consent` (`records/0036`, nullable, no backfill) is copied from the Contact person's answer when the draft is created: `RecordViewSet.perform_create` and a continuation draft (`continuation._create_child`, from the primary owner), through `apps.records.ai_answer.store_on_new_draft`. Nothing else writes it except the No-to-Yes "all my papers" choice. Null is "no answer stored" and the gate refuses it. No serializer can write it.
- **The gate reads it (decision 17).** `apps/ai/policy/records.py` gives consent only for `ai_consent is True`. The two Postgres indexing functions and the access fence read the same column (`publication/0010`), so the AI runtime and Django agree. `Record.dpa_accepted_at` stays as a legacy column and counts for nothing.
- **Submit asks nothing.** `POST /records/<id>/submit/` no longer requires or stamps `dpa_accepted`, and no longer calls `send_to_ai_on_commit`: the paper's answer was stored at creation, so the gate decides at upload, and every act that opens it later (embargo release, IP release, No-to-Yes "all") queues the paper itself. Publish's `DpaConsentInline` is deleted and `recordsApi.submit(id)` sends no body.
- **Changing an answer (decisions 11-13).** `POST /api/v1/users/me/ai-answer/` `{ai_consent, papers?}` (`user-me-ai-answer`), for the signed-in person only: there is no id to name. Yes to No changes the person's answer and no paper. No to Yes needs `papers`: `from_now`, or `all`, which turns the papers they are **Contact person** of that hold a No into Yes and sends each through `send_to_ai_on_commit`, so the embargo and IP checks still apply. Without `papers` it is a 400 `papers_choice_required` and nothing is written. The same choice is required at `POST /auth/notice/answer/`. Blocked until the current notice is answered, like every route.
- **At upload (decision 16).** No new wiring: extraction, chunking, embedding and the AI Overview already run behind the gate when a manuscript lands, and the gate now reads the stored answer. A paper still starts embargoed (ADR-052), so in practice this runs once a released paper with a Yes gets its manuscript.
- **PDF suggestions off for a No.** `metadata_suggestions` already reports `unsupported` for any paper the gate refuses, and the Publish dialog then shows its neutral "fill them in" line, as it does under an embargo. No frontend change was needed.
- **Wording.** Help's "Publishing your research" now says the text is sent at upload or at release, not at submit, and that Publish asks no consent. `EmbargoStep.tsx` named in the ticket no longer exists (IR-882 deleted it), so there was nothing to reword there.
- **Test data answers Yes (decision 18).** `seed_demo` stores its Contact person's Yes on every draft it makes and stops stamping at submit; a re-run gives its own papers from before IR-791 a Yes. `load_corpus` stores a Yes on every loaded paper except `--origin institutional` (it stays embargoed either way). `clear_for_ai` (`--clear-for-ai`, `mark_corpus_cleared`) stores a Yes on a paper storing no answer and leaves a stored No.
- **Audit.** `AI_ANSWER_CHANGED` (`audit/0021`), with `previous_ai_consent`, `ai_consent`, `papers`, `papers_updated` and `paper_ids` (no titles). Added to `docs/evaluation/AUDIT_EXPORT.md` and the audit log labels.
- **Authorization matrix:** `user-me-ai-answer` (any signed-in account; anonymous refused) in `apps/tests/access/table.py`.

**Not in this ticket:** a Settings screen and a notice pop-up that call the new route (IR-790). Until then the route is reachable through the API only. ADR-054 decision 8 (telling a reader a paper's owner said No) is a separate subtask.

**Migrations renumbered on merging `main`:** main took records `0034`/`0035` and audit `0020`, so the branch's `records/0034_record_ai_consent` became `0036` (on `0035_record_adviser_name`) and `audit/0020_ai_answer_changed_event` became `0021` (on `0020_account_deletion_events`), regenerated from the merged model so its choices carry both lists. The branch's `audit/0019_merge_audit_leaves` was identical to main's and is main's. `makemigrations --check --dry-run`: "No changes detected".

**Tests changed because behaviour changed (deliberate, not to make red tests green):**

- `apps/records/tests.py`: `DpaConsentAtSubmitTests` becomes `SubmitAsksNoConsentTests`. The tests that a submit without consent is refused, that a refused submit stays in draft, that `false` is refused and that submit stamps who and when are replaced by: a submit without consent goes through, and submit stamps nothing and leaves the stored answer. The serializer and PATCH tests gain `ai_consent`. `ConsentSurvivesANewVersionTests` also checks the stored answer.
- `apps/records/test_edit_policy.py`: "a co-owner submits and the consent is theirs" becomes "a co-owner submits and no consent is taken from them".
- `apps/records/test_seed_demo.py`: "submitted records carry DPA consent" becomes "every seeded paper keeps a Yes and no stamp", plus a re-run test.
- `apps/records/test_mark_corpus_cleared.py`, `test_load_corpus.py`: assert the stored Yes instead of the stamp; "consent someone already gave keeps their name" becomes "a No the paper already stores is kept".
- `apps/ai/policy/tests/test_record_adapter.py`: the fake record carries `ai_consent`, parametrized Yes/No/none.
- About twenty fixture files that gave a paper consent with `dpa_accepted_at=...` so the gate would open now give it `ai_consent=True` (and `None` where they withdrew it). The assertions are unchanged. Migration tests that build historical rows were left alone.
- Frontend: `PublishDialog.test.tsx` ("needs consent, and sends the DPA flag" becomes "asks no consent at submit and sends no consent flag"; the other tests stop ticking the removed box), `api/records.test.ts` (submit sends no body), `DpaConsent.test.tsx` (the terms-text test moves from the deleted inline checkbox to `DpaConsentModal`).

**New tests:** `apps/records/test_ai_answer.py`, one class per acceptance criterion: the stored answer at creation (Yes, No, none, no Contact person, co-owner ignored, later change and Contact-person change ignored); the gate on real rows for Yes, No and none, the old stamp not counting, the person's current answer not read; submit without `dpa_accepted` stamps nothing and sends nothing; the change route (signed-in only, own answer only, answer required, Yes to No forward only, No to Yes refused without a choice and writing nothing, `from_now`, `all` reaching only Contact papers holding a No, embargo and IP still refusing, a repeat changing nothing, blocked while the notice is unanswered); the same choice at the notice; upload reading a released paper only with a Yes; the audit event's metadata. Also two `load_corpus` tests (a Yes; none for institutional).
