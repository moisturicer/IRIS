## IR-1204

**Requirement:** **A reviewer may open a review thread on a supporting document, one upload of one of the record's slots, as a whole. It is a thread in every other respect.** [ADR-032](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) §7, *Amendment, 2026-11-18*, accepted with PR #892. Subtask 6 of IR-1199; no FR- id. Decided by Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-18.

Decided with Hermoine on 2026-11-18, while planning the code:
- **A reviewer starts one from the reader:** "Comment on this document" sits in the reader's strip beside "Back to the manuscript", as on the manuscript, where you comment on what you are reading.
- **Only the newest upload takes a new thread.** An older one gets no button, and the server refuses it, so a thread is never born "from an earlier upload".
- **One numbered list:** document threads sit in Threads in the order they were sent, labelled with their document, not in a section of their own.

**Implementation, by point of the amendment:**
- **1, a thread may name a document upload** (`reviews/models.py`, migration `reviews/0033`):
  - `ReviewComment.anchor_upload` points at the `RecordUpload`, with `RESTRICT`.
  - The check constraint `review_thread_names_one_place` stops a thread naming both a passage and a document.
  - `comments.post_comment` takes `document: <upload id>` (`_document`). It refuses:
    - an owner;
    - an upload of another record, or a missing one;
    - an upload that is not its document's newest;
    - a thread that also names a passage or suggests wording;
    - a reply or a Conversation comment that names a document.
  - An office's attachment is a `RecordFile`, not a `RecordUpload`, so it can't be named.
- **2, everything else is a thread's:** severity, draft or Post, party, replies, mentions, resolve, reopen and the answer all go through the existing code unchanged. The authors' notice names the document: *"ITSO posted a Must fix on Consent form, v1 of "<title>"."*
- **3 and 4, who opens and who reads:** an owner is refused (point 1). Reading is `may_read_review`, as for every comment: no new rule.
- **5, a newer upload:** the payload's `document` is `{upload, slot, version, label, earlier, extension}`. `earlier` is true once a newer upload of that document exists. The card then shows the chip "From an earlier upload", and the thread stays on its upload.
- **6, what it blocks:** `threads.open_must_fix`, the Clear and Accept refusals, Submit vN's refusal and `party_counts` already counted every thread, so no change. The tests show document threads are included.
- **7, the response letter:** `response_letter._entry` adds `document`, e.g. "Consent form, v1". The letter shows it after the party and severity (`ResponseLetter.tsx`). Letters frozen before this have no such key, and show as before.
- **8, a Decision closes it:** `close_by_decision` closes every open thread, unchanged.
- **9, opening it shows the document:** the card's link, *Open Consent form, v1*, opens that upload in the review reader. It goes through `onShowDocument` in `ReviewWorkspaceLayout.tsx` and `useThreadsWorkspace.tsx` to `PaperViewPage.tsx`'s shown document. A slot upload stored with another extension is named without a link, as the Documents tab offers it no Open.
- **10, an upload with a thread is kept:** `records.versions.deletion_refused_reason` refuses it, a draft's included: *"A review thread is about this file, so it can't be deleted. Upload a corrected version instead."* `RESTRICT` refuses at the database too, and deleting the whole Record still cascades.

**The reader** (`DocumentInReader.tsx`, `readerDocument.ts`, `DocumentsTab.tsx`, `threads/readerAnnotations.tsx`):
- The Documents tab marks a slot's newest upload with `threadLabel` (`slotUploadDocument`). The strip offers "Comment on this document" only on that upload, and only where the workspace grants `open_general_thread`, the capability a general thread already uses.
- The composer then reads "Comment on Consent form, v1". It offers severity, Post and Save as draft, and no suggested wording.

**Tests:** `reviews/test_document_threads.py`, at the HTTP routes, 16 tests:
- Opening and naming:
  - a reviewer's thread names the upload by slot and version;
  - a general thread names none, and a document thread is a draft until sent;
  - the authors' notice names the document.
- Refusals: an owner, another record's upload, a missing upload, an older upload, both a passage and a document, suggested wording, a reply, a Conversation comment.
- A newer upload makes it "earlier", and the thread stays open on its upload.
- Blocking and closing:
  - its party's Must fix blocks the Clear and is counted in Readiness;
  - Submit vN waits for its answer, and the letter carries "Consent form, v1";
  - a Decision closes it with its reason.
- Deletion: nobody deletes the upload, the owner or RDCO, and `RESTRICT` refuses at the database while a Record's own delete still cascades.
