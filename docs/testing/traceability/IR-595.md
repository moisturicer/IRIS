## IR-595 — public reasoning summaries and activity, separate from private provider traces

**Requirement.** [ADR-042](../../adr/042-general-tools-conversation-contract.md) and the [general-tools spec](../../architecture-review/2026-10-15-askiris-general-tools-spec.md) §6, acceptance scenarios A11, A13, A14: the reader protocol carries `activity` and an optional `reasoning_summary` and nothing diagnostic; the same rules apply live, on replay and reconnect, and to old Turns; a reasoning-only completion is unavailable.

**Built.** `apps/ai/public.py` is the one server-side allowlist (`protocol=public-1`, chosen per request by body or query field or the `X-IRIS-Protocol` header; unknown values are a 400; omitting it keeps the legacy body byte-for-byte). It serves `/ask/`, `/ask/stream/`, `/ask/runs/<id>/stream/` (replay and reconnect, activity not repeated, an activity frame shares its event's `id:`) and `GET /conversations/<id>/` (old Turns projected; `routing`, `research`, `reasoning`, `had_reasoning`, validation codes and `agent_step` are never sent, and nothing is deleted). `Turn.reasoning_summary` and `reasoning_summary_source` (migration 0032) store the summary with its provenance and Turn lifecycle; it is shown only on a generated answer whose stored citations are all still readable. `safe_summary` omits (never repairs) a summary with tool or process narration, markup, citations, a vendor name, or a number, quoted phrase or proper noun absent from the answer and the cited passages; no extra model call. Adapters declare `LLMCapabilities` through their dialect (`display_summary`, `continuation_artifacts`; OpenRouter only); a vendor summary is a private `ToolCompletion.provider_summary` candidate, and opaque `ContinuationArtifact`s are echoed back unchanged by the controller and planner and never rendered.

**Allowlists and limits.** Nested blobs (`listing`, `comparison`, `sources`, citations, candidate `source`, `latest_run`) have explicit allowlists, so `observed` figures and topic `method`/`version` fields are never sent; run `reason` values are kept only from a fixed code set; `capacity_limited` becomes a detail-free `waiting` activity. The summary fact check covers numbers, quotes and capitalised names (sentence openers included, bar a small function-word set); lowercase claims are not checked. The code-owned listing wording still names the topic-count method as prose.

**Not built here.** No transcript-export endpoint exists, so there is nothing to project; a future one must use `turns_for_reader(..., public=True)`. The reader UI requests the protocol as of IR-596. `latest_run` on conversation detail is unchanged. The OpenRouter `reasoning_details` shapes follow the vendor's published format and were not run against a live account.

**Evidence (2026-10-16).** `apps/ai/tests/test_public_protocol_http.py` (supported, unsupported, narrating and think-tag summaries, reasoning-only completions, activity dedupe, legacy body unchanged), `test_public_history_http.py` (legacy history, summary withheld after a revoked source, queued replay and reconnect), `test_continuation_http.py` (signature round trip), `providers/tests/test_reasoning_capabilities.py` (supported, unsupported, redacted) and `answers/tests/test_assurance.py`, all on fakes.
