## IR-1246

**Requirement:** On a phone, Settings shows every section, so the open one, Help included, is never scrolled out of view

**Design:** Jira IR-1246 (found while screenshotting IR-1245, 2026-11-20; fix requested by Ashlee Sean). Below `lg` the section list wraps instead of scrolling sideways. Scrolling the open section into view was the alternative; four short labels wrap without hiding anything, and wrapping needs no scroll logic

**Implementation:** `frontend/src/features/settings/SettingsPage.tsx`, the "Settings sections" nav: `flex flex-wrap lg:flex-col lg:flex-nowrap` in place of `flex lg:flex-col overflow-x-auto scrollbar-thin`. Desktop unchanged

**Test:** `frontend/src/features/settings/SettingsPage.test.tsx`, "wraps its sections on a narrow screen rather than scrolling one out of view (IR-1246)": the nav wraps, doesn't scroll, and marks Help current. jsdom draws no layout, so the screen itself was checked by hand

**Evidence:**

- 2026-11-20, locally: the new test failed first, then `vitest run src/features/settings` passed (34 tests); `tsc --noEmit` and `eslint` clean.
- Hand check, 2026-11-20: this branch's dev server against the local stack, signed in as `student@iris.test`, at `/settings?tab=help` in headless Chromium. At 390 px and 360 px all four sections show on three lines, Help (highlighted) sits at x 171–253, inside the screen, and the page does not scroll sideways. At 1440 px the vertical list is unchanged.
