## IR-1337

**Requirement:** **A reviewer's My Reviews, and an Adviser's My Templates, sit in a sidebar group named for the user's role.** No FR- id; requested by Hermoine Zozobrado, 2026-11-25.

**Before:** My Reviews (with Office Console under it for a coordinator) and an Adviser's My Templates were in the sidebar's main list, with no heading. RDCO's own pages were in a group headed **RDCO**.

**Implementation:** `lib/access.ts`.
- The three entries (`reviewQueue`, its child `reviewOffice`, and `myTemplates`) take section `"Role"`. `navFor` resolves that to the user's own group:
  - **Adviser:** a new group, headed in title case, like "Administration".
  - **ITSO, IERC, KTTO:** a new group each, headed with the office's name.
  - **RDCO:** the existing group, where My Reviews now comes first.
  - A role without a group, such as the System Admin, keeps them in the main list.
- `NAV_SECTION_ORDER` gains Adviser, ITSO, IERC and KTTO, after Main and before RDCO.
- `navFor` now returns entries in that section order, so its list reads as the sidebar draws it.
- Who sees each item, and every route, is unchanged. A student's sidebar is unchanged.

**Test:**
- **`access.test.ts`, changed deliberately:** the main-list order test now expects the Adviser's and each office's review pages in their own group. The RDCO group test expects My Reviews and Office Console first.
- **`Sidebar.test.tsx`, new:**
  - an Adviser's group holds My Reviews and My Templates;
  - an ITSO member's My Reviews is under ITSO;
  - a student has no role group.
- **`Sidebar.test.tsx`, changed deliberately:** RDCO's first link is now My Reviews, not Import Records.

**Evidence:** 2026-11-25, local.
- Before the change, the rewritten order tests failed: they found the review pages in Main.
- After it, the `lib/access` and `components/layout` tests pass, 154 of 154. `npm test` passes: 255 files, 3801 tests. `tsc` is clean.
- The full smoke tier passes, 564 of 564.
- Sidebar screenshots of the smoke build show each layout: Student, Adviser, ITSO coordinator, and RDCO coordinator.
