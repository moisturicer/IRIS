# IR-833 — Autofill source-backed metadata without overwriting the author's edits

**Affected requirement labels:** none by FR- id. This changes the Publish dialog's prefill (IR-406, IR-409) under the [approved Publish contract, IR-830](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-830) decisions 14–18, and supersedes IR-409's chip-only, 90-second behaviour for Publish. No matrix status of another row changes.

## What changed

**Suggestions contract** (`backend/apps/records/metadata_suggestions.py`). `GET /records/<id>/metadata-suggestions/` stays owner-only and keeps `state`, the legacy `suggestions` title/abstract projection, `source`, `manuscript_revision`, `phase` and `retryable`. It adds `fields`: one entry per title, abstract, authors, keywords and publication year, `null` when nothing was found. An entry carries `value`, `basis` (`document_title`, `abstract_section`, `labelled_front_matter`), `page` and `region` only when the stored structure records them, `auto_apply`, and `exceeds_limit`. It has no confidence value. It still reads one stored structure, with no model or vendor call.

**Extraction policy.**
- Title: the structured title, never a file-name fallback.
- Abstract: a standalone, numbered or inline label (`Abstract`, `1. Abstract`, `I. ABSTRACT`, `Abstract—…`, `ABSTRACT We…`), or its Filipino or Spanish heading (`Abstrak`, `Buod`, `Resumen`). An inline label counts only in the first 60 elements of the paper. Paragraphs are kept, page furniture is dropped, and the abstract stops at the next heading or a labelled line such as *Keywords*. A sentence that merely begins with "Abstract" is not taken for one.
- Authors, keywords and publication year come only from labelled front-matter lines, read before the first body heading and within the first 60 elements. A second reading of the same label is ambiguous and offers nothing. So do names that might be surname-first, and two different years.
- Overlong values are offered whole and never auto-applied.
- Only a title or abstract is ever `auto_apply`. Nothing is read into owners, the adviser, origin, embargo, routing, the hints or the completion year.

**Saved decisions and revision-checked saves** (`records/metadata_sources.py`, migration `records/0029`). `Record.details_revision` moves on every detail save, but not on a manuscript upload alone. A save naming an older `expected_details_revision` is refused with `409 stale_edit` and changes nothing. A save naming none is unchecked, as before. `Record.metadata_sources` holds one validated decision per autofillable field: `placeholder`, `automatic` (with `previous` for Undo), `accepted`, `authored` or `dismissed`, each with an optional opaque `source_revision`. Only owners see it or the revision.

**Publish** (`frontend/src/features/publish/`).
- `autofill.ts` holds the rules. A reliable title or abstract is filled into an empty or placeholder field, or one still holding the previous file's untouched automatic value. It is marked *Found in your PDF* (with the page when known) and offers Undo.
- Typed, cleared, accepted, dismissed and undone values survive late, repeated and retried readings. A value the author edited is not offered back. Authors, keywords and year are chips the author accepts.
- `prefill.ts` backs off from 2 s to 15 s and stops after two minutes as *Still processing*, with *Check again*. Reopening resumes. A final retryable failure offers *Retry extraction*. A reading with nothing found says so.
- A replacement invalidates the old reading as it begins. A refused replacement re-reads the kept manuscript.
- `autosave.ts` saves Details a moment after typing stops, one save at a time. Every save names the revision. Invalid fields and owner emails wait for Continue. The footer says *Saving…*, *All changes saved.*, *Some changes aren't saved yet*, or that a save failed (with *Try again*). A conflict stops saving and offers *Reload draft*.
- A new draft saves its file-name title as a `placeholder`.

## Behavioural evidence

- `backend/apps/records/test_metadata_autofill.py` (18, REST seam, stored structure written with the extraction serializer): inline/numbered/standalone abstracts, Filipino and Spanish headings, labels deep in a heading-less paper ignored, the not-an-abstract sentence, paragraphs/furniture/Keywords stop, overlong offered-not-applied, non-English text, only title/abstract auto-apply, labelled authors/keywords/year in order, surname-first and two-year ambiguity, unlabelled names and dates not read, body labels not front matter, no adviser/origin/completion-year fields, page/region only when recorded, no raw structure or storage path, no-text scan ready with nothing.
- `backend/apps/records/test_draft_revision.py` (11): current-revision save moves it; a stale save is `409 stale_edit` and changes nothing; unchecked save; revision must be a number; a stranger gets 404 with no revision; decisions round-trip on reopening; a new draft's placeholder; malformed decisions refused; only owners see decisions or the revision; decisions never change owners, adviser or routing; **two simultaneous saves from one revision against real PostgreSQL in independent threads keep exactly one**.
- `backend/apps/records/test_metadata_suggestions.py` (16, unchanged) still passes: the legacy projection and owner-only refusals hold.
- `frontend/src/features/publish/prefill.test.ts` (14, fake timers): backoff schedule, two-minute stall and Check again, retrying phase, terminal states, inactivity, re-entry, generation forgetting and stale-reply dropping.
- `frontend/src/features/publish/PublishDialog.test.tsx`, the "autofill from the manuscript" and "saving Details as the author works" blocks (32, accessible tree), cover:
  - fill and provenance; Undo; typing and clearing during extraction; an edited value not offered back
  - accepting authors, keywords and year without touching the completion year; Dismiss; overlong review
  - resumed drafts with and without saved decisions; a reopened placeholder
  - pending, retrying and stalled; Check again; neutral lines; Retry extraction; nothing found; re-entry
  - replacement updating only untouched automatic values; the stale previous-file note; a late old-file reply ignored; a refused replacement keeping the accepted file's values
  - submission with suggestions failing; no confidence text and axe clean
  - decisions saved on Continue without routing fields; autosave naming the revision; invalid fields withheld; conflict stopping autosave and Reload draft; a stale Continue refused; a failed save with Try again

**Deliberately changed tests.** IR-409's "prefill from the manuscript" block asserted the behaviour IR-830 supersedes: chips only, never auto-filling, and a 90-second stop. It is replaced by the block above. `prefill.test.ts`'s schedule tests are rewritten for the backoff. The draft-creation test now expects the `placeholder` decision, and the embargo block's "offered with no embargo" test expects the title filled in. No author-control guarantee was weakened: every IR-409 "never overwrites" case is still asserted.
