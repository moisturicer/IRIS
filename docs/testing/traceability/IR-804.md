# IR-804: registration advice and verification send cooldowns

**Requirements:** FR-M6-01 (authentication), NFR-U2 (accessibility); ADR-051 decisions 24–25 and ADR-045 retry guidance. Overall requirement statuses are unchanged.

The signup confirmation tells someone who entered the wrong address to register again with the right one, and says an unverified signup is deleted after 30 days. The old statement that RDCO must decline the abandoned request is removed. Sign-in retains its advice to check the verification email or register again if the link expired.

HTTP 429 alerts use the server's `Retry-After` header first, then its recovery `retry_after` body value, then the existing DRF wait text. Malformed or nonpositive values fall through rather than inventing a wait. The signup form retains the answers after refusal.
