# IR-906: states matrix for Discover, Paper View, My Library, Ask IRIS and Review

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** no requirement row changes. Child of IR-895 (UI state consistency). The matrix is [`docs/ui-ux/17-ui-states-matrix.md`](../../ui-ux/17-ui-states-matrix.md); it adopts `InterruptedState`, which ADR-061 left as an interim for IR-895.

**The change.** `readFailureKind` sorts a failed read into interrupted, unavailable (403 and 404 alike) or failed. The five surfaces use it, so every cell of the matrix is implemented or N/A with a reason.

**What proves it**

**Not covered, said plainly.**

- The review timeline and tracker panels do not report a lost Record to Paper View (named in the matrix as a known gap).
- Colour contrast of the new states, which jsdom cannot check.
- The pages still fetch in their own `useEffect`; this ticket classifies failures and does not add cancellation (ADR-061 follow-up).
- Nothing was looked at in a browser; the states are proved in jsdom only.
- The matrix is a document. Nothing fails if a surface drops a state later.
