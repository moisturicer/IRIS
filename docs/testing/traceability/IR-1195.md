## IR-1195 — Show a change in the paper: outlined, centred and focused

**Basis and scope.** IR-1195; authority ADR-058 §3 (the Changes tab, and a change's kind told in words, never colour alone) and ADR-032 §7 (review threads anchored to a passage). Frontend only: the comparison payload already carries each change's page and regions, and a thread's anchor its own.

**Before.** *Show in vN, p. X* switched Version and scrolled to the first region by the citation rule (its top just under the reader's toolbar). Nothing was drawn on the passage, and focus stayed on the button.

**What changed.**
- `threads/readerAnnotations.tsx`: the reader's `landing` is now a `ReaderLanding` — the passage's Version, page, every region, an accessible `label` and an optional `tag` (the change's kind) — and `LandingMark` draws it on a page: a brand outline per region, flashed once (`motion-safe`), and at the first region on the landing page a focusable group named by `label`, with the `tag` in words above it. Keyed by the landing, so a second *Show* remounts it and flashes again.
- `PaperPdfReader.tsx`: a landing is drawn and landed on only when its Version is the one shown. It is centred (`readerGeometry.centredLandingDelta` over `passageBand`, the passage's extent on that page) in the same reading band citations use — below the toolbar, above the Review action bar — and then focused with `preventScroll`, so the focus doesn't scroll again. A passage taller than the band lands at its start in reading order. Reduced motion scrolls instantly.
- `changes/ChangeItem.tsx`: *Show in* passes the kind and a label, e.g. "Added in v2, page 18, Methodology" (the section omitted when it has no heading).
- `threads/useThreadsWorkspace.tsx`: *Show in paper* from the Threads tab sends the same landing, labelled as its pin is ("Thread 3, ITSO, Must fix") plus its page. The pin's label moved into `threadLabel` so the two can't drift.

**Tests.**
- `readerGeometry.test.ts`: `centredLandingDelta` centres a passage's middle; a passage taller than the view lands at its start, and at its reading-order start in two columns. `passageBand` spans a passage's regions on a page.
- `PaperPdfReader.test.tsx`, "landing on a review passage (IR-1195)": a two-column passage is outlined on every region of its page and none elsewhere, its kind shows in words, and its named group takes focus; it is centred in the reading band (smooth); reduced motion lands with `auto`; a second key lands and focuses again; a passage of a Version not shown draws nothing and doesn't scroll; axe finds no serious or critical violation.
- `changes/ChangesWorkspace.test.tsx` and `threads/ThreadsWorkspace.test.tsx`: both tabs land the reader on the passage's Version with its label.
- **Changed deliberately:** `ChangesTab.test.tsx` "opens either Version at the change's page" now expects `tag` and `label` in what *Show in* sends, and `ChangesWorkspace.test.tsx`'s stand-in reader reports the landing's Version and label. The contract grew; nothing was loosened.
