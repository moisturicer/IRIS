# IR-692: published paper Comments in Overview

**Requirements:** ADR-032 §7 (public Comments); IR-420, subtask 4.

`PaperViewPage` shows the Comments surface only for a published record in Overview. Discover cards and the Review discussion remain separate. The surface renders the public thread oldest first, the count, author and edited labels, reply links, writer controls, deletion markers, reporting dialog, empty state, and server-provided errors. Plain-text input is limited to 2,000 characters.

The frontend currently assumes REST paths `/records/{id}/comments/`, `/records/{id}/comments/{commentId}/`, and `/records/{id}/comments/{commentId}/reports/`. It also expects server-computed `can_edit` and `can_report` flags alongside `author_name`, `body`, `created_at`, `edited_at`, `is_author`, `reply_to`, and `deletion_kind`. IR-690/691 do not yet specify these exact paths or permission fields and both tickets remain To Do. Confirm that contract when those endpoints are implemented; end-to-end behavior remains dependent on them.
