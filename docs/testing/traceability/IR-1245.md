## IR-1245

**Requirement:** Help, opened from Settings, stays in Settings: it is a Settings section at `/settings?tab=help`, its contents links still jump to each section, and an old `/help` link lands there

**Design:** Jira IR-1245 (Ashlee Sean, 2026-11-19). IR-1217 moved Help's entry into Settings as a link to `/help`, which left Settings and showed the breadcrumb "Discover / Help". This replaces that link with a fourth section

**Implementation:**

- `frontend/src/features/settings/SettingsPage.tsx`: a `help` tab beside the other three, showing `HelpGuide` in a Settings `Panel`.
- `frontend/src/features/help/HelpGuide.tsx` (was `HelpPage.tsx`): the same contents list and sections, with no page title of its own. Its headings are now `h3` under the panel's `h2`, and it is two columns only at `xl`, where Settings' content column has room for both.
- `frontend/src/router/redirects.tsx`: `/help` redirects to `/settings?tab=help`, and every redirect now keeps the URL fragment, so `/help#ask-iris` lands on `/settings?tab=help#ask-iris`.
- The `help` screen key (`lib/access.ts`), the Help route and its `matrixRoutes.json` entry are removed. The e2e smoke screen `help` now opens `/settings?tab=help`, and the CSP walkthrough visits it.

**Test:**

- `SettingsPage.test.tsx`, "opens Help as one of its sections, without leaving Settings (IR-1245)": clicking Help keeps the address at `/settings?tab=help` and the Settings heading, marks Help current, and shows the contents with in-page links. It replaces IR-1217's "offers Help in its section list, as a link to the Help page", deliberately.
- `SettingsPage.test.tsx`, "opens straight on Help from its address, and on the section it names": `/settings?tab=help#discover` opens Help and scrolls to Finding research.
- `SettingsPage.test.tsx`, "has the account sections only": now four sections, Help last (changed deliberately).
- `redirects.test.tsx`, "an old link into Help keeps the section it named", plus the table-driven `/help` → `/settings?tab=help` case.
- `HelpGuide.test.tsx`: the IR-745 role tests, unchanged except that they render `HelpGuide` and no longer look for a page title.

**Evidence:**

- 2026-11-19, locally: the four new or changed tests failed first, then `vitest run src/features/settings src/features/help src/router/redirects.test.tsx src/lib/access.test.ts src/components/layout` passed (12 files, 205 tests). `tsc --noEmit` and `eslint` clean.
- `documentTitle`, `roleNavigation` and `viewportMatrix` don't load in this worktree (its linked `node_modules` puts `pdf.worker` outside Vite's allowed folder). None of them mentions Help; CI runs them, and the e2e smoke.
