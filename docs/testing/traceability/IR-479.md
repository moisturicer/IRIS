## IR-479

**Requirement:** **Reviewer names and review comments are read by participants only** — security; no FR- id

**Design:** [ADR-032 §10](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md), amended 2026-10-08 (IR-479) · design settled with the project lead 2026-10-08 (grilling) · Jira IR-479

**Implementation:** `core.permissions.may_read_review`: an owner, anyone who has ever held a seat, or a member of an office whose assignment on the record is active now (its pool included). It gates, as `null` (not disclosed): record detail's `reviews` and each clearance's `comment` and `reviewed_by_name`; the tracker's `reviews`, per-seat `seats` (IR-269's, widened from `is_record_participant`), each resubmission's `reason`, `requested_by` and `resolved_by`, each routing movement's `actor` and `reason`, and `current_holders[].opened_by` (on detail too). `clearance_payload` takes `readable` as a required keyword, so no caller can forget it. Outcomes, party states and `workflow_state` stay visible to every reader. No RDCO exception; document requests keep IR-349's `may_read_requests`. Frontend: the gated types are nullable, and the timeline treats undisclosed reviews as none to list; no reader-facing screen read these fields

**Test:** `apps/reviews/test_review_privacy.py` (REST seam: a student on a published record, an office not taking part, RDCO never holding, and the owner, a done seat, a pool member and a legacy intake reviewer reading it) · `apps/reviews/test_office_review.py` and `apps/records/test_tracker.py` (participants unchanged)

**Evidence:** CI on the IR-479 PR (flake8, pytest, lint, Vitest, build); per the team rule these were not run locally
