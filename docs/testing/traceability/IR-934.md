## IR-934

**Requirement:** **Discover's Filters button names itself with the words it shows, the manuscript cover's placeholder text has enough contrast, and the 360px reflow check measures the settled layout** — no FR- id; WCAG 2.1 2.5.3 Label in Name and 1.4.10 Reflow, found by IR-902's pinned-build run (`label-content-name-mismatch`, serious; a reported 59px of overflow). Touches NFR-A1

**Design:** Jira IR-934 · IR-902 · [`../CORE_JOURNEYS.md`](../CORE_JOURNEYS.md) row 9

**Implementation:** `frontend/src/features/discover/DiscoverFilterBar.tsx`: a real space between the visible *Filters* text and its count, so the text a checker reads is *Filters 1 active*, which the accessible name *Filters, 1 active* contains. `e2e/support/a11y.ts`: `reflow360` waits (3 s at most) for CSS transitions to finish after resizing before it measures

`ManuscriptCover.tsx`: the *Preparing first page…* placeholder is `text-stone-600`, not `text-stone-500` (4.39:1 on the `stone-100` panel, under the 4.5:1 minimum; WCAG 1.4.3). Found by the second pinned run once the first two findings were gone; jsdom cannot evaluate contrast, so the pinned run is its check

**Finding on the overflow:** it is not a Discover defect. Reproduced against the built app with the smoke tier's mocked API: after the window narrows from desktop to 360px, the app shell animates its left margin from 240px to 0 over about 200 ms, and the page is wider than the viewport only until that ends. The settled page has `scrollWidth` 360 (measured after 2.5 s, and at 100 ms intervals up to 3 s). A reader who opens Discover at 360px never sees it. The helper measured immediately after the resize, so it reported the transition

**Test:** `DiscoverPage.test.tsx` › *reads its visible text and count as separate words…* fails without the space (`Filters1 active`) and passes with it. axe's rule itself needs a layout and was checked in Chromium against the built app: the Filters button reports the violation with the old markup and none with the space. The full `DiscoverPage.test.tsx` passes, 54 of 54
