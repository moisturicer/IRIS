## IR-565

**Requirement:** **A long Manuscript opens at its first or cited page, and only a bounded window of pages is painted** — no FR- id; subtask 12 of IR-552 (User Story 21)

**Design:** Jira IR-565 · IR-552 · [ADR-031](../../adr/031-pdf-citation-overlay.md) (the reader it changes)

**Implementation:** `paper-view/usePdfDocument.ts` (new) owns the paper's load: the fetch and the pdf.js open both take an `AbortSignal`, the reader is ready once the page it opens at (the citation's, else page 1) has loaded, and the rest load in the background eight at a time; a change of session, record or version aborts the load and releases the document exactly once (`lib/pdf.ts`'s `destroy` is idempotent, and the hook calls it once). `PaperPdfReader` is remounted per `sessionEpoch` + record + version, so a new sign-in never sees the last account's document or Find state. Every page keeps a box of its measured size (the spacer; an unloaded page is sized from one that has loaded), and only the pages `readerGeometry.liveWindow` names carry a canvas and a text layer: those in view, two either side, and a citation's or Find's target, at most `MAX_LIVE_PAGES` (10). A citation or Find lands only once its page has painted at the current scale. Find reads text through the document's page loader, independent of painting, and starts extracting the first time Find opens. `useReadingPosition` reports the last page in view as well as the current one, measures once on opening, and restores the reader's place when a loaded page turns out to differ in size from the estimate (keyed on those pages only, so a paper of one page size never restores mid-load). Both manuscript endpoints (`RecordViewSet.manuscript`, `.version_manuscript`) now send `Cache-Control: private, no-store`; neither sent any before, so a browser could keep an authenticated PDF on disk after logout. No byte or document reuse is added, so there is nothing to bound

**Test:** `readerGeometry.test.ts` (`liveWindow`: the view and two either side, the paper's edges, a far pinned page, ignored pins, never more than `MAX_LIVE_PAGES`, an empty paper) · `PaperPdfReader.test.tsx` (a 100-page fixture: the cited page and page 1 each shown while every other page is still loading; a newly cited page navigated to only once painted; 3 canvases on opening and 5 after scrolling to page 50 of 100, never over the bound; Find on page 90 painting that page before landing; a uniform paper finishing its load not pulling the reader back; a Find landing forgotten when Find closes; a new session discarding the document and Find state; a fetch aborted in flight; each version's document released once; one that finished opening after the reader left released once) · `apps/records/test_manuscript.py`, `test_record_versions.py` (the header on each endpoint). **Two existing assertions changed, deliberately:** the fetch is now expected with its abort signal, and the IR-354 refit test counts landings rather than every `scrollBy`, since the reader now also keeps its place through the refit

Before: page 1 ready in 747–1441 ms and page 60 in 1428–1624 ms, each only once all 100 pages had loaded, with 101 canvases and 100 text layers. After: page 1 in 793–894 ms and page 60 in 631–742 ms with 17–25 pages loaded, peak 7–8 canvases and 6–7 text layers; Find marked page 90 in every run. Report: `docs/testing/evidence/IR-565/reader-benchmark.json`. jsdom counts on the fixture, before → after: pages needed before the paper shows 100 → 1, live canvases 100 → 3. `npx tsc --noEmit` clean, eslint clean on the touched files, the two reader test files 83 passed; the backend tests ran in a throwaway container against this tree, 2 passed. The full suites are CI's
