## IR-803 — The Contact person dropdown on Paper View

**Requirement:** any owner names another owner of the same paper its Contact person from one dropdown on Paper View, on every record type and at every stage, including published — ADR-051 decision 27, under IR-779. No FR- id. The act itself is IR-801's `POST /records/<id>/contact-person/`.

**Decisions, Hermoine Zozobrado, 2026-11-11:**
1. **A capability, not an owner check on the screen.** `record_capabilities` offers `change_contact_person` to any owner, in every state, with one owner too. IR-801 had chosen no key; this ticket's acceptance criteria take the control from capabilities, so the screen compares no role names and doesn't decide who may act. With one owner the key shows that owner's name and offers no choice.
2. **Choose, then save.** Picking an owner doesn't send anything; "Make Contact person" does. Arrowing through a closed native select fires a change on some platforms, and each save tells two people in-app.
3. **The line says the emails only:** "This person gets the review emails." ADR-051 decision 27 also shares the Contact person's email on a Discoverable Proposal, which isn't built (IR-421, post-MVP), so the screen doesn't describe it yet. Add it with IR-421.
4. **Closed accounts are left out of the choice** (after code review). A deleted account keeps its ownership row as "Former …" (ADR-051 decision 28), and IR-801 refuses it, so offering it only invites a refusal. A paper with one owner who can take it shows that owner by name, with no dropdown.
5. **Overview only** (after code review). The card sits in Record details, on Paper View's Overview, which every owner can open at every stage. It isn't repeated in the Review section.

**Implementation:**
- `core/permissions.py::record_capabilities`: `change_contact_person` for any owner, beside the owner's other keys.
- `RecordOwnerSerializer.account_closed`: true for an owner whose account is closed (`is_active` false, which only account deletion sets). It adds nothing a reader couldn't already see: a deleted owner's name is already "Former …".
- `frontend/src/features/records/paper-view/ContactPersonCard.tsx`, in Record details between Institutional Governance and Embargo (`PaperViewPage.tsx`):
  - Not rendered without `change_contact_person`.
  - The choices are every owner whose account isn't closed, plus the Contact person whoever they are.
  - Two or more choices: a `Select` labelled "Contact person" at the `lg` step (44 px, `12-accessibility.md` §4), each owner by name (their email when the name is blank), the Contact person selected, the line above as its description, and "Make Contact person" (44 px), enabled only for a different owner.
  - Fewer than two: the Contact person's name and the line, no dropdown and no button.
  - Success: "<name> is now the Contact person." as a status, then the paper is read again, so the owners' order and the selection follow the server.
  - Refusal: the server's message for `owner`, else its `detail`, as an alert, and the dropdown goes back to the Contact person before the attempt.
  - At 360 px the dropdown may shrink below its longest name (`min-w-0 max-w-full`), and a lone name wraps.
- `recordsApi.changeContactPerson(id, ownerUserId)`, sharing `OwnersAnswer` with `addOwner`, and the `Capability` union.
