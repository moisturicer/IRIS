## IR-1242

**Requirement:** After a deliberate sign-out, the next sign-in on that device, by the same account or another, lands on that account's own landing page (`landingPathFor`, IR-534, unchanged), never on the page the previous session left open. A session that ended on its own (expired, idle, lost on load) still returns the *same* account to the page it was on (IR-236, IR-1081); any other account lands on its own landing page. A page asked for while signed out, in a tab with no session on record, is still followed

**Design:** Jira IR-1242 (reported by Lee Jasmin Adolfo, 2026-11-19: RDCO on Help signed out, a student signed in and landed on Help). Extends IR-1207, which only skipped a saved page the new account's role may not open; Help is open to every role, so it passed

**Implementation:**

- `frontend/src/store/auth.store.ts`: `signOut()` revokes the session on the server (a failure still signs out) and calls `logout({ deliberate: true })`, which sets `signedOut` in the same update that clears the token; `login()` clears it. `login()` and `hydrateAuth()` record the account id; a deliberate logout forgets it, any other logout keeps it.
- `frontend/src/lib/authSession.ts`: `rememberSessionAccount`, `forgetSessionAccount`, `sessionAccount` keep that one id in `sessionStorage` (`iris_session_account`), so it survives the idle-timeout path's full page load and ends with the tab. Unavailable storage means no account is known.
- `frontend/src/components/auth/ProtectedRoute.tsx`: with no token and `signedOut`, redirects to `/login` with no destination and no reason.
- `frontend/src/features/auth/LoginPage.tsx`: reads the recorded account on mount and follows a saved page only when none is recorded or it is the account signing in, and (IR-1207) the role may open it.
- `AccountMenu.tsx`, `NoticeAnswerScreen.tsx`, `PendingApprovalPage.tsx`: every Sign out button goes through `signOut()`.

Known limit: after an account's session expires, a different person who arrives at sign-in through a link they were sent lands on their home page rather than the link, once. The next sign-in records them.

**Test:**

- `frontend/src/features/auth/LoginPage.test.tsx`, "after another account on this device", through the real route guard on `/help`: RDCO signs out, KTTO signs in and lands on its home page; the same account signing back in after a sign-out also lands home; KTTO's expired session returns KTTO to `/help`; RDCO's expired session sends KTTO home; a `?next=` left by another account's session sends KTTO home. The existing destination tests (router state, `?next=`, IR-1081, IR-1207) still pass with no account recorded.
- `frontend/src/components/auth/ProtectedRoute.test.tsx`: a deliberate sign-out carries no destination and no reason; after the next sign-in the destination is carried again.
- `frontend/src/lib/authSession.test.ts`, "whose session this tab last held": none in a fresh tab, remembered, forgotten, corrupted value read as none.
- `PendingApprovalPage.test.tsx`, `NoticeAnswerScreen.test.tsx`, `Sidebar.test.tsx`, unchanged: each Sign out still revokes and ends the session, including when the revoke fails.
