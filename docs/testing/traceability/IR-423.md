## IR-423

**Requirement:** **A paper longer than voyage-context-4's 32k-token window can be indexed** — extends FR-M3-02 (stable label only)

**Design:** [ADR-015](../../adr/015-voyage-embedding-and-reranking.md) (contextualized chunk embedding)

**Implementation:** `apps/ai/providers/batching.py` (`EMBED_WINDOW_TOKENS` = 30,000 and `window_for_embedding`, the existing `batch_by_token_budget` rule named for this use) · `apps/ai/indexing.py` (`embed_active_chunk_set` presents a long paper as consecutive windows, each its own document, and reports `windows` on the outcome) · `apps/ai/tasks.py` (carries `windows`). The Voyage adapter and the `EmbeddingProvider` port are unchanged: the adapter still refuses to split a document, and what counts as one is decided where the chunks are known. **Trade-off recorded:** a chunk is contextualized only against its own window, so chunks either side of a boundary no longer see each other; a paper that fits one window is unchanged. Before this, `embed_active_chunk_set` sent every chunk of a paper as one example and Voyage answered 400 ("Contextualized chunk embeddings do not support truncation"), so 7 of 20 newly loaded papers had no vectors

**Test:** `apps/ai/providers/tests/test_batching.py::EmbeddingWindowTests` (8 — under the cap, one window when short, consecutive and ordered, none over budget, just-under and just-over, an oversized chunk left alone, budget read at call time) · `apps/ai/tests/test_chunk_embedding.py::LongDocumentWindowTests` (6 — a ~42k-token paper is several documents, none over the window, one vector per chunk, vectors stay on the right chunk across windows, `windows` reported, a short paper is still one document)

**Evidence:** `pytest apps/ai/providers/tests/test_batching.py apps/ai/tests/test_chunk_embedding.py` green 2026-09-30. **Against the real vendor:** the 7 papers that had failed (49k, 44k, 53k tokens and four just over 30k) embedded 839 chunks with zero failures; `backfill_embeddings --dry-run` then reported all 40 papers embedded

**Status:** **IMPLEMENTED, tested against the deterministic fake and the real vendor.** Not yet measured: whether window boundaries cost retrieval quality (the eval harness can now answer that on the seven long papers)
