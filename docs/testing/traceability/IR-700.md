## IR-700 — The profile page, and the names that link to it

**Basis and scope.** IR-700 (spec IR-694, builds on IR-696). Another person's Profile at `/people/:id`: a circular photo or initials, the name, the role (Student, Adviser or the office), college and department, the university, About, Research interests, **Research** and **Advised** (each paper a `ResearchCard` with its own views, ten at a time, newest first), and a **Collaborators** column (twelve at a time, most papers shared first) that sits beside the sections once the page's own width allows and falls under them otherwise. There are no totals, no citation counts and no email address. A field left empty is left out, and a person with nothing published shows one line, "No published papers yet." Your own id redirects to `/profile`. A person the server hides answers 404 and gets the standard not-found screen, the same as an address that is not an id.

The page reads the **draft IR-696 contract** (below) through one module, `src/api/people.ts`; the types are `src/types/people.ts`; the data, with a first page of each list read at once and every list paged on its own, is `usePersonProfile.ts`. A list that fails keeps its place with its own retry and never hides the rest. A read that gets no answer in time is shown as interrupted, with Retry, not as a refusal.

**Names that link.** On a paper's page the Adviser's name opens their Profile when the record says `adviser_has_profile`, and an **Owners** line lists the owners, linking those with `has_profile`; it shows only when at least one owner has a Profile to open. An absent or false flag leaves the name plain text, so a suspended or former user never links to a not-found page, and until IR-696 sends the flags nothing links. Names in the review discussion do not link. **Comment writers are not linked here: IR-700 says they wait for IR-692.**

**Edit profile and your own Profile** lose the middle-initial field and the **ORCID iD**: the field, its checksum helper (`src/lib/orcid.ts`), its tests and the `orcid` payload field are removed. This departs from IR-694, IR-695's acceptance criteria and IR-699; the design review of 2026-11-07 decided it, and IR-695 has been told (reported by the author on 2026-11-07; not recorded on the Jira card). A middle initial already on an account is not sent and still shows in the display name.

### IR-696 contract (agreed with Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-07, as reported by the author; not yet recorded on the IR-696 card)

Signed-in users only. A suspended, inactive or unapproved account, a System Admin or a missing id answers **404**, with the same body, so the API never confirms a hidden account exists. Never returned: email, coordinator status, lock state, or anything unpublished. Paper lists follow Discover's visibility.

- `GET /api/v1/users/<id>/profile/` → `{ id, first_name, middle_initial, last_name, role_label, college_name, department_name, course_name, bio, research_interests, photo_url }`. `role_label` is `Student`, `Adviser`, `ITSO`, `IERC`, `KTTO` or `RDCO`. `photo_url` is the existing `/users/<id>/photo/?v=` path, or `null`; empty text is `""` and `[]`. The three lists are not in this payload.
- `GET /api/v1/users/<id>/profile/research/?page=&page_size=` and `.../advised/` → `{ next, results: [{ id, title, authors: string[], classification_name, record_type_name, publication_year, views }] }`, newest published first. `next` is `null` on the last page. **No `count`**: nothing on the page shows a total.
- `GET /api/v1/users/<id>/profile/collaborators/?page=&page_size=` → `{ next, results: [{ id, first_name, middle_initial, last_name, photo_url, shared_papers }] }`, most papers shared first, then name. Suspended, inactive and former accounts are left out.
- On the record detail payload: `adviser_has_profile` (boolean) and `owners[].has_profile` (boolean), true only on a published paper and only when that person's `GET /users/<id>/profile/` would answer 200.

Settled sizes: papers 10 a page, collaborators 12.

**Added while building IR-696 (decided 2026-11-07, see [IR-696](IR-696.md)):** "unapproved" is an account with no role; an unverified account also has no profile; `views` is the paper's raw `access_count`, not distinct accounts (distinct since IR-369, 2026-11-12); `page_size` over 50 is capped at 50; a `page` or `page_size` that is not a whole number of 1 or more answers 400; a page past the end answers an empty 200.

**Not yet true end to end.** *Update: [IR-696](IR-696.md) now implements this contract on its own branch; until it merges, the paragraph below still holds, and after it merges the page has still not been run against a real backend.* IR-696 was not in code when this was written (checked 2026-11-07: no backend branch or pull request, nothing on `main`, and the card was still To Do), so every id answers 404 today and lands on the not-found screen, and nothing links to it, which is why this can merge before IR-696 without sending anyone to a broken page. Nothing has been run against a real backend. **Not done:** comment-writer links (IR-692), a backend that sends the contract above, and the e2e matrix run, which CI performs.

**Tests.** `src/features/profile/PersonProfilePage.test.tsx` (24 cases through the accessible tree, with axe, `peopleApi` mocked at its module): the header, every section, what is asked of the API, a paper's views and link, no email or citation text, collaborator links, the authenticated photo and its initials, paging of Research, Advised and Collaborators with focus on the first new row, a failed next page and its retry, empty sections left out, the one empty line, an office as the role, the not-found screen for a hidden person and for an address that is not an id, the redirect for your own id (neither asks the server), the loading announcement, a failed read and its retry, an interrupted read, and a failed list that keeps its place. `PaperViewPage.test.tsx` gains four cases for the Adviser and owner links. `EditProfilePage.test.tsx` and `ProfilePage.test.tsx` lose their ORCID cases and gain one that the M.I. and ORCID fields are absent and a recorded middle initial is not sent. `e2e/smoke/support/screens.ts` has a `person-profile` row with mocked endpoints for the viewport, axe and coverage specs.
