## IR-700 — The profile page, and the names that link to it (prototype)

**Basis and scope.** IR-700 (spec IR-694, builds on IR-696). This change is the **prototype** of another person's Profile at `/people/:id`, built over sample people because IR-696 is not in code. It shows a circular photo or initials, the name, the role (Student, Adviser or the office), college and department, the university, About, Research interests, **Research** and **Advised** (each paper a `ResearchCard` with its own views, ten at a time, newest first), and a **Collaborators** column (twelve at a time) that sits beside the sections once the page's own width allows and falls under them otherwise. There are no totals, no citation counts and no email address. A field left empty is left out, and a person with nothing published shows one line, "No published papers yet." Your own id redirects to `/profile`, and a profile the server would hide answers with the standard not-found screen.

The draft types are `src/features/profile/publicProfile.ts`; the page is `PersonProfilePage.tsx` and `PersonProfileView.tsx`; `PersonAvatar.tsx` is shared with the rail and, later, People search.

**Also changed here.** Edit profile and your own Profile no longer have a middle-initial field or an **ORCID iD**: the field, its checksum helper (`src/lib/orcid.ts`), its tests and the `orcid` payload field are removed. This departs from IR-694, IR-695's acceptance criteria and IR-699; the design review of 2026-11-07 decided it, and IR-695 needs to be told. A middle initial already on an account is untouched and still shows in the display name.

**Not yet true end to end.** Nothing here reads the API. `resolveSample` and the sample people stand in for IR-696, and the "Prototype, sample people" strip is for review only. **Not done:** the names that link to a profile from a published paper's owners and Adviser (comment writers wait on IR-692), the server's not-found answer for a suspended account (IR-698), the authenticated photo fetch (`useAuthedImage` is built, the prototype passes a ready URL), and a "has a visible Profile" flag so a suspended or former user's name shows as plain text (needs IR-696's payload). `PersonProfilePage` also has no route entry in `lib/access.ts`; `/people/:id` is added to the router and to `matrixRoutes.json` only.

**Tests.** `src/features/profile/PersonProfilePage.test.tsx` (16 cases through the accessible tree, with axe): the header, every section, a paper's views and link, no email or citation text, collaborator links, ten-at-a-time and twelve-at-a-time paging with focus on the first new row, no Show more when everything fits, empty sections left out, the one empty line, an office as the role, a collaborator's own page, the not-found screen for a suspended and a missing person, and the redirect for your own id. `EditProfilePage.test.tsx` and `ProfilePage.test.tsx` lose their ORCID cases and gain one that the M.I. and ORCID fields are absent and a recorded middle initial is not sent.

**Executed evidence (2026-11-07).** `npx vitest run src/features/profile src/features/settings src/router/viewportMatrix.test.ts`: 5 files, 51 tests passed. `tsc --noEmit` and `eslint` on every touched file reported nothing, and the Impeccable detector reported nothing. Looked at in a browser at 390, 1024 and 1280 px against a throwaway stand-in API: no horizontal scroll at 390, and at a 1024 px window the page's own width (713 px) kept Collaborators under the sections. `npm run test:changed` then ran every test that imports a changed file: 14 files, 152 tests passed. An earlier wider run of the router suite had shown one unrelated timing failure in `roleNavigation.test.tsx` that passed alone, as in IR-933; it did not recur.
