## IR-1307

**Requirement:** In the sidebar's account menu, a long signed-in email stays on one line and ends in "…" instead of wrapping under the name. The whole address shows on hover, is read whole by screen readers, and is shown in full on Profile. A short email looks as it did

**Design:** Jira IR-1307 (reported and decided by Lee Jasmin Adolfo, 2026-11-23). The popover is as narrow as the sidebar, so `hermoinejoyce.zozobrado@cit.edu` put its `.edu` on a second line. DESIGN.md's Full Text Rule (IR-1218) gains an **eighth allowed cut**, "the signed-in email in the account menu"

**Implementation:**

- `frontend/src/components/layout/AccountMenu.tsx`: the email is `truncate` (was `break-words`) with `title` set to the full address.
- `DESIGN.md`, `docs/engineering/CODING_STANDARDS.md` and `frontend/src/test/fullTextRule.test.ts` name the eighth cut, allowed only in `AccountMenu.tsx`.

**Test:** `Sidebar.test.tsx` "keeps the email on one line, cut short, with the full address as its title". jsdom does no layout, so the test checks the class and the title, not the drawn ellipsis.

**Changed deliberately:** `fullTextRule.test.ts`'s allowlist gains this file.

**Evidence:**

- 2026-11-23, locally: `vitest run src/components/layout/Sidebar.test.tsx -t "account menu"` passed, 11 tests. The Full Text Rule guard failed on the new `truncate`, then passed once the cut was named.
- Not checked by hand in a browser yet.
