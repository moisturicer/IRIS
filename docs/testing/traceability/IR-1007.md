## IR-1007 — Burst-test the runtime agent with many readers at once

**Basis and scope.** IR-1007 under IR-955. `eval_pilot_capacity` (IR-550) drives Django's `/ask/stream/`, which IR-957 retired, so nothing measured the runtime agent under simultaneous load. The capacity limits in `docs/engineering/AI_RUNTIME_LIMITS.md` are assumed, not measured. This adds one command and records one paid burst. It is one measurement, not IR-755's release evidence. Spending approved by Jive Tyler I. Revalde in chat on 2026-11-10: 100 submissions, bounded by provider admission on the development campaign.

**What changed.**
- `apps/ai/evaluation/runtime_burst.py`: `plan_items` spreads questions over readers in turn and marks a share deep, deterministically; `run_burst` holds every submission until all are ready, then follows each through `collect_case_runtime`; `summarize` counts every submission (`503 busy` is `refused`, failures stay in the denominator), reports latency from submission per depth, and claims `measured` only for a real, cold, simultaneous burst of at least 100 distinct questions. No latency objective is approved for the runtime, so it states none.
- `apps/ai/management/commands/eval_runtime_capacity.py`: one Conversation per question, as `--reader` accounts in turn, through the gateway with the edge's grant made in-process. Grant fetches are capped at 4 at once, Django's production worker count, and each returns its connection. With `--providers real` it requires enforced admission and records campaign spend and per-run attempts from the ledger.

**Tests.** `apps/ai/evaluation/tests/test_runtime_burst.py` (7, fake gateway, no vendor): readers and depths spread deterministically; a busy refusal stays in the denominator; a failed run is a failure; a seam that raises is a failure and the burst goes on; no submission is sent until every one is ready; only a real cold burst of 100 distinct questions is a measurement; latency per depth from submission.

**Paid bursts.** Eval copy `iris_ir956_eval` (proxy corpus, PostgreSQL `max_connections` 100), one runs worker, `openai/gpt-6-luna:floor` pinned `agent-research-8` (IR-1004's branch). The limits are AI_RUNTIME_LIMITS.md's proposed and assumed values: 8 worker slots, 4 deep, 5 active per reader, queue 200, research lane 16, account 24, run cap US$0.60, tool concurrency 4, query-vector cache on. 20 readers, 100 distinct questions, 25 deep. The vendor's automatic prompt caching is not controlled.

**Burst 1 is not a measurement of the runtime.** The harness gave each of its 100 threads its own Django connection for the grant edge. With the runtime's own connections, that exhausted PostgreSQL's 100 slots: 43 submissions got HTTP 500 (`TooManyConnectionsError`) and 8 more failed on the harness side. Burst 2 caps the edge at 4 connections. The file is kept because it shows two real defects:
- The gateway and worker open a fresh `asyncpg` connection per operation, with no pool and no cap.
- When PostgreSQL refuses a connection, the gateway answers 500, not `503 busy`.

**Reading burst 2.**
- Latency includes queue wait. With 8 slots and 100 runs arriving at once, most of an ordinary run's 184 s median is waiting, not running. The 120 s ordinary deadline counts from the claim, so no run expired.
- Deep runs finished sooner than ordinary ones at the median. With 4 deep slots, 25 deep runs queue for less time than 75 ordinary runs do for the remaining slots.
- One worker sustained about 20 answers a minute. Readers who arrive together wait about three minutes for an ordinary answer.
- Average spend was US$0.0037 per submission.

**Acceptance criteria.**
1. Covered by tests with a fake gateway, no vendor call in CI: `test_runtime_burst.py`. **Met.**
2. A paid burst of at least 100 distinct questions, file committed, conditions recorded: burst 2, claim `measured`. **Met.**
3. Spending approved: 2026-11-10, in chat. **Met.**

**Not done here.** A connection pool or cap in the runtime, and `503 busy` when PostgreSQL refuses a connection. Bursts with more than one worker. No latency objective is approved, so none is judged.
