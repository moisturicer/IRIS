## IR-1353

**Requirement:** **Every page, for every role, has the same left and right margin and the same width, except Ask IRIS, so switching pages does not move the content sideways.** No FR- id; asked for by Ashlee Sean, 2026-11-28.

**Owner's answers (2026-11-28):**
- The PDF reader (Paper tab) and the review workspace (Review tab) stay full-width, like Ask IRIS.
- The record Overview keeps its 1600 px cap (`wide-left`, IR-1144).
- Workflow analytics takes the frame.

**Cause:** page width and margin were decided in four places.
- `AppShell`'s `main` padded every page `p-4 sm:p-7`, except full-bleed ones.
- Routes chose a width with `handle.width`. Seven admin screens (Workflow analytics, Role requests, Download requests, Delete requests, Users on both routes, Audit log, Account deletions) used `"full"`, with no cap.
- Discover was full-bleed and drew its own frame, with its padding inside the 1440 px cap instead of outside it.
- The record Overview had its own `sm:pl-14 sm:pr-8 pt-8` inside its cap.

Measured on main (`6b2acd35`) on the local stack at 1920 px, the content's left edge was at x = 360.5 on My Library, Settings and My Reviews, 388.5 on Discover, 284 on every admin table and Workflow analytics, and 336.5 for the Overview's heading.

**Implementation:**
- `PageContainer.tsx` is the one definition: `PAGE_GUTTER` (`p-4 sm:p-7`) outside `PAGE_WIDTH.standard` (`max-w-page`, 1440 px, centred). `AppShell` draws it around every page; `main` no longer pads.
- The `"full"` width, `RouteHandle.width` and every `width: "full"` handle are removed. Removed deliberately: no page needs them. Each admin table already scrolls inside its own box (`DataTable`'s `overflow-x-auto`, and the same wrapper on the request tables and analytics).
- Discover is no longer full-bleed and draws no frame of its own.
- The Overview takes `PAGE_GUTTER` on its `overview` container, outside its `wide-left` grid. The container now measures its width inside the gutter, so the Record details rail moves beside the column at a window 56 px wider than before, when the column has 88 px more room than it had at the old threshold.
- A dead branch in Paper View's layout (`onReviewTab` padding, never reached because `reviewWorkspace` is `onReviewTab`) is removed.

**Exceptions, by the owner's decision:** Ask IRIS, the PDF reader and the review workspace fill the window. The Overview's 1600 px cap means that once a window is wide enough for the 1440 px cap to bind (above about 1770 px with the full sidebar and a visible scrollbar), its content starts up to 80 px further left than other pages'.

**Tests:**
- New `e2e/smoke/one-page-frame.spec.ts`. For every signed-in screen except the workspaces above, at 375, 768, 1280, 1920 and 2560 px, the content box of the outermost capped element under the screen's heading must be exactly where the frame puts it, measured against `main`, and the page must not scroll sideways. The Overview is checked against its 1600 px cap.
- `AppShell.test.tsx`: the frame (gutter, then cap, directly under `main`, which pads nothing) for a Student's, an Adviser's, an ITSO member's, RDCO's and a System Admin's pages, including Discover and two admin table pages; no frame on Ask IRIS or a record.
- `PageContainer.test.tsx`: the gutter sits outside the cap, and there is no `full` width. Its "lets full pages use the whole width" test is removed deliberately with the variant.

**Evidence:** 2026-11-28, local.
- Against a build of main, `one-page-frame.spec.ts` failed 10 of 30: the seven admin screens, Discover (at 1920 and 2560 px) and the Overview (from 768 px). With the change, 30 of 30 pass.
- Before and after renders of the local preview (`npm run preview:local`) at 375, 1280 and 1920 px, with the frame's edges marked, were shown to the owner before the PR.

**Status:** **IMPLEMENTED**; awaiting CI and review.
