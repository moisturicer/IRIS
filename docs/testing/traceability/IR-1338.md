# IR-1338: a supplied document shows New, with no Accept or Reject

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** Hermoine Zozobrado asked for this on 2026-11-27. There is no FR- id. [ADR-022](../../adr/022-explicit-document-requests.md) Amendment 10 holds the decision. It was accepted by Lee Jasmin Adolfo when PR #1024 merged on 2026-11-27, as relayed by Hermoine Zozobrado.

- A document sent in answer to a request is **Supplied** at once. Reviewers no longer Accept or Reject it.
- A wrong file is asked for again with a new request.
- A reviewer sees **New** on a supplied version, and on its document, until they open that version in the reader. A download doesn't clear it. Each reviewer's New is their own.

## Design

**Server.**
- `UploadSeen` (migration `documents/0031`): one row per person and upload, unique.
- `RecordUploadSerializer.is_new`: true for a non-owner, on a sent upload that answered a request item, with no mark from them.
- `POST /documents/uploads/<id>/seen/` (`RecordUploadSeenView`): 204 and idempotent; 404 for a paper the caller can't see, 403 without document access. It is in the access matrix with the download's rules.
- Removed: `PATCH /document-request-items/<id>/`, `ItemDecisionSerializer`, `accept_item`, `reject_item`, `notify_document_rejected`, and the `document_accepted`/`document_rejected` audit writers. Stored rejections, their reasons and the old audit types stay readable.
- `UploadSeen` is exported as `uploads_seen` and deleted with the account.

**Screens.**
- `itemStatus`: `uploaded`, and `accepted` from before, read **Supplied** (settled tone). *Replacement needed* stays for old rejections.
- `ReviewerDocumentRequests` lists only open requests, with no Accept, Reject or rejection form.
- `DocumentsTab` shows a **New** badge (active tone) on the document row and on the version. Opening the version's title calls `documentsApi.uploadSeen` and clears the badge at once.
