# IR-1092: Phone record bar and 44 px touch targets

**Requirements:** the audit findings RES-03 (on a phone the record chrome takes a fifth of the screen) and RES-02 (many touch targets are 34–38 px), in `docs/frontend_ui_audit_spec.md` §4.7 (IR-1071). The matrix has no `FR-` row for them. Epic IR-920.

## What was wrong

- Below 640 px the record bar wrapped onto two rows: Back and the title on the first (56 px, the title's own height), a full-width Overview / Paper / Review control on the second, so the chrome was about 170 px under the app's 56 px top bar.
- Measured by the audit at 360 px: the bell was 34 px, Close menu 36 px, sidebar links 38 px. In the record pages themselves, several buttons and labels were `min-h-9` (36 px) or `py-1.5` (about 32 px) on touch.

## What changed

**Client only; no server change.**

- `ReadingHeader.tsx`: one 56 px row at every width (`h-14 flex-nowrap`). Below `reading-split` (640 px of the `reading` container): Back, and Back to answer when a citation adds it, are 44 px icon buttons whose labels stay as `sr-only` text; the section control takes the rest of the row; the title stays as the page's `h1` for screen readers but is not drawn; the compact version picker is not drawn (the Overview's version picker still offers it). From `reading-split` nothing moves. `READING_HEADER_PX` was already 56, so the sticky offsets under the bar are now true on a phone.
- `components/layout`: the phone bell is 44 px (34 px from `md`), Close menu 44 px, navigation links and Help `min-h-11` (compact from `md`), the account menu's items `min-h-11`, the bell's Mark all read and View all notifications `min-h-11`. From `md`, where a pointer is, the old sizes return.
- `features/records/paper-view`: Files' Download / Remove, Review's All documents, the IP-type chips and Save classification, the revision-upload label, the mention and severity choices and the review-thread filter are 44 px on touch. From `reading-split` Download / Remove, All documents, the mention and severity choices and the thread filter keep their old 36 px; the IP-type chips, Save classification and the upload label were about 28–32 px (`py-1` / `py-1.5`) and are now 36 px, and the chips went from `px-2.5` to `px-3`.
- `DESIGN.md` states the phone record bar and the 44 px rule.

## Open for the reviewer

- **The phone loses the title and the Paper tab's version picker from the bar.** The title is still the heading for assistive technology, and Overview still offers the version picker, but a reader on the Paper tab cannot switch version without going back to Overview. This follows the audit prototype (`.record-bar .ver-pick { display: none }`); the alternative is a version control in the Paper pane's own toolbar.
- **At 360 px with a citation's Back to answer present** the section control has about 232 px for three tabs; the tab labels may truncate. Check that case.
- The rule in `readingStandards.test.ts` catches `min-h-8` to `min-h-10`, not a control that is short for another reason (`py-1`, a fixed `h-9`).

## Not done: the shared Button, and what uses it

The audit's "every control on record pages" is **not met**. `components/ui/Button.tsx` sizes `sm` (`py-1.5`, about 32 px) and `md` (`py-2`, about 37 px) have no 44 px touch size, and record-page controls built on them are still short: the reader toolbar's Comment and Try again (`PaperPdfReader.tsx`), Mention someone and Stop mentioning (`Mentions.tsx`), SavedReplies' buttons, ReviewThreads' New comment and Accept wording, RelatedWorks' Try again, and the footer buttons of the review dialogs (`RouteDialog`, `DecisionDialog`, `RequestRevisionDialog`, `NewVersionDialog`, and others). The new `readingStandards` rule cannot see them, because it matches `min-h-8` to `min-h-10` and these get their height from the primitive.

The one-place fix is to give `sm` and `md` a touch size in `Button.tsx`, the way `icon` already has one (`w-11 h-11 lg:w-9 lg:h-9`). It was not made here because it changes every `sm` and `md` button in the app below the pointer breakpoint (Discover, Publish, dialogs, not only records) and uses the window `lg:` variant that DESIGN.md forbids for sizing on the record page; that is a design decision for the frontend owner, not something to slip into this change.

## Other limits

- `md:` in `components/layout` is the shell's window breakpoint, not a pointer test: from 768 px the bell, sidebar links and the tablet icon rail (IR-1086) return to their compact sizes even on a touch tablet.
- `reading-split` is a container query, not a phone query: a narrow reading container at a wide window (a tablet with the sidebar open) also gets the icon-only Back and loses the drawn title and version.
- Hiding the compact version picker removes it from the accessibility tree as well as from view, and a single-version "v2" label is not drawn on a phone. A reviewer on the Review tab has to go to Overview to switch version.
- The section control has about 232–236 px for three tabs at 360 px beside Back and Back to answer; the tabs may truncate.
