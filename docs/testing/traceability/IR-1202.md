## IR-1202: Details keeps only who and what, and Version history lists each Version's documents

**Basis and scope.** IR-1202 is subtask 4 of IR-1199 (the review-page cleanup), decided with Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-17. It changes the Details tab of Paper View's Review section for everyone: reviewers, and the owners who got Details in IR-1206. It has no FR- id. It reads the `versions` payload as IR-872 and IR-1201 serve it, and it changes nothing on the server.

**Why.** Details mixed who and what with a document list. That list had download links, supplementary files, open requests, an "All documents" button, a Compare button and Copy link. The parent story moves documents to their own tab (IR-1203) and leaves Details with who and what.

**What changed** (`ReviewDetails.tsx`):
- **Details sections.** Details shows Reviewers, Researchers, Adviser, Classification, Flagged by the authors, and Version history.
  - The Participants section and Copy link to this review are removed. The participants payload still names reviewers on their rows.
  - The owner's Edit details, lineage links and Continue as (IR-1206) stay above them.
- **Version history replaces Version and Documents.** It is plain text, as decided on 2026-11-17, with no buttons or links.
  - Each manuscript Version gets one line, **oldest at the top**: "Manuscript v1 · submitted October 7, 2026".
  - Beneath each Version are the documents it was submitted with and those that arrived while it was the latest (`documents`, `documents_added`, IR-872): "Consent Paper v1 · submitted October 8, 2026".
  - Each upload is listed once, under the first Version that has it. A Version's `documents` names every slot's file as submitted, so an unchanged upload recurs under later Versions.
- **Rejection reasons.** A rejected upload carries one line per rejection, for example "Replacement asked by IERC: The signature page is missing." It uses `rejections` from IR-1201. That field is null to a viewer who may not read document requests (ADR-022 §Amendment 5), and then nothing is shown.
- **Pre-IR-872 Versions.** A Version from before IR-872 shows its reconstructed list with no "best guess" note.
- **Removed from Details, by decision of 2026-11-17:**
  - the download links, the supplementary files and the open document requests, which belong to the Documents tab (IR-1203);
  - the "All documents" button, removed now because IR-1203 replaces the drawer;
  - "Compare N versions", because the Changes tab compares.
- **Until IR-1203.** A reviewer reaches the drawer from a Readiness link, and an owner from the Documents button on Readiness.
- `types/records.ts`: `VersionDocument.rejections` (`VersionDocumentRejection[] | null`).
- `ReviewWorkspaceLayout.tsx` no longer passes `onAllDocuments` or `onCompare`.

**Tests changed on purpose** (`PaperViewPage.test.tsx`):
- "lists every party with its people…" (IR-685) and "renders paper details from the payload" (IR-686): the expected headings end with "Version history". The IR-686 test checks the history line instead of the Version line, and checks that consent.pdf is no longer in Details instead of downloading it.
- "draws one row per reviewer with Kept, a revision request, Compare and Copy link" (IR-1193) is now "…with Kept and a revision request". Its Compare, Copy link and Version checks are gone with those controls.
- "reports an authenticated document download failure and allows retry" (IR-686) is removed. Details no longer downloads anything, and the documents drawer keeps its own download tests.

**Tests added** (`PaperViewPage.test.tsx`, "Version history (IR-1202)"; the page is rendered and queried by role and name):
- Details' headings are exactly the six above. There is no Copy link, All documents, Compare or download button.
- Versions appear oldest first, each with its documents beneath as text. v2's repeated uploads are listed once, and the history has no button or link.
- A rejected upload shows "Replacement asked by IERC: …". The same upload with `rejections: null` shows nothing.
- A reconstructed Version shows its list with no "best guess".
- As the owner at 320 px: the pane opens from *Open review pane*, End moves from Readiness to Details, the history is there, and axe finds no serious or critical violation.
