**Design:** [ADR-032 §4, §9, §10, §12, §13](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) · [frontend_redesign_spec](../../frontend_redesign_spec.md) §4.2, §4.7, Appendix D · B3 (built once, 2026-10-07), Appendix F, Appendix L · Jira IR-415

**Implementation:** `ReviewerSeat` (`reviews/0009`) with `SeatState` / `SeatSource`; `User.is_office_coordinator` (`accounts/0010`); the ADR-032 §13 backfill (`reviews/0010`: Adviser assignments seat `record.adviser`, offices and RDCO seat their most recent reviewer, the rest stay seatless as the pool; intake is never seated). `core.permissions` gains `is_office_member`, `is_office_coordinator`, `holds_seat` and `is_record_participant`. `apps/reviews/seats.py` holds claim, assign, reassign, withdraw, add reviewer, nominate (for IR-261's `route()`), open review, `complete_seat` (for IR-269) and the office completion rule, which closes an assignment only on a new-model (`in_review`) record; until IR-260 the legacy pipeline's `shadow.sync()` seats the Adviser when its assignment opens and settles seats when it closes one. Endpoints at `/api/v1/assignments/<id>/{claim,assign,add-reviewer}/` and `/api/v1/seats/<id>/{open,reassign,withdraw}/` refuse 404 / 403 / 400 per ADR-022 §Amendment 4. Record detail carries `my_seats` and `is_participant`; `/users/me/` and the sign-in response carry `review_access`; `PATCH /users/<id>/coordinator/` is administrator-only. Frontend: *Open review* records the viewer's unopened seat, the capabilities adapter counts a past seat holder as a participant, and `seatsApi` is ready for IR-268's rows
