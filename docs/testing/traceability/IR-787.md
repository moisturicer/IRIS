# IR-787: Settings offers "Delete my account" and "Download my data"

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** ADR-051 §8, decision 11 (what a deletion removes and what stays), decision 13 (the download) and decision 18 (withdrawing Signup consent means deletion), under IR-779. Builds on IR-785 (the request endpoint) and IR-786 (the download endpoint). No FR- id.

**What changed:**

- `features/settings/YourDataPanels.tsx`, shown on Settings → Data privacy & consent:
  - **Download my data** calls `GET /users/me/data/` and saves `iris-my-data.json`. While it runs it says "Preparing your file…"; afterwards it says the file was saved, or that it wasn't (a 429 says they've downloaded several times recently).
  - **Delete my account** reads `GET /users/me/deletion-request/`. With no pending request it lists what goes and what stays, per ADR-051 decision 11, then asks before sending. A pending request shows its date, what blocks it in plain words (or that nothing does) and Cancel my request. A rejection shows its date and reason, and the person may ask again.
- `SettingsPage.tsx`: the consent text said withdrawal meant contacting RDCO. It now says withdrawing means deleting the account, which the panel below offers (ADR-051 decision 18).
- `api/accounts.ts`: `myDeletionRequest`, `cancelMyDeletion`, `downloadMyData`, and a typed `requestMyDeletion`.
- Backend: `MyDeletionRequestSerializer` gains `blockers`, the same plain-words list the System Admin's page shows, computed for the caller's own pending request only, and empty otherwise. Each one names a paper the person owns or a review they hold, both of which they can already read.
