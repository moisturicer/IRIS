## IR-1316 — Ask IRIS knows "recent" from the added, completion and publication dates of published papers

**Basis and scope.** IR-1316; authority ADR-049 and its 2026-11-24 amendment, **Proposed**. Built at the owner's direction.

**What exists now.**
- `ai/research/recency.py`: today (campus time) and the recent ranges.
- `ai/research/tools.py`: `COMPLETION_YEAR`, `DATE_ADDED`, ten filter parameters, `list_records(sort=)`, `Count.undated`, dates on search results, listings and details.
- `ai/agent/tools.py`: `DATED_SEARCH`, `DATED_COUNT`, `DATED_LIST`, `DATED_DETAILS`, offered only under `research-14`. Also fixes `list_records` refusing `offset` when calls run together (IR-1003's early fetch passed it as a filter).
- `ai/agent/prompts.py`: `DATED_INSTRUCTIONS`, `TODAY_PREFIX`; `ai/runs/agent_profile.py` pins `agent-research-15`/`research-14`.
- `backend/apps/records/completion.py`: Discover's year filter, facets and year ordering read the one completion year; the paper page shows it.
- `manage.py fill_test_years`: made-up years for loaded test papers only. Development only, seeded, never overwrites, `--undo`.
- `docs/evaluation/recency/questionnaire.md`: 25 questions with expected answers computed from the database.
