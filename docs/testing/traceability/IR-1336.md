## IR-1336

**Requirement:** **A review not yet started is covered by a grey layer over the Review tab's pane, with *Start review* in its middle; *Start review* replaces *Open review*.** No FR- id; [ADR-032](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) §10 Amendment 2026-11-25. Requested by Hermoine Zozobrado, 2026-11-25.

**Before:** a reviewer arriving from My Reviews' *Examine review* landed on the Review tab with the pane fully usable.
- *Open review* was in the pane's footer, and also under the title on the Overview.
- A failed recording opened the review anyway (IR-1206).

**Implementation:**
- **`ReviewWorkspaceLayout.tsx`:**
  - The cover: while the viewer has an unopened seat (`open_review` offered and `seatToOpen` finds one), and no Claim is waiting, a `review-cover` group named "Your review hasn't started" fills the pane. It has *Start review* and, in the drawer, *Back to paper*.
  - The pane's header, content, footer and any side column take `inert` and `aria-hidden` while covered.
  - The footer's *Open review* is gone.
  - A failed start keeps the cover and shows "Your review could not be started. Try again."
- **`YourPartFooter.tsx`:** takes `hidden`.
- **`reviewWorkspace.css`:** `.review-cover`, grey at 85% with a slight blur.
- **`PaperViewPage.tsx`:** `openReview` becomes `startReview`. It records the seat and re-reads the Record (IR-1273), lets a failure through, and no longer navigates. The Overview's primary action no longer includes `open_review`.
- **`EditorialHeader.tsx`:** the *Open review* button and its `primary` and `onOpenReview` props are gone.
- **`ReadinessPanel.tsx`:** the `open_review` link reads *Start review*.
- **`tour/tourSteps.ts`:** the tour step reads "1. Claim or start it".

**Test:**
- **New, in `ThreadsWorkspace.test.tsx`, "a review not yet started"** (replacing "asks a seat not yet opened to open it"):
  - the cover holds Start review, and the tabs, the footer and Finish review cannot be reached (axe clean);
  - Start review calls the host;
  - a failed start keeps the cover with its message;
  - an opened seat has no cover.
- **`PaperViewPage.test.tsx`, changed deliberately because they asserted *Open review*:**
  - The four "review control" tests became three: no review button on the Overview, which stays readable; the cover until the seat is recorded, then lifted; no cover once every seat is open.
  - The unopened footer test now finds Start review on the cover, over a hidden footer.
  - The Claim test finds Start review after claiming.
  - The Readiness test uses an opened review with a blocker of its own.
  - The IR-1273 re-read test starts from the cover.
  - "still opens the review when recording the seat fails" (IR-1206) is replaced by "keeps the cover, says so, and lets Start review be pressed again".
- **`ReviewTour.test.tsx`:** the step's new name.

**Evidence:** 2026-11-25, local.
- Before the change, the three new cover tests failed.
- After it, `npm test` passes: 255 files, 3811 tests. `tsc` is clean.
- The full smoke tier passes, 564 of 564.
- Screenshots of the smoke build at 1440px and at 800px (drawer opened) show the grey cover with Start review over the pane, with the paper beside it.
