# IR-988 — Corpus cutover to Unstructured and LlamaParse removal

**Affected requirement labels:** none change status. Part of IR-980; ADR-067 decision 1. Unblocked by IR-989 (passed by the owner's decision) and IR-983.

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Implementation:**

- `apps/ai/extraction/selection.py`: `unstructured` is the only `EXTRACTION_BACKEND` and the default. Production refuses to start without `UNSTRUCTURED_PLATFORM_API_KEY`; development starts and reads fail as unavailable. `figure_limits()` keeps the `UNSTRUCTURED_*` settings inside the package (`trial_reads.py` read them directly).
- `apps/ai/reextraction.py`, `manage.py reextract_manuscripts`: plans at Unstructured's US$ per page (IR-983), skips Extractions already read by Unstructured, re-reads LlamaParse and Docling ones, refuses a real run without `--max-cost` (US$) or above it. `--rollback` restores the ChunkSet before an Unstructured read.
- `apps/ai/admission/limits.py`, `extraction.py`: `AI_ADMISSION_EXTRACTION_UNSTRUCTURED_USD_PER_PAGE` is the only extraction price.
- Deleted: `llamaparse_client.py`, `llamaextract_client.py`, `shape_llamaparse` (shared caption, front-matter and formula rules stay in `shaping.py`), the stored-extractor branch in `read_document`, the LlamaExtract task, hook and admission wrapper, `apps/records/extracted_metadata.py`, `extractor_gate` (its IR-989 report stays), all `LLAMAPARSE_*`/`LLAMAEXTRACT_*` settings, the LlamaParse and LlamaExtract admission prices, `PUBLISH_AUTOFILL_MIN_CONFIDENCE`, and LlamaParse's recorded fixtures.
- Kept, unread: stored LlamaParse raw results and `ManuscriptMetadata` rows (ADR-058). A LlamaParse Extraction not yet re-read is read from its stored structure, and Publish suggestions read it by the structure rules.
- `backend/.env.example`, CI's production-startup env, `docker-compose.prod.yml`, CLAUDE.md, ADR-056 (Superseded by ADR-067) and ADR-067 (Built).

**Tests:** new `apps/ai/extraction/tests/test_shared_shaping.py`; rewritten `test_reextraction.py`, `test_selection.py` (adds a guard that no production code names LlamaParse or LlamaExtract), `apps/documents/tests/test_shaped_extraction.py`, `test_unstructured_price.py`. Changed deliberately because the requirement changed: price fixtures in `test_limits.py`, `test_offcampus_extraction.py`, `test_unstructured_admission.py`; production-startup env in five tests; `test_figure_descriptions.py` builds its structure directly. Deleted with their code: LlamaParse client, shaping and recording tests, LlamaExtract tests, `test_manuscript_metadata.py`, `test_extractor_gate.py`.
