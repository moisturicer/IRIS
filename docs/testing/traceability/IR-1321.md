# IR-1321: Waiting on you takes the Reading shelf's place

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** My Library's Overview keeps what an author must act on in one place, its right column. *Waiting on you* lists each paper waiting on them, in the server's words, with one link per open request to where it is answered. With nothing waiting, it says so, but only once the list has loaded. The Reading shelf card is gone: *Continue reading* and *Recently opened* stay under Recent submissions, and *Your folders* leaves the Overview (folders stay in Saved). Submission rows no longer carry a button per request; a waiting paper's row opens its Documents tab. Requested by Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-24. No FR- id.

**Design:** frontend only. The status words, the requests and their links are the server's (`submitter_status`) and `waitingOnYou.ts`'s, unchanged since [IR-1318](IR-1318.md).

## What changed

- `LibraryOverview.tsx`: a two-column grid. `WaitingOnYou` is the right column, first in reading order, and its counter is a maroon outline. The left column has Recent submissions, then *Continue reading* and *Recently opened*. The `folders` prop is removed.
- `SubmissionRow.tsx`: the per-request links are removed. `rowHref` sends a waiting paper to `waitingHref`, and continuing a Proposal stays.
- `docs/ui-ux/01-design-system.md`: a *My Library Overview* row.
