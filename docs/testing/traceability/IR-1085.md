# IR-1085: Navigation: review pages nested under My Reviews, Help in the sidebar footer, addressable Settings tabs

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Epic IR-920 (S2 Epic: Frontend UI/UX Redesign). Audit findings NAV-02, NAV-03, NAV-04 and NAV-08 ([`frontend_ui_audit_spec.md`](../../frontend_ui_audit_spec.md) §4.7). Decision D5 (nest, URLs kept) is recorded on the ticket and in `DESIGN.md`.

## What changed

- **Office console and Workflow analytics nest under My Reviews** in the sidebar (NAV-02). `lib/access.ts` gains a `reviewOffice` screen (`/review/office`, the four offices) and gives it and `reviewAnalytics` a `nav` entry with `parent: "reviewQueue"`. `navFor()` returns a child right after its parent and only while the parent is listed, so with no review work neither appears. The console also needs `review_access.is_coordinator` (`needsCoordinator`), the flag My Reviews' own header link already read; both now read it through one `isCoordinator()` beside `hasReviewWork()`. No role name is checked in the sidebar: `SCREEN_ACCESS` decides. Both URLs are unchanged.
- **The router reads the same gate.** `/review/office` was gated by the reviewer roles and `ReviewAccessRoute`; it is now gated by `rolesFor("reviewOffice")` (offices) and still `ReviewAccessRoute`, so a link cannot outrun its gate (IR-160). An Adviser who types the address now gets the forbidden screen instead of a page the server then refused.
- **Help is a link in the sidebar footer** for every role (NAV-04), above the account menu, with the rail's tooltip and name. The account menu no longer lists it.
- **Breadcrumbs say "Discover"**, not "Home" (NAV-03). On Discover itself the trail is the one current-page step rather than "Home / Discover".
- **Settings tabs are addressable** (NAV-08): `/settings?tab=security|privacy|role`. A missing or unknown value opens Account & security. Switching a tab pushes an entry (Back returns to the previous tab) and keeps any other parameter.

## Tests

- `frontend/src/lib/access.test.ts`: the main list per role with analytics for offices; a coordinator gets the console and analytics at `/review/office` and `/review/analytics`; a non-coordinator office member gets analytics only; Student, Adviser and System Admin get neither, whatever the flags; no My Reviews means no nested pages; `reviewOffice` is the offices' alone; every nav entry is one its role can open (now including nested ones).
- `frontend/src/components/layout/Sidebar.test.tsx`, by role and accessible name: the nested list inside My Reviews' row, hrefs, `aria-current` on the nested page and not on My Reviews, opening the console from the sidebar, nested links named on the collapsed rail, axe; Help in the footer for all seven roles and not in the main list, opening it, `aria-current`, rail tooltip and name, closing the phone drawer; the account menu holds Profile, Settings and Sign out and not Help.
- `frontend/src/router/roleNavigation.test.tsx`: `/review/office` is refused for Adviser, Student and System Admin even with review work and the coordinator flag; it opens for ITSO and RDCO coordinators, with the sidebar link to it; an office member with no review work still lands on Discover. `access.test.ts` adds the RDCO coordinator and a missing flag.
- `frontend/src/components/layout/Breadcrumbs.test.tsx` (new): first step is a link named Discover and never Home; named once, as the current page, on Discover; nested parents sit between.
- `frontend/src/features/settings/SettingsPage.test.tsx`: `?tab=privacy` and `?tab=role` open their tab; switching updates the URL; an unknown tab opens the first.
- **Changed deliberately (CI):** `e2e/smoke/support/screens.ts`, three screens (notifications, office console, workflow analytics) named the breadcrumb's first step "Home" as their primary action, so `Frontend smoke` failed all 12 of their viewport cases. They now look for the link named Discover inside the Breadcrumb navigation, so the sidebar's own Discover link cannot satisfy it. The check is the same one: the step must be reachable at every width.
- **Changed deliberately:** the two account-menu tests in `Sidebar.test.tsx` expected Help in the menu (`holds Profile, Settings, Help and Sign out`; the `Help` absence checks after choosing Profile and Settings). Help moved, so the first now asserts it is not there and the others check the menu itself closed (`Sign out` gone), which is what they were after. The main-list tests in `access.test.ts` gain Workflow analytics for offices.

## Evidence

- Run locally 2026-11-14: `tsc --noEmit` clean; eslint reports no errors; `npm run test:changed`: 39 files, 848 tests passed, before the review fixes; after them the affected files (layout, settings, MyReviews, router, access), 219 tests, passed. The full suite is left to CI.
- **Open question for the ticket (recorded, not decided here):** the acceptance criterion says "a coordinator reaches Console and Analytics; other roles don't see them", and the prototype shows analytics to a coordinator only. D5 and `SCREEN_ACCESS` give analytics to every office member (`reviewAnalytics` is `OFFICES`, and My Reviews' header already links it to them), so this shows it to all four offices and the console to coordinators. Narrowing analytics to coordinators is one `needsCoordinator` flag and a test.
- **Link vs address:** nested pages hang from My Reviews, so an office member with no review work has no sidebar link to analytics, though `/review/analytics` still opens by address, as before.
- **Not checked:** a browser or a signed-in session against the API, a screen reader, and how the nested rows look on the 76 px rail.
- **Known gaps:** the nested pages' breadcrumbs still read "Discover / Office console" and "Discover / Workflow analytics", not through My Reviews, because their routes are siblings of `/review` and the URLs were kept (D5). Settings' own tab buttons are 40 px tall, as before.
