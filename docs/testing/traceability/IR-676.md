## IR-676

**Requirement:** Every copy saved through the download route names who downloaded it, or is not served

**Design:** Jira IR-676 (epic IR-687); ADR-048 decisions 10-12

**Implementation:** `apps/records/watermark.py` builds each copy in memory: a footer on top of every page, a grey diagonal "CIT-U" beneath the content, and the same identity in the metadata. An encrypted or malformed file, or a library error, gets a 422 `watermark_failed` and a `DOWNLOAD` audit row with that outcome. The stored file is untouched

**Test:** `apps/records/test_download_watermark.py`

**Evidence:** Passed locally in Docker; see PR

**Status:** **PARTIAL** until IR-677 routes the reader's button through it
