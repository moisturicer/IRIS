## IR-261

**Requirement:** **Every record enters at its Adviser; the Adviser accepts and routes, and offices route onward, into office pools** — touches FR-M5-01 (stable label only)

**Design:** [ADR-032 §1, §3, §4](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) · design settled with the project lead 2026-10-07 (grilling) · Jira IR-261

**Implementation:** `apps/reviews/routing.py`: `enter_at_adviser` (new-model entry: `in_review`, an Adviser assignment with an `entry` seat, no intake; reached by the seed and the tests until IR-260 makes submission call it), `accept_and_route` (the Adviser's `approved` Review, their seat done and assignment completed through `seats.complete_seat`), `route` (onward, the router stays active), and `route_options`. The ADR-032 §4 party graph; offices land in pools or seat a nominee; a pending clearance is created where none exists and never reset; a target already holding the record needs a nominee. `lifecycle.entry_party_for` returns `adviser` for `in_review` records only. Endpoints `POST /records/<id>/accept-and-route/`, `POST /records/<id>/route/`, `GET /records/<id>/route-options/` (404 / 403 / 400); record detail's `routing` flags; `notify_routed` (office members, nominees, owners on accept). Frontend: *Accept & route…* and *Route to office…* in F6's action bar through `RouteDialog`; the author's hints shown, nothing pre-ticked. `seed_demo` adds one new-model Thesis at its Adviser

**Test:** `apps/reviews/test_routing.py` (REST seam, one class per criterion) · `apps/records/test_seed_demo.py::test_one_new_model_thesis_waits_at_its_adviser` · `paper-view/RouteDialog.test.tsx` (accessible names, axe) · `paper-view/ReviewActionBar.test.tsx` (action order) · `features/records/capabilities.test.ts`

**Evidence:** CI on the IR-261 PR (flake8, pytest, lint, Vitest, build); per the team rule these were not run locally
