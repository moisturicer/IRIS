## IR-826 — The AI gateway stops receiving DATABASE_URL, or ADR-014's precondition is amended

**Basis.** [ADR-014](../../adr/014-ai-gateway-as-a-service.md) precondition 4 (no direct database access) and its amendment of 2026-11-03. Gap found by IR-617 and IR-670.

**Done here.**

- `ai-gateway` no longer receives `DATABASE_URL` in `docker-compose.yml` or `docker-compose.prod.yml`. No code under `ai/` read it (`ai/infrastructure/settings.py` declares no such field, and `extra="ignore"` drops unknown keys). It handed Django's login to a service on the `egress` network. The Compose comment names this ticket, not IR-58, which Jira doesn't have.
- ADR-014 gains a **Proposed** amendment. Jive Tyler I. Revalde is named as decider, for the `iris_ai_runtime` login that AI-owned runs (IR-722, IR-723) and research tools (IR-719) hold.

**Not done.** The first acceptance criterion, "a named person decides", **is not met**: the amendment names the decider and stays Proposed until they decide. It also lists two things the decider must settle first: what proves the row policy matches `visible_to(user)`, and that `RUNS_MIGRATION_DATABASE_URL` sits in the same `ai/.env` both long-running services load. ADR-049 is not on `main`, so the amendment can't reconcile against its text.

**Tests.** `backend/apps/tests/test_gateway_database_access.py` (4, reads files): the gateway's active lines in both Compose files, and `ai-runs-worker`'s in `docker-compose.yml`, carry no `DATABASE_URL` or `DB_USER`/`DB_PASSWORD`/`DB_NAME`, and the gateway's block names IR-826 and not IR-58. The first version ran red before the Compose change (4 failed) and green after. After review the worker case was added; the five Compose-reading test files then passed together, alongside `test_traceability_shape.py`. Run locally with throwaway settings values; the worktree has no `backend/.env`.
