## ADR-026 §12-§15 · ADR-027 §9

**Requirement:** **Retry, multi-part questions, Listing questions and reader-feedback privacy** — no FR- id exists for these; the ADRs are the requirement, and the reader-facing vocabulary is [`CONTEXT.md`](../../../CONTEXT.md) (Retry, Reader feedback, Listing question)

**Design:** [ADR-026](../../adr/026-conversational-retrieval-and-memory.md) §12 (a Retry is a new Turn that searches again on the previous Turn's Resolved question — never a reuse of its stored citations, which would skip the retrieval-time `visible_to(user)` check §10 enforces, and never an overwrite), §13 (failed and refused Turns stay visible to the reader and are excluded from the history handed to the model and the rewriter), §14 (the multi-part flag comes from the routing classifier — **closing the open question §2 left** — with per-sub-question retrieval, merge by rank position, at least one passage per sub-question, and the sub-questions shown), §15 (feedback stores the verdict and cited Record ids only unless the reader separately chooses to share the exchange; deleting a Conversation deletes its feedback; feedback is never a ranking input) · [ADR-027](../../adr/027-corpus-level-questions.md) §9 (**listing** as a fourth routing outcome, answered from a catalogue search with `visible_to(user)` applied **before** ranking, and the model may describe the list but never extend it — §4's computes-then-reports rule applied to Records instead of counts; a separate ADR was rejected because that guard already exists here)
