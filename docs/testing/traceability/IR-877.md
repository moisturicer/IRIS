# IR-877: Viewed marks, saved replies and @mentions

**Requirements:** no FR- id. The *Conveniences* of [ADR-032](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) §7 (2026-11-04), with its IR-877 build notes (2026-11-10), and [ADR-058](../../adr/058-each-version-keeps-its-text-and-its-files.md) §3 as amended, point 7, for Viewed.

Built in three parts, one PR each: saved replies, then Viewed marks, then @mentions.

## Decisions (ADR-032 §7, IR-877 build notes)

1. A Viewed mark follows its text (`text_hash`), not its Version.
2. Anyone who reads the review may use Viewed marks. Anyone signed in keeps saved replies.
3. A mention is a person picked beside the text. An edit can add one, notified once.
4. A finished review sends one mention notice per person, counting their comments.

## Part 1: saved replies

### What changed

- `SavedReply` (`apps/reviews/models.py`, migration `reviews/0027`): an owner, a title (60 characters, unique per person ignoring case, enforced by the database) and a text (bounded like a review comment, 10,000 characters). At most 50 per person.
- `apps/reviews/saved_replies.py`:
  - `GET` and `POST /reviews/saved-replies/`;
  - `PATCH` and `DELETE /reviews/saved-replies/<id>/`.
  - Every query starts from the caller, so another person's reply is the same 404 as a missing one.
- The access matrix declares both routes: listing and saving are anyone's, and a reply is its owner's alone.
- The personal-data export carries `saved_replies`, and account deletion deletes them.
- The frontend's `SavedReplies` menu sits under every box where a review comment or reply is written or edited: the thread composer, a thread's reply box, and the Conversation tab's comment, reply and edit boxes. An *Addressed* or *Declined* note is an answer, not a comment, and gets none.
  - Choosing a reply adds its text at the cursor, after any selection, or at the end of a box nobody has been in. Nothing written is replaced.
  - The list loads when the menu is first opened.
  - A response of the wrong shape reads as a failed load, and an entry of the wrong shape is left out. A save or edit answered with the wrong shape reads the list again.
  - *Manage saved replies* adds, edits and deletes them, and is offered only over a list that loaded. Deleting asks first.
- The composer's keyboard test now tabs past *Saved replies*, which sits between the comment box and the severity choice. That changes the tab order on purpose.

## Part 2: Viewed marks

### What changed

- `ViewedMark` (`apps/reviews/models.py`, migration `reviews/0028`) belongs to a person, a record, the Version and the reading it was made on, the section's `key`, and its `text_hash`. One mark per person, reading and section.
- `POST /records/<id>/versions/compare/viewed/` `{from, to, side, key, viewed}` → `{viewed}` (`apps/reviews/viewed.py`).
  - It finds the section in the same two readings the comparison uses (`readings.pair`), so a mark is made on the text the person saw.
  - It marks the section on the side the comparison reads it from (`mark_side`), and refuses any other `side`, so a mark always shows where it was made.
  - It refuses with a 400 a pair that can't be compared, a comparison not ready yet, one listed by page, an unknown section, or a malformed body.
  - Anyone `may_read_review` refuses gets the 404 a missing paper gets. A mark is not audited: it decides nothing.
- `GET /records/<id>/versions/compare/` gives each section `viewed` for the caller alone.
  - It is true when the caller marked a section with that `text_hash` on this record, in any Version or reading. A heading with no text of its own is matched by its `key` as well.
  - A section is marked on its later side, or on its earlier side if the later Version removed it.
  - No reader or reading is named.
- `comparison.by_page` and `version_comparison.as_reading` are now public, replacing the private helpers `thread_places.py` already imported.
- The access matrix declares the route with the comparison's readers. The export carries `viewed_marks`, and account deletion deletes them.
- In the Changes tab, each section has a *Viewed* checkbox.
  - Ticking it folds the section's changes away and adds *Viewed* to its row in *Changed sections*. The section's threads stay shown.
  - The box is locked until its answer arrives. A refusal puts it back with the server's words. An answer of the wrong shape, or a `viewed` that isn't a boolean, counts as a failure or as not viewed.
  - A comparison listed by page has no boxes.

### Evidence

- PR: https://github.com/moisturicer/IRIS/pull/664
- `apps/reviews/test_viewed_marks.py`: 17 tests at the REST seam. They failed before the change (no route) and pass after:
