## IR-1351

**Requirement:** Pages that show what other people change, but that nobody acts on together, are current whenever someone looks again.
- **Discover**, the **Audit log**, **Workflow analytics**, **Users** (RDCO and System Admin) and **people/:id** read again when the tab is shown: window focus, or the page becoming visible.
- The re-read is quiet. There is no spinner, filters, page, search and scroll stay, and a failed read keeps what is shown.
- These pages never re-read on a live hint.

**Design:** [ADR-073](../../adr/073-live-updates-by-server-sent-hints.md) §Amendment 2026-11-28, accepted by Lee Jasmin Adolfo on 2026-11-28 (PR #1034).

**Implementation:**

- `hooks/useFocusRefresh.ts` (new):
  - `useCoalescedReload` holds one 500 ms timer;
  - `useTabShown` listens for focus and visibility;
  - `useFocusRefresh(reload, enabled)` joins the two.
- `hooks/useLiveRefresh.ts` is rebuilt on those parts, so a hint, a reconnect and the tab being shown share one timer. Its behaviour is unchanged, and its tests pass unchanged.
- **Discover** (`features/discover/DiscoverPage.tsx`):
  - reruns the catalogue at the depth already shown, then replaces the rows, count and page;
  - skips while results or Load more are loading, after a failed read, while the search box has focus or holds text not yet searched, on the People view, and on Best (For you, see below);
  - is fenced by the query generation and by any Load more started meanwhile.
- **Audit log** and **Workflow analytics** re-run their read with the same filters, without the loading state, and ignore a failed quiet read.
- **Users** (`features/accounts/UserListPage.tsx`) re-reads the page, role and search the table was read with, never unsearched text. A sequence number lets a newer read win.
- **people/:id** (`usePersonProfile.refresh`) re-reads the person and each list still on its first page. A list the reader has shown more of keeps its place, a 404 reads as not found, and any other failure keeps the profile.

**Not covered: Best (For you) on Discover.** Its cursor and pending dismissals would be reset by a fresh first page, so it is left to its own refresh. The amendment says "Discover reruns its current search" and does not mention Best. This is flagged in the PR for a decision.

**Test:**

- `src/hooks/useFocusRefresh.test.ts` (7):
  - focus and visibility reload, and a hidden page does not;
  - focus and visibility together reload once;
  - every live hint is ignored;
  - disabled or unmounted drops a pending reload;
  - the latest `reload` is called;
  - `useLiveRefresh` reloads once for a hint and focus together.
- `src/features/discover/DiscoverPage.test.tsx` (+4): a paper published meanwhile shows with the search and filter kept; a failed read keeps the results without an alert; no read while the search box has focus; Best is left alone.
- `src/features/audit/AuditLogPage.test.tsx` (+2): new events show with the same filter and page; a failed read keeps the rows.
- `src/features/review/ReviewAnalyticsPage.test.tsx` (+2): the same figures are read again without the loading state; a failed read keeps them.
- `src/features/accounts/UserListPage.test.tsx` (+2): the re-read uses the searched text, not text typed since; a failed read keeps the rows.
- `src/features/profile/PersonProfilePage.test.tsx` (+3): a paper published meanwhile shows; a list shown more of keeps its rows; a failed read keeps the profile.
