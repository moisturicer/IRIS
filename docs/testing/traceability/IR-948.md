## IR-948 — Your own Profile uses the shared Profile design

**Basis and scope.** IR-948 (sub-task of IR-694, after IR-700 and IR-933). Your own Profile at `/profile` rendered its own copy of the header and sections, so it lacked what another person's Profile at `/people/:id` shows (the university line, the same section layout) and its note, "This is how other people signed in to IRIS see you", was not fully true. It now mounts the same `PersonProfileView`, so there is one design for everyone including yourself. The Edit profile bar and the "Profile saved." confirmation stay on `ProfilePage`, above the view.

`PersonProfileView` takes the person (`ProfileSubject`: a `PublicProfile` fits, and so does the signed-in user once their optional fields are defaulted) and an optional `lists`. Without `lists` the page has no Research, Advised or Collaborators and does **not** say "No published papers yet.": that line would be wrong for someone whose papers have simply not been loaded. Your own Profile passes none. `PersonProfilePage` passes the three lists as before. An account with no role yet shows no role line.

**Not done.** Research, Advised and Collaborators on your own Profile are IR-949, which waits on IR-696. Nothing here calls the people endpoints.

**Tests.** `src/features/profile/ProfilePage.test.tsx` gains three cases: the university line under the affiliation, no Research, Advised or Collaborators heading and no "No published papers yet.", and no role line for an account with no role. Its existing cases (name, role and affiliation, bio and interests, empty fields left out, no email, initials and the authenticated photo, Edit profile, the note, the saved confirmation, axe) pass unchanged. `PersonProfilePage.test.tsx` passes unchanged, which is what shows the people page still renders the papers, the empty line and the Collaborators column.

**Executed evidence (2026-11-07).** `tsc --noEmit` and `eslint src/features/profile` clean; `vitest run src/features/profile`: 3 files, 51 tests passed; `npm run test:changed`: 11 of 170 files, 134 tests passed. CI runs the whole suite and the e2e smoke matrix, whose `profile` row is unchanged.

**Status.** **PARTIAL.** The shared design is in and tested through the accessible tree with axe. Not looked at in a browser at 375 and 1280 px: this change was made in a worktree with no backend to sign in to, so the layout at those widths is unchecked and rests on the view being the one IR-700 already looked at in a browser.
