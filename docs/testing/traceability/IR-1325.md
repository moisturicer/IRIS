# IR-1325: The Overview's side cards are one height, plus Saved Papers' folders

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** Hermoine Zozobrado asked for this on 2026-11-24, after [IR-1324](IR-1324.md). There is no FR- id.
- On My Library's Overview, *Waiting on you* and *Recently opened* are the same height, shorter than *Waiting on you* was (28 rem).
- A faint line under My Library's tabs separates them from the page.
- Folder tiles are slightly narrower.
- With no folders, Saved Papers shows the same empty state as *No saved papers yet*, but about folders.
- ADR-044's amendment of 2026-11-24 is marked Accepted, since IR-1324's PR merged after review.

**Design:** frontend only.
- `LibraryOverview.tsx` gives both cards one constant, `SIDE_CARD_HEIGHT` (`h-96`, 24 rem). *Waiting on you* shrinks from 28 rem; *Recently opened* grows from 20 rem.
- `MyLibraryPage.tsx` wraps the tablist in a `border-b border-stone-200` rule.
- `ReadingPanel.tsx`: tiles go from 2/3/5 columns to 2/4/5/6 (`sm`/`lg`/`xl`). With no folders, the shelf shows a framed `EmptyState` (*No folders yet*, a *New folder* button, hidden at the folder limit) instead of the dashed one-line tile.
