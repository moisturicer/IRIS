# IR-1079: Notes and Ask IRIS corner buttons never cover reading text

**Requirements:** the audit finding CMP-02 (two floating buttons sit over the last lines of the record Overview and Paper; High on phones, Medium on desktop), in `docs/frontend_ui_audit_spec.md` §4.7 (IR-1071). The matrix has no `FR-` row for it. Epic IR-920.

## What was wrong

Notes (44 px, `bottom-24`) floats above Ask IRIS (56 px, `bottom-5`) at the bottom right of the record page. Overview ended in 3rem of padding and the Paper reader's scroll area in 1rem, so the last lines could never scroll above the pair. DESIGN.md said Ask IRIS was "the one corner icon", which Notes had already contradicted.

## What changed

**Client only.** Notes stays above Ask IRIS (frontend owner's decision, 13 Nov 2026); the buttons do not move.

- `AskIrisFab.tsx` exports `CORNER_CLEARANCE_CLASS` (`pb-44`, 11rem). Notes' top edge is 140 px up (`bottom-24` + `h-11`), so 176 px leaves 36 px of air. The constant sits beside the buttons so they change together.
- `PaperViewPage.tsx`: Overview's content wrapper (every section that is neither Paper nor Review) uses it in place of `pb-12`. Files is not one of those surfaces: it renders only inside the Review workspace, which has no corner buttons.
- `PaperPdfReader.tsx`: a new `clearCorner` prop gives a `fill` pane that padding at the bottom of its own scroll area instead of `py-4`'s 1rem. `PaperViewPage` passes it on the Paper tab only; the reviewer workspace has no corner buttons and keeps 1rem.
- `ReadingHeader.tsx` comment, `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` (the sidecar that mirrors it) now name both corner icons, Notes above Ask IRIS, and the bottom-padding rule.
