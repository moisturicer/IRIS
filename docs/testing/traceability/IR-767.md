## IR-767 · RDCO owns routed review and may decide at any time (ADR-047)

**Basis.** ADR-047, accepted 2026-11-01 and amended the same day (PR #416, merged into this branch): only RDCO publishes a Thesis/Research or Project, and RDCO may override open Revision requests. Human PR review remains required.

**Amendment (2026-11-01, follow-up commit).** The Adviser's outcomes on a Thesis/Research or Project are `reject` only (`decisions.OUTCOMES_FOR`), so `accept_publish` is never offered to them and `decide/` refuses their publish with a 400 naming RDCO. The tracker shows RDCO *Awaiting* from submission while a Thesis/Project is in review, and *Not required* once an Adviser's rejection ends it. `seed_demo` publishes its catalogue through Adviser → RDCO. Readiness tells an RDCO decider *RDCO can decide at any time.* Paper View follows the [prototype](../../ui-ux/prototypes/review-workspace.html): the Adviser's primary is *Accept & send to RDCO…* with RDCO pre-ticked, a pool-or-officers note and the author's flags beside the suggestions; an office's *Route to office…* is *Request review…* with a *Send request* button and a *Why? (required)* reason; RDCO's confirmation opens with the executive-Decision warning. **Verified 2026-11-01 after this amendment:** PostgreSQL run of `apps/reviews`, `apps/records`, `apps/audit`, `apps/notifications` and `apps/tests` — 1,178 passed; the 14 failures/errors are `test_throttle_configuration.py` and `test_transport_security.py`, whose settings subprocesses refuse the local `.env` (`AI_DISCLOSURE_BYPASS_FOR_DEVELOPMENT`, unexported `SECRET_KEY`) and which this change does not touch. This run also corrected four `test_paper_details.py` cases that already failed on the branch: RDCO now holds every sent record, so it is a participant. Frontend `src/features/records`: 489 passed; `tsc --noEmit`, targeted ESLint, Flake8 F and `manage.py check` clean.

**Behavior.** The Adviser’s send opens only RDCO’s assignment. RDCO and specialist offices request whole specialist offices or multiple named officers with a reason. Adviser suggestions route nothing. Office completion settles its Clearance and notifies the existing RDCO holder; no hand-back opens a turn. RDCO’s Decision withdraws open reviews, documents and Revision requests with its identity as the reason. Owners are told which Revision requests it withdrew. Readiness reads the Decision’s closure facts, and the confirmation and history expose the override. An uploaded but unsubmitted manuscript is restored to the last submitted Version before deciding. Staleness includes revision requests, named reviewers and recorded Findings.

**Migration.** `reviews/0022` adds suggestions and Decision provenance; `0023` opens an RDCO Pool only for eligible in-flight Thesis/Research and Project records. Re-running opens no duplicate. Reversal refuses once a cutover turn acquires review, extension, document-request, revision-request or reminder history.

**Test seams.** `apps/reviews/test_rdco_executive.py` drives the public record endpoints and real workflow services: target restrictions, multiple officers, malformed/duplicate nominations, office completion without hand-back, executive publication with a preserved Clearance, overrides and owner notices, stale confirmation, manuscript restoration, former reviewer accounts, and cutover forward/reverse behavior. Existing review, privacy, capabilities, tracker, deadline, seeding and audit tests use Adviser → RDCO → specialist fixtures. The routing dialog tests cover whole offices, multiple officers, separate Adviser suggestions, refusal recovery and accessibility; Decision and timeline tests cover override confirmation and provenance.

**Office meanings and author flags (2026-11-01).** Hermoine Zozobrado gave the offices' meanings: ITSO handles technical review, KTTO evaluates IP submissions, IERC handles ethics compliance and RDCO gives final approval. `routing._AUTHOR_FLAGS` now points the author's `is_ip` flag at KTTO, and no flag at ITSO (ADR-018 note). The hints also read the form's own flags, because the form never wrote `requested_itso/ierc/ktto`, so real submissions showed no flags. `apps/records/test_paper_details.py` and `apps/reviews/test_decisions.py` changed deliberately to set the form's flags.
