## IR-412

**Requirement:** **The Review section: the paper, a timeline and an action bar** — F6 of the IR-374 frontend redesign; no FR- id

**Design:** [ADR-032 §10](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) (actions offered by capability) · [ADR-022](../../adr/022-explicit-document-requests.md) (Request documents) · [frontend_redesign_spec](../../frontend_redesign_spec.md) §0 invariant 1, §4.6, §4.7, §4.13, Appendix F · F6, Appendix K (IR-352, IR-354) · Jira IR-412

**Implementation:** `ReviewSection` puts the unchanged `PaperPdfReader` (given the bar's height since IR-477, below) beside a review pane at `lg` (sticky, the reader's height, the Paper tab's pane geometry) and stacks the paper above the pane below `lg`, with the action bar sticky to the bottom of the screen. `timelineEntries.ts` builds one oldest-first list from the tracker alone -- routing, reviews and findings, requests for changes and their resolution, document requests -- each with actor, party and time in the server's words; it marks nothing as current, so a retired party such as Intake is never the present step. A declined review and its `ResubmissionRequest` are one act: the tracker's resubmission rows now carry `review`, the declined review's id, and the timeline shows the act once; they also carry `resolved_by`, so a resubmission or withdrawal names who closed it. `ReviewActionBar` is a `toolbar` landmark rendering one button per `REVIEW_ACTIONS` entry whose capability the adapter grants; a blocked one is disabled with its reason as its description; a terminal one is last and confirmed through `ConfirmDialog`, now built on `Modal` (labelled, focus-trapped, Escape, focus returned). *Request documents* is the first entry; IR-261/269–273 add theirs without changing the bar. *Record a decision (current form)* links to the untouched `EvaluationPage` until IR-274. No Ask IRIS in the section; *Ask about this paper* opens the Paper tab with the chat open.
