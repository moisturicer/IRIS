## IR-656

**Requirement:** The sign-in lockout counts the real client behind the proxies: a stranger's guesses no longer lock the owner out, and a forged `X-Forwarded-For` entry neither escapes a lock nor locks someone else out

**Design:** Jira IR-656 (spec IR-652); ASVS 4.0.3 item 2.2.1

**Implementation:** `apps/accounts/client_address.py` is `AXES_CLIENT_IP_CALLABLE`: the `TRUSTED_PROXY_COUNT`th entry from the right, or `REMOTE_ADDR` when that entry is missing or no address. Production refuses to start without a count of at least 1. `AXES_LOCKOUT_PARAMETERS` replaces the deprecated setting with the same username + IP key

**Test:** `apps/accounts/test_client_address.py` through the real login endpoint and the real production settings module. Before the fix 5 tests failed. The count of 2 assumes a TLS proxy that appends to `X-Forwarded-For`; no such proxy exists to test against

**Evidence:** 2026-10-31, local Docker: `apps/accounts apps/tests` 240 passed, 11 skipped

**Status:** **IMPLEMENTED**
