## IR-1332

**Requirement:** **On the review page's Documents tab, Remove is a maroon trash-can icon, the one a draft has in My Library.** No FR- id; requested by Hermoine Zozobrado, 2026-11-24.

**Before:** Remove was a small outlined pill with a trash icon and the word "Remove", the same as Download.

**Implementation:** `DocumentsTab.tsx`'s `RemoveButton` now draws the trash can that `DeleteRecord` uses:
- an icon-only round button in `text-brand`, turning `brand-dark` on hover, with no fill or outline at rest;
- "Remove" as its tooltip.

Details:
- Its accessible name is unchanged ("Remove Patent Draft version 4").
- Its target is the row actions' own: 44px on touch, 36px beside a pointer. So no row grows.
- It applies wherever Remove is offered: a version, a held upload, and a supplementary attachment.
- Download is unchanged. Its download icon was already drawn by Font Awesome; it was missing only from screenshots taken without Font Awesome.

**Test:** `DocumentsTab.test.tsx` adds "draws Remove as a trash can with no word, and Download with its word". The tests that remove by name pass unchanged.

**Evidence:** 2026-11-24, local.
- Before the change, the new test failed. After it, `npm test` passed: 255 files, 3805 tests. `tsc` and `eslint` are clean.
- The before-and-after screenshots come from the smoke build of the Documents tab, with Font Awesome 6.5.1 served locally in place of its CDN. They show the maroon trash can and its "Remove" tooltip on hover.

**Status:** **IMPLEMENTED**; awaiting CI and review.
