# IR-1331: reading history is a pop-up, cleared by choice, with no pause

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** Hermoine Zozobrado asked for this on 2026-11-24, after [IR-1328](IR-1328.md), choosing option C of three in chat. There is no FR- id. [ADR-044](../../adr/044-account-backed-research-library.md)'s amendment of 2026-11-24 b (**Proposed**) holds the decision.

- *Manage history* (Overview) and *Manage reading history* (Saved Papers, for a reader with no Overview) open a History pop-up. The tab does not change.
- Each paper is a compact row: a tick box, its title (a link that opens the paper and closes the pop-up) and the day it was opened. A paper the reader can no longer read is a row with no title or link.
- The reader ticks papers, or *Select all*, and *Clear history* asks once more, naming how many, before only those rows are deleted.
- There is no Pause control. A history paused earlier says so and offers *Turn history back on*.

## Design

**Server.**
- `POST /api/v1/library/history/remove/` (`ReadingHistoryRemoveView`, route `library-history-remove`) deletes the caller's own rows for `record_ids` in one statement and answers 204 whatever the ids name.
- It refuses (400) anything but a list of 1 to 50 integers, so an empty or malformed body never clears everything.
- `DELETE /library/history/` and `LibraryPreferences.keep_history` are unchanged: the server still records nothing while history is paused.

**Screens.**
- New `HistoryDialog.tsx`: a `md` `Modal` whose footer holds *Select all* (indeterminate when some rows are ticked) and *Clear history* (disabled until a row is ticked), and a `ConfirmDialog` for the second question.
- Titles wrap rather than truncate, as DESIGN.md's Full Text Rule requires.
- `history.store` replaces `clear()` with `remove(recordIds)`: optimistic, and it puts the rows back if the server refuses. `libraryApi.clearHistory` is replaced by `removeHistory`.
- The History view is removed from `SavedView`, and `{ kind: "history" }` from `LibraryView`. `MyLibraryPage` holds the pop-up's open state.
