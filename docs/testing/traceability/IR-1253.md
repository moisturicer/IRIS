## IR-1253

**Requirement:** In Ask IRIS's conversation list, each row stays one line, ending in "…" when its title doesn't fit, and never changes height on hover, focus or with its menu open. One ⋯ menu holds Pin/Unpin, Rename and Delete. A cut title stays whole for everyone

**Design:** Jira IR-1253 (reported by Ashlee Sean, asked for by Hermoine Zozobrado, 2026-11-21: "exactly like a Claude chat", with Pin, Rename and Delete under ⋯). On hover the link gained `pr-24` for three floating buttons, and the wrapping title (`break-words`) reflowed onto more lines, so the row jumped. DESIGN.md's Full Text Rule (IR-1218) gains a **seventh allowed cut**, "a conversation's title in Ask IRIS's list". Hermoine set that rule and asked for this cut

**Implementation:**

- `frontend/src/features/ai/components/ConversationSidebar.tsx` `HistoryRow`:
  - The title is `truncate` on one line.
  - The three `RowAction` buttons are replaced by the shared `RowMenu` (IR-1077), labelled `Actions for <title>`. Delete is its danger item, and the menu is shown on hover and focus, kept while open (`has-[[aria-expanded=true]]`), and always shown on touch.
  - While the title is cut, the full title shows in the shared `Tooltip` on hover and keyboard focus. The native `title` is removed so there's one tip, not two.
  - Rename still edits in place, and Escape or save returns focus to the row's ⋯.
- `frontend/src/features/accounts/RowMenu.tsx`: a `small` trigger, 32 px, or 44 px on touch (`touch:`).
- `DESIGN.md`, `docs/engineering/CODING_STANDARDS.md` and `frontend/src/test/fullTextRule.test.ts` name the seventh cut, allowed only in `ConversationSidebar.tsx`.

**Test:** `ConversationSidebar.test.tsx` covers:

- One ⋯ button per row, holding Pin, Rename and Delete in that order (with axe).
- A cut title shows whole in a tooltip on keyboard focus and is read whole; a title that fits shows no tooltip.
- Delete from the menu.
- Rename by mouse and by keyboard, where Escape returns focus to the ⋯.
- An over-long title, and a server refusal.
- Pin, then Unpin after a reload.

**Changed deliberately:** the IR-1160 tests used buttons `Rename <title>`, `Pin <title>` and `Unpin <title>`. Those are now menu items under `Actions for <title>`, and focus returns to that button. `fullTextRule.test.ts`'s allowlist gains this file.
