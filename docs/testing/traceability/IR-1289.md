## IR-1289

**Requirement:** The person who made a request sees it decided without a reload.
- When RDCO approves or declines a role request, or an admin changes someone's role, that person's open app shows their new standing or role: the pending-approval screen, sidebar and routes follow, and the live stream hears the new role's channel.
- When RDCO confirms, rejects or removes a Call, its poster's **My Calls** and **Calls and conferences** show it.
- Nobody else is told.

**Design:** [ADR-073](../../adr/073-live-updates-by-server-sent-hints.md) §Amendment 2026-11-23 (b). **Proposed**, not yet accepted.

**Implementation:**

- **Backend:**
  - `apps/live/channels.py`: topics `ACCOUNT` and `CALLS`.
  - `apps/live/signals.py`:
    - `account_changed(user_id)`;
    - `post_save` on `RoleRequest` tells its user `account`;
    - `post_save` and `post_delete` on `Opportunity` tell its poster `calls`.
  - `apps/accounts/models.py`: `User.assign_role` calls `account_changed`.
- **Frontend:**
  - `lib/live.ts`: `restartLive`, plus a generation counter so a replaced read loop stops instead of reconnecting.
  - `components/auth/AuthBootstrap.tsx`: `useLiveRefresh(["account"])` re-reads `/auth/me` into the store and restarts the stream when `role_name` changed. A failed read keeps the account shown.
  - `features/opportunities/MyCallsPage.tsx` and `CallsAndConferencesPage.tsx`: `useLiveRefresh(["calls"])` with a quiet refresh.

**Test:**

- `backend/apps/live/test_request_hints.py` (6):
  - asking, approving and declining tell the asker only;
  - an admin's role change tells that person;
  - confirming, rejecting and removing a Call tell its poster only;
  - a Call nobody posted tells nobody.
- **Frontend:**
  - `src/lib/live.test.ts` (+1): restart opens one new stream, and the replaced loop never reconnects.
  - `src/components/auth/AuthBootstrap.live.test.tsx` (3): a new role from an `account` hint restarts the stream; an unchanged role does not; a failed read keeps the account.
  - `src/features/opportunities/MyCallsPage.test.tsx` (+2): a decision shows after a `calls` hint, and a failed live read keeps the list.
