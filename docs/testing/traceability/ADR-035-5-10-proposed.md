## ADR-035 §5 · §10 (proposed)

**Requirement:** **A question's evidence requirement is detected and recorded** — no FR- id; [ADR-035](../../adr/035-ask-iris-decides-whether-it-needs-evidence.md) §5 is the requirement **once accepted**, and it is `Proposed`, not accepted. IR-464 under [IR-456](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-456) — the evidence decision, Phase 0 and a reader-invisible shadow pilot. Nobody has established whether a given question needs corpus evidence at all; this is the deterministic half of establishing it, and the instrument that measures it

**Design:** ADR-035 §5 (five rules, five reason codes, raw-and-Resolved by OR, terms read at startup, no restricted-evidence rule, a rule may add retrieval and may never drive a refusal) · ADR-035 §10 (two instruments never conflated: the curated set supplies accuracy, production shadowing supplies operational figures and carries no ground truth) · ADR-005 (institution and Area terms are per-deployment) · **extends IR-463's annotation schema** with an optional `resolved_question`, which §5's OR rule cannot be measured without

**Implementation:** `apps/ai/evidence/` — `rules.py` (`scope_record` structural; `institution_term`, `document_reference`, `sourcing_demand`, `aggregate_shape` lexical, matched as whole phrases on a normalization that folds punctuation, so `CIT-U`, `CIT U` and `cit–u` are one term and `paperwork` is not `paper`; `RuleSet.digest` is a SHA-256 over the **complete** active set, generic English terms included) · `detector.py` (`EvidenceDetector.detect(question, resolved_question=, record_scoped=)` ORs the two lanes and keeps per-rule hits tagged by source; `Verdict` carries `evidence_required`, `hits`, `rule_set_digest` and `undetermined` and **has no field a consumer could read as a refusal**; unreadable text — judged on the normalized form, so `???` counts — fails safe to a requirement) · `config.py` (`AI_EVIDENCE_DECISION` accepts `off`/`shadow` and **rejects `on`**; `AI_EVIDENCE_INSTITUTION_TERMS` / `AI_EVIDENCE_AREA_TERMS` read **once** in `AiConfig.ready()` and cached, logged there with their digest and nowhere else) · `apps/ai/evaluation/evidence.py` — the curated instrument: per-reason-code over-fires and `silent on required` for the `raw`, `resolved` and `combined` lanes separately, coverage, institutional questions apart, and categories under `--min-examples` as **inconclusive**; `model` is an explicitly `null` sibling section, never mixed with detector numbers · `manage.py eval_evidence` · `labels.py` gains `resolved_question` and `QuestionSet.resolved` · `proxy_starter.json` labels four follow-ups with a Resolved form. **Nothing consumes a verdict**: no view, no task, no answer-state change, no web search, no agent loop

**Test:** `apps/ai/evidence/tests/test_rules.py` (28 — one case per rule, a general question raising nothing, spelling folding, phrase-not-substring, the digest moving on a term and not on a reordering, and `test_there_is_no_restricted_evidence_rule`, which fails if `confidential`/`restricted`/`embargo` ever enter a word list) · `test_detector.py` (19 — **a rewrite that drops a sourcing demand or an institution name still yields a requirement, asserted directly**, and the reverse: a rewrite that introduces one does too; `scope_record` on every record-scoped question including an unreadable one; seven unreadable inputs failing safe; `test_the_detector_cannot_express_a_refusal` pinning the `Verdict` field set) · `test_configuration.py` (12 — `on` refused, an unknown mode refused, an empty term list refused **while enabled** and fine while off, and `test_a_loaded_rule_set_does_not_change_when_a_setting_does`, which is what "read at startup" means) · `apps/ai/evaluation/tests/test_evidence_report.py` (14 — over-fires attributed per rule, a per-rule silence that is **not** a detector miss, the resolved lane covering only questions that carry one, the combined lane as the OR, inconclusive categories, and the empty `model` section) · `apps/ai/tests/test_eval_evidence_command.py` (6 — **no `Conversation` and no `Turn` created**, `composition_root` monkeypatched to raise so a model call fails the test, the results file's digest and `null` model slot, an unannotated set refused rather than scored zero) · `evaluation/tests/test_evidence_requirement.py` gains 4 tests (7 collected cases) for `resolved_question` · `apps/tests/test_enum_vocabulary.py` gains an `ALLOWED` entry for `ai/evidence/rules.py`, with its reason: the `document_reference` rule's English term `"published"` is a word a reader types, not `PipelineStatus.PUBLISHED`, and importing it from `core.enums` would tie what the detector recognises to the workflow's internal spelling — the same "two concepts, one string" shape as the existing `embedding_space.py` entry

**Evidence:** `pytest apps/ai/evidence apps/ai/evaluation apps/ai/tests/test_eval_evidence_command.py` — **150 passed** 2026-10-06 against the compose Postgres/pgvector (79 of them new). `pytest apps/ai` — 4 failures, all pre-existing on `origin/main` and all driven by this host's `backend/.env` (`LLM_TEMPERATURE=0.5`, `LLM_REASONING_EFFORT`, `LLM_RESOLVE_*`) in `inference/tests/test_profiles.py` and `providers/tests/test_llm_contracts.py`; neither file is touched by this change. `flake8 apps/ai` clean, `manage.py check` clean. The three startup behaviours were verified against a real Django boot, not only in tests: `AI_EVIDENCE_DECISION=on` and `=shadow` with a blank term list each raise `ImproperlyConfigured` from `manage.py check`, and `=shadow` with terms starts. `manage.py eval_evidence --questions ../docs/evaluation/proxy_starter.json --no-write` run the same day: **10 of 62 questions annotated**, raw 9/10, resolved 3/4, combined 10/10, zero over-fires, and all five categories reported INCONCLUSIVE at two examples each

**Status:** **BUILT and executed, CONSUMED BY NOTHING — and NOT merged behaviour that the governance gate covers.** [ADR-035 is `Proposed`, not accepted](../../adr/035-ask-iris-decides-whether-it-needs-evidence.md), and IR-461 sits In Review with no human approval recorded, so **IR-464's own blocker is not cleared**. What landed is the half that contradicts nothing (IR-456 §Governance: "work that contradicts nothing may merge first") — a detector no code path calls and an instrument that reads it. Production routing, the Celery shadow task, `ToolCallingLLM`, answer-state changes, web search and any agent loop are **not here**, and must not land before the ADR is accepted. Two numbers must not be misread: **combined 10/10 is a demonstration that the OR rule works on ten questions, not a measurement of the detector** — every category holds two examples and is reported inconclusive for that reason; and the **raw-lane miss `q61` is the predicted shape** (ADR-035 §Context's `s02`), a question with no repository vocabulary, which the model decision (IR-465) is what covers. The detector is also **not registered in `evaluation/techniques.py`** — ADR-035 §1 asks for that, and it is left to IR-465/IR-466 rather than added to the six ADR-033 §5 switches every retrieval run records. **Superseded by IR-466**, which registers `evidence_decision` (off/shadow)
