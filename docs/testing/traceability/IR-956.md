## IR-956 — Evaluate answer quality with a comprehensive question set on paid runs

**Basis and scope.** IR-956 under IR-955. The authority is ADR-049 §24–26 as amended 2026-11-07 by this ticket. The owner's decisions behind it are recorded in IR-955. Jira plans the work; it does not define it. This adds one command, a pure scoring module, a frozen-set format and a 108-question set. The earlier IR-605/IR-741 machinery (named review, three repetitions, the 90%/95% readiness gates) stays in the code, unused by this method; IR-957 retires what the switch no longer needs. The paid run itself is evidence, not a CI test, and **has not been run**.

**What changed.**
- `apps/ai/evaluation/answer_quality.py` (new, pure). It holds:
  - the set validator and the freeze (`make_lock`, `check_lock`);
  - the fixed rubric `aq-rubric-1` and the `ModelGrader`;
  - the exact checks and the scorer (`grader_payload`, `score_case`);
  - collection with a budget stop (`collect_both`, `Budget`) and grading with retry (`grade_all`);
  - the summary, IR-955's verdict and the Markdown report.
- `apps/ai/management/commands/eval_answer_quality.py` (new). Flags: `--validate`, `--write-lock`, `--run`, `--rescore` and `--reuse-django`.
- `replay.collect_case` and `runtime_replay.collect_case_runtime` now:
  - open a Paper Chat Conversation for a scoped case (Django: `record` on create; runtime: `X-IRIS-Record` through `RuntimeSeam.scoped`);
  - run a compaction turn: the runtime's own compaction route, or `not_supported` on Django, with nothing sent.
- `docs/evaluation/answer-quality/`: the set, its builder, the exported corpus titles and the method README.
- ADR-049 amendment 2026-11-07.

**Acceptance criteria.**
1. *Twelve families, at least eight each.* `test_every_family_needs_eight_questions` and `test_the_committed_set_has_twelve_families_of_at_least_eight_over_the_proxy_corpus`. The committed set has 108 questions, 8–10 per family.
2. *Reuses existing material; every question labelled.* `test_labels_must_name_corpus_papers_and_carry_a_reference_or_count`, plus the committed-set test, which requires the `proxy_starter`, `conversation_set_v1` and `long-context-draft` sources. The 77 page labels were audited against the development database's chunk text on 2026-11-07: all match, two through `also`.
3. *Frozen; a change is a new version.* `test_a_lock_covers_questions_labels_rubric_and_grader`, `test_a_changed_set_under_a_new_version_needs_its_own_lock` and `test_a_lock_is_written_once_and_never_rewritten_with_different_content`. v1 frozen 2026-11-08 in `answer-quality-v1.lock.json`; v2 (F12 moved to another unpublished paper) frozen the same day in `answer-quality-v2.lock.json`, with the owner's grader choice, `anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5`. `test_the_committed_lock_freezes_the_committed_set_with_its_pinned_grader` keeps the set and the lock in step.
4. *One paid run per engine, under equal conditions; failures count.* Covered by:
   - `test_failed_capped_and_unrun_questions_count_as_failures_and_are_not_graded`;
   - `test_both_engines_answer_each_case_before_the_next_and_a_spent_budget_stops_both`;
   - `test_a_collector_that_raises_records_a_failure_and_the_run_goes_on`.

   The command refuses a run when the two engines use different answer models, when admission is off, when the campaign is not fresh, or when the access state is not what the set needs (`test_a_paid_run_refuses_before_sending_without_a_runtime_and_enforced_admission`).
5. *Automatic scoring; the grader sees only what the reader could read.* Covered by:
   - `test_the_grader_sees_only_what_the_reader_could_read_and_a_leak_is_counted` and `test_naming_the_unreadable_paper_in_the_text_is_a_leak`;
   - `test_counts_are_checked_exactly_whatever_the_grader_says` and `test_a_refusal_is_read_from_the_engine_when_it_says_so_and_from_the_grader_otherwise`;
   - `test_unsupported_and_uncited_claims_lower_citation_support` and the model-grader parsing tests.
6. *A report per family and per engine, with the verdict and the families that decided it.* `test_the_summary_reports_each_measure_per_family_and_overall`, the five verdict tests and `test_the_report_states_the_verdict_and_every_family`.
7. *Spend stays within the ceiling and stops before sending.* `test_a_budget_covers_every_turn_of_both_engines_and_both_grades_at_the_run_ceiling` and `test_grading_stops_before_a_call_the_budget_cannot_cover`. Every engine turn and grading call is admitted under enforced provider admission.
8. *ADR-049 §24–26 amended.* Amendment 2026-11-07.
9. *Tests cover the validator, the freeze and the scorer with a recording provider.* `test_answer_quality.py` (38 tests) and `test_answer_quality_command.py` (5 tests); the collector additions are in `test_runtime_replay.py` (3 tests) and `test_replay.py` (1 test).
