# IR-1043: three corner radii, named dialog sizes, one skeleton and no ad-hoc shadows

**Requirement:** `docs/frontend_redesign_spec.md` sections 4.12 and 4.13 (shadow only for what floats; dialog behaviour); `docs/ui-ux/01-design-system.md` "Radius, shadow, spacing", "Dialog sizes" and "Loading placeholders" (written by this change).

**Radius.** `tailwind.config.js` now *replaces* Tailwind's `borderRadius` scale with `none`, `control` (12 px), `card` (16 px), `dialog` (20 px) and `full`. The ticket's guess of "near `rounded-lg` and `rounded-xl`" is kept for control and card; dialog is one step up so the three differ. Mapping used for the 391 class migrations across about 145 files: `rounded`, `-sm`, `-md`, `-lg` to `control`; `-xl`, `-2xl` to `card`; the dialog surfaces (`Modal`, `AccountLockedModal`, `RequestDocumentDialog`, Paper Chat's bottom sheet) to `dialog`. Raw CSS in `index.css` and `reviewWorkspace.css` reads `theme('borderRadius.*')`.

**Judgement calls, each a visible change (AC 2 asks for these to be listed).** There are no allowlisted exceptions; every `rounded-sm` and arbitrary radius was resolved:

- `rounded-none` where a shape must meet geometry it does not own: `ReaderHighlights`, `readerAnnotations` and `CitationOverlay` (highlights over a PDF's own text boxes, was 2 px), the search-match marks in `discoverUtils` and `searchTerms`, the deleted-text mark in `ChangeItem`, the Stop square in `ChatInput` (was 3 px) and the chat bubble's tail corner (`rounded-tr-md`, 6 px, now square). At 12 px each of these would read as a pill.
- `rounded-full` for the two `PaperThumbnail` bars (3 and 5 px tall, were `rounded-sm`).
- `before:rounded-bl-[14px]` on a thread connector (`CommentsSection`) is `before:rounded-bl-card` (16 px) on a 22 x 27 px box.
- Cards that were `rounded-2xl` (20 px) are now 16 px; a chip or row that was `rounded`, `-sm` or `-md` (2 to 8 px) is now 12 px; a dialog that was 16 px is now 20 px. `.scrollbar-thin`'s thumb is 12 px (a pill at 6 px wide) and `.find-hit` is square.
- The section thumb's concentric `rounded-md` is retired: thumb and track are both `control`.

**Modal.** `size` is `"sm" | "md" | "lg" | "xl"` (`ModalSize`), backed by four `maxWidth` tokens (`max-w-dialog-*`: 24 rem, 32 rem, 45 rem, 1180 px); a width class or an unknown name is a type error (`Modal.test.tsx` holds two `@ts-expect-error` lines that `tsc` checks). Default is `md` (32 rem, the old `max-w-lg`). **A deviation from the ticket's suggestion:** a fourth size, `xl`, exists because Publish's second step is two panes at 1180 px and fits nothing smaller. All 12 callers that passed a width are migrated (`max-w-sm` to `sm`; `max-w-md` to `md`, 28 rem becoming 32; `max-w-2xl`, `max-w-3xl`, `max-w-[720px]` and `md:max-w-2xl` to `lg`, 45 rem). A `sheet` now starts its width at `md`, because it is full screen below that (the old default `max-w-lg` capped a sheet at 512 px on a 640 px tablet). No change to `useDialogFocus`, Escape, the trap or return focus.

**Skeletons.** `SkeletonBlock` (`components/ui/Skeleton.tsx`: `tone` soft or strong, `shape` control, card or pill; the pulse and its reduced-motion rule live there) is the one placeholder shape. `Skeleton` draws its rows with it. `DiscoverPage`'s `ResultsSkeleton`, `CallsAndConferencesPage`'s `BoardSkeleton`, `PersonProfilePage`'s `ProfileSkeleton` and `PaperViewPage`'s `LoadingSkeleton` are `LoadingRegion`s of `SkeletonBlock`s with the same layout classes as before, so nothing shifts; the last three previously had their own hand-written `role="status"` and, for Calls, a copy of `Skeleton`'s announce-on-next-tick effect, and now also get `LoadingRegion`'s slow-load line after 2 s. `AssistantMessageSkeleton` keeps its `aria-busy` wrapper (its text is its announcement) and draws its three lines with `SkeletonBlock`. Not in the ticket's list and left alone, still drawing their own pulse boxes: `PaperAiOverview`, `ReadingHeader`, `RelatedWorks`, `RelatedPublishedResearch`, `ReviewTimeline`, `ReviewRoutingTracker`, `RequestDocumentDialog`, and the pulse cards in `LibraryOverview`, `MyResearchList`, `SavedView` and `DownloadRequestsView`. The design doc says so. `SkeletonBlock`'s default corner is `control`, so a skeleton text line goes from 8 px to 12 px; on a 12 px bar that reads as a pill. The slow-load line (2 s) can add a row under a skeleton that previously had none.

**Shadow.** The four arbitrary shadows (the ticket says five; four exist in non-test source on this commit): Ask IRIS's two drawer shadows and the paper workspace panel's become `shadow-card-md`; the section switch's recessed inset shadow is removed (a flat `stone-200` track).

**Panels.** The mechanical `rounded-lg` to `control` mapping left twelve bordered, padded blocks (notices, previews, the Discover feed strips, Publish's match warnings, the response letter) as `control`; they are `card`. Menus and list rows inside a card stay `control`.

**Guard.** `src/test/radius.ts` and `radius.test.ts` scan non-test `.ts`, `.tsx` and `.css` for any default-scale or arbitrary `rounded-*` and any arbitrary `shadow-[..]`, comments excluded, no allowlist.

**Tests edited, deliberately (CLAUDE.md "do not modify a test to make it pass").** Both assert a class name this ticket renames, not behaviour: `PaperAiOverview.test.tsx` (a negative check that the section is not a card, `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-card`) and `readingStandards.test.ts` (`CONTROL_SHAPE` is `rounded-control`; the "three `rounded-md`s" allowlist becomes "no Tailwind radius size in the reading surfaces"). No `Modal` focus, Escape or return-focus test was touched (AC 7).

Executed evidence (2026-11-15):

- `eslint src`: no errors; the two warnings (`UserListPage` hook dependency, an unused disable in `PaperViewPage`) exist on `main`.
- `src/test/radius.test.ts` 10 / 10, `Modal.test.tsx`, `readingStandards.test.ts`, `PaperAiOverview.test.tsx`, `ConfirmDialog.test.tsx`: pass.

- `vite build`: succeeds, and the emitted CSS contains `rounded-control`, `rounded-card`, `rounded-dialog`, `rounded-t-dialog`, `md:rounded-dialog`, `before:rounded-bl-card`, `max-w-dialog-lg` and `md:max-w-dialog-xl`.

Not covered by the guard: raw `border-radius` in a `style` prop and the inline scrim `boxShadow` in `PhotoCropDialog` and `GuidedTour` (not `shadow-[..]` classes, and not in the ticket's five).
