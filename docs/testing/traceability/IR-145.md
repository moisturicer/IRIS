# IR-145: routing evidence, written walkthrough

**Requirements:** FR-M5-01 (already VERIFIED; this adds the walkthrough it was missing). Rules: [ADR-032](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) §1–§5, [ADR-047](../../adr/047-rdco-routes-and-decides-at-any-time.md) §1–§5 and §9, and [ADR-003](../../adr/003-clearance-aware-resubmission.md).

Each step below is something a person does in IRIS, the API call behind it, what IRIS does, and the test that proves it at the REST seam. Every test named here passes on `main` as of 2026-11-04 (run below). The routes come from `apps/reviews/routing.py` (`ROUTE_TARGETS`), not from per-type branches.

| Step | Who and what | What IRIS does | Proven by |

| 2 | Owner submits v2 (`POST /records/<id>/new-version/`) | **Only IERC** resets and reviews again. ITSO's Clearance is kept and marked *preserved*.
