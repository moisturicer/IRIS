## IR-1201: each rejection's reason is saved on the upload it rejected

**Basis and scope.** IR-1201, subtask 2 of IR-1199 (the review-page cleanup). Authority: [ADR-022](../../adr/022-explicit-document-requests.md) §3.4 (a rejected item goes back to `missing` with a reason) and §Amendment 5 (reject reasons are document-request data). It has no FR- id. Version history (IR-1202) is what shows the result; this ticket stores and serves it.

**Why.** `reject_item` sent the item back to `missing`, cleared `item.upload` and overwrote `item.rejection_reason`. A second rejection lost the first reason, and nothing outside the audit log said which version had been rejected.

**What changed.**
- New model `documents.DocumentRejection`, migration `documents/0025`. It holds the item, the rejected upload, who rejected it, when and the reason. The upload is `RESTRICT`, so a rejected file can't be deleted on its own, but it still goes when its Record does. No backfill: the dev database holds demo data.
- `requests.reject_item` writes one row per rejection, in the same transaction, before it forgets the upload. `item.rejection_reason` is unchanged, because the owner's panel still reads it.
- `records.versions.versions_payload(record, *, disclose_requests)`: each document in `documents` and `documents_added` carries `rejections` (party, reason, rejected-by name, time, oldest first).
  - `rejections` is `None` unless the viewer passes `may_read_requests`.
  - The record detail serializer and `tracker_payload` pass that rule. The tracker's existing `disclose_requests` is reused.
- `versions.deletion_refused_reason` also refuses an upload that was rejected: it answered a request, as an accepted one did.
- The personal-data export lists the person's rejections (`workflow_acts.document_rejections`). Account deletion keeps them as review history (`deletion/registry.py`).

**Tests.** `apps/documents/tests/test_rejection_history.py`, at the HTTP seam: the requesting party rejects through `PATCH /document-request-items/<id>/`, and the test reads the record detail's or the tracker's `versions`.
- Two rejections of one item each stay on their own upload, with party, name and order.
- An unrejected upload has `[]`.
- The requesting party sees the rejections. The tracker carries the same list.
- A reviewer who has since left their office still reads the review but gets `rejections: None`.
- The uploads list gives a rejected upload's `delete_refused`.

The owner's upload is made through `resolve_item_for_upload`, `create_upload` and `fulfil_item`, not `documents/submit/`, because the PDF check runs only on Linux. The upload route is covered by `test_document_request_decisions.py`.

**Runs.** 2026-11-17, Windows, against a throwaway pgvector container:
- `test_rejection_history.py`, `accounts/test_personal_data_export.py` and `accounts/test_account_deletion.py`: 84 passed.
- `test_document_request_decisions.py`, `test_document_requests.py`, `test_document_request_read_access.py`, `reviews/test_version_documents.py`, `records/test_review_text.py` and `records/test_tracker.py`: 48 failed and 90 passed, the same 48 test IDs as on unchanged `main`. All are PDF uploads, which fail on Windows because `apps/documents/pdf_inspection.py` imports the Unix-only `resource` module. CI on Linux runs them.
- **Migration on a copy of the dev database** (18 records, documents at `0012`): `migrate` applied every pending migration through `documents/0025`. `migrate documents 0024` unapplied it and `migrate documents` reapplied it, and `makemigrations --check` reports no changes. The one existing request item is untouched, and `versions_payload` reads that copy's Versions.
