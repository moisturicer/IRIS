# IR-882: every paper starts embargoed, and only the Adviser or RDCO date or release it

**Requirements:** FR-M3-02 (a stable label only). The rule is [ADR-052](../../adr/052-each-paper-states-its-embargo-and-a-person-ends-it.md)'s *Amendment — 2026-11-04 (IR-879): embargoed by default*, its first 2026-11-04 amendment (an embargo stops publishing) and the *Built by IR-882* notes at its end. ADR-047 §10 is corrected to match.

This reverses IR-816 (Publish's first question) and narrows IR-812 (owners no longer set an embargo). Their notes describe behaviour that is now gone.

What changed:

- **`Record.is_embargoed`** (default true) is the embargo. A database check holds it false exactly when `embargo_released_at`/`_by` are set. `embargoed_until` stays the optional reminder, and `embargo_date_set_at`/`_by` (renamed from the answer stamps) say who set it. `embargo_state` is Active or Released. None and Unknown are gone, and the disclosure gate passes only Released.
- **Migrations.**
  - `records/0032` turns "no embargo" answers into releases by whoever answered, and leaves every other paper embargoed. It flushes deferred foreign-key checks after its data step, because PostgreSQL refuses the `ALTER TABLE` steps after it otherwise.
  - `publication/0009` rewrites both indexing functions to read `NOT r.is_embargoed`, has the access fence watch the flag, and grants the new column to `iris_authz_verifier`. The date stamps are renamed in Django only. They keep their columns, `embargo_answered_at`/`_by_id`, so the old functions stay valid at every step.
- **Publish asks nothing.** `POST /records/<id>/embargo-answer/` is deleted, `submit/` needs no answer, and the Publish dialog opens on step 1. The Review step says the paper starts embargoed and to tell the Adviser about an agreement or patent.
- **`embargo-set/` is the Adviser's or RDCO's** (`may_set_embargo`). An owner gets a 403, and `capabilities` no longer offers owners `set_embargo`.
- **RDCO can't publish an embargoed paper.** `decisions.outcomes_for` drops `publish`, `decide/` refuses it with *"Under embargo [until <date>]. Release the embargo, then publish."*, and the Decision dialog says why when keeping it unlisted.
- **Test data.** `seed_demo` keeps two submitted papers embargoed, one dated (in front of RDCO) and one undated, and their Adviser releases the rest. `load_corpus --clear-for-ai` and `mark_corpus_cleared` release test papers.
- **Paper View's embargo card** reads Active with or without a date, and offers *Add review date*, *Change date* or *Embargo again*.
