## IR-594 — grounded answers with typed validation and one bounded repair

**Requirement.** [ADR-042](../../adr/042-general-tools-conversation-contract.md) and the [general-tools spec](../../architecture-review/2026-10-15-askiris-general-tools-spec.md) §5, §6.2: model-composed answers at the requested depth (two-paper tables allowed, no tool narration); a draft with source bindings, typed numeric facts and an optional analytical summary; validation of citation identity, current access and source version, numeric bindings and completeness; harmless numbering, equations and quoted phrases pass while a number bound to the wrong paper or metric fails; exactly one repair, then supported parts kept and security/version failures fenced; the validated answer delivered whole, and an honest unavailable state with safe sources on model failure.

**Built.** `apps/ai/answers/assurance.py` is the contract and its checks: `parse_draft` splits the Markdown from an optional fenced `bindings` JSON block (facts bound to a source and metric or to a computed fact's name, papers bound to a source, `summary`), which never reaches a reader; `check` works per sentence/table row and returns reason codes (`unknown_citation`, `source_withdrawn`, `unsupported_number`, `unsupported_count`, `wrong_binding`, `completeness_overclaim`, `malformed_bindings`). Math, code, quotes, figure/table labels and list/heading numbering are skipped; a number in a cited sentence must be in a cited source or a computed/typed fact, an uncited percentage, decimal or count must be a computed or validated typed fact, and a count of papers may equal the distinct papers the sentence cites. An exhaustive claim needs a metadata count and no limitation. `safe_summary` drops a summary carrying markers, snake_case identifiers or tool/routing words, or a number the answer lacks. `controller/compose.AnswerComposer` replaces IR-593's single call: a new answer policy and contract prompt (`ANSWER_PROMPT_VERSION = "answer-1"`, now pinned per run), one repair with code-level feedback quoting the draft's own failing sentences, skipped when a source was withdrawn or the run is cancelled; afterwards failing sentences are removed with a plain note, an answer left empty becomes `unavailable` with `UNSUPPORTED_TEXT`, and an outage or reasoning-only reply is `unavailable` with the still-current sources. The controller supplies `ReadyToCompose.current` (readable, disclosable, chunk in an active, undeleted `ChunkSet`) and records the final codes in `ResearchRun.validation_codes`. `GroundedAnswer` gains `reasoning_summary` and `validation`.

**Not built here.** The summary is produced and validated but not stored on the Turn or sent to the reader: the public `reasoning_summary`/`activity` protocol is IR-595, the UI IR-596. The repair uses the same evidence; the spec's optional targeted re-retrieval is not built. The legacy lanes keep their behaviour per ADR-042, including `research/validation.py` whole-answer withholding and the comparison lane's sentence filter. Untyped numbers are checked by literal membership in the cited sentence's sources, so it cannot tell a right number under the wrong metric, or which of two papers cited together a number belongs to; typed bindings and human review cover those. Years in a cited sentence are not checked. Numbers derived by arithmetic fail and are removed after the repair. An exhaustive claim passes on any metadata count with no limitation, whatever that count's predicate. A paper named in prose without a `papers` binding is not checked; "never invent a title" is held by the prompt alone. `NEW_RUN_ENGINE` is unchanged. `test_a_run_pinned_to_the_legacy_engine_never_reaches_the_controller` fails on a developer `.env` on clean `origin/main` as well (a legacy-path PLAN call); it is not caused by this change.

**Evidence (2026-10-16).** `apps/ai/answers/tests/test_assurance.py` (16, pure): A10 harmless formatting, years and abbreviations pass; removal leaves no orphan list markers or header-only tables; wrong-paper and unsupported numbers fail; uncited counts and percentages; invalid markers and handles; wrong metric, paper and computed-fact bindings; malformed bindings; exhaustive claims; withdrawn sources fenced; summary safety. `apps/ai/tests/test_answer_composer.py` (13, no database): one repair with feedback, no second; partial delivery with a note; nothing supported → unavailable with sources; fencing without repair; zero reserved repairs; outage, reasoning-only and blank replies unavailable; repair outage keeps supported parts; two-paper table and policy text; no model call with no evidence. `apps/ai/tests/test_grounded_answer_http.py` (6, `/ask/` and `/ask/stream/`, fakes): the final public payload and stored Turn carry the repaired answer with no internal wording (A10); an invented citation removed after a failed repair; a wrong count corrected against the computed fact (A06); a two-paper table delivered whole (A07); an answer-model outage returning sources and no findings (A09). Semantic quality stays human-evaluated.
