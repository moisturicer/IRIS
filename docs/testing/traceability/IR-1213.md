## IR-1213

**Requirement:** Open pages update by themselves when papers, reviews and requests change, without a manual refresh. A hint never reaches someone who can't open the record. A dropped connection catches up. An open stream doesn't count as activity and doesn't outlive its access token

**Design:** [ADR-073](../../adr/073-live-updates-by-server-sent-hints.md), accepted 2026-11-19 by Lee Jasmin Adolfo (verbally, recorded by Ashlee Sean). The questions it left open were settled by Ashlee: the proposed pages, a silent pause when Redis is down, and capacity measured before merge

**Implementation:**

- **Backend, `apps/live`:**
  - `channels.py`: server-assigned channels, `live:user:<id>` and `live:role:<role>`. An office's pool is its role's channel.
  - `signals.py`: publishes on commit.
    - A `Notification` hints its recipient (or the role it's broadcast to) with `notifications`, plus `reviews`, `library` and `record:<id>` when it names a record.
    - An office's `RecordAssignment` or `ReviewerSeat` hints that office's role with `reviews`, and no record id. A seat's holder also hears `record:<id>`.
    - A `RoleRequest`, `DownloadRequest`, `DeleteRequest` or `Opportunity` hints RDCO with `queues`.
  - `publish.py`: on commit, failures swallowed; `LIVE_ENABLED` turns it off and the test harness does so.
  - `stream.py`: one `PSUBSCRIBE live:*` per process (`Hub`), with bounded per-stream queues.
  - `views.py`: `GET /api/v1/live/`, an async class view.
    - It checks the `Bearer` token, the account, the credential version and the session (`sessions.check_open`, which records nothing), and the data notice.
    - It resolves the channels in sync code and lets go of its database connection.
    - It streams `: connected`, hints and a ping every 25 s until its access token expires.
- **Frontend:**
  - `lib/live.ts`: one fetch-read stream per tab while signed in, started by `AuthBootstrap`. A 401 refreshes through `refreshSession`; failures back off from 1 s to 60 s; a reconnect resyncs.
  - `hooks/useLiveRefresh.ts`: reloads on a matching hint, a resync or focus, once per 500 ms burst.
  - `useApiRead`, `usePagedRead` and `useCallList` gain a quiet `refresh` that keeps the shown list.
  - **Wired:**
    - the sidebar's queue counts and its My Reviews and My Library counts;
    - the notification bell (also IR-1033's refetch on focus);
    - My Reviews and the Office console;
    - My Library;
    - the review page (`record:<id>`);
    - Role, Download, Delete and Call requests.
- **Test harness:** the smoke mock answers `GET /live/` with 503.

**Test:**

- `backend/apps/live/test_live.py` (19):
  - **Publishing:** each publish point, nothing sent before commit, publishing off when disabled, and a failing publisher never failing the write.
  - **Hub and stream:** delivery only to the right channel, unregistering, overflow closing a stream, and hint/ping/expiry.
  - **View:** no or bad token and ended or idle sessions refused, with the idle check writing nothing; a stream that delivers its own user's hint and not another's, then unregisters.
- `backend/apps/tests/access/table.py` declares `live-stream`: every `force_authenticate` caller gets 401, because the view reads its own `Bearer` token.
- **Frontend:**
  - `src/lib/live.test.ts` (9): parsing, backoff, opening with the token, refreshing on 401, stopping when refresh fails, resync only on reconnect, and abort.
  - `src/hooks/useLiveRefresh.test.ts` (5).
  - `src/hooks/useApiRead.test.ts`: refresh keeps data and survives failure.
