## IR-1127

**Requirement:** A downloaded copy's "CIT-U" mark is visible on every page, whatever the paper paints first, without making the text unreadable or adding to the copy's text

**Design:** Jira IR-1127; ADR-048 decision 10, as amended 2026-11-15

**Implementation:** `apps/records/watermark.py` draws "CIT-U" as stroked lines and arcs, translucent, over each page's content (`_mark_overlay`), then the footer last. It was a Helvetica string merged beneath the content

**Test:** `apps/records/test_download_watermark.py`: `test_the_mark_shows_on_a_page_that_paints_an_opaque_background`, `test_the_mark_shows_on_a_scanned_page`, `test_the_mark_is_faint_so_the_text_beneath_stays_readable`, `test_the_mark_adds_no_text_to_the_copy`, `test_the_footer_is_drawn_last_so_nothing_covers_it`. They render page 1 with pypdfium2 (pdfplumber's renderer)

**Evidence:**

- 2026-11-15, locally, before the change: `watermark_pdf` on `sample6_v1.pdf` (a class project exported from Word, whose pages begin with a full-page white fill) gave a footer and no visible mark; `sample8_v1.pdf` showed the mark.
- 2026-11-15, locally, after the change: a harness repeating the new tests' checks against `watermark_pdf` passed every check (darkest pixel under the mark on white 230 of 255; on a scanned page 230; lightest on black 20; "CIT-U" once per page, in the footer). Both samples show the mark when rendered. Timing unchanged: sample6 (27 pages) 1.29 s, sample8 (9 pages) 0.31 s.
- The Django test module itself was **not** run locally: this machine's Postgres has no pgvector and Docker was not running. CI is the record for it; see the PR.

**Status:** **PARTIAL** until CI passes and the PR is reviewed
