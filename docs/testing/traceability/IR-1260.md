## IR-1260

**Requirement:** **A visible sidebar button collapses and expands the sidebar** — a usability finding, no FR- id; version A of the prototype `iris-uiux-audit/prototype/sidebar-toggle.html`, chosen by Lee Jasmin Adolfo on 2026-11-21 (versions B, an edge handle, and C, a labelled row, were not chosen). Follow-up the same day, after PR #946 merged: on the rail the mark itself is the expand button and shows the sidebar icon on hover, rather than a second button under it.

**Cause:** the IRIS mark was the only control that collapsed the sidebar (IR-1086). It reads as a logo, so nothing said it could be pressed; its "Collapse sidebar" tooltip only appeared to someone already pointing at it.

**Implementation:**

- `frontend/src/components/layout/Sidebar.tsx`. A sidebar button of its own, named "Collapse sidebar" or "Expand sidebar", with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls="app-sidebar"`, and a drawn panel icon whose chevron points the way the sidebar will move. On the full sidebar it is after the bell. It does what the mark did: on the desktop it toggles the saved collapse preference; from 768 to 1023 px it opens the full sidebar over the page, and closes it. It is `hidden md:flex`; the phone keeps Open menu and Close menu.
- `[` does the same from 768 px up. A document `keydown` listener ignores the key while focus is in an input, textarea, select or contenteditable, inside a `dialog` / `alertdialog` / `aria-modal` element, with Ctrl, Meta or Alt held, on auto-repeat, or when another handler already took it. No other `[` handler exists in the app.
- On the rail the IRIS mark is the expand button, the only one: "Expand sidebar", `aria-expanded="false"`, `data-sidebar-toggle`. At rest it draws the logo; on hover or keyboard focus the logo fades out and the sidebar icon fades in over a `bg-stone-100` square (`group-hover` / `group-focus-visible` opacity, no transition under reduced motion). Its tooltip is "Expand sidebar" with the `[` key cap. The full sidebar's separate button is not rendered on the rail.
- On the full sidebar and in the phone drawer the IRIS mark is a `Link` to `landingPathFor(user)` (IR-534), named "IRIS home". Choosing it closes the drawer like any other link.
- `frontend/src/components/ui/Tooltip.tsx`. A `shortcut` prop draws the key after the label as a key cap. A press on the wrapped control now closes the tip until the pointer leaves or focus moves: the tip is placed once when it opens, and the sidebar button moves (from the brand row to under the mark) and renames itself when pressed.
- `frontend/src/components/layout/AppShell.tsx`. Focus returns to `[data-sidebar-toggle]` (was `[data-sidebar-mark]`) when a tablet's opened sidebar closes.

**Test:** `Sidebar.test.tsx`:

- Desktop: the button is found by role and name in both states, with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
- On the rail the mark is the one "Expand sidebar" button, the first button in the sidebar, with no "IRIS home" link; its logo and icon carry the hover and focus opacity classes; pressing it expands and the link comes back.
- On the full sidebar the mark is a link home and not a button; choosing it opens Discover without collapsing anything; for a System Admin it goes to the Users path. It is named "IRIS home" (an earlier "IRIS, go to Discover" also matched the not-found screen's "Go to Discover" in the smoke tier).
- The tooltip shows the action and the `[` key cap.
- `[` collapses and expands; typed into a field it is a character and changes nothing; inside a `role="dialog"` and with Ctrl held it changes nothing.
- Tablet (768 and 1023 px): `[` opens the full sidebar with focus on "Collapse sidebar", and `[` again closes it with focus back on "Expand sidebar", saving no preference. The existing rail tests now use the button and the new name.
- Phone: `[` changes nothing, and the button's wrapper is `hidden` below `md`.
- axe stays clean in the existing rail and desktop cases.

**Not checked:** Safari and Firefox, and a screen reader. jsdom applies no CSS, so the hover swap and the hidden phone button are checked by class, and by eye in a browser (Chromium through the smoke tier's mocked API: rail at rest, on hover and on keyboard focus; tablet opened; phone drawer). On a touch screen there is no hover, so the rail shows the logo and a tap expands.
