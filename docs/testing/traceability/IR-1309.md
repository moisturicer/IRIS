## IR-1309

**Requirement:** The Review workspace is not self-explanatory, so its guided tour (IR-997) explains how to use it, not only where things are: how to comment on a passage and what the pins mean, what the pane's header says, what each tab is for and what to do there, the columns button, the *Your part* footer, and the usual order of a review (claim or open it, comment, finish with a verdict). A step still appears only for something on screen, and the tour still reads and changes nothing about the review.

**Design:** IR-997's rules stand. Steps are built from what is on screen and what the server grants, never from the role: the passage-comment advice needs `start_thread`, the Conversation comment box `comment_review`, and drafts `finish_review`. Each tab gets its own step on its own tab button. A tab moved to the right column is described there instead.

**Implementation:**

- `frontend/.../paper-view/tour/tourSteps.ts`: new steps for the pane's header, the tab row, each tab, the columns button and *How a review goes*. The paper, launcher and *Your part* steps now list what to do. New targets: `head`, `tab(name)`, `beside`, `side`.
- `frontend/.../paper-view/ReviewWorkspaceLayout.tsx`: puts those ids on the header, the tab buttons, the columns button and the right column, and passes the side tab and the three capabilities to the steps. The first-visit offer no longer says "four-step".
- `frontend/.../paper-view/TabBesideMenu.tsx`: optional `buttonId`.
- `frontend/.../paper-view/tour/GuidedTour.tsx`: the card is 360 px wide and scrolls inside the window when a step is long.

**Test:** `ReviewTour.test.tsx`

- The whole walk, in order: paper, header, tabs, Documents, Conversation, Threads, Changes, Details, *Your part*, *How a review goes*. Changes keeps its step on the first Version.
- No paper drops the paper's step. A narrow workspace with the pane closed shows only the paper and the launcher; opening the pane restarts the walk.
- The columns button has a step only at a width that shows it. A tab in the right column is described as "on the right" and not in the row.
- Commenting on a passage is explained only with `start_thread`. Drafts are explained only with `finish_review`. The resize edge is mentioned only where it exists.
- The existing IR-997 tests still hold: Skip, Escape, focus back to the opener, the reviewer left on their tab, starting again, no blocking axe violations.
