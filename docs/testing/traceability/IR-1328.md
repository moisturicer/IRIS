# IR-1328: the side cards keep their rule when empty

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** Hermoine Zozobrado asked for this on 2026-11-24, after [IR-1325](IR-1325.md). There is no FR- id.

- The rule under *Waiting on you*'s and *Recently opened*'s headings is always shown: with items, empty, loading or failed.
- With no papers yet, *Recently opened* says "Papers you open appear here." under the rule.
- With nothing waiting, *Waiting on you* says "Nothing needs your action. When a reviewer asks you for changes, it appears here." under the rule, as plain text with no circle-check.
- A list with items is spaced as before.

## Design

- The rule was each list's `border-t`, so it went away with the list.
- `SideCard` in `LibraryOverview.tsx` now draws it as the heading row's `border-b` (`pb-3`), and its scroll area starts with `pt-3`. It stays put while the list scrolls.
- The lists drop their `border-t`, and their first item drops its top padding (`first:pt-0`). The gaps above and below the rule are 12 px each, as before.
