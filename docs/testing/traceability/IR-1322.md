# IR-1322: Request changes, and the owner sends what they added

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** [ADR-022 Amendment 8](../../adr/022-explicit-document-requests.md) (accepted 2026-11-24; Ashlee Sean reviewed point 4 for security).
- **The reviewer acts once.** *Request revision* becomes *Request changes*, and one act can ask for a new version, for documents, or for both.
- **Uploads are held until sent.** An owner's upload to a submitted paper is theirs alone until *Submit new version* or *Submit added documents* sends it. Nobody else is served it, and it answers no request.
- **The owner can change their mind.** The owner may delete a held upload, and may upload into any slot while something is asked for.
- **One item per party.** *Waiting on you* shows one item per party: *Changes requested by …*.

Asked for by Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-24. There is no FR- id.

## What changed

**Server**
- **The `RecordUpload.sent_at` field** (migration `documents/0029`).
  - The migration backfills every existing upload to its `created_at`, so nothing already uploaded is held.
  - The field defaults to now. Only `create_upload` holds a file, and only an owner's upload to a non-draft record.
  - It comes with `RecordUploadQuerySet.sent()`, `held()` and `visible_to()`.
- **Every reader of a record's uploads reads sent ones only**, unless the reader is an owner. That covers the slot and upload listings, downloads, version documents, *added during*, review anchors, download-all, and upload reviews. A held upload answers a non-owner with a 404.
- **`POST /records/<id>/documents/send/`** (`send_held_uploads`) sends every held upload. It answers missing items on open requests (the newest upload per slot; an Other item through the slot named for it) and notifies each party once.
  - It refuses with 409 while a revision is open, 400 when nothing is held, and 403 for a non-owner.
  - It returns `{sent, requests_fulfilled}` and is audited as `UPLOAD`.
- ***Submit new version* sends held uploads first.** A held upload counts as a change.
- **`delete_upload` lets an owner remove a held upload**, whatever its version number.
- **`finish-review` with `request_revision` takes `documents` (the picklist or Other) and `new_version` (default true).**
  - Documents alone records a *Comment only* round and opens a document request.
  - Asking for neither, sending an item that isn't on the picklist, or sending documents with any other verdict is refused, and nothing changes.
- **`waiting_on_you` returns one item per party**, labelled *Changes requested by …*.

**Screens**
- **Documents tab (owner):**
  - One bar at the top. It shows *Submit new version* during a revision and *Submit added documents* otherwise. The button is grey and disabled, with its reason, until there is something to send, and solid maroon after that.
  - Every slot is listed with Upload while anything is asked for.
  - A held file reads *Added, not sent yet* and has a trash can.
- **Finish review:** *Request changes* adds a panel with *The paper itself needs changes* (ticked) and, for a reviewer who may request documents, the type's documents and *Other document*.
- **Wording:** the footer menu, the tour and the Overview's action card use the new names.

**Tests changed deliberately:**
- **Uploads now need a send.** In `test_document_requests.py`, the `upload_against` and `upload_to_slot` helpers press *Submit added documents* after an owner's upload, and `test_rejection_history.upload_directly` sends instead of fulfilling.
- **Six more tests.** `test_workflow_events.upload` sends after uploading. `RejectedThenSlotUploadTests` sends before checking the answer. The ad-hoc slot test now expects a sent upload to answer every Other item that names its slot (Amendment 8 point 5); it used to say only `request_item` answers one. `UnsentTests` uploads through `create_upload`, because `unsent()` lists held files only. `test_version_documents.upload` dates `sent_at` as well as `created_at`.
- **New labels.** `test_submitter_status.py` expects *Changes requested by*.
- **Upload on every slot.** In `DocumentsTab.test.tsx`, the fake server holds uploads until a send. The "Upload on the requested slot only" tests now expect Upload on every slot while something is asked for, here and in `PaperViewPage.test.tsx`.
- **The grey button.** *Submit new version* is now disabled while the server says nothing has changed. It used to open its dialog anyway (IR-876).
- **New names.** The tests for the verdict label (`threadModel`, `FinishReviewDialog`, `ReviewFooterActions`) expect *Request changes*.

**Known and left as it was:** an answered Other item shows twice in the Documents tab: once as the slot named for it, and once as the item. This also happens on `main`. The slot listing doesn't say which slots belong to the record, so merging the two rows needs an API field.
