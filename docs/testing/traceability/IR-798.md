## IR-798

**Requirement:** **RDCO's controls on a paper: replace the Adviser, add an owner, withdraw.** ADR-051 decision 10 is its rule, as subtask 18 of IR-779. It builds on IR-797's endpoints and touches FR-M5-01 (a stable label only).

**Design:** [ADR-051](../../adr/051-system-admin-account-deletion-and-consent.md) decision 10 · [IR-797](IR-797.md), the endpoints and their capabilities · ADR-032 §10 (controls come from `capabilities`) · Jira IR-798

**The ADR and the server differ on one point, recorded here and not reconciled.** ADR-051 decision 10 says RDCO can replace the Adviser of *any paper*. IR-797's `record_capabilities` offers `replace_adviser` only while the paper's Adviser review is open, and `replace-adviser/` refuses otherwise ("No Adviser review is open on this paper."). The screen follows the server's offer, so after the Adviser has finished, RDCO sees no *Replace Adviser* button. The ADR is the higher authority, so whether the server or the ADR should change is a decision for IR-797's owner or the ADR's. This ticket doesn't make it.

**Implementation (frontend only):**

- `frontend/src/features/records/paper-view/RdcoPaperControls.tsx` is a new **RDCO controls** card in Paper View's Record details rail, below *Unlisted*.
  - **Which controls show.** Each button shows only while its capability is in the record's `capabilities`: `replace_adviser`, `add_owner`, `withdraw_paper`. With none of the three, the card is absent. It compares no role names.
  - **Replace Adviser** opens a dialog with the existing `AdviserCombobox`, listing from `GET /users/advisers/`, plus an optional reason. It leaves out the current Adviser and the paper's owners (IR-260). Before RDCO confirms, it says the current Adviser's review ends and their open seat is withdrawn, and who is told: the old Adviser and the new one. It sends `POST /records/<id>/replace-adviser/`.
  - **Add owner** opens a dialog with an email field. It says the account must be active and verified, the Contact person stays the same and the review carries on, and who is told: the new owner and the current owners. It sends `POST /records/<id>/add-owner/`.
  - **Withdraw paper** opens a dialog with a required reason. It states that **the paper's review stops** and the paper is deleted and can't be restored. It says every open review, revision request and document request closes, and who is told: every owner in IRIS and by email with the reason, and each reviewer whose review closes, in IRIS. It sends `POST /records/<id>/withdraw/`.
  - **When the server refuses.** A refusal shows the server's words in the dialog: the field's message (`adviser`, `email`, `reason`) first, else `detail`. It then reloads the record. If the reload takes the offer away (someone acted first), the dialog closes and the refusal stays on the rail as an alert.
- `PaperViewPage.tsx` places the card. After a withdrawal the paper is deleted, so the page shows a success toast and replaces the route with `/review`.
- `api/records.ts` adds `replaceAdviser`, `addOwner` and `rdcoWithdraw`. `types/records.ts` adds the three keys to `Capability`.
