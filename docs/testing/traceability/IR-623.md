## IR-623 — coordinator console

**Authority.** ADR-032 §4 (a coordinator assigns, reassigns and withdraws seats in their own office) and [ADR-043 §6](../../adr/043-review-turnaround-reminders-and-escalation.md) (a coordinator extends with a reason). Parent IR-612. No new decision right is added.

**Implemented.** `GET /api/v1/reviews/office/` (`apps/reviews/office_console.py`): one office's unfinished seats and unclaimed pool assignments on records in review, each with the IR-619 deadline projection, ordered overdue first, then soonest due, then undated; and every active member's open and overdue seat count, idle members included. `office` defaults to the office the caller coordinates; anyone who does not coordinate it gets 403, declared in IR-615's matrix (`REVIEW_DEADLINES["reviews-office"]`, still a partial `reviews` declaration). *Reassign* now requires a reason: the new seat stores `reason` and `reassigned_from` (`reviews/0018`), and `SEAT_REASSIGNED`'s metadata carries it (`docs/evaluation/AUDIT_EXPORT.md`). The tracker gains `reassignments`, a `label` on each deadline-history row and `actor_name` on each extension; names and reasons are null to a viewer who may not read the review (IR-479). Frontend: `/review/office` (`OfficeConsolePage`, with `ReassignDialog` and `ExtendDueDateDialog`, and *Assign* through the existing `AssignReviewerDialog`), linked from My Reviews only when `review_access.is_coordinator` is set; the Review timeline shows reassignments and extensions (`timelineEntries.ts`).

**Evidence (2026-10-29).** Frontend, run locally: `npx vitest run` gave 99 files and **1215 tests passed**, including `OfficeConsolePage.test.tsx` (accessible-tree queries and axe), the two My Reviews entry-point tests and the two new `timelineEntries` cases. Each was observed red before its implementation. `tsc --noEmit` was clean, and eslint reported no finding in a touched file. Backend, run locally: `manage.py check` (one existing `axes.W004` warning), `makemigrations --check` (no changes) and flake8 on the touched modules were all clean. **The backend tests were not run locally**: Docker's engine was down and no local PostgreSQL with pgvector was reachable. `test_office_console.py`, the reassign cases in `test_seats.py`, the audit metadata case in `apps/audit/tests/test_workflow_events.py` and the access matrix are left to CI on the PR, and `reviews/0018` has not yet been applied to a copy of a realistic database.
