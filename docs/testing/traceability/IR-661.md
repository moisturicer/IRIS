## IR-661 · Login and registration have their own rate limits (NFR-S6)

`auth_login` (`API_THROTTLE_LOGIN`, default `300/hour`) and `auth_register`
(`API_THROTTLE_REGISTER`, default `60/hour`) replace the general throttles on those
two endpoints. Each counts per client address through `client_address()` (IR-656),
because DRF's default identification takes a client-supplied `X-Forwarded-For`
whole. The default numbers are a judgement, not a measurement: no load has been
observed on a campus network.

**Evidence:** `backend/apps/accounts/test_auth_throttles.py` drives both real
endpoints: each scope is enforced alone with a 429 and `Retry-After`, neither spends
the anonymous budget, a forged forwarded address buys no budget, clients are counted
apart, and the axes lockout still answers with its own code.
`LoginPage.test.tsx` and `SignupPage.test.tsx`: a rate-limit 429 says how long to
wait, and three of them on the sign-in screen do not open the Account Locked dialog,
which they would otherwise have done with a 15-minute browser lock.
`backend/apps/tests/test_throttle_configuration.py` covers defaults, overrides and
malformed values for both keys.
2026-11-01: these two files, `test_lockout.py` and `test_client_address.py` passed
(72) in the backend container; frontend 1,576 passed. Full-suite and CI evidence is
on the PR.
