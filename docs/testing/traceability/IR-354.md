## IR-354

**Requirement:** **A citation lands on the highlighted passage, clear of the header and the chat** — corrects where IR-335's reader scrolls to

**Design:** [ADR-031](../../adr/031-pdf-citation-overlay.md); Jira IR-354

**Implementation:** `PaperPdfReader.tsx` no longer calls `scrollIntoView` on the page (it put the page's top edge under the 58px header, and a passage low on a page off the screen). `readerGeometry.citationLandingDelta` lands the citation's first region on the page, in reading order (`firstRegionOn`), 48px below the reader's toolbar, giving up that context before letting a tall region run out of view; a citation with no region lands on the page's top edge 16px below the toolbar. At `lg` the window brings the contained pane to rest at `PANE_TOP_PX` and the pane scrolls to the passage; below `lg` the window scrolls, under where the sticky toolbar rests once stuck (`READER_TOOLBAR_TOP_PX`). A refit within 1.5s of landing (a window scrollbar appearing once the pages load) lands again; a later one keeps the reader's place. `paneLayout.ts` gains the two offsets as numbers, and `PAGE_SCROLL_MARGIN` is deleted with the `scrollIntoView` it served. Below `lg` a citation followed while Paper Chat is open minimizes the bottom sheet to a bar (`PaperChatPanel` `minimized`, `MINIMIZED_SHEET_CLASS`); the bar, named "Show Paper Chat", restores the sheet with the transcript at the scroll position it had. **Deviation from the ticket, recorded:** it minimizes below `lg` (1024px), not below 768px, because the sheet is 70vh at every width below `lg` and at 897px it covered a passage landing at 228px

**Test:** `readerGeometry.test.ts` (landing arithmetic: region at 85% and at 2% of a page, a tall region, no region; first region in reading order), `paneLayout.test.ts` (numeric offsets match the Tailwind classes), `PaperPdfReader.test.tsx` (the window scroll target is the region's top, not the page's, clear of header + toolbar; the pane path at `lg`; no region; a repeated navigation lands again; a refit just after landing lands again, one much later does not; the three `scrollIntoView` tests are replaced, deliberately, since the mechanism they asserted is gone), `PaperChatDock.test.tsx` (minimized bar, restore without a second fetch, axe clean), `PaperViewPage.test.tsx` (following a citation from Paper Chat minimizes the sheet below `lg` and not at `lg`; the highlight survives closing and reopening the chat)
