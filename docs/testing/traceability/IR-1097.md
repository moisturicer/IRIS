# IR-1097: One look per kind of highlight: here, selected, reference, match, pen and status

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Epic IR-920 (S2 Epic: Frontend UI/UX Redesign). Audit finding in [`frontend_ui_audit_spec.md`](../../frontend_ui_audit_spec.md) §4.9. Decision D6 (adopt the highlight roles, yellow for matches, the prototype's values) was taken on 2026-11-14 in IR-1072 and is recorded in `DESIGN.md` "Decided changes".

## What changed

- **Tokens** (`frontend/tailwind.config.js`): `hl` (#F7E08F) and `hl-now` (#F2C94C) for matches; `pen` (#B4474B), `pen-wash` (#FBEAEA) and `pen-now` (#F5D5D6) for a reviewer's mark; `brand-press` (#5A0C0F). `gold` and `cream` are removed; the typography plugin's blockquote rule no longer reads gold; the citation flash is maroon, not amber.
- **One place for the roles** (`components/ui/highlightRoles.ts`): `HERE`, `SELECTED`, `SELECTED_RING`, `SELECTED_SEGMENT`, `REFERENCE`, `MATCH`, with the table of which role is which. `statusTones.ts` and `pillStyles.ts` build on it.
- **Here** (Desk Stone fill, Ink text, maroon icon, no stripe): the current sidebar page and Settings tab, a comment you jumped to, the delete hover in the conversation list.
- **Selected** (Ink outline on white, never maroon): filter chips and dropdowns, the Library layout toggle (a white raised segment), Calls and conferences, My Reviews' pressed chips, notification filter, option cards (Publish, Finish review, Sign-up), the reader's `CONTROL_ON`, review-thread filters, the saved-to-collection pill, the model menu, the ghost button's `aria-pressed`.
- **Reference** (maroon text on white, Rule Stone hairline, maroon fill on hover): Ask IRIS source numbers and comparison citations, the source panel's number.
- **Match**: Discover's search words use `bg-hl`; Find in the PDF uses #F7E08F and #F2C94C, multiplied into the page so the words stay Ink on yellow.
- **Pen**: a thread's passage is a rose fill with a 2 px pen underline; the **open** thread's passage (`ReaderMark.open`, from the workspace's `focus`) is `pen-now`; its numbered pin is `pen`.
- **Status**: `active` is white with maroon text and a Rule Stone outline; `settled` is an Ink outline on white (it was solid Ink); only `attention` is filled. Discover's IP tag and the Library's and paper page's chips follow.
- **Press and card hover.** A filled maroon button darkens to `brand-press` while pressed (`Button` primary, `PILL_PRIMARY`). Cards share one hover, a Rule Stone border and a soft lift (`hover:border-stone-300 hover:shadow-card-md`, or the ring equivalent): `ResearchCard`, Related works, Related published research, Paper documents.
- **Notices** (warnings, errors, failed reads) are white with a Rule Stone outline and maroon text; avatars are maroon or stone; progress tracks are stone; the pending-approval card lost its gold top edge.
- **The guard** (`test/palette.ts`, `palette.test.ts`): `retiredTintClasses` bans `brand-50`, `brand-100`, `brand-200`, maroon at 5 or 10 percent (`bg-brand/10` is the same pink by opacity; the AI avatars, the Author tag, the warning callout and the scope chip moved to stone or white), `gold*` and `cream` on any colour utility, and `strayHexes` bans `#fbe4e4`, `#fcfcfb`, `#5a0c0f` and `#fdf2f2`, in shipped source (test files are exempt: they name them as fixtures). **Why `palette.test.ts` changed:** D6 retires Maroon Wash, and the hue-family rule alone could not stop `brand-50` coming back; the new tokens (`hl`, `pen`) were already outside its hue list, so they needed no allowlist.
- `DESIGN.md` and `docs/ui-ux/01-design-system.md` §0 edited as IR-1072 said IR-1097 would: the palette, status tones, reading controls, register tiles, avatar, guard and token rows, with the Maroon Wash, Thread and gold lines and the Four Inks Rule.

## Tests

- `frontend/src/test/palette.test.ts`: the two new matchers' own cases (what they catch, what they let through), and the whole-source guards for retired tints and stray hexes.
- `frontend/src/components/ui/highlightRoles.test.ts` (new): Ink on `hl` and on `hl-now` is at least 4.5:1 (computed from the token values; actual 14:1 and above); the pen underline is at least 3:1 on white; no selection role is maroon; here is a fill and selected an outline; a reference fills only on hover; no other role uses `hl` or `pen`.
- `frontend/src/components/ui/surfaceRoles.test.ts`: the token values; only Attention is filled.
- **Changed deliberately:** `surfaceRoles.test.ts` asserted `TONES.active` contains `ring-brand-200` and `TONES.settled` is `bg-stone-900`; D6 changes both tones, so it now asserts the new looks. `Toast.test.tsx` allowed an opaque `bg-overlay|brand|stone-900` for every toast; success is now opaque white (`bg-surface`), so the pattern admits it. The success toast's dismiss button went from white to stone for the same reason.

## Evidence

- Run locally 2026-11-14: `tsc --noEmit` clean; eslint, no errors (two warnings, neither in a changed line); `npm run test:changed`: 129 files, 1,901 tests passed after the review fixes (the first run had one failure, the Toast pattern above, fixed deliberately). The full suite is left to CI.
- **Not checked:** a browser. Colours were not looked at on `#/discover` or a review with passage marks; the criterion "Ink on the match colour is at least 4.5:1" is checked from the token values, not by eye. A screen reader and the five widths (1440, 1024, 768, 390, 360) are also unchecked.
- **Open questions (recorded, not decided here):** (1) the reader's own highlight (IR-583, `.reader-highlight`) still draws a yellow-brown wash, which D6 says belongs to matches only; the spec does not say what replaces it. (2) A citation's passage on the PDF (`CitationOverlay`) is a maroon ring that flashes a maroon fill for 1.6 s, as a reference, not a match. (4) `mix-blend-mode: multiply` on the Find and pen marks is what keeps the page text visible through an opaque colour, so the colour on screen is a little darker than its hex; the current Find match also gets an Ink 2 px outline the spec does not state. (5) `settled` is an Ink outline like `SELECTED`; the word and icon tell a status from a selection. (6) The CSS repeats the `hl` and `pen` hexes that `tailwind.config.js` owns, since `index.css` does not read the theme. (3) The Library's "Waiting on you" panel and the opportunity urgent row lost their pink fill and are now white with a Rule Stone outline, so their urgency rests on text and icon alone.
