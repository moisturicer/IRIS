## IR-996 — Remove runtime send guards, source screening and the no-training rule

**Basis and scope.** IR-996 under IR-955; authority ADR-049 §Amendment — 2026-11-09 (IR-996), with notes on ADR-015 and ADR-036. Owner decision, 2026-11-09.

**What exists now.**
- `ai/admission/guard.py` is deleted. `AdmittedAgentProvider` reserves, sends and settles; it checks nothing in the request. It still labels a model turn with the sources its history carried (IR-994).
- `ai/agent/tools.py`: no screen; results go to the model as the research tools return them, and a resolve miss keeps its `matches`. `ai/agent/executor.py`: earlier answers, a compacted summary and the Paper Chat note are always sent.
- `ai/research/tools.py`: query embeddings and reranking send without a check; `ResearchTools`, `RunService` and the worker take no guard.
- No no-training requirement: `AGENT_LLM_DATA_POLICY` is optional and a `Pin`'s `data_policy` may be empty; `VoyageContextEmbeddings`, `index_worker` and `ingestion.indexing.space` take or check no policy; Django's `configured_provider_permits` admits any configured model; `inference.startup` no longer refuses a zero-retention task off OpenRouter.
- `RUNS_PROXY_SEND_APPROVAL` and `RUNS_DEPLOYMENT_STAGE` are gone from settings and `ai/.env.example`.

**Kept.** Reader visibility inside the research tools, the spending ledger, the indexing disclosure gate, the context-capacity marker for a result that cannot fit the model's window, and the answer checks on the model's own draft.

**Tests.**
- `ai/tests/test_admitted_transport.py`: paper text is sent unchanged with no approval and no data policy; a result naming no source and an unlabelled earlier answer are sent too.
- `ai/tests/test_agent_profile.py`: the data policy is an optional label (empty, `vendor-default`, `no-training`).
- `backend/apps/ai/tests/test_ai_runtime_admission_db.py`: any paper reaches the provider; an OpenRouter payload carries its routing and the passage whatever its data policy.
- `test_ai_runtime_followups_contract.py`: an earlier answer and a summary are always sent whole. `test_ai_runtime_ir995_contract.py`: a search result reaches the model whole.
- `test_ai_runtime_agent_loop_db.py`, `test_ai_runtime_recovery_db.py`, `test_ir886_hybrid_search_db.py`: the admitted agent, a resumed run and reranking send paper text with no approval.

**Tests removed deliberately** (they asserted the removed behaviour): `ai/tests/test_send_guard.py`; in `test_ai_runtime_admission_db.py` the no-training, withheld-evidence, proxy-approval, changed-proxy, expired-approval and the two "between admission and send" tests; `test_ai_runtime_grant_renewal_db.py::test_a_lapsed_grant_is_refused_at_provider_admission_too` and the send-time half of the revoked-reader test; the withheld follow-up, failed-screen and unsendable-paper-chat contract tests; `test_reranking_sends_nothing_unless_the_send_guard_admits_every_candidate`; the guard-shape asserts in `test_ir886_hybrid_search_contract.py`. `test_agent_profile.py` and `test_ai_runtime_openai_agent_contract.py` no longer expect `AGENT_LLM_DATA_POLICY` to be required or `vendor-default` refused, and `test_index_publication.py` no longer passes a policy.

**Not done here.**
- Django's own send-time `OutboundGuard` (`apps/ai/policy/outbound.py`, the old Ask IRIS engine) is untouched.
- A paper whose embargo, IP or consent changes after indexing is not removed from the index.
