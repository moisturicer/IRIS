# IR-1255: Pages start with their own title

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** a page reached from the sidebar starts with its own title, with no breadcrumb such as "Discover / Calls & Conferences" above it. Every page's title is set in Discover's font and size. Reported by Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-21. No FR- id.

**Design:** this supersedes the breadcrumb part of IR-1085 (audit finding NAV-03), which kept the trail and made its first step read "Discover". The routes are flat, so the trail read "Discover / <page>" almost everywhere. Its one link, Discover, is also in the sidebar. Edit profile, the one page nested under another, has its own Cancel back to Profile.

## What changed

- **No breadcrumb** (`AppShell.tsx`): the shell no longer draws one. `Breadcrumbs.tsx` and its test are deleted.
- **A route names its tab with `title`** (`routeHandle.ts`, `router/index.tsx`): the `crumb` field is renamed to `title`, because nothing draws a crumb any more. `useRouteDocumentTitle` reads `title` alone, so every tab keeps its name.
- **Titles were already Discover's.** Every sidebar page draws its title with `PageHeader`, whose `h1` uses exactly Discover's classes: `font-display font-semibold text-title sm:text-display text-stone-900`. Nothing in them changed; the breadcrumb above them was the difference.
- **Smoke screens** (`e2e/smoke/support/screens.ts`): three screens used the breadcrumb's Discover link as their main control. They now use:
  - Notifications: *Mark all as read*
  - Office console: *Back to My Reviews*
  - Workflow analytics: the *From* date
