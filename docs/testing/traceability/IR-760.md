# IR-760

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**A Call carries a type, event dates, location, fee and Areas, and the old fields are gone.** Built to [ADR-050](../../adr/050-calls-for-papers-and-rdco-confirmation.md) decisions 2-5 and 16, and amendment 1 (Areas are Classifications). Written 2026-11-15.

## What changed

- **Types:** Conference, Congress, Symposium, Workshop, Journal, Book Chapter. Internal Call, Conference Deadline, Funding Window and Institutional Grant are refused.
- **Required:**
  - type, title, about (`description`), paper deadline (`due_date`), location, at least one Area (`areas`, Classification ids) and link (`external_url`, `http://` or `https://` only).
  - On a PATCH too: a save that leaves out a field the Call doesn't have is refused. This is how a migrated Call gets its fields at its next save.
- **Optional:** start date, end date, registration fee (free text, up to 200 characters).
- **Removed:** posting office, audience, funding ceiling, tags, source.
- **No event dates on Journal or Book Chapter.** `Opportunity.save` clears both dates for those types, so changing a Call to one of them clears its dates. The server checks no date (decision 5).
- **Posted by** stays read-only and automatic. The calendar file now names the poster's account ("Posted by: Maria Santos") instead of a typed office.
- **The API read shape** gains `location`, `areas`, `area_names`, `start_date`, `end_date` and `registration_fee`. The personal-data download's `opportunities_posted` follows the same change.
- **Featured** is unchanged: the same ordering and the same people may set it (IR-762 changes both).
- **Audit:** a new `CALL_DELETED` event type (`audit/0028`), shared with IR-763. Its metadata is `reason` and `call`, a copy of the Call. Documented in `docs/evaluation/AUDIT_EXPORT.md`.
- **Migration `opportunities/0002`:**
  - A Conference Deadline becomes a Conference. Its location, Areas and fee stay blank; its title, about, link, deadline, Featured and poster are kept.
  - An Internal Call, Funding Window or Institutional Grant is deleted, each with a `CALL_DELETED` event: no actor, a reason naming the retired type, and a copy of every field.
  - Reversing turns every Call back into a Conference Deadline with a blank posting office. Deleted Calls are not restored; their copies stay in the Audit Log.
- **Posting form, minimal (the owner's choice, 2026-11-15):**
  - The new fields replace the old ones: Type with the six types, Title, About, Paper deadline, Location, Areas as checkboxes, Link, Start date, End date and Registration fee.
  - The redesign is IR-764's: the two tags, the Featured star, the filters, and hiding dates and filling "Online" for Journal and Book Chapter.
  - The board's rows show location and Areas where they showed office, audience, ceiling and tags.
- **`scripts/seed_demo_opportunities.py`** now seeds eight calls for papers: two Conferences, a Congress, a Symposium, a Workshop, two Journals and a Book Chapter.
