## IR-615 every route is declared in the authorization matrix (NFR-S4)

**Design:** IR-611's spec (slice 3), [ADR-009](../../adr/009-authorization-model.md), and the 404-versus-403 convention of [ADR-022 §Amendment 4](../../adr/022-explicit-document-requests.md), including its one recorded exception.

- **Enumeration:** `apps/tests/access/routes.py` walks `get_resolver()` recursively. Nothing is hand-listed. A route is keyed by its app and URL name, and the methods are read from the view.
- **The table:** `apps/tests/access/table.py` declares one outcome per role (anonymous, other user, owner, assigned Adviser, ITSO reviewer seated and unseated, ITSO coordinator, RDCO, role-less superuser) for every records and documents route and method. The other six apps are `PENDING` on IR-616. Django admin and the debug toolbar are declared out of scope, with the reason.
- **A route with no row fails CI**, and so does a row with no route or the wrong methods.
- **Refusals:** a refusal of anyone who cannot see the object must be byte-identical to the response for a missing id. This is checked from `visible_to()`, not from the table. The only waiver is the `authorize_record_documents` exception, and the table cites it by name.
- **Defects found:** IR-627. Three upload and file routes answered 403 for a hidden Record and 404 for a missing id. The table kept the correct outcome under `KnownDefect("IR-627")`; IR-627 fixed the routes and removed the markers.

**Tests:** `apps/tests/test_access_matrix.py`, 16 tests in three classes:
- `RouteDeclarationTests` (7, no database): every route is declared, methods match exactly, the shadowed `api-root` really is unreachable, and the pending markers are live. A dummy route two `include()`s deep in a test URLconf is detected by name.
- `AccessMatrixTests` (3, Postgres): every records and documents cell, fired as all nine roles, each inside a rolled-back savepoint.
- `JudgeTests` (6): the judging rules on synthetic responses.

Run 2026-10-29 in the backend image against the dev Postgres: 16 passed. `apps/tests`, `apps/documents` and `apps/records` together: 521 passed, 13 skipped.

Two mutations were applied and then reverted, to prove the matrix fails:
- Dropping the owner gate on `submit/` failed 5 cells, each naming its role.
- Turning `uploads/<id>/download/`'s 403 into a 404 failed the `KnownDefect` cells ("remove its KnownDefect marker"). It also flagged the Adviser, who can see the Record and should still get 403.

**Status:** IMPLEMENTED, tested. Records and documents only. The other apps wait on IR-616.
