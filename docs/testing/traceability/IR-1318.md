# IR-1318: Upload and submit in the Documents tab

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirement:** an owner answers a reviewer's request in one place: Review's Documents tab. While a revision is asked for, the top of the tab names who asked and what isn't sent yet, with *Submit new version* at its top right. Each file that no version carries yet is marked *not yet submitted*. A document request with no revision is answered by the upload itself, and the tab says so. The Overview keeps the reasons and the list of what's asked for, and sends the owner to Documents. It has no upload or submit control. Requested by Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-24. No FR- id.

**Design:** this supersedes [IR-1046](IR-1046.md), which put the revised-manuscript upload on the Overview's *Revision requested* card, and the Overview placement of IR-273's *Submit new version*. The dialog, the server's checks and clearance-aware resubmission are unchanged ([ADR-032](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) §5). Only where the controls are has moved.

## What changed

- **Backend.** The new-version hint (`apps/reviews/new_version.py`, `new_version_hint`) gains `unsent`: whether the stored manuscript and the details changed since the latest version, and each upload made since it (`id`, slot name). It is read from upload times, the same way `documents_added_during` lists a version's additions, and nothing new is stored. It is sent only to an owner while a revision is open, as the hint already was.
- **Documents tab.** `SubmitNewVersionBar.tsx` has a revision's bar, with *N changes not sent yet: …*, the server's reason it can't be sent yet (which also describes the button), *who reviews vN* and *Submit new version*. It has `DocumentsAskedNote` for a document request alone. `DocumentsTab.tsx` marks a slot's newest upload *Uploaded, not yet submitted* when `unsent` names it and no request item covers that slot. A requested item's own status chip already says where it stands.
- **Overview.** `OverviewSection.tsx`'s card keeps the reasons and gains *Go to Documents*. It loses the manuscript upload and *Submit new version*. `ActionRequiredPanel.tsx` lists what's asked for, each item's status and any refusal reason. It loses each item's upload and gains *Upload in Documents*. `RevisionManuscriptUpload.tsx` and its test are deleted.
- **Page.** `PaperViewPage.tsx` holds `NewVersionDialog` and its status region whatever section is shown, so the Documents tab can open it.
- **My Library.** `waitingOnYou.ts`: *Submit a new version* and *Upload the documents* both open `?section=review&documents=open`.
- **Smoke.** `e2e/smoke`: the *submit a new version* dialog case and the keyboard journey open the Documents tab.
