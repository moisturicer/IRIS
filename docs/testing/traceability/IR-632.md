## IR-632

**Requirement:** **Under `RESTART_ALL` every reset office that had completed reviews the new version again, with the same reviewer** — ADR-004's control arm repeats the review work instead of differing only by a clearance label; thesis-critical; touches FR-M5-03 (stable label only)

**Design:** [ADR-032 §5](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md), 2026-10-29 IR-632 amendment (pending the adviser's review) · [ADR-004](../../adr/004-restart-all-comparison-mode.md) · Jira IR-632

**Implementation:** `apps/reviews/new_version.py`: `_reopened_assignments` finds each reset office's latest assignment that completed and did not ask; `submit_new_version` reactivates it and returns its `done` seats to `in_review` with the same reviewer, in parallel with the requesting party, who are notified by the same `notify_new_version`. An office still reviewing, the Adviser and RDCO are untouched unless they asked. Under `CLEARANCE_AWARE` the reset offices are the requesting parties, so nothing more reopens there. `RESUBMITTED.rereview_parties` and the record's `revision.new_version.rereview` name the reopened offices. `docs/evaluation/AUDIT_EXPORT.md`'s turnaround recipe starts a reopened office's second clock at `RESUBMITTED`

**Test:** `apps/reviews/test_new_version.py` (completed office reopened with the same reviewer and assignment active; Adviser not reopened; RDCO asking reopens the completed office beside it; an office still reviewing keeps its seat; reopened reviewer notified under `RESTART_ALL` only; record still reaches a decision; owner told who reviews again; `CLEARANCE_AWARE` unchanged) · `apps/audit/tests/test_workflow_events.py` (`rereview_parties` includes the reopened office; `MetricsFromTheExportTests::test_a_reopened_office_gets_a_second_clock_under_restart_all`)

**Evidence:** `test_the_policies_differ_only_in_which_clearances_reset` asserted the old behaviour and was **replaced deliberately**: the arms now differ in which offices review again. 2026-10-29, one-off backend container on the compose Postgres: `apps/reviews apps/audit apps/notifications` **412 passed**, 0 failed (a first run lost 3 migration tests to host memory exhaustion, `Errno 12`; they passed alone and on the rerun)

**Status:** **IMPLEMENTED**
