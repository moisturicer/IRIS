# IR-1094: The Review workspace fits the viewport and lays out by its own width

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Epic IR-920 (S2 Epic: Frontend UI/UX Redesign). Audit findings LAY-07, RES-06, WF-07, VIS-09, US-17 and US-18 ([`frontend_ui_audit_spec.md`](../../frontend_ui_audit_spec.md) §4.8, `iris-uiux-audit/redesign-recommendations.md` §17). Thesis-critical: the workspace is where clearance-aware resubmission is read and decided. Follows IR-870, whose UI subtasks (IR-871 to IR-879, IR-997) are Done; only IR-1010, a backend decision, is open.

## What changed

- **Two columns, not three.** The People & details column is gone (`ReviewWorkspaceLayout.tsx`); it repeated the Details tab. Details is a tab at every width, and the pane gets 520px by default (`reviewGeometry.ts`).
- **One tab may open on the right.** From a workspace 1180px wide, a columns button at the end of the tab row (`TabBesideMenu.tsx`) opens Conversation, Threads, Changes or Details in a right column with its own close button and resize edge. The choice is remembered (`iris-review-side-tab`); a narrower workspace puts the tab back in the row. The paper stays on the left, because comments anchor to its passages.
- **Fills the viewport.** `reviewWorkspace.css` pins the workspace's one row to `minmax(0, 1fr)`, so a column's content scrolls inside the column and cannot stretch the row (audit LAY-07).
- **By its own width.** The layout reads the workspace's container width: paper and pane side by side down to 900px, a drawer with *Back to paper* below it. The old 1200px fold is gone, so the open pane never lies over the paper at 1024px (RES-06). The CSS container query and the React breakpoint both read `DRAWER_BELOW_PX`, kept equal by a test.
- **"Your part" footer** (`YourPartFooter.tsx`, `yourPart.ts`) replaces the Readiness card and the pane head's summary: a status icon, one heading, one line and the actions the server grants, one of them filled. Every string is the server's (its headline, a fact's label, the seat's own verdict or state); the browser adds only a count of drafts held. A finished review shows its verdict, not "This record is not in review." (WF-07). The red side stripe is gone from the Readiness card too (VIS-09).
- **The facts behind it** are a Readiness section at the top of Details, with the same links as before (Open review, Withdraw request, Open Documents, Show threads), reached from the footer's *See what it depends on*. Facts that only restate a party's status (`party_reviewing`, `pool_unclaimed`) are not listed: Reviewers says that once.
- **One status per party** (`ReviewDetails.tsx`, US-17): a party with people says where it stands through them, each with their own state; a party with none, or an unclaimed Pool, says it on its own row. Never both.
- **Short version label** (`VersionPicker.tsx`, US-18): the header's compact picker reads "v2 (current)" and is 128px wide, so it is not cut off. The cause and date are in Details and Changes.
- **Tour**: the "People & details" step is gone; the tabs step mentions the columns button only where it is offered; the footer step is "Your part".

## Not changed

- The author's Review pane (`AuthorReviewWorkspace`) keeps its page scroll and Readiness card (without the red stripe). The ticket is the reviewer's workspace.
- The footer has no "⋯" menu for rarer actions (prototype §17): the granted secondary actions stay as pills beside the one filled action. Right-clicking a tab does not open the columns menu.
- Uppercase eyebrows in shared components (`RailHeading`, the Threads filter line) are other audit items; the workspace's own "Review workspace / current record", "Record context" and "Your readiness" eyebrows are gone.
