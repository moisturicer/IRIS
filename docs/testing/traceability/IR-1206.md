## IR-1206: the author's Review uses the reviewers' frame

**Basis and scope.** IR-1206, subtask of IR-1199 (the review-page cleanup), decided with Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-17. The design was approved verbally by Ashlee, as relayed in IR-1199. It changes Paper View's Review section for the paper's owners only. Overview and Paper are unchanged, and it has no FR- id.

**Why.** On Review, owners saw the whole editorial header above the reader: title, status, authors, adviser, origin, "Completed …", lineage, Continue as, the version picker, and Edit details, View, Save, Cite and Share. Reviewers saw only Back, the title and the version picker, and their workspace filled the page.

**What changed.**
- `PaperViewPage.tsx`: `reviewWorkspace` is now every viewer on Review. Before, it was `onReviewTab && !userIsOwner`. Owners get the full-height frame, with the title and version picker in `ReadingHeader` and no editorial header.
- Edit details (while `edit_details` is granted), the "Developed from Proposal" / "Continued as" links and Continue as (`continue_as`) are handed to the Review as `authorDetails`. Overview's header keeps them as before, through the same `lineageLinks`.
- `ReviewWorkspace.tsx`: the separate `AuthorReviewWorkspace` is removed. Everyone renders `ReviewWorkspaceLayout`, which stays the one place the Documents drawer opens from.
- `ReviewWorkspaceLayout.tsx`, author mode (`authorView`):
  - The tabs are **Readiness · Conversation · Threads · Details**, opening on Readiness. Reviewers keep Conversation · Threads · Changes · Details.
  - Readiness holds the status badge, the Documents button (until IR-1203) and the Readiness panel.
  - Details shows `authorDetails` above the same `ReviewDetails` reviewers see, without its Readiness section. There is no Compare link.
  - The guided tour is not offered, because it walks a reviewer's tabs.
  - The foot holds the author's granted review actions (`ReviewActionBar`) and is absent when none is granted. The reviewers' *Your part* footer is not shown.
- The author's sticky action bar over the reader is gone, and with it the reader's bottom inset for Review (IR-477). The bar now sits in the pane, beside the reader or in its drawer.
- `e2e/smoke/support/screens.ts`: a viewport-matrix row, `paper-view-author-review`, opens an owner's in-review paper on Review.

**Tests changed on purpose.**
- `ReviewWorkspace.test.tsx` tested IR-477's bottom inset, which no longer exists. It now tests that the author's actions sit in the pane's foot, that the foot is absent without an action, and that there is no *Your part* or tour.
- Two `PaperViewPage.test.tsx` owner tests ("gives an owner the timeline and no action bar", "gives the review discussion's composer only where the server offers comment_review") open the Conversation tab first, because an owner's Review now opens on Readiness.

**Tests added.** `PaperViewPage.test.tsx`, "the author's Review uses the reviewers' frame (IR-1206)", rendered as the owner unless stated:
- only Back and the title above the reader (one level-1 heading; no Authors, Adviser, "Completed 2026", View, Cite, Share or Save);
- the tab order, with Readiness selected and the status at its top;
- Edit details, the lineage link and Continue as in Details, with no Readiness section there;
- none of those without the capabilities;
- a reviewer keeps their four tabs and gets no Edit details;
- Overview's header is unchanged;
- arrow keys, Home and End move between the author's tabs;
- at 320 px the paper comes first, the pane opens from *Open review pane* on Readiness, and axe finds no serious or critical violation.
