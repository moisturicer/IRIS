## IR-1194 — Conversation comments are discussion; a reviewer posts a thread now or saves it as a draft

**Basis and scope.** IR-1194; authority ADR-032 §7, amendment 2026-11-16 (accepted by the project lead on behalf of the team, recorded in the ADR). Built on PR #852 (IR-1191/IR-1192). Found by hand: on `main`, a seated reviewer's comment typed in Conversation became an unsent draft shown only in Threads.

**What changed, backend.**
- `ReviewComment.kind` (`discussion` | `thread`, default `thread`; `core.enums.ReviewCommentKind`), migration `reviews/0031`. `reviews/0032` makes a top-level comment discussion when it has no passage, is not *Must fix*, has no round, no answer, no event and is not a draft, clears its severity, and gives replies their parent's kind. Its reverse makes every row a thread again but leaves those severities blank.
- `comments.post_comment` takes `kind` and `post_now`. A discussion posts at once for any participant, seated or not, and refuses severity, passage and wording (400). A reply takes its parent's kind. An owner's thread with no passage is refused (400), with a pointer to Conversation. A seated reviewer's thread is a draft unless `post_now`; a reviewer's thread that posts at once (`post_now`, or a former reviewer's) tells the owners in-app (`notify_thread_posted`, type "Review Thread Posted").
- `POST /records/<id>/review-comments/<id>/post/` (`comments.post_draft`): the writer posts one draft on its own. It is dated now, joins no round, tells the people it mentions and the owners. Someone else's draft is a 404; a sent one is a 400.
- Every thread rule reads `threads.all_threads` (kind `thread` only): Readiness, blocks, Decisions closing threads, Finish review's drafts, following passages, response to reviewers and version comparison. The thread acts on a discussion are a 404.
- The payload adds `kind` and `can_post`; a discussion carries the thread fields empty and its writer's label ("Author", "ITSO"...). A review round's payload adds `must_fix`.
- New capability `open_general_thread`: participants who are not owners, where `comment_review` is offered.
- **Not done:** posting a thread outside a round writes no audit event (an owner's thread never did either).

**What changed, frontend.**
- Conversation (`ReviewTimeline.tsx`, `timelineEntries.ts`): shows discussion and its replies only. A finished review is one line ("Reviewed v2: 2 threads, 1 Must fix"; a verdict's own review gets "Sent 2 threads"), and a thread sent outside a review is one line ("Commented on p.18", with a *Must fix* tag), each with *View in Threads*. New comments post as discussion. The "Added to your drafts" note is gone. Edit moved into a ⋯ menu on one's own comments and replies; replies show their writer's label.
- Threads (`ReviewThreads.tsx`, `threadModel.ts`): discussion is left out. A seated reviewer's composer has **Post** (primary, and Enter) and **Save as draft**. Each own draft offers **Post** where `can_post`. *New comment* shows only under `open_general_thread`; an owner is told a remark on the whole paper goes in Conversation.
- Footer (`ReviewFooterActions.tsx`): **Send N comments…** while drafts wait, opening *Finish review* on *Comment only*.
- The Conversation tab's count counts discussion only.

**Tests changed on purpose.** Owners' general comments in `test_review_comments.py` and `test_mentions.py` now post as discussion (`discuss`/`discussed`), and owners' threads in `test_review_threads.py` are on a passage (`owner_thread`), because the server now refuses an owner's general thread. `test_capabilities.py` expects `open_general_thread` for every non-owner participant and probes it. Frontend fixtures for Conversation carry `kind: "discussion"`; "Add to review" assertions read "Save as draft" or "Post".

**Tests added.**
- Backend, `test_discussion.py`: a seated reviewer's discussion is seen at once; a discussion refuses thread fields and an unknown kind; an owner's general thread is refused; a reply takes its parent's kind; no thread act reaches a discussion; a Decision does not close it; Readiness and *Finish review* ignore it; *Post* sends with no round and tells the owner ("ITSO posted a Must fix on p. 3 of …"); a draft without it stays a draft; an owner's thread tells nobody; posting one draft leaves the other for *Finish review*, tells the people it mentions, and is the writer's alone and only once; a round counts its threads and *Must fix*. `test_review_threads_migration.py`: `0032` sorts ten kinds of row and rewrites nothing else.
- Frontend: `timelineEntries.test.ts`, `ReviewTimeline.test.tsx` (thread lines and links, no thread text, ⋯ Edit on comments and replies), `ReviewThreads.test.tsx` (Post, Save as draft, Post on a draft, *New comment* gating), `threadModel.test.ts`, `ReviewFooterActions.test.tsx` (Send N comments).
