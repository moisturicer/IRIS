## IR-270

**Requirement:** **The Adviser accepts & publishes or rejects a Thesis/Project; RDCO decides specialist-path records** — touches FR-M5-01 (stable label only)

**Design:** [ADR-032 §3, §10, §11](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md), amended 2026-10-09 (IR-270) · ADR-021 §12 · ADR-022 §3 · design settled with the project lead 2026-10-09 (grilling; 22 decisions, Jira comment 10586) · `CONTEXT.md` *Decision*, *Unlisted*, *Archived* · Jira IR-270

**Implementation:** `apps/reviews/decisions.py`: `decide` lets the record's Adviser holding their open seat *publish* or *reject*, and RDCO's seat holder also *keep unlisted*, on a new-model Thesis/Research or Project. It writes the decider's `approved`/`rejected` Review against the latest version, completes their seat and assignment, withdraws every other active assignment, its open seats and every open `DocumentRequest` (each naming the decision in the new `closed_by_decision` on assignments, seats and requests: `reviews/0012`, `reviews/0013` and `documents/0011`), leaves withdrawn rounds' clearances as they were, and writes `published`/`completed`/`rejected` directly (not through `lifecycle.apply()`). It locks the record and every active assignment; `create_request` now takes the record lock and refuses a party whose turn closed meanwhile. `workflow_state_label` reads *Archived* for `rejected` and *Unlisted* for a Thesis/Project at `completed`, and the frontend's status label reads *Archived* too. Refuses 404 / 403 (no deciding seat: the routed Adviser, an unseated RDCO member, offices, owners) / 400 (Proposal, legacy pipeline, unknown or disallowed outcome, reject with no reason, unopened seat, open revision request, missing token) / 409 (stale token, with the fresh `decision` block). `POST /records/<id>/decide/`; record detail's `decision` (outcomes, blocked, closes, token, author hints). Tracker rows gain `withdrawn_by_decision` and list a decision-withdrawn assignment's seats; the RDCO row reads *Not required* on an Adviser-published record. Owners and the reviewers cut off are notified. `seed_demo` adds a specialist-path Thesis in RDCO's pool through the real acts. Frontend: *Accept & publish…* (`accept_publish`, the primary), *Keep unlisted…* (`keep_unlisted`, renamed from `final_decide`) and *Reject…* (`reject`, last) in F6's action bar; one `DecisionDialog` states the consequence, the author's flags (the Adviser's "Publish without specialist review?") and what it closes; the typed text survives closing the dialog and a 409 (IR-143's carried criteria)

**Test:** `apps/reviews/test_decisions.py` (REST seam, one class per criterion plus the grilled decisions, including IR-268's *Published* filter hand-off and the predicate-level Discover/Ask IRIS visibility) · `apps/records/test_seed_demo.py` · `paper-view/DecisionDialog.test.tsx` · `paper-view/ReviewActionBar.test.tsx` · `features/records/capabilities.test.ts`
