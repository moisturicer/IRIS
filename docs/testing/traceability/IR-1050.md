## IR-1050

**Requirement:** **Chunker consolidation (part A): the production strategy under the contract tests, and a target size with a safety ceiling** — subtask of IR-980; no FR- id

**Design:** [ADR-067](../../adr/067-unstructured-replaces-llamaparse.md) decisions 11, 12 and 14 (amends [ADR-013](../../adr/013-chunk-level-rag-pipeline.md)'s 2026-09-21 amendment) · Jira IR-1050

**Implementation:**
- `labeled-structure-v1` is registered; `ingestion/pipeline.py` selects every strategy through `build_chunker`.
- `chunking/outline.py` (heading stack, trail and its budget, section key) and `chunking/assembly.py` (drafts, short-chunk merging, heading fold, final assembly) are shared by all three strategies. The post-packing `ContextPathChunker` is deleted: each strategy counts its trail inside the target.
- `ChunkingOptions.target_tokens` (`AI_CHUNK_TARGET_TOKENS`, blank = 700) and `max_tokens` (`AI_CHUNK_MAX_TOKENS`, default 2,000). `finalize` raises if any chunk passes the ceiling. Labeled keeps a figure, list or one-row table whole up to the ceiling and splits it past it; structural lets a repeated table header carry a fragment past the target, never the ceiling.
- Labeled merges short chunks within a section and folds heading-only chunks forward, like structural.
- `overlap_tokens` and `repeat_table_header` are deleted; stored options without `target_tokens` read back with `max_tokens` as the target, and `context_path_max_tokens` defaults to 64.
- `numbering.py` moved to `apps/ai/extraction/`. `formulas.py` stays in `chunking/`: labeled calls it, and importing `apps.ai.extraction` from the chunker would be circular.
- `Chunk.section_key` and `DocumentChunk.section_key` (migration `ai/0046`, nullable JSON): the full heading path of the chunk's section, not hashed. An unchanged re-chunk fills missing keys and embeds nothing.

**Test:** `apps/ai/chunking/tests/test_consolidation.py` (labeled registered and chosen through the registry; property: no embedded text past the ceiling, every strategy; property: prose with its trail within the target, every strategy; property: each structural/labeled chunk holds one section's content under its key; full key when the trail is cut; key not hashed; labeled folds and merges; figure whole up to the ceiling, split past it; defaults; deleted options; no private import in `strategies/`) · `apps/ai/tests/test_section_key_persistence.py` (keys round-trip; an unchanged re-chunk fills null keys with nothing to embed; old options read back) · contract and property suites in `test_chunker_core.py` now include labeled

**Evidence:** 2026-11-13, worktree, local Postgres: the two new files, 22 passed. Also run because the change deletes a module they import: `apps/ai/chunking`, `extraction/tests/test_numbering.py` and `ingestion/tests/test_pipeline.py` all passed with 2 skips; `apps/ai/ingestion`, `apps/ai/extraction/tests`, the chunk repository contract and Django tests and `test_incremental_rechunk.py` passed after one fix. The full backend suite was not run. Existing tests changed: structural cascade tests run with no trail, two labeled-only display-hash cases in `test_regions.py` are skipped (labeled hashes display and evidence by design), and size-sensitive tests got sizes that leave room for the trail.
