## IR-643 — Resend account verification email

**Basis and scope.** [ADR-045](../../adr/045-account-recovery-contract.md), accepted 2026-10-30 (IR-641), is the contract; this is its second journey, beside IR-642's password reset. No FR- id. Three choices were made with Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-07, none of which changes the ADR: (1) the backend and the screens ship together in this ticket; (2) the retired activation links are handled here, which is the work [IR-937](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-937) tracked, because ADR-045 §8 retires them in the same rollout; (3) registration's email goes through the same limits as a resend, as ADR-045 §5–§6 say, so an address that has used up its allowance gets its registration email late.

**What exists now.**
- **Reused from IR-642, unchanged:** `RecoveryIntent`, `RecoveryToken`, `RecoveryGeneration`, the shared counters (`recovery/limits.py`), encryption, the worker and dispatcher (`recovery/delivery.py`), the audit helper and every `RECOVERY_*` setting. No migration: the schema already carried the `email_verification` purpose.
- `POST /api/v1/auth/verification/resend/` (`VerificationResendView`, name `verification-resend`): `{"email"}`, always the generic 202, its own IP counters (`verification_request`, 10/10 min and 50/day). Counts and admits without looking the address up; the worker decides who is sent mail.
- `POST /api/v1/auth/verification/confirm/` (`VerificationConfirmView`, name `verification-confirm`): `{"token"}`, 200 `email_verified` or the one `invalid_link` 400, its own IP counter (`verification_confirm`, 30/10 min). GET and HEAD are 405 and change nothing.
- `recovery/service.py::confirm_verification`: locks user then token (the shared `_locked_link`, which the reset now uses too), checks purpose, digest, expiry, email binding and eligibility under lock, then sets `is_verified`, consumes the token and writes `EMAIL_VERIFICATION_COMPLETED` in one transaction. A rejection is audited and that row commits. RDCO's role-request notice (IR-784) is sent here on the first verification, in the same transaction; a refused or repeated link sends none.
- `purpose_applies`: a verified account is sent no verification email, and its existing verification links are refused (ADR-045 §1). Shared by issuance, sending and consumption.
- The verification email (`delivery._compose`): subject "Verify your IRIS email address", one link `{FRONTEND_URL}/verify-email#token=…`, its exact UTC expiry (24 hours), "If you did not request this, ignore this email" and the RDCO/administrator contact line. No role, approval or restriction is mentioned.
- **Registration** (`RegisterView.perform_create`) admits an `email_verification` intent in the same transaction as the account. A rollback leaves no intent and sends nothing. Its email is mailed by the worker after commit, under the shared recipient limits and 60-second cooldown.
- **Retired:** `GET /auth/activate/<uid>/<token>/` and `POST /auth/activate/` now answer only `invalid_link` (400, no-store) and read nothing. `send_verification_email` and `activate_user`, Django's default-generator verification, are deleted. The URL names stay so a cached page or an old inbox link gets the guidance rather than a 404. Log redaction for the old path shape is unchanged.
- **Screens.** `VerifyEmailPage` (`/verify-email`, and the retired `/activate` and `/activate/:uidb64/:token`): reads `#token=` once, replaces the address with `/verify-email` at once, sets no-referrer, and verifies only when "Verify email" is pressed. Every unusable link, and every old activation link, gets the same guidance and a "Resend verification email" link. `ResendVerificationPage` (`/verify-email/resend`) uses `EmailRequestForm`, the form `ForgotPasswordPage` now uses too (moved out of it unchanged). An address can arrive in router state from sign-in or the sign-up confirmation, never in a URL. Sign-in's "Email not verified" alert and the sign-up confirmation link to it; "register again" is no longer offered.

**Tests.**
- `backend/apps/accounts/test_email_verification.py` (37 tests): register → worker → link → POST → sign-in; resend → verify without registering again; email content and 24-hour lifetime; GET/HEAD change nothing; no-store and no-referrer. Verification changes `is_verified` alone, returns no credentials, leaves the role request pending, and notifies RDCO once. Identical 202 for unverified, verified, locked, inactive and unknown addresses, with mail only for the first; no account lookup on the request path. Single use, the exact 24-hour boundary, a newer email retiring the older link, malformed and unknown tokens, a verification link refused by reset confirm and a reset link refused here, lock/deactivation/verification-elsewhere/email-change after issuance, a password change leaving the link working. Cooldown, registration sharing it, reset and resend sharing it, 5 an hour, the 10/10-min IP limit with Retry-After, separate IP counters per purpose, the 30/10-min confirm limit. A registration rolling back after admission leaves no intent or mail; enqueue only after commit; a failed send retried with the same token. Completion audited with allowlisted metadata; no address or token in any audit row. On real Postgres connections: two posts of one link have one winner and one RDCO notice, and a new link's issuance racing the old one's use leaves one consistent outcome.
- `backend/apps/accounts/test_activation_retired.py`: genuinely valid old-scheme links, in both shapes, verify no one and notify no one; any body gets the same answer; the redaction backstop.
- `backend/apps/accounts/test_role_request_notification.py`: now follows real single-use links.
- `backend/apps/tests/access/table.py` declares both new routes public; `test_notice_block_every_route.py` lists them, and the retired GET, as authenticating no one.
- `frontend/src/features/auth/VerifyEmailPage.test.tsx`, `ResendVerificationPage.test.tsx`, plus `LoginPage`, `SignupPage`, `entryHeadings`, `entryTokens` and `api/auth` tests; `ForgotPasswordPage.test.tsx` passes unchanged on the shared form.
- `e2e/`: the smoke catalogue draws `/verify-email`, `/verify-email/resend` and both retired shapes; `verify()` and the account-lifecycle journey follow the new link and press the button.
