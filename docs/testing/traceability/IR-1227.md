## IR-1227

**Requirement:** **An unsubmitted draft is its creator's alone, and opening it opens the Publish form.** [ADR-009](../../adr/009-authorization-model.md) decision 6, *Amendment, 2026-11-19 (IR-1227)*, decided by Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-18 and restated on 2026-11-19. It is proposed in this PR, is accepted when it merges, and needs Ashlee Sean's security review. It also amends [ADR-051](../../adr/051-system-admin-account-deletion-and-consent.md) decision 9 (a person's drafts on account deletion) and [ADR-032](../../adr/032-adviser-first-review-and-office-reviewer-pools.md) §10's IR-517 amendment (who is told when a draft is deleted). IR-1228 was closed as its duplicate; its points (no admin escape hatch in the app, Django's `/admin/` out of scope, a record sent back for revision unaffected) are in the amendment. No FR- id (NFR-S4).

Decided with Hermoine on 2026-11-19, while building:
- **The creator alone, not every owner.** The card said "a draft has exactly one owner", but co-owners are named before submission: the Publish form's owner emails, and a continuation's copied Proposal owners. Only the account that created the draft (`Record.added_by`) reads it.
- **The Audit Log is left as it is.** It still lists a draft's events with its title for the System Admin.

**Implementation:**
- **Reading** (`apps/records/models.py`): `Record.objects.visible_to` gives a draft to `added_by` alone. Ownership, office staff, the named Adviser and the public catalogue hold only for a submitted record.
- **Own lists** (`Record.objects.owned_by`): a person's records minus drafts someone else created. Used by My submissions (`records/mine/`), the dashboard's counts and the unrouted `MyRecordsViewSet`.
- **Documents and embargo** (`core/permissions.py`): `hides_draft(user, record)` is true for a draft the user did not create. `owns_or_staffs_record`, `may_read_record_documents` and `may_read_embargo` refuse it. `authorize_record_documents` (`apps/documents/views.py`) answers it with the same 404 as a missing record, `{"detail": "Record not found."}`, while another person's submitted record keeps its 403.
- **Queueing a record for indexing** (`apps/ai/views/embedding.py`, office only): a record the caller cannot read is a 404, as a missing one, and nothing is queued.
- **The AI runtime's copy** (`apps/ai/migrations/0049_research_reads_hide_drafts.py`): replaces `iris_authz.research_record_ids()` with the same condition. It is built from 0037's SQL with only the readable clause swapped, and reverses to 0037's body. It reads `added_by_id`, which `provision_research_roles` now grants the verifier.
- **Deletion:** deleting a draft tells nobody (`RecordViewSet.perform_destroy`). Deleting an account deletes the drafts it created, co-owned ones included (`accounts.deletion.service._settle_papers`).
- **Duplicates:** *resume your draft* is offered to the creator alone (`records.duplicates`).
- **Presentation** (`frontend/src/features/records/paper-view/PaperViewPage.tsx`): a draft that loads, only ever its creator's, replaces the address with `/?publish=<id>`, so an old `?section=review` link lands there too. The page's *Continue* action is removed, since no draft reaches it.
- **Closing Publish** (`frontend/src/features/publish/PublishDialog.tsx`): one `closing` question at a time:
  - while uploading, the existing *Stop the upload and close?*;
  - with a draft, *Leave this submission?*: **Keep as draft** (focused), **Delete draft** and **Keep editing**. *Delete draft* asks *Delete this draft? This can't be undone.* and calls `DELETE /records/<id>/`; a failure stays on screen;
  - with a type chosen and no manuscript, *Discard what you've entered?*: **Keep editing** (focused) and **Discard**. A type is the only thing entered before the draft exists: the upload is disabled until a type is chosen;
  - with nothing entered, or outside the form, it closes.

**Dependents checked:**
- Every list already leaves drafts out: Discover, the review queues and the office consoles.
- Notifications reach the Adviser and the offices only at Submit or later, and comments and mentions refuse a draft.
- `seed_demo`'s draft is opened only by its creator.
- The manuscript duplicate check reads through `visible_to`, so it no longer reports someone else's draft to an Adviser, an office account or a co-owner.

**Tests:**
- `apps/records/test_draft_visibility.py`, at the HTTP routes, on a draft that names a co-owner:
  - Who gets 404: a co-owner, the named Adviser, ITSO, IERC, KTTO, RDCO, the System Admin and another student.
  - Where: the draft's detail, tracker, manuscript, similar, document requests and review comments, and its slots, uploads, files and download-all. Each answers exactly as for a missing record, body included.
  - RDCO queueing it for indexing gets the same 404 as for a missing id, and no job is created.
  - The creator still reads it and is offered `continue_draft` and `delete_record`.
  - My submissions and the dashboard's count include it for the creator alone, and deleting it notifies nobody.
  - Once submitted, its Adviser, RDCO, ITSO and the co-owner read it and its files, and another student's 403 on its files is unchanged.
- `apps/records/test_capabilities.py`, changed deliberately: the `draft` row gives the Adviser and every office `None` (a 404) where it gave `cite` and the office set.
- `apps/ai/tests/test_research_reads.py`: in database mode, neither a co-owner, the Adviser nor an office reads the draft, and both modes still read the same population as `visible_to`, the co-owner included. `apps/ai/tests/corpus.py`'s `make_record` now records its owner as the creator, as Publish does.
- `apps/ai/tests/test_research_read_policy_db.py`, changed deliberately: the revoked-grounds test showed the Adviser reading a draft. It now shows the Adviser refused on the draft, reading it once it is in review, and losing it when the ground is revoked.
- `apps/accounts/test_account_deletion.py`, changed deliberately: a co-owned draft the person created is now deleted with the account, where it used to stay, and a draft someone else created stays.
- `apps/tests` (access matrix, enum vocabulary, traceability shape) passed unchanged. Its routes are checked on submitted records.

**Fixed after the first CI run** (all four backend shards failed, 111 cases):
- **Fixtures that made a record with no creator.** About 25 test files built a draft with `Record.objects.create` and no `added_by`, so after this change even its owner got a 404. Each now records its owner as the creator, as Publish does. No assertion changed.
- **Old-rule assertions, changed deliberately.** Each asserted that someone other than the creator reaches a draft:
  - `apps/records/tests.py`: `test_assigned_adviser_cannot_retrieve_the_draft` and `test_office_staff_cannot_retrieve_a_draft` (reversed from "can"), and RDCO submitting someone's draft is a 404, not a 403.
  - Offices and the Adviser acting on a draft now get the 404 of a missing record, not a 400 or 403: `test_edit_policy`, `test_delete_policy`, `test_rdco_acts` (replace Adviser, add owner, withdraw), `test_ip_release`, `test_embargo_acts`, `test_research_metadata`, `reviews/test_decisions`.
  - `test_record_document_authorization`: a stranger on the draft fixture gets 404, not 403. The Adviser subclass and the office test now use a record in review, and a new test shows RDCO gets 404 on every document route of a draft. `test_document_access_control` does the same for the Adviser.
  - `test_contact_person`: tests where a co-owner who did not create the paper acts now use a paper in review, and a new test shows that co-owner gets 404 on a draft. `test_edit_policy`'s co-owner tests make the co-owner the creator, as a continuation does.
  - `test_delete_notifications`: deleting a co-owned draft now tells nobody. A new test shows a rejected record's co-owner is still told.
  - `test_manuscript_duplicates`: a co-owned draft someone else created blocks no upload, and it does once submitted.
  - The AI retrieval and runtime tests (`test_visibility`, `test_fusion`, `test_ai_runtime_revocation_db`): the Adviser and an office receive a submitted record and never a draft.
  - Office filing tests (`test_supplementary_file_types`, `test_accepted_source`'s general route) file on a record in review.
- **`apps/ai/views/embedding.py`** checks through `grants.reads.research_records`, the one AI entry point `test_one_retrieval_stack` requires, instead of calling `visible_to` directly.

**Frontend:**
- `PaperViewPage.test.tsx`: the creator opening a draft, by its address or by `?section=review`, lands on `/?publish=<id>` with no paper heading or tabs. A submitted record opens its page as before. The three Edit details tests that used a draft now use a revision-requested record, the other state that offers it (changed deliberately), and the *Continue* test is replaced.
- `PublishDialog.test.tsx` (closing): the four cases above. Each test checks the default focus, that *Keep as draft* deletes nothing, that deletion waits for its confirmation, that *Keep draft* goes back, that a failed deletion stays, that *Discard* saves and deletes nothing, and that *Keep editing* returns to the form.
