# IR-878: RDCO publishes an Unlisted paper later, once its embargo is released

**Requirements:** none with an FR- id. The rule is [ADR-047](../../adr/047-rdco-routes-and-decides-at-any-time.md) §10 (2026-11-04, corrected for IR-882) and [ADR-052](../../adr/052-each-paper-states-its-embargo-and-a-person-ends-it.md) decision 14 as amended. The design decisions from IR-878's session are recorded in ADR-047 §10's *Built by IR-878* note.

**Jira and the ADR differ, and the ADR wins.** The card's acceptance criteria say publishing needs the embargo to be "None or Released", and that "an Active or Unknown embargo is refused". IR-882 removed None and Unknown (ADR-052, IR-879 amendment), and ADR-047 §10's correction reads that as **Released** only. Built to the ADR: every embargo that isn't Released is refused. The card's summary, description and acceptance criteria were corrected to say Released on 2026-11-07. The card's "RDCO's Decision on an embargoed Record offers no `accept_publish`" and "the warning-only path is removed" were already built by IR-882 (`decisions.outcomes_for`), and are covered by its tests, not new ones.

What changed:

- **`POST /records/<id>/publish-unlisted/`** (`apps/records/unlisted.py`). `IsRDCO` before the lookup, then the per-record lock. Works only on a `completed` Thesis/Research or Project whose embargo is Released, with an optional note (1,000 characters at most). It writes an `UnlistedPublication`, sets `published`, writes one audit event and tells the owners and the Adviser after commit. No `Review`, Version or Clearance is written, and nothing reopens.
- **`records.UnlistedPublication`** (migration `records/0034`): who, when and the note, one per paper.
- **The history stays true.** `decisions.withdrawn_by_label` (the tracker's "RDCO … the record") and My Reviews' outcome (`my_reviews._outcome` and `_outcome_q`) used to read *published* from the status. A paper published later now keeps its Decision as *kept unlisted* / *Accepted*. The tracker gains `published_later` (`label`, `published_at`, and `published_by_name` and `note`, which are `null` to a viewer who may not read the review).
- **Audit:** `PUBLISHED` with `via: "publish_unlisted"`, and no `DECISION_RECORDED` (`docs/evaluation/AUDIT_EXPORT.md`).
- **Record detail:** the capability `publish_unlisted` for any RDCO member on an Unlisted paper, and `publish_unlisted: {blocked}`, the embargo's reason while it holds.
- **Paper View:** an *Unlisted* card in Record details, beside the Embargo card, with *Publish to Discover*, disabled while the embargo holds, and its confirmation dialog with the note. A timeline entry, "Published it to Discover from Unlisted". The Decision dialog's *Keep unlisted* copy now says RDCO can publish it later, once released, instead of "Publishing it later is not yet possible in IRIS".
