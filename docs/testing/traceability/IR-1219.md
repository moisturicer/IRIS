## IR-1219

**Requirement:** **Every screen's text fills its box, reflows when the window changes, and is never cut off or hidden.** Requested by Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-17, clarified with Ashlee Sean the same day. It applies `DESIGN.md`'s Full Text Rule, which IR-1218 wrote. No ADR: it is a visual rule. No FR- id.

**Decided by Hermoine Zozobrado, 2026-11-19.** Each option was shown as a sketch first.
1. **A long review comment keeps See more.** Its first 300 characters end in `…`, and See more shows the whole comment in place.
2. **A call's description on the Calls board keeps its two-line clamp, with a new Show more.** The board had no other way to the rest: *View & apply* leaves IRIS, and not every call has it.
3. **Discover's rotating example search stays on one line.** It is decorative and hidden from screen readers.
4. **Ask IRIS is included,** although its redesign (IR-1229) is in progress.

So `DESIGN.md` now names **five allowed cuts**: the two IR-1218 named, plus these three (decisions 1 to 3). CODING_STANDARDS F2 names the same five.

### Changed

**Cuts removed.** Each now wraps with `break-words`, inside a `min-w-0` box where it sits in a row:
- **The account menu:** the name and role in the sidebar, and the name and email in the open menu (`AccountMenu`). On `main` a long name was clipped on every signed-in screen.
- **The sidebar:** each nav label, and the "Research-to-IP Platform" line (`Sidebar`).
- **Paper titles:**
  - a grid card's title (`ResearchCard`) and the Related works titles (`RelatedWorks`);
  - My Library's titles (`LibraryOverview`, `LibraryRecordTable`);
  - the Delete and Download Requests tables' titles. Their cells also lose the 200 px and 180 px caps, and their reasons are justified;
  - the Cite dialog's title (`PaperCiteModal`), the reading header's title and tabs (`ReadingHeader`), and the title in Notes and in Paper Chat (`NotesPanel`, `PaperChatDock`).
- **Names and people:**
  - bylines (`ResearchCard`, `SourceContextPanel`);
  - My Library's lines of people and dates (`LibraryRecordTable`, `SubmissionRow`, `LibraryOverview`);
  - affiliations on Discover's people results, and a person's papers on their profile (`DiscoverPeopleResults`, `PersonProfileView`).
- **Menus and lists:**
  - the filter dropdown's value and options (`DiscoverFilterDropdown`);
  - the Save dropdown's options and folder names (`PaperSaveDropdown`), and folder names in My Library (`LibraryOverview`);
  - the Changes tab's section names (`ChangesTab`) and Paper Chat's recent chats (`RecentChats`).
- **Ask IRIS (decision 4):**
  - the chat's title and its "Ask IRIS" label (`ChatToolbar`), and each conversation's title (`ConversationSidebar`);
  - in the source panel, the section, quote, title and byline (`SourceContextPanel`);
  - a cited passage's quote (`PassageQuote`).
- **Other text:**
  - the Publish form's suggested values and their source excerpts (`PrefillChip`);
  - an upload's file name, now beside a percentage that keeps its own width (`UploadDropzone`);
  - a document's name (`PaperDocuments`);
  - RDCO's note beside a download, which loses its 20rem cap and its `title`-only full text (`PaperDownloadControl`);
  - the unavailable-overview note (`OverviewNote`).
- **The reader's watermark footer** (`ReaderWatermark`). It names who opened the paper, so it wraps rather than cutting a long email. The tilted stamp drawn on the page is unchanged.

**`whitespace-nowrap` removed** where the text can be long:
- the Tooltip, which shows a person's full name. It now wraps inside its own box, at most 18rem or the screen less 5rem;
- a download grant's line;
- the Download Requests note cell's 20rem cap.

**Width caps removed** where text sat in a wider box:
- the sign-in pages' headline and sentence (`EntryFrame`);
- page descriptions (`PageHeader`), empty-state messages (`EmptyState`) and the Notifications messages (75ch);
- RDCO's note in its dialog, and the Calls board and My Calls descriptions (`max-w-prose`);
- a profile's bio, and the Sign-up confirmation;
- Publish's success message;
- Workflow analytics' two explanations;
- the Ask IRIS hub's two paragraphs and the empty chat's line (`AIHubPage`, `ChatMessageList`).

My Library's folder picker can now take its whole row (`max-w-full`, was 12rem). A native `<select>` still cuts a name longer than the row; the browser draws it, and no class changes that.

**The Calls board's Show more** (`CallDetails`, decision 2). The clamp is measured, again whenever the card's width changes (`ResizeObserver`). Show more appears only when the description runs past two lines. It has `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and shows the description whole and justified, becoming Show less. Screen readers always read the whole description, because the clamp is visual only.

### Kept, and why

- **Allowed cuts (10 matches):**
  - an abstract's preview in a list card that links to the paper: `ResearchCard` (2) and `DiscoverResultCard`;
  - a call's description on the board, with Show more: `CallDetails`;
  - Discover's rotating example: `DiscoverSearchComposer`;
  - the other 6 are a review comment's See more, which cuts in code (`truncateBody` in `commentThreads.ts` and `CommentItem.tsx`), not with a class.
- **`whitespace-nowrap` (18), all short and fixed:**
  - table column headers (`DataTable`), a card's date (`ResearchCard` meta) and the origin badge (`ResearchOriginBadge`);
  - dates in tables (Users' "Last active", the Delete and Download Requests dates, the routing tracker's dates) and the time in Notes;
  - status chips (`LibraryRecordTable`, `ReviewDetails`, and the server-worded call tags in `CallTags`);
  - tab and filter names (`MyLibraryPage`, `CallsAndConferencesPage`, Settings' sections);
  - "Take a tour" and "Back to paper" (`ReviewWorkspaceLayout`), and a thread's `p. 3 · v2` place (`ReviewThreads`).
- **`max-w-*` (49), all boxes rather than caps on text:**
  - dialogs and sheets (`Modal`, the account-locked and session dialogs, the Request documents dialog), and page containers (`PageContainer`, `EntryFrame`'s frame and form);
  - popovers (the notification bell, the model picker) and the status screen's panel;
  - form columns and inputs (Settings, Edit profile, Retention, the Users search, the Calls search);
  - profile pages' column, the paper's page images (`PaperPdfReader`, `ImageReader`), the paper view's main column and the Overview rail;
  - the chat column and opening column (`answerLayout`), a sent message's bubble and a quoted selection (85% of the column), and the answer skeleton;
  - the version picker, comment boxes and the contact person card (`max-w-full`, which only stops overflow);
  - the notice answer card, and the Calls row's action column.
- **`overflow-hidden` (27):** each clips a rounded card's corners, an image or crop, a progress bar or the watermark. Otherwise it closes a pane whose content scrolls inside it (the app shell on Ask IRIS, Notes, Paper Chat, the conversation list, the source panel, the Publish preview, the paper's reading pane), or it is the Overview details column IR-1178 set.
  - Every table inside one of these cards scrolls sideways in its own `overflow-x-auto` wrapper, so no column is cut.

### Tests

- **`frontend/src/test/fullTextRule.test.ts` (new), the guard.** It reads every non-test source file and fails on any `truncate`, `text-ellipsis` or `line-clamp-*` that is not one of the five allowed cuts. Each allowed file lists exactly the classes it may hold.
  - **Before (on `main`'s files):** it failed on 29 files with cuts the rule forbids.
  - **After:** 4 passed.
- **`CallsAndConferencesPage.test.tsx`, *a call's About on the board* (new):**
  - Show more appears when the description runs past two lines, is `aria-expanded="false"`, and becomes Show less (`true`) and back;
  - nothing is offered when the description fits.
  - **Before:** it failed ("Unable to find … Show more"). **After:** passed.
- **`e2e/smoke/full-text.spec.ts`, *no screen cuts its text* (new), all 42 smoke screens.** Each is signed in, where it has a role, as an account with a long name ("Maria Cristina Remedios Dela Cruz-Santos y Villanueva") and email. The screens' own fixtures supply long paper titles.
  - Each is measured at **1745, then 360, then 1280 px in one page**, so text is checked after it re-wraps as the window narrows and widens again.
  - It fails on visible text that is clipped sideways, clamped, or partly past the edge of a box that hides its overflow. An allowed clamp passes only with a Show more that reveals it, or inside a card that links to the paper.
  - **On `main` (built with this branch's test):** 34 failed and 8 passed. The 8 are the signed-out screens. The account menu's name was clipped on every signed-in screen, and the Delete and Download Requests tables clamped a long title.
  - **After:** 42 passed, and with the file's three earlier tests, 45 passed.
  - The viewport matrix already fails a screen that scrolls sideways.
- **Tests changed deliberately:** none. No existing test relied on truncation. `vitest --changed origin/main`: 118 files, 2,151 passed, 3 skipped.

**Resize check (local, 2026-11-19):** a smoke-tier build (`npm run build`, mocked API) in Chromium. The resize check is the smoke spec above: all 42 screens at 1745, 360 and 1280 px in one page. No screenshots were kept.
