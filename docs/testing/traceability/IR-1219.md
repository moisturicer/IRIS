## IR-1219

**Requirement:** **Every screen's text fills its box, reflows when the window changes, and is never cut off or hidden.** Requested by Hermoine Zozobrado on 2026-11-17, clarified with Ashlee Sean the same day. It applies `DESIGN.md`'s Full Text Rule, which IR-1218 wrote. No ADR: it is a visual rule. No FR- id.

**Decided by Hermoine Zozobrado, 2026-11-19.** Each option was shown as a sketch first.
1. **A long review comment keeps See more.** Its first 300 characters end in `…`, and See more shows the whole comment in place.
2. **A call's description on the Calls board keeps its two-line clamp, with a new Show more.** The board had no other way to the rest: *View & apply* leaves IRIS, and not every call has it.
3. **Discover's rotating example search stays on one line.** It is decorative and hidden from screen readers.
4. **Ask IRIS is included,** although its redesign (IR-1229) is in progress.
5. **A paper's title in the reading bar stays on one line, ending in `…`.** The bar is one 56px row (IR-1092), and Paper Chat's dock and the reading pane are sized from that fixed height, so a wrapped title would spill over the paper. The full title shows on hover and focus, screen readers read it whole, and the Overview shows it in full. Chosen over a two-line clamp and over a bar that grows with its title.

So `DESIGN.md` now names **six allowed cuts**: the two IR-1218 named, plus these four (decisions 1, 2, 3 and 5). CODING_STANDARDS F2 names the same six.

### Changed

**Cuts removed.** Each now wraps with `break-words`, inside a `min-w-0` box where it sits in a row:
- **The account menu:** the name and role in the sidebar, and the name and email in the open menu (`AccountMenu`). On `main` a long name was clipped on every signed-in screen.
- **The sidebar:** each nav label, and the "Research-to-IP Platform" line (`Sidebar`).
- **Paper titles:**
  - a grid card's title (`ResearchCard`) and the Related works titles (`RelatedWorks`);
  - My Library's titles (`LibraryOverview`, `LibraryRecordTable`);
  - the Delete and Download Requests tables' titles. Their cells also lose the 200 px and 180 px caps, and a delete reason now wraps in full;
  - the Cite dialog's title (`PaperCiteModal`), and the title in Notes and in Paper Chat (`NotesPanel`, `PaperChatDock`).
- **Names and people:**
  - bylines (`ResearchCard`, `SourceContextPanel`);
  - My Library's lines of people and dates (`LibraryRecordTable`, `SubmissionRow`, `LibraryOverview`);
  - affiliations on Discover's people results, and a person's papers on their profile (`DiscoverPeopleResults`, `PersonProfileView`).
- **Menus and lists:**
  - the filter dropdown's value and options (`DiscoverFilterDropdown`). Its button is now at least 44px tall rather than exactly 44px (`min-h-11`), so a long value grows the button instead of spilling out of it;
  - the Save dropdown's options and folder names (`PaperSaveDropdown`), and folder names in My Library (`LibraryOverview`);
  - the Changes tab's section names (`ChangesTab`), Paper Chat's recent chats (`RecentChats`), and the reading bar's tab labels (`ReadingHeader`).
- **Ask IRIS (decision 4):**
  - the chat's title and its "Ask IRIS" label (`ChatToolbar`), and each conversation's title (`ConversationSidebar`);
  - in the source panel, the section, quote, title and byline (`SourceContextPanel`);
  - a cited passage's quote (`PassageQuote`).
- **Other text:**
  - the Publish form's suggested values and their source excerpts (`PrefillChip`);
  - an upload's file name, now beside a percentage that keeps its own width (`UploadDropzone`);
  - a document's name (`PaperDocuments`);
  - RDCO's note beside a download, which loses its 20rem cap and its `title`-only full text (`PaperDownloadControl`);
  - the unavailable-overview note (`OverviewNote`).
- **The reader's watermark footer** (`ReaderWatermark`). It names who opened the paper, so it wraps rather than cutting a long email. The tilted stamp drawn on the page is unchanged.

**The reading bar's title (decision 5) did not hold on `main`.** It carried `truncate`, but `reading-split:not-sr-only` resets `white-space` and `overflow` at the same width, and a breakpoint class outranks a plain one. So a long title wrapped to three lines and ran from 14px above the 56px bar to 14px below it. It is now `reading-split:truncate`: one line, with the full title in the heading's `title` and for screen readers.

**`whitespace-nowrap` removed** where the text can be long:
- the Tooltip, which shows a person's full name. It now wraps inside its own box, at most 18rem or the screen less 5rem;
- a download grant's line;
- the Download Requests note cell's 20rem cap.

**Width caps removed** where text sat in a wider box:
- the sign-in pages' headline and sentence (`EntryFrame`);
- page descriptions (`PageHeader`), empty-state messages (`EmptyState`) and the Notifications messages (75ch);
- RDCO's note in its dialog, and the Calls board and My Calls descriptions (`max-w-prose`);
- a profile's bio, and the Sign-up confirmation;
- Publish's success message;
- Workflow analytics' two explanations;
- the Ask IRIS hub's two paragraphs and the empty chat's line (`AIHubPage`, `ChatMessageList`).

My Library's folder picker can now take its whole row (`max-w-full`, was 12rem). A native `<select>` still cuts a name longer than the row; the browser draws it, and no class changes that.

**The Calls board's Show more** (`CallDetails`, decision 2):
- The clamp is measured, and measured again whenever the card's width changes (`ResizeObserver`) and once the page's fonts have loaded.
- Show more appears only when the description runs past two lines. It has `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and shows the description whole and justified, becoming Show less.
- Show less on a description that now fits leaves no button, so focus moves to the description instead of the page.
- Screen readers always read the whole description, because the clamp is visual only.

### Fixed heights on text

The ticket says a class search won't find these, so every fixed `h-*` in `frontend/src/` was read. Only two hold text that can be long:
- the filter dropdown's button, now `min-h-11`;
- the reading bar, now decision 5.

The rest hold fixed labels or nothing: the phone's top bar (`AppShell`, a menu button and the logo), the Ask IRIS history header ("New chat"), Discover's Filters button, citation and count badges, the people-results sort `<select>`, and dividers.

  - dialogs and sheets (`Modal`, the account-locked and session dialogs, the Request documents dialog), and page containers (`PageContainer`, `EntryFrame`'s frame and form);

- **`overflow-hidden` (27):**
  - Most clip a rounded card's corners, an image or crop, a progress bar, the research-progress thread or the watermark.
  - Others close a pane whose content scrolls inside it: the app shell on Ask IRIS, Ask IRIS's reader, Notes, Paper Chat, the conversation list, the source panel, the Publish preview, and the paper's reading pane.
  - One is the Overview details column IR-1178 set.
  - Every table inside one of these cards scrolls sideways in its own `overflow-x-auto` wrapper, so no column is cut.

### Tests

- **`frontend/src/test/fullTextRule.test.ts` (new), the guard.** It reads every non-test source file and fails on any `truncate`, `text-ellipsis` or `line-clamp-*` that is not one of the six allowed cuts. Each allowed file lists exactly the classes it may hold.
  - **Before (on `main`'s files):** it failed on 29 files with cuts the rule forbids.
  - **After:** 4 passed.
- **`CallsAndConferencesPage.test.tsx`, *a call's About on the board* (new):**
  - Show more appears when the description runs past two lines, is `aria-expanded="false"`, and becomes Show less (`true`) and back;
  - nothing is offered when the description fits.
  - **Before:** it failed ("Unable to find … Show more"). **After:** passed.
- **`e2e/smoke/full-text.spec.ts`, *no screen cuts its text* (new), all 42 smoke screens.**
  - **What it makes long:** each screen is signed in, where it has a role, as an account with a long name ("Maria Cristina Remedios Dela Cruz-Santos y Villanueva") and email. Every title the mocked API returns gains about 150 characters.
  - **How it measures:** each screen is checked at **1745, then 360, then 1280 px in one page**, so text is checked after it re-wraps as the window narrows and widens again. It waits for layout and any running animation after each resize.
  - **What fails it:** visible text that is clipped sideways, clamped, partly past the edge of a box that hides its overflow, or a block that runs past the top or bottom of a box too short for it.
  - **What passes as allowed:**
    - a clamp, only with a Show more that reveals it, or on a preview paragraph in a card that links to the paper;
    - a one-line cut, only when its whole text is its `title`.
  - **On `main` (built with this branch's test):** 34 failed and 8 passed. The 8 are the signed-out screens.
    - The account menu's name was clipped on every signed-in screen.
    - The Delete and Download Requests tables clamped a long title.
    - The reading bar's title spilled out of the bar on the five review screens.
  - **After:** 42 passed, and with the file's three earlier tests, 45 passed.
  - The viewport matrix already fails a screen that scrolls sideways.
- **Tests changed deliberately:** none. No existing test relied on truncation.
- **Other runs:**
  - Before the review fixes: `vitest --changed origin/main`, 118 files: 2,151 passed, 3 skipped.
  - After them: the opportunities, Discover, admin and library folders and the guard: 441 passed. After decision 5: the paper-view and opportunities folders and the guard: 64 files, 1,132 passed.
