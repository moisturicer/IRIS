# IR-1017: Rotate the logs of every production service

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Epic IR-805 (S2 Epic: Deployment). Found on 2026-11-11 while working on IR-1016.

## What changed

- **Seven services in `docker-compose.prod.yml` now rotate their logs:**
  - `migrate`, `ai-gateway` and `ai-runs-worker`;
  - `celery-default`, `celery-extraction`, `celery-embedding` and `celery-beat`.

  Each uses `json-file` with `max-size: "10m"` and `max-file: "3"`, the same as the other five. They used Docker's default, which never deletes old lines.
- IR-1015's check, `deploy/tests/check_restart_policies.py`, is renamed `deploy/tests/check_compose_prod.py`, because it now checks two things:
  - every long-running service restarts by itself (unchanged);
  - every service's logs are rotated (new).
- The `server-bootstrap` CI job runs it under its new name, and `deploy/README.md` lists it.
- Server-wide defaults in `/etc/docker/daemon.json` were not chosen: they would live on one server only, outside the repo.

## Evidence

- Local, 2026-11-11:
  - **Red:** before the compose change, `check_compose_prod.py` named exactly the seven services above and exited 1.
  - **Green:** after the change, it exits 0.
  - `docker compose -f docker-compose.prod.yml config`, run with throwaway values for its required variables, shows all twelve services with `json-file`, `max-size: 10m` and `max-file: 3`.
