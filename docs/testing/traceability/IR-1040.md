# IR-1040: Discover, My Library, notifications and shared record cards use the type roles

**Requirement:** `docs/frontend_redesign_spec.md` section 4.12 (type scale, nothing under 12 px); IR-452 (named type scale); IR-1037 (the font-size guard).

Fourteen files lose every `text-[Npx]`: `discover/{DiscoverPage,DiscoverResultCard,DiscoverFilterBar,DiscoverFilterDropdown,MyFeeds,PaperCiteModal,discoverUtils}`, `library/LibraryRecordTable`, `notifications/NotificationsPage`, `layout/{NotificationBell,Breadcrumbs}`, `profile/PersonProfileView`, `shared/{ResearchCard,RequestStatusChip}`. Mapping: 9 to 12 px to `text-label`, 13 px to `text-small`, 14 and 15 px to `text-body`. Their entries are removed from both groups in `src/test/fontSizeAllowance.ts`, so the guard fails if an arbitrary size returns. No text and no icon renders under 12 px.

`ManuscriptCover` (in the ticket's file list) no longer exists on `main`: IR-1082 removed it, and `ResearchCard` and the Discover card's abstract hook lost their arbitrary sizes in the same rework. Nothing was left to migrate in them beyond what `main` already had.

One layout adjustment, per the rule that a label moves up rather than the text shrinking: the notification bell's unread badge goes from a 17 px to a 20 px box (`min-w-5 h-5`), since its count is now 12/16 text. The 14 px My Library title becomes `text-body` (15 px), per IR-1038's 14 px mapping; `text-control` is reserved for form fields and page buttons.

Uppercase and tracking on the Discover topic tag, the My Library column heads and the bell heading are left as they were: sentence case is IR-1087. At 12 px they now fall inside the spec's rule (no all-caps tracking *below* 12 px).

Status wording on cards still comes from the server; no client map was added.

Executed evidence (2026-11-15, on `main` at 27c05495):

- `src/test/fontSize.test.ts` 9 / 9 with the fourteen entries removed (before the migration it failed on the sub-12 px files).
- `tsc --noEmit` and `eslint` on the touched folders: clean.
- `npm run test:changed`: 38 files, 790 tests passed. No test file was edited, so no assertion changed.
- Layout, real `LibraryRecordTable` (list and grid) and `DiscoverResultCard` in a throwaway page (not committed), measured in the browser pane before the rebase onto IR-1082: at 360 and at 1280 px there is no horizontal scroll, the smallest rendered text is 12 px, the smallest icon is 12 px and no label is clipped. The Discover card was reworked upstream since, but its remaining sizes are the same one-to-one swaps. Not checked in the browser: the notification bell panel, `Breadcrumbs` (needs a data router), `DiscoverPage` and `NotificationsPage` as whole pages (no backend here), and the Font Awesome icons with their stylesheet loaded.
