# IR-1086: Tablet: the sidebar becomes a labelled icon rail below 1024 px

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** no FR- id. Epic IR-920 (S2 Epic: Frontend UI/UX Redesign). Audit findings NAV-06 and NAV-07 ([`frontend_ui_audit_spec.md`](../../frontend_ui_audit_spec.md) §4.7).

## What changed

- **Three shapes, one hook.** `useSidebarMode()` in `lib/layout.ts` returns `drawer` below 768 px, `rail` from 768 to 1023 px and `desktop` from 1024 px. `isRail()` says whether the sidebar is drawn as the rail: always on a tablet unless the reader has opened it, the saved collapse preference on a desktop, never in the phone drawer. `SIDEBAR_WIDTH_PX` (230, unused and not the real width) is gone.
- **The rail is 76 px** (`w-rail`, `md:ml-rail`, a new spacing token), on a tablet and for the desktop's own collapse, which was 64 px. The content's margin is the rail's from `md` and the full sidebar's (`lg:ml-60`) only from `lg`, so the content is never squeezed (NAV-06).
- **The IRIS mark opens the full sidebar over the page** on a tablet, with the backdrop, inert page, focus move and Escape the phone drawer already has, and focus back on the mark when it closes. It saves nothing: the desktop's stored collapse choice is neither read nor written below 1024 px. Choosing a link closes it.
- **Every rail control has a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus** (NAV-07): the mark, each link, the bell and the account button, through a new `ui/Tooltip`. It dismisses on Escape and on leave or blur (WCAG 1.4.13), is drawn in a portal because the nav scrolls, and is not an `aria-describedby`, so a screen reader does not hear the name twice. Hover opens it for a mouse pointer only (a tap does not leave it stuck), focus that arrives with a mouse press does not, and a scroll or resize closes it. The native `title` it replaces is removed from these controls (the bell keeps it elsewhere through `showTitle`).

## Tests

- `frontend/src/components/layout/Sidebar.test.tsx`, at 768 and 1023 px: the rail shows whatever width was saved; every control is found by role and accessible name; labels stay as `sr-only` text; the content margin is `md:ml-rail` with `lg:ml-60` after it; hover and keyboard focus show a named tooltip and Escape hides it; the mark, bell and account button have tooltips; the mark opens the full sidebar over an inert page, focuses it, saves no preference, and Escape returns focus to the mark; a chosen link closes it; widening to 1280 px closes an opened sidebar and narrowing again returns to the rail with no preference saved; axe finds nothing serious. At 1024 and 1280 px: the full sidebar and the saved collapse still apply.

## Evidence

- Run locally 2026-11-14: `tsc --noEmit` clean; eslint reports no errors; `npm run test:changed`: 70 files, 1152 tests passed. The full suite is left to CI.
- **Checked in a browser** (a throwaway page mounting the real `AppShell`; not committed): at 768 and 1023 px the sidebar is 76 px, the content starts at 76 px and the page is no wider than the window; at 1024 px the sidebar is 240 px. Hovering an icon showed its tooltip; the mark opened the full sidebar over the page, and Escape put focus back on it.
- **Not checked:** a real signed-in session against the API, a touch device, and a screen reader.
- **Decisions to confirm:** (1) From 1024 px the reader's saved collapse (and Paper View's reading rail) still apply, so "the full sidebar shows" holds by default, not when they chose the rail. (2) The desktop's own collapsed rail moved from 64 to 76 px so there is one rail width.
- **Known gaps:** rail controls are 36 px (the mark and rows) and 34 px (the bell), as before, under the 44 px touch target; on a touch tablet there is no hover, so a tap navigates and the tooltip is a keyboard and mouse aid. Closing the opened sidebar with a click on the backdrop returns focus to the mark by script, which shows its tooltip until the next pointer move.
