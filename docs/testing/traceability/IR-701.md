## IR-701 — Discover shows a People row and a Papers / People switch

**Basis and scope.** IR-701, subtask 7 of [IR-694](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-694), builds on IR-697's signed-in people search. Discover uses the same search text for people and paper results. Papers remains the default view; matching people appear in a preview above the papers, and the People view shows the paged, filterable directory.

**What changed.** `DiscoverPeopleResults.tsx` requests people for the current query, caps the preview at five, and shows profile links with a protected circular photo or initials, name, affiliation, role and published-paper count. “See all people” and the Papers / People switch open the directory, where College, Department and Role filters are sent with the search. `api/people.ts` contains the request and response types.

**Backend dependency.** On 2026-11-09, IR-697 was still To Do and no people-search endpoint had landed on `main`. The frontend adapter therefore expects `GET /users/search/` with `q`, `page`, `page_size`, `college`, `department` and `role`, returning `{ count, next, results }`. This is an explicit integration assumption, not verified against a running backend. Align it with IR-697 before treating this feature as end-to-end verified.

**Tests.** `DiscoverPage.test.tsx` exercises the visible five-person preview, circular avatar fallback, profile link, affiliation, role, paper count, People switch, filters and query parameters through the accessible UI, runs axe on the page, and confirms a malformed people response leaves Discover usable.

**Executed evidence (2026-11-09).** The Discover page file passed all 73 tests. Changed-file ESLint passed, and a scoped TypeScript check of the changed frontend files and test passed. The full frontend run completed with 182 test files passing and 2,527 tests passing; two unrelated `PublishDialog.test.tsx` cases timed out, and `src/test/contract/contract.test.ts` could not load the absent `ajv` package (the declared `ts-json-schema-generator` package is also absent in this worktree). The repository-wide `tsc --noEmit` is consequently not green in this worktree.

**Status.** **PARTIAL** until IR-697's endpoint contract is confirmed and exercised end to end. The frontend behavior is covered with a mocked people search.
