# IR-815: test data answers the embargo question, and the development bypass is deleted

[Back to the matrix](../TRACEABILITY.md)

**Requirements:** ADR-052 decisions 15 to 17, ADR-015 §A development bypass and ADR-054 decision 18. No FR- id.

**Basis:** ADR-052 decisions 15 to 17, ADR-015 §A development bypass, and ADR-054 decision 18.

**Test data:**

- `seed_demo` answers "no embargo" through `_submit`, which the three scenarios that stamped consent by hand now share. `[DEMO] Thesis ITSO cleared, RDCO's turn to decide` carries a date a year ahead, so the refusal can be seen and IR-818's warning has a paper in front of RDCO. The draft stays unanswered for Publish. Re-running answers for this command's own papers that an older run left Unknown, and for nothing else.
- `load_corpus --clear-for-ai` answers "no embargo", with consent, for each new paper. Without it a paper stays Unknown. With it, the worker embeds the paper's chunks and queues its AI Overview, which spends credits, and retrieval can't find the paper until `backfill_embeddings` adds its title-and-abstract vector. The command's report says so.
- `mark_corpus_cleared` does the same for papers already loaded, for one owner's unanswered papers, drafts excepted. It never replaces an answer and sends nothing. Finding nothing, it says whose papers are waiting, because the 40 proxy papers aren't on the machine this was built on. The steps are in `docs/evaluation/README.md` §Clearing the proxy corpus.
- Both refuse with `DEBUG` off, and `--force` doesn't change that. `load_corpus` refuses the option with `--origin institutional`, and `mark_corpus_cleared` leaves such a paper Unknown and names it. That origin check goes beyond ADR-052. It can't catch a real paper whose origin was never declared.

**Contradiction recorded: ADR-054 decision 18** says `seed_demo` and `load_corpus` give every account and paper a Yes, and names this ticket. Only part of that is here. `seed_demo` already stamps its accounts and submitted papers. A default `load_corpus` writes no consent, because a stamp on a real paper would be an answer nobody gave, and it's opt-in as this ticket asks. No per-person answer exists until IR-791, whose acceptance criteria carry the rest.

**Deleted:** `apps/ai/policy/bypass.py`, its startup calls, `index_with_disclosure_bypass`, `AI_DISCLOSURE_BYPASS_FOR_DEVELOPMENT` in settings and `.env.example`, the flag on `GET /api/v1/ai/status/`, the Ask IRIS banner and the checks in `eval_retrieval` and `eval_replay_conversations`. Beyond the ticket's list, because the bypass was their only reason: the `permits` argument on `embed_record_summary` and `embed_active_chunk_set`, and `composition_root_installed`.

**Deleted deliberately with the bypass:** `apps/ai/tests/test_disclosure_bypass.py`. Its "refuses by default" cases are kept by `test_embargo_every_way_out.py::TestIndexing`, which runs the shipped gate on real rows.

**Tests:** `apps/records/test_seed_demo.py` (`SeedDemoEmbargoTests`, `SeedDemoEarlierPapersTests`), `apps/records/test_load_corpus.py` (`LoadCorpusClearingTests`), `apps/records/test_mark_corpus_cleared.py`, and `apps/ai/tests/test_embargo_every_way_out.py` (`TestTestData` sends a loaded and a seeded paper to fake vendors through the real gate; `TestTheBypassIsGone` fails if any of it comes back, including when the old setting is left set).

**Evidence, 2026-11-02:**

- **Full backend suite**, before the review's fixes and before merging `main`: 5,508 passed and 2 failed. Both failures are local-only and untouched here: `test_the_proxy_audit_pins_the_proxy_set_it_audited` (Windows line endings) and `test_property_token_ceiling_is_a_guarantee[fixed-window]` (Hypothesis). After the fixes and the merge, the affected files were re-run: 228 passed, flake8 clean.
- **A copy of one developer's dev database**, starting from `records/0021` and migrated forward: 18 seeded papers, none answered. `seed_demo` answered 17 and created 5 more, leaving 21 "no embargo", 1 dated 2027-11-02 and the draft unanswered. A second run answered none.
- **The same copy, with papers loaded for the check and no worker running:** two loaded without the option stayed Unknown, and one loaded with it was answered and reported what it spends. `--origin institutional` was refused. `mark_corpus_cleared` under the wrong owner marked 0 and named `corpus-loader@cit.edu (2)`, then marked those 2 under the right one, and left the draft alone. With `DEBUG` off it refused. `backfill_embeddings --dry-run` ran. **Nothing real was embedded, because no worker ran and no vendor was called.**
- **A real browser on the real backend:** Ask IRIS loaded with no bypass flag on `GET /api/v1/ai/status/`, no banner, and no script errors.
- **The bypass tests failed first:** with the bypass still in place, the check that a leftover setting opens nothing failed on the real permissive root, so it can see the thing it guards against.

**PARTIAL:** the blocked state shows today through the `embargo` field on the Record API and an AI Overview reading `unavailable`, and no page names it until IR-817 (Paper View shows a paper's embargo). **The 40 proxy papers are not marked** (ADR-052 decision 16): they aren't on the machine this was built on, so whoever holds them runs the steps.
