# IR-733: Answer from full Manuscripts with honest reading coverage

**Jira:** [IR-733](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-733)

## Parent

[IR-715](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-715) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

A reader can ask about middle and late sections of selected papers; the agent reads the complete extracted text when it fits and follows explicit ranges when it does not.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] When useful and affordable, load the active permitted extracted Manuscript in document order through the unified context budget, including middle/late evidence rather than a fixed first-Passage prefix.
- [ ] Deduplicate overlapping extracted content while retaining canonical page/Passage/source-revision bindings for citations.
- [ ] Oversized selections produce bounded explicit covered/missing ranges and source-bound continuation. Track inspected, currently model-visible and recoverable content separately.
- [ ] Source replacement, revoked access or foreign continuation cannot silently move a read to a different Manuscript; exact citation identities survive prompt reordering/eviction.
- [ ] Incomplete extraction and uninterpreted Figures/Formulas are honest coverage limitations. Full extracted text does not claim visual interpretation or exhaustive research.

## Independent demonstration

Ask for a fact planted in the middle and at the end of two synthetic long papers, then repeat with a small verified window, overlap and an extraction gap; reopen the cited answer and its page binding.

## Scope boundary

Own complete-document reading and coverage in the actual agent. Do not introduce a new extractor or load the whole corpus by default.
