# IR-721: Remove revoked evidence from reopened Conversations and review exports

**Jira:** [IR-721](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-721)

## Parent

[IR-712](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-712) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

After access changes, a reader or reviewer cannot recover revoked research by reopening a saved answer, reconnecting, exporting it or opening an evaluation packet.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Create a delivered research Turn through the new authenticated path, then revoke/replace/delete its dependencies. History, reconnect, citation resolution and export enforce current access rather than trusting the stored result.
- [ ] IR-605/IR-608-compatible packet creation and packet access enforce the reviewer's own access; a copied artifact address never becomes a capability for hidden Records.
- [ ] Derived plans, cached excerpts, native context and later follow-up requests cannot reintroduce removed content. Preserve unrelated permitted findings or use a safe unavailable outcome.
- [ ] Exercise direct restricted-role reads, pooled readers and read/send/commit races against real PostgreSQL; security fixtures belong to this integrated flow in addition to the tests already required by IR-717–IR-720.
- [ ] Record executed and unavailable evidence classes separately; do not represent deterministic security results as human-reviewed quality or remote-provider capacity.

## Independent demonstration

Deliver a cited answer, remove the source's authorization, and attempt live/reopened/replayed/exported/reviewer-packet access as owner, another reader and a reviewer.

## Scope boundary

## Delivery and evidence

Include the minimal persistence, authenticated entry point, public projection or operator surface, and behavioral tests needed for this outcome. Reuse existing reader components where no new control is needed. Each feature must land with its own applicable tests; later integration tickets do not postpone them.

Use real restricted-role PostgreSQL and independent workers where database permissions, leases, races or shared limits are claimed. A deterministic provider proves contracts, never paid capacity or human-reviewed quality. Record actual checks, failures and unavailable environments.

Keep prerequisites independently green through additive expansion, controlled migration and final contraction. Preserve current institutional authority, one writer per dataset, source/disclosure rules and the original AskRun allowance. Ticket publication does not authorize a live provider campaign, deployment, traffic switch or data reset. A PR and independent human approval remain required.
