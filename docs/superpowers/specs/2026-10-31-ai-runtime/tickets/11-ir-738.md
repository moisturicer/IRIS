# IR-738: Extract a Manuscript revision without activating stale research

**Jira:** [IR-738](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-738)

## Parent

[IR-716](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-716) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

An owner sees a Manuscript progress from pending to extracted or failed, with page-bound text available to the next processing stage and no stale activation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Run the existing chosen extraction/chunking semantics in isolated AI ingestion workers, pinning the exact Manuscript/content/extraction revision and preserving page/structured-content provenance.
- [ ] Write immutable pending extraction/chunk revisions under one AI migration/writer owner in the isolated target path; duplicate work reuses completed output.
- [ ] Failed extraction and incomplete coverage are processing states, not zero research findings. Reader/owner status reflects the actual outcome without exposing unrelated data.
- [ ] Superseded or revoked work cannot publish as current. Inject out-of-order completion and source deletion before write; stale pending output never replaces the active revision.
- [ ] Use additive ownership expansion and verify local extraction behavior on a synthetic document plus deterministic failure fixtures; no embedding/provider stage or live writer switch is hidden in this task.
