# IR-750: Resolve selected Records and answer exact metadata counts

**Jira:** [IR-750](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-750)

## Parent

[IR-712](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-712) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

A reader can identify a named paper and ask exact counts/facets over the permitted population without a model inventing catalogue totals.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Resolve titles/identifiers into validated run-scoped Record handles through the existing agent tool registry; ambiguous names yield candidates or a focused clarification without leaking denied Records.
- [ ] Aggregate/count/facet operations compute typed results in the authorized database population, with scope, filters and provenance sufficient for final validation.
- [ ] Semantic screening is labelled as a classified subset and never promoted to an exhaustive catalogue total. Missing, withheld and no-match outcomes do not expose private populations.
- [ ] Paper Chat cannot expand scope through a metadata operation; foreign/stale handles and counts after revocation are denied/recomputed under current policy.
- [ ] A recording provider receives typed metadata facts and original evidence without a hidden screening/brief writer; the reader answer and reopened state retain the distinction between exact totals and semantic subsets.

## Independent demonstration

Ask 'Which paper is this?' followed by an exact Area count as two different readers, then request a semantic subset; compare the grounded answers to each reader's authorized database result.

Use real restricted-role PostgreSQL and independent workers where database permissions, leases, races or shared limits are claimed. A deterministic provider proves contracts, never paid capacity or human-reviewed quality. Record actual checks, failures and unavailable environments.

Keep prerequisites independently green through additive expansion, controlled migration and final contraction. Preserve current institutional authority, one writer per dataset, source/disclosure rules and the original AskRun allowance. Ticket publication does not authorize a live provider campaign, deployment, traffic switch or data reset. A PR and independent human approval remain required.
