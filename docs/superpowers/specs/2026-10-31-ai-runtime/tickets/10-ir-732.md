# IR-732: Fit the complete outgoing request within verified model capacity

**Jira:** [IR-732](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-732)

## Parent

[IR-715](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-715) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

A bounded question either sends a fully accounted request that fits the selected endpoint or returns an honest capacity outcome before transport.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Count the actual serialized instructions, tool schemas, complete history/exchanges, evidence, opaque continuation and framing, with provider-specific input/output/reasoning and byte constraints.
- [ ] Verify model/endpoint capabilities; test both a small and a declared million-token-capable fixture. Unknown capacity never defaults to a million and no unsupported smaller-model fallback occurs.
- [ ] Reserve output/reasoning, next-result headroom, final answer and at most one repair before admitting evidence. Explicitly repack, request compaction when supported, or refuse; never silently clip a tool group or invent full coverage.
- [ ] Single-request occupancy, cumulative AskRun allowance and stored Conversation size remain distinct; token estimates do not replace conservative byte-based paid reservations.
- [ ] Through the direct AGENT request seam, record the actual outgoing payload and safe capacity status. An oversized tool-result fixture is rejected/bounded before another send, and opaque payload size is accounted without logging it.

## Independent demonstration

Send a direct question with large schema/history/opaque fixtures under small and verified large capacities; inspect the recorded serialized size and prove no over-limit transport or silent clipping occurs.

## Scope boundary

Own provider-capacity packing before the research loop is enabled. Automatic compaction and full-paper allocation extend this contract later.

## Delivery and evidence

Include the minimal persistence, authenticated entry point, public projection or operator surface, and behavioral tests needed for this outcome. Reuse existing reader components where no new control is needed. Each feature must land with its own applicable tests; later integration tickets do not postpone them.

Use real restricted-role PostgreSQL and independent workers where database permissions, leases, races or shared limits are claimed. A deterministic provider proves contracts, never paid capacity or human-reviewed quality. Record actual checks, failures and unavailable environments.

Keep prerequisites independently green through additive expansion, controlled migration and final contraction. Preserve current institutional authority, one writer per dataset, source/disclosure rules and the original AskRun allowance. Ticket publication does not authorize a live provider campaign, deployment, traffic switch or data reset. A PR and independent human approval remain required.
