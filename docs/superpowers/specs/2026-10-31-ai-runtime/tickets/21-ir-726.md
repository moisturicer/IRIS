# IR-726: Stop and reconnect to the same investigation across worker races

**Jira:** [IR-726](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-726)

## Parent

[IR-713](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-713) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

A reader can Stop, reconnect or explicitly Retry without duplicate delivery, continued admissions or hidden budget renewal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Authenticated Stop fences new tool/provider admissions and final state advancement atomically; an already transmitted request may finish and remain charged.
- [ ] Race Stop against final commitment and worker takeover in independent processes: there is one coherent terminal outcome and at most one Turn.
- [ ] Reconnect with a journal cursor replays safe ordered progress from the existing run; foreign ownership, stale/deleted Conversation access and private traces are denied.
- [ ] A reader-requested Retry creates an explicitly linked new AskRun with separately admitted original limits; worker redelivery and reconnect never reset the first run's allowance.
- [ ] Browser disconnect, lost wakeup, run deletion and delayed worker completion are covered through the real request/worker seam and recorded in an IR-608-compatible result.

## Independent demonstration

Disconnect during a queued investigation, reconnect, race Stop with the final response, then use explicit Retry; show original/new run identities, terminal state and conservative spending.

## Scope boundary

Own reader lifecycle and cross-process race integration, not a deferred test gate for the foundational run/queue/accounting tickets.

## Delivery and evidence

Include the minimal persistence, authenticated entry point, public projection or operator surface, and behavioral tests needed for this outcome. Reuse existing reader components where no new control is needed. Each feature must land with its own applicable tests; later integration tickets do not postpone them.

Use real restricted-role PostgreSQL and independent workers where database permissions, leases, races or shared limits are claimed. A deterministic provider proves contracts, never paid capacity or human-reviewed quality. Record actual checks, failures and unavailable environments.

Keep prerequisites independently green through additive expansion, controlled migration and final contraction. Preserve current institutional authority, one writer per dataset, source/disclosure rules and the original AskRun allowance. Ticket publication does not authorize a live provider campaign, deployment, traffic switch or data reset. A PR and independent human approval remain required.
