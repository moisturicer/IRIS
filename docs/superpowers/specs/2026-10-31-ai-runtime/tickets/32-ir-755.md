[IR-716](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-716) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

The release owner obtains actual reviewed repeated-campaign and workload evidence for the new runtime, or a precise blocked report when approvals/environments are absent.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Before any live send, record funding/provider/data-transmission approval, explicit operational caps, the frozen case pack, model/corpus/data/cache conditions and predeclared workload/latency/cost profile.
- [ ] Collect at least three repetitions through IR-605; retain unsuccessful/capped/cancelled attempts and conservative unknown usage, with separate cold/warm and matched-versus-expanded reports.
- [ ] Obtain two independent human reviews and adjudication as required, preserving original baseline/held-out artifacts. An AI judge cannot substitute for missing people or make a small family reportable.
- [ ] Execute required real-database/independent-worker, browser/accessibility and bounded ordinary/deep/ingestion load exercises; record queue-inclusive timing, resource pressure and complete cost.
- [ ] Use IR-741 to publish observed objectives, uncertainty, baseline comparison and safety gates. Failed or unavailable required evidence prevents release; completion of a report does not imply that the architecture passed.
- [ ] Without external approvals or environments, stop before paid collection and record not-ready/blocked. This ticket authorizes no deployment, institutional-data bypass or Conversation reset.

## Delivery and evidence

Include the minimal persistence, authenticated entry point, public projection or operator surface, and behavioral tests needed for this outcome. Reuse existing reader components where no new control is needed. Each feature must land with its own applicable tests; later integration tickets do not postpone them.

Use real restricted-role PostgreSQL and independent workers where database permissions, leases, races or shared limits are claimed. A deterministic provider proves contracts, never paid capacity or human-reviewed quality. Record actual checks, failures and unavailable environments.

Keep prerequisites independently green through additive expansion, controlled migration and final contraction. Preserve current institutional authority, one writer per dataset, source/disclosure rules and the original AskRun allowance. Ticket publication does not authorize a live provider campaign, deployment, traffic switch or data reset. A PR and independent human approval remain required.
