# IR-737: Track a committed Manuscript change as recoverable research work

**Jira:** [IR-737](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-737)

## Parent

[IR-716](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-716) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

An owner saves a Manuscript change and can see that processing is pending/recoverable even if a queue notification is lost.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Commit canonical Manuscript/revision changes and their outbox work atomically; a rolled-back save publishes no job. A duplicate notification maps to one durable work identity.
- [ ] Issue and verify a Django-authorized ingestion grant bound to the committed item, exact Manuscript revision and permitted processing purpose; reject forged/unrelated/stale/revoked job authority.
- [ ] An AI worker acknowledges durable work and exposes a safe existing processing-status surface; lose Redis delivery and recover the committed item through reconciliation.
- [ ] Out-of-order deletion/access/revision facts never restore permission. Canonical database rules deny access before projection cleanup; renewal rechecks current work rather than extending obsolete authority.
- [ ] Keep institutional outbox writes in Django and AI receipt/derived state in the AI owner. Processing status cannot be mistaken for completed extraction/indexing or a research finding.

## Independent demonstration

Save a Manuscript through its existing authenticated flow, roll back one save, lose another wakeup and reconcile; observe one recoverable current-revision job with a restricted processing grant.

## Scope boundary

Own committed institutional publication/receipt and job authority. Pure extraction is IR-738; provider indexing and active research publication are IR-751.
