## What to build

A reader asks a paper question; one agent searches, reads, assesses evidence and answers while retaining the whole ordered investigation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A direct question and a search/read question use the same pinned AGENT responsibility and preserve original intent, complete exchanges and compatible opaque artifacts.
- [ ] Local core tools expose original evidence without mandatory RESOLVE/PLAN/ANSWER/SCREEN/BRIEF inference. Outgoing attempt-purpose records demonstrate the absence of hidden specialist writers.
- [ ] Task-state updates use bounded stable IDs/revisions and actual execution state; tools cannot widen Paper Chat, forge identity or extend allowance.
- [ ] Time/token/cost/step/tool/no-progress bounds disable further research and retain final-answer plus at-most-one-repair capacity. A tool-only or truncated completion cannot become a delivered answer.
- [ ] Reuse existing deterministic citation/source/fact safeguards for the first answer and commit atomically; IR-729 adds actionable same-Conversation repair, not permission to emit unchecked drafts meanwhile.
- [ ] Follow-up shown ordering, selections/exclusions and one materially necessary clarification are preserved. Optional thoroughness selects a configured bounded profile without an engine/provider selector.

## Independent demonstration

Ask a two-paper question through the authenticated entry point using a recording provider, follow up with 'the second one', force a no-progress loop and show bounded supported finalization with no second writer.

## Scope boundary

Own the continuous search/read agent. Exact resolve/count tools, full-paper expansion, durable recovery and partial-outage integration are separately owned slices.

## Delivery and evidence

Include the minimal persistence, authenticated entry point, public projection or operator surface, and behavioral tests needed for this outcome. Reuse existing reader components where no new control is needed. Each feature must land with its own applicable tests; later integration tickets do not postpone them.

Use real restricted-role PostgreSQL and independent workers where database permissions, leases, races or shared limits are claimed. A deterministic provider proves contracts, never paid capacity or human-reviewed quality. Record actual checks, failures and unavailable environments.

Keep prerequisites independently green through additive expansion, controlled migration and final contraction. Preserve current institutional authority, one writer per dataset, source/disclosure rules and the original AskRun allowance. Ticket publication does not authorize a live provider campaign, deployment, traffic switch or data reset. A PR and independent human approval remain required.
