# IR-751: Publish a fully indexed current Manuscript for research

**Jira:** [IR-751](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-751)

## Parent

[IR-716](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-716) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

A reader can research a newly processed Manuscript once its permitted extraction and embeddings are complete, with citations bound to that active version.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Continue committed extracted work through admitted/disclosure-checked document embeddings using the existing embedding semantics; preserve completed attempt/output identity on redelivery.
- [ ] Build immutable pending Record/Passage vectors for the current Embedding Space and source revision; a duplicate job does not repeat a known completed paid step.
- [ ] Atomically activate only a complete current permitted revision; failed, stale, deleted or superseded work cannot become active even when its worker finishes last.
- [ ] Actual local research queries and saved citations resolve the new active permitted revision; query/document embedding identities and dimensions match.
- [ ] Unknown remote outcomes retain conservative reservations and any repeat is separately admitted; do not promise exactly-once billing. Separate ingestion capacity from interactive research.

## Independent demonstration

Process a synthetic revision with recording embedding/model transports, interrupt after a completed embedding batch, redeliver and ask a cited question; then finish an older job and prove it cannot reactivate.

## Scope boundary

## Delivery and evidence

Include the minimal persistence, authenticated entry point, public projection or operator surface, and behavioral tests needed for this outcome. Reuse existing reader components where no new control is needed. Each feature must land with its own applicable tests; later integration tickets do not postpone them.

Use real restricted-role PostgreSQL and independent workers where database permissions, leases, races or shared limits are claimed. A deterministic provider proves contracts, never paid capacity or human-reviewed quality. Record actual checks, failures and unavailable environments.

Keep prerequisites independently green through additive expansion, controlled migration and final contraction. Preserve current institutional authority, one writer per dataset, source/disclosure rules and the original AskRun allowance. Ticket publication does not authorize a live provider campaign, deployment, traffic switch or data reset. A PR and independent human approval remain required.
