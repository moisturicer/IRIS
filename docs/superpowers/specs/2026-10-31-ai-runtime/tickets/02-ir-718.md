# IR-718: Read the same permitted research in Django and the AI runtime

**Jira:** [IR-718](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-718)

## Parent

[IR-712](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-712) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

A reader sees the same authorized Record population through both runtimes, including direct dependent-content reads and counts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Owner, adviser, office reviewer, catalogue reader, anonymous/disabled account and scope fixtures preserve the intended current access outcomes in both runtimes.
- [ ] Shared database enforcement protects dependent extracted text, metadata, vectors, provenance and aggregation as well as Record rows; direct access to dependent relations cannot evade the policy.
- [ ] Missing verified context, deleted/revoked Records and mismatched purpose return no protected rows. Missing and denied public references reveal no differing private titles or counts.
- [ ] Switch research-read callers only after real restricted-role parity passes; replace the duplicate research-read predicate without changing workflow-write authorization.
- [ ] Migration and startup checks reject runtime owner/bypass credentials; an additive rollout keeps the existing application green until the explicit switch. No two competing research access policies become the destination.
- [ ] Job-purpose content reads remain unavailable until IR-737 can verify the committed canonical work item; recognizing a signed grant format alone does not authorize ingestion.

## Delivery and evidence

Include the minimal persistence, authenticated entry point, public projection or operator surface, and behavioral tests needed for this outcome. Reuse existing reader components where no new control is needed. Each feature must land with its own applicable tests; later integration tickets do not postpone them.

Use real restricted-role PostgreSQL and independent workers where database permissions, leases, races or shared limits are claimed. A deterministic provider proves contracts, never paid capacity or human-reviewed quality. Record actual checks, failures and unavailable environments.

Keep prerequisites independently green through additive expansion, controlled migration and final contraction. Preserve current institutional authority, one writer per dataset, source/disclosure rules and the original AskRun allowance. Ticket publication does not authorize a live provider campaign, deployment, traffic switch or data reset. A PR and independent human approval remain required.
