# IR-723: Keep queued research running after the browser disconnects

**Jira:** [IR-723](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-723)

## Parent

[IR-713](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-713) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

A reader submits work, closes the browser and later reconnects to the same bounded run while ordinary and deep work receive fair worker capacity.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Commit durable work before notifying Redis. Lose a notification, restart dispatch and redeliver: independent workers recover the committed run without duplicate terminal delivery.
- [ ] Separate request handling, interactive/deep execution and ingestion capacity; configured per-reader and per-Conversation populations and pool sizes remain bounded.
- [ ] At most one run advances a Conversation revision. A second stale/conflicting request is rejected or queued by the declared contract without silently remapping selected paper order.
- [ ] A declared synthetic workload proves protected ordinary capacity and eventual deep scheduling; ingestion cannot consume the interactive pool. Do not invent production concurrency values.
- [ ] Queue waiting counts against the original deadline and queue-inclusive latency. Busy, capped and expired work expose safe public states; disconnect alone never cancels.

## Independent demonstration

Submit ordinary and deep questions through authenticated requests, disconnect, suppress a Redis wakeup, restart dispatch and reconnect while independent workers run a declared bounded test workload.

## Scope boundary

Own durable dispatch and admission fairness. A completed-step worker crash is handled in IR-724; reader Stop races are integrated in IR-726.

## Delivery and evidence

Include the minimal persistence, authenticated entry point, public projection or operator surface, and behavioral tests needed for this outcome. Reuse existing reader components where no new control is needed. Each feature must land with its own applicable tests; later integration tickets do not postpone them.

Use real restricted-role PostgreSQL and independent workers where database permissions, leases, races or shared limits are claimed. A deterministic provider proves contracts, never paid capacity or human-reviewed quality. Record actual checks, failures and unavailable environments.

Keep prerequisites independently green through additive expansion, controlled migration and final contraction. Preserve current institutional authority, one writer per dataset, source/disclosure rules and the original AskRun allowance. Ticket publication does not authorize a live provider campaign, deployment, traffic switch or data reset. A PR and independent human approval remain required.
