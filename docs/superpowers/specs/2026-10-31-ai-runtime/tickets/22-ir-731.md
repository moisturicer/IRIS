# IR-731: Keep supported findings when a research tool fails

**Jira:** [IR-731](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-731)

## Parent

[IR-714](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-714) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

A reader still receives independent supported findings when one research operation fails, with an accurate explanation of what could not be established.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Inject the A09-style partial outage after useful evidence is acquired; preserve supported findings and distinguish unavailable tool, absent metadata, no matches and insufficient evidence.
- [ ] Do not convert an outage into a zero count, claim a complete literature search or invent a missing paper value. Typed counts and completeness claims still pass final assurance.
- [ ] Exercise malformed calls, parallel results, necessary clarification and complete continuation groups through authenticated saved Conversations, allowing multiple valid safe tool orders.
- [ ] A01–A15-compatible core task cases produce safe live/reopened/replayed/exported projections and stable paper ordering. Add end-to-end regression coverage here without deferring feature tests from earlier slices.
- [ ] Export the deterministic contract results through existing evaluation-compatible artifacts and label them as contract evidence, not human-reviewed quality. Dedicated long-context scenarios are owned by IR-736.

## Independent demonstration

Compare two permitted papers, fail a later operation for one paper, and show a cited partial answer with accurate limitations that remains the same after reopening/export.

## Scope boundary

Own partial-outage behavior and integrated core task regressions. Remove the unnecessary wait for the entire long-context suite.
