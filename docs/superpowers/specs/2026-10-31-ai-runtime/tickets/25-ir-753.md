# IR-753: Review and freeze long-context evaluation cases before collection

**Jira:** [IR-753](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-753)

## Parent

[IR-716](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-716) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

Human reviewers receive a versioned long-paper/Conversation case pack with clear labels and rubrics, frozen before counted outputs are opened.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Add long-Manuscript, middle/late evidence, repeated/manual compaction, exact-detail recovery, revocation, partial-outage and interrupted-research cases grounded in the accepted specs.
- [ ] Retain reviewed task expectations, source/page bindings and independent review/adjudication roles. Include at least ten distinct cases in each family proposed for reporting; a smaller family remains explicitly inconclusive.
- [ ] Separate development/proxy from held-out material; lock prompts, labels, rubric, quality thresholds and scenario identity before counted collection, preserving old baseline sets.
- [ ] Run IR-605 manifest/count/preflight checks without opening counted outputs; do not tune held-out labels from model answers or replace human label approval with agent-generated labels.
- [ ] Record named human label review and freeze evidence. Until that review exists, publish a draft pack with not-ready/blocked state rather than claiming approved ground truth.
