# IR-724: Resume a worker-interrupted investigation without restarting its allowance

**Jira:** [IR-724](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-724)

## Parent

[IR-713](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-713) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

A replacement worker resumes a real agent investigation from completed exchanges, keeping its original evidence, remaining allowance and single final result.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Persist complete ordered assistant/tool-call/result groups, stable call IDs, task/context revision, source pointers, pinned compatibility and consumed/reserved attempts after completed steps.
- [ ] Kill an independent worker after a tool result, after a provider response and around checkpoint commit; the next worker restores verified completed work and preserves original deadline/spend reservations.
- [ ] Takeover advances a fencing token. A delayed old worker can neither admit new work nor commit a checkpoint/final Turn after losing its lease.
- [ ] Recovered evidence is reauthorized; access-stale or incompatible state is rebuilt from permitted originals or fails safely. No live database connection is held across model inference.
- [ ] An uncertain remote attempt retains its reservation; a repeat is a separately admitted physical attempt. Recovery guarantees at most one terminal Turn, not exactly-once remote billing.

## Independent demonstration

Interrupt a two-step tool-using investigation in a separate process, replace the worker, and show one final saved answer with restored call groups and no allowance reset.

## Scope boundary

Own step recovery for the actual agent protocol. Do not build a second placeholder checkpoint format before the agent loop exists; compact-state recovery is extended in IR-734.
