# IR-720: Prevent withheld or revoked evidence from reaching a provider

**Jira:** [IR-720](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-720)

## Parent

[IR-712](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-712) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

A reader can keep independently permitted findings while withheld or newly revoked material is excluded from each outgoing research request.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Use IR-250's canonical embargo facts, writer and deliberate backfill; missing consent/IP/embargo/provider-policy facts refuse disclosure. Reading permission alone never permits transmission.
- [ ] An outbound guard rechecks current principal/scope, source revision, disclosure and provider policy for each physical attempt, including embedding, reranking, model retry, repair and compaction. Callers receive a reusable dependency-aware authorization result.
- [ ] Changing a governing fact after a read and before send or commit invalidates affected evidence, cached excerpts, plan text, handoffs and native continuation; reconstruct from permitted dependencies or stop safely.
- [ ] Unknown or withheld material contributes no identifying clues, titles, counts or raw diagnostics to reader-facing limitations. Independently permitted evidence remains usable.
- [ ] Tests inspect actual fake-provider payloads for a read/send/commit interleaving, including derived artifacts. State the boundary honestly: data already transmitted cannot be recalled and network transmission cannot be atomically revoked.

## Independent demonstration

Read permitted and withheld fixtures, revoke one permitted source immediately before a recorded outbound attempt, and show the outgoing payload and resulting safe limitation retain only independent permitted content.

## Scope boundary

Own disclosure and invalidation enforcement, not IR-250's institutional policy/backfill. Synthetic fixtures can exercise development before that prerequisite, but ordinary real-Record acceptance stays blocked.

## Delivery and evidence

Include the minimal persistence, authenticated entry point, public projection or operator surface, and behavioral tests needed for this outcome. Reuse existing reader components where no new control is needed. Each feature must land with its own applicable tests; later integration tickets do not postpone them.

Use real restricted-role PostgreSQL and independent workers where database permissions, leases, races or shared limits are claimed. A deterministic provider proves contracts, never paid capacity or human-reviewed quality. Record actual checks, failures and unavailable environments.

Keep prerequisites independently green through additive expansion, controlled migration and final contraction. Preserve current institutional authority, one writer per dataset, source/disclosure rules and the original AskRun allowance. Ticket publication does not authorize a live provider campaign, deployment, traffic switch or data reset. A PR and independent human approval remain required.
