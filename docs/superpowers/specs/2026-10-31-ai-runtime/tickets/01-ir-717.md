# IR-717: Open a research session with a verified reader identity

**Jira:** [IR-717](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-717)

## Parent

[IR-712](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-712) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

A signed-in reader opens an owned research session; the AI runtime can verify the delegated identity without holding an issuer secret or acquiring unrestricted research access.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Django issues a bounded grant for an existing, owned run/session binding; issuing a grant never creates a fictitious AskRun or trusts a client-supplied reader ID. Existing Django-owned runs remain authoritative until IR-722 transfers ownership.
- [ ] Real PostgreSQL verification rejects forged signatures, wrong key/audience/deployment, expired validity, revoked account/generation and wrong run or scope. A raw session setting cannot establish a principal.
- [ ] Ordinary authenticated Django Record reads require no Conversation/AskRun; reader-run and ingestion-purpose grants cannot be replayed across purposes. Job execution and canonical work-item validation are completed in IR-737.
- [ ] Runtime roles cannot mint grants, read the HMAC key, own protected institutional tables or bypass row protection. Verifier privileges/search paths and transaction-local identity remain safe across pooled readers, rollback and retry.
- [ ] Renewal revalidates current ownership/account/scope and preserves the original run allowance; key identifiers permit a bounded rotation overlap. Invalid or unavailable authorization produces a safe public session outcome.

## Independent demonstration

With two authenticated readers and actual restricted PostgreSQL roles, open one owned session, try foreign/forged/expired grants and reuse the same pool connection; show that no identity or protected content crosses sessions.

## Scope boundary

Identity expansion/prefactor first. Preserve the existing reader route; no full research-policy switch, institutional writer transfer or model call is enabled here.
