# IR-739: Promote or roll back a verified Embedding Space safely

**Jira:** [IR-739](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-739)

## Parent

[IR-716](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-716) under [IR-146](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-146). Authority: ADR-049 and the accepted parent specification. Do not close or modify the parent.

## What to build

An operator can deliberately activate a complete compatible embedding upgrade or restore a compatible prior version without exposing revoked research.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Refuse promotion for incomplete source coverage, wrong dimensions/query-document semantics, failed retrieval checks or stale canonical revisions.
- [ ] Activate the verified Record/Passage space pair atomically; readers and in-flight runs obey the declared pin/compatibility contract rather than mixing vector spaces.
- [ ] Keep a compatible previous revision for deliberate rollback. Current access/deletion/disclosure constraints still apply; rollback cannot resurrect revoked sources.
- [ ] Exercise promotion/query races and a failed activation transaction against real PostgreSQL; partial activation leaves the previous active space intact.
- [ ] Expose a safe operator check/dry-run/result and verify the reader's search/current-version citation before and after promotion/rollback without authorizing a live production upgrade.

## Independent demonstration

Refuse an incomplete two-space fixture, promote the complete one, delete/revoke a source and roll back; query results must remain compatible and currently authorized throughout.

## Scope boundary

Own activation and rollback only. Building and schema expansion belong to IR-752; production upgrade execution remains a deliberate operational action.

## Spec coverage

## Delivery and evidence

Include the minimal persistence, authenticated entry point, public projection or operator surface, and behavioral tests needed for this outcome. Reuse existing reader components where no new control is needed. Each feature must land with its own applicable tests; later integration tickets do not postpone them.

Use real restricted-role PostgreSQL and independent workers where database permissions, leases, races or shared limits are claimed. A deterministic provider proves contracts, never paid capacity or human-reviewed quality. Record actual checks, failures and unavailable environments.

Keep prerequisites independently green through additive expansion, controlled migration and final contraction. Preserve current institutional authority, one writer per dataset, source/disclosure rules and the original AskRun allowance. Ticket publication does not authorize a live provider campaign, deployment, traffic switch or data reset. A PR and independent human approval remain required.
