Folio Ribbon, in motion.

The artwork is the original concept, unchanged: its four maroon pieces were cut apart along the white lines already in the drawing, so each can move on its own. The I is never drawn. It appears in the space the pieces leave when they settle.

Opening · plays once on load

  1. 0 ms · the upper-left leaf unfolds from the spine
  2. 120 ms · the lower-right leaf unfolds to meet it
  3. 380 ms · both ribbons unspool outward from the gutter, and the I is left standing between them
  4. 900 ms · the wordmark rises, letter by letter · finished by 1.6 s

Inverse · on maroon

Replays together with the opening.

Loading · loops

The concept board's loading storyboard, played in a 2 s loop: one leaf, then its partner, then the ribbons close and the I appears. Under reduced motion it becomes a slow fade.
