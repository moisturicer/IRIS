Abstract

This study identifies the activity preferences and level of physical activity participation of the administrative employees of Cebu Institute of Technology – University. It uses a descriptive method, with a survey questionnaire and the International Physical Activity Questionnaire (IPAQ) long version as instruments. Means and percentages were computed and presented in tables and figures. Zumba, aero dance, yoga, badminton, bowling and taebo were the five most preferred activities, and the overall level of participation across the four dimensions was moderate. A continuing fitness programme is recommended to raise leisure-related activity among employees.
