## Part 7 — Background the team asserted, which the respondent did not dispute

The questionnaire's Section 1 reconstructed IPAMS from the running system. The respondent
corrected the documents table (EB1–EB4) and commented on record types (EB5–EB9). **The following
assertions drew no comment, which is weak evidence of accuracy — silence on a reconstruction is
not confirmation of it.**

- **Record-type mechanics.** A Proposal is required before a Thesis/Research. A Thesis/Research
  is created only from inside its Proposal, and only by the Proposal's owners. Any non-guest may
  create a Proposal or a Project.
- **Statuses IPAMS tracks.** Patent application: *For Application → Reviewed → Filed → Approved
  or Disapproved*, with comments at Reviewed and Filed. Commercialization: *For Commercialization
  → Reviewed*, with recommendations.
- **Fields IPAMS collects on every record.** Name, Course, Year, Subject, Adviser, Title, Date
  Uploaded, Date Accomplished, Date Presented, Funding Source, Collaborators, and Documentation
  (as a zipped file).
- **The IPAMS grouping** of Patent, Utility Model, Industrial Design, Trademark and Copyright
  under a single "Patent Search Report" heading. *(EB13 comments on what those five mean, not on
  the grouping.)*

## Part 8 — What the respondent was told IRIS does

The questionnaire's Background told the respondent, in these words, what the AI features do. Any
answer about AI — above all **EB27** — was given against this description and no other:

> IRIS […] also adds AI features: question answering with citations, paper overviews, and
> similar-work detection. These features send paper text to US-based services (Voyage AI, and
> Groq or OpenRouter), which state they don't retain it or use it for training.

**The description is accurate but incomplete in one way that matters.** It says the services
*state* they don't train on the content. For Voyage that is true only once an account-level
opt-out toggle is flipped, which requires a payment method and is **not retroactive** — see
[ADR-015 §Security Impact](../adr/015-voyage-embedding-and-reranking.md) requirement 2. The
respondent was not told that, and EB27 was answered without it.
