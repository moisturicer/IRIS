## Context and decision

The owner prioritizes scalability, performance, future model upgrades, local research tools, and large-context research. Keeping every tool behind Django adds runtime coupling; moving unrestricted queries into the AI runtime would instead bypass current access rules.

1. **Target placement:** use a FastAPI-hosted AI runtime for agent execution and local research tools, with controlled database access and one authoritative institutional access implementation. Django retains accounts, authoritative Records and institutional workflow. Row-level protection is accepted in decision 10; exact identity propagation and privilege implementation remain unresolved.
2. **Research allowance:** detailed research may use more time and paid allowance than simple requests, within enforced limits. Evaluate research quality separately from response time. No numeric deadline, concurrency, context occupancy, or spending limit is accepted here.
3. **Development Conversations:** existing Conversations are development data and may be discarded during a deliberate cutover. Preserving historical Conversations and their citation addresses is not a migration requirement. Records, Manuscripts, accounts, review history and other datasets are outside this exception. No cleanup action is approved for immediate execution by this record.
4. **AI data ownership:** after cutover, the AI runtime is the sole authoritative writer of Conversations, Turns, citations, AskRuns, AI progress and AI spending records. Institutional data remains Django-owned. Define atomic commitment, cross-owner deletion and migration behavior in the detailed specs; avoid competing writers.
5. **Initial storage topology:** use one PostgreSQL deployment with logically separated ownership and restricted database accounts. Separate databases are not required initially; reconsider when measurements establish a need. Exact schemas, privileges and policy implementation remain open.
6. **Replacement and evaluation:** build and test incrementally, make a controlled cutover, then retire the old AI execution implementation. Do not maintain two permanent AI paths. Use [IR-605](https://citiris.atlassian.net/browse/IR-605)'s evaluation workflow as the basis for validating replacement, retaining human review, locked data, honest cost and safety reporting. Define extensions for the new runtime and new capabilities in the specs; this decision neither changes evaluation thresholds nor proves a campaign passed. Preserve a tested deployment rollback plan.
7. **Shared research access:** the database enforces one research-access implementation for both runtimes, using restricted credentials. Do not maintain separate Django/FastAPI interpretations. Preserve current intended reader access through parity tests; reader access and permission to transmit content to a provider remain separate. Decision 10 chooses the baseline mechanism; exact policies/functions remain to be specified.
8. **Identity authority:** Django remains the sole account/login authority. The AI runtime verifies delegated reader identity and permitted scope and checks current access during research. A caller-supplied database user-ID variable is not identity verification. Token format, expiry, revocation and database identity propagation require detailed specification.
9. **Evaluation comparisons:** extend IR-605 with two separately reported evaluations: matched model/corpus/questions/allowances to compare architectures, and the new runtime's larger-allowance capability evaluation to measure quality/cost tradeoffs. Add versioned long-document, long-Conversation, compaction and interrupted-research cases. Freeze held-out cases before inspecting outputs; preserve named human review and safety gates. Changed allowances cannot be presented as a matched-condition architecture improvement.
10. **Research-read protection:** row-level protection is the baseline for ordinary application research reads, with predefined research operations where useful. Runtime accounts must not own protected tables or bypass row protection. Verified reader identity and safe pooled-connection handling are required; models receive neither credentials nor arbitrary SQL execution.
11. **Changing access:** check current access before further provider requests and final delivery. Invalidate and reconstruct affected evidence, summaries and other derived context when permissions change, then continue within the remaining allowance. Do not use the original permission snapshot to justify a later answer. Do not reveal protected details when explaining gaps. Already-transmitted text cannot be recalled.
12. **Unknown disclosure facts:** deny transmission of affected content when consent/embargo or other governing facts cannot establish permission. Continue with independently permitted evidence where useful; otherwise report insufficient permitted evidence or inability to complete the task. This retains ADR-015's fail-closed policy and ADR-042's useful partial-result behavior; it does not create a new transmission approval.
13. **Disconnect versus cancellation:** a browser disconnect does not cancel an AskRun. Explicit Stop cancels it; original time/spending bounds apply while the reader is away.
14. **Checkpoint recovery:** resume from durable verified progress with current access/source checks and the same remaining allowance. Repeat only work that cannot safely be recovered. Unknown remote outcomes remain accountable; prevent duplicate final Turns without promising exactly-once provider billing.
15. **Scheduling:** protect short interactive capacity, separate ingestion work, and use fair research scheduling with per-reader bounds. Deep research must not starve. Actual queue/concurrency values require measurement and explicit configuration.
16. **Agent autonomy:** one model may plan, select permitted research tools, assess evidence sufficiency and draft answers without per-tool confirmation. Ask the reader when ambiguity materially prevents progress. Scope, permissions, allowance and no-progress bounds remain application-enforced. Institutional data is read-only; updating AI-owned task state does not authorize changing Records or review decisions.

## Tradeoffs

The destination enables independent AI execution and provider/context upgrades and removes per-tool Django calls. It adds access-policy migration, database privilege design, durable execution ownership, compatibility testing and operational work. A large context window does not itself guarantee better answers or more throughput.

Discarding development Conversations reduces migration complexity but sacrifices their history and links. It does not justify clearing unrelated data or assuming that paid-attempt audit and research artifacts are disposable.

One PostgreSQL deployment avoids immediate cross-database synchronization but shares capacity between institutional and AI workloads. Logical ownership and restricted privileges must be paired with bounded connections, query work and load evidence. Independent runtime scaling alone does not increase database capacity.

## Reconciliation and limits

- **ADR-014:** the target direction revisits its prohibition on gateway retrieval/database access. Its protective intent remains: one institutional authorization policy, verified identity, current access/disclosure checks, restricted privileges and no model-controlled database credentials. Do not grant access or deploy this topology until the detailed access specification is approved and verified.
- **ADR-024:** moving embedding/indexing execution is a later publication/upgrade decision. That workload is not moved by this record.
- **ADR-026 and ADR-042:** decision 16 accepts the direction of one model conducting research through answer drafting, replacing the separate PLAN/ANSWER handoff in the target. Detailed memory, final validation, provider-task mapping and reader-protocol changes remain open and must be reconciled in the specs before implementation. Preserve scope, source safety and validated delivery requirements.
- **ADR-040/041:** detailed proposals and numeric production limits are not retroactively accepted. Their relevant safety requirements remain part of the design work.
