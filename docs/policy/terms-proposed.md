# IRIS Terms of Service

Proposed version 1.0 · Prepared 4 November 2026

Complete proposed wording for the CIT-U pilot. This version is awaiting institutional approval and is not yet effective.

## 1. The service

IRIS supports the Cebu Institute of Technology – University research and intellectual property disclosure workflow. It lets authorized users submit research, manage versions and supporting files, obtain institutional review, discover published research and use configured research assistance features.

These Terms cover the institutional pilot. Access to IRIS does not replace university research, ethics, intellectual property or records rules, an agreement with a sponsor, or an obligation to a research participant.

## 2. Your account

Provide accurate registration and affiliation details, use your own institutional email address, and protect your credentials. Email verification and role approval are separate steps. Requesting a role does not grant its permissions. Do not share an account or act under another person's identity.

If your affiliation or responsibilities change, ask RDCO to review your access. Report suspected account compromise promptly to the university office responsible for your IRIS account.

## 3. Your research and responsibilities

Submit only material you have authority to provide for the intended review, audience and processing. Obtain any necessary coauthor, sponsor, rights-holder and participant permissions. Remove unnecessary personal information and restricted material before uploading. Do not place confidential information in a question simply because IRIS can answer it.

Uploading does not establish ownership, patentability, ethical approval or permission from another person. These Terms do not transfer ownership of research to the developers or create new university ownership rights. Existing rights remain governed by applicable law, university policy and agreements.

By submitting authorized material, you permit its storage, copying, text extraction, indexing, review and display to the extent needed for the IRIS workflow and the permissions applicable to that material. This permission does not authorize unrelated advertising, sale of the material or unrestricted model training. External AI processing requires the separate conditions described below; uploading alone is not permission for every external use.

## 4. Who can read a submission

Access depends on ownership, assigned adviser, office role, publication state and document permissions. Office staff may have access beyond the records individually assigned to them. System administrators have privileged access needed to operate and support the service.

Published catalogue records can be available to signed-in users. Publication in IRIS does not mean anonymous internet access, an open licence, or permission to redistribute a manuscript. A document may have additional download restrictions. Do not bypass those restrictions or redistribute material without authority.

The review workflow records institutional decisions. Submission, an office clearance and publication are distinct actions. None by itself settles intellectual property ownership or grants permissions outside the recorded decision.

## 5. Embargoes and intellectual property

An IRIS embargo concerns whether research content may be sent to external AI providers. **It does not, by itself, make a record private or prevent institutional publication.** If a confidentiality agreement also restricts human access or publication, raise that restriction with your adviser and RDCO before submitting or publishing; an embargo toggle alone is insufficient.

A paper's embargo status is separate from its publication status, intellectual property status and disclosure consent. An unanswered embargo question is treated as restricted for external AI processing. An active embargo remains active after its review date: a date is a reminder, not permission to release research.

Under the adopted design, owners or the assigned adviser may set or extend an embargo. A new manuscript version inherits the existing embargo answer; uploading a revision does not clear a restriction or create fresh disclosure authorization. Availability of the corresponding controls depends on the pilot's implementation.

The release rules adopted for IRIS reserve embargo release to the assigned adviser or RDCO, and IP release to KTTO. Owners must not assume that a release action is available in the current pilot or that changing a tag is an authorized substitute. Ask the responsible office when a control is unavailable. A paper subject to both restrictions needs both resolved, with disclosure consent still applicable.

Give accurate embargo information at submission and promptly report new or extended restrictions. A later restriction cannot recall text already transmitted, copies already downloaded or information already seen. Do not rely on a later embargo to undo an earlier disclosure.

## 6. AI research assistance

When enabled, AI features may send questions, relevant conversation context and permitted research text to configured external providers. Embedding and reranking support search; language models generate answers and summaries. A summary can require text from the whole manuscript. Read the [Privacy Policy](privacy-proposed.md) before using these features.

AI answers may be wrong, incomplete or unsupported. Check the cited paper and underlying evidence. An answer is not an institutional clearance, patent opinion, ethics approval or professional determination. Missing AI results do not establish that research does not exist: access restrictions, embargoes, indexing and provider availability can affect results.

## 7. Responsible use

Do not impersonate others, attempt unauthorized access, interfere with the service, bypass disclosure or download controls, upload malicious files, or disclose material you are not authorized to provide. Respect confidentiality and the rights of other people when reading, downloading, quoting or submitting research.

Report an access error, inappropriate disclosure or suspected misuse to RDCO or the university office responsible for the record. Avoid including additional sensitive material in the initial report. University action remains subject to its applicable authority and procedures; these Terms do not give the development team independent disciplinary powers.

## 8. Corrections, deletion and leaving the service

Use available profile and record controls for permitted corrections. Ask RDCO about corrections or deletion that require institutional review. Deletion of a research record may hide it rather than erase all files, audit history, backups and external copies. Deleting a conversation is distinct from deleting research or withdrawing disclosure consent.

Stopping use of IRIS does not automatically erase institutional records or cancel obligations attached to a submission. The Privacy Policy explains current retention behavior and how to raise a privacy concern.

## 9. Availability and changes

The pilot may be interrupted for maintenance, failures or changes. Keep your own authorized originals; do not use IRIS as your only copy of important work. No continuous availability or permanent hosting commitment is made by this pilot wording.

A revised policy must identify its version and effective date. These proposed Terms do not retroactively convert historical signup consent into acceptance of a document that did not exist. Any new acceptance process or access restriction must be separately implemented and communicated.

## 10. Questions

For research workflow and account questions, approach RDCO through an established university channel. For privacy concerns, approach the university Data Protection Officer. Use a verified university directory or an existing institutional contact; this proposal supplies no unverified email address or support endpoint.
