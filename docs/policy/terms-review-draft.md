# IRIS Terms of Service — institutional review draft

**DRAFT FOR REVIEW — NOT EFFECTIVE.** Draft identifier: `terms-review-2026-10-30-01`. No effective date or institutional approval has been assigned. This draft does not replace the interim data notice, create an agreement, or authorize deployment. Every **institutional decision required** marker must be resolved before publication.

## Scope and operator

These proposed Terms cover the CIT-U institutional pilot of IRIS, a system for research records, intellectual property disclosures, institutional review and research assistance. They do not cover a commercial service for other institutions.

**Institutional decision required — T1:** Confirm the operator's full legal identity, authority to offer the pilot, eligible users, any arrangements for users below the applicable age of consent, and the person authorized to approve these Terms. CIT-U is the proposed institutional owner; that proposal is not evidence of institutional authorization.

## Accounts and policy acceptance

Use accurate registration details and protect your account credentials. Verification of an email address and approval of a role are separate steps; a requested role does not itself grant that role's access.

**Proposed future acceptance design:** You will be asked to accept identified Terms and separately acknowledge the Privacy Policy. Any processing consent required for a particular purpose will be identified separately. A privacy acknowledgment does not stand for unrestricted processing consent. Acceptance at account creation does not substitute for consent associated with an individual research disclosure.

Existing users would review the approved Terms before new submissions or new Ask IRIS questions. The proposal preserves authorized access to existing information and the privacy contact process. These controls are not implemented by this draft, and historical signup booleans will not be treated as acceptance of these Terms.

**Institutional decision required — T2:** Approve the acceptance wording, effective date, rollout, notice period and consequences of refusal. Confirm how policy changes affect existing users and which changes require renewed acceptance or consent.

## Research permissions and confidentiality

Submit only material you are authorized to provide for the intended processing and audience. Respect confidentiality, coauthor permissions, research-participant protections and applicable institutional research and IP rules. Uploading a document does not establish ownership or permission from others whose work or information appears in it.

The proposed Terms do not transfer ownership of research to CIT-U or the developers. They propose only the permissions needed to store, process, review and display material under approved institutional rules. They do not settle ownership already governed by law, an existing agreement or university policy.

**Institutional decision required — T3:** Supply the applicable IP/research rules and approve the precise scope, duration and termination of processing/display permissions, including extraction, AI processing, coauthored work, confidential research and third-party personal information. A service-use permission must not be represented as permission for unrelated research or model training.

## Research access and review

**Current behavior to verify against the pilot:** Access depends on role, ownership, adviser assignment and publication state. Published catalogue records are available to signed-in users; publication in IRIS is not a statement that the record is accessible anonymously on the internet. Office-role access is broader than assignment to an individual review. System administrators also have privileged operational access.

Research review decisions are made through the institutional workflow. Submission, clearance, publication in IRIS and an AI response do not by themselves establish IP ownership or approval for uses beyond the workflow.

**Institutional decision required — T4:** Approve the audience for each record/document category, office and administrator access, disclosure/publication controls, and any changes needed to align implementation with institutional confidentiality requirements.

## Ask IRIS and AI features

When enabled and configured, AI features can process your questions and research passages through external providers. IRIS stores conversations and associated data, including questions, answers, citations and model-generated reasoning. The [Privacy Policy draft](privacy-review-draft.md) explains the current evidence and unresolved handling rules.

AI output can contain errors or omit relevant material. Check the underlying sources before relying on an answer. An AI answer is not an institutional research clearance, legal determination or grant of research rights.

**Institutional decision required — T5:** Approve actual providers, enabled features, permitted inputs, transfer arrangements and any consent or alternative process required for AI use. Do not infer that all implemented providers are deployed, that processing is local, or that providers retain no data.

## Acceptable use and account security

Do not impersonate another user, share credentials, bypass access controls, seek records you are not authorized to access, or use IRIS to disclose material you are not authorized to provide. Report suspected account compromise or inappropriate disclosure through the approved reporting channel once established.

**Institutional decision required — T6:** Confirm the reporting contact and procedures, responsibilities for investigating reports, and any additional institutional acceptable-use rules. Do not publish a contact that has not agreed to handle reports.

## Reports, restrictions and appeals

**Institutional decision required — T7:** Establish who may restrict an account or remove access, the grounds and scope of restrictions, emergency measures, notice, review and appeal arrangements, and treatment of existing submissions. Approve how authorized access to existing information and privacy requests is preserved. This draft creates no new suspension power and promises no unimplemented appeal tool.
